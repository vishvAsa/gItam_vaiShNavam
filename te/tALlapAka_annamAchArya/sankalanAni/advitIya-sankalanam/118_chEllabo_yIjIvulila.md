**Cellabō Yījīvulila**  
चॆल्लबो यीजीवु लिल जेसिनपाप मॆन्तो  
वुल्लमुन नुन्नहरि वूरके दव्वाय ॥॥ चॆल्लबो ॥  
कन्नचोटने हरि कलडन्नवारिकि  
विन्नचोटने विष्णुडु विवेकुलकु  
वुन्नति गॊलुवलेक वॊद्दनुण्डगा गॊन्दरु  
मिन्नुमीद वॆदकेरु मितिमीऱ जदिवि ॥॥ चॆल्लबो ॥  
पट्टिनदे ब्रह्ममु परमार्थवेत्तलकु  
तिट्टुलना दैवमु दिव्युलकुनु  
मुट्टि चेत मॊक्कलेरु मुन्दटने वुण्डगानु  
बट्टबयलु पाकेरु बहुकर्मविदुल ॥॥ चॆल्लबो ॥  
ऊपिरिलो देवुडुन्नाडु योगीन्द्रुलकु  
दापुन नुन्नाडु हरिदासुलकुनु  
येपुन श्रीवेङ्कटेशु नेचि शरणनलेक  
चापलान वॆदकेरु सकलदेवतल ॥॥ चॆल्लबो ॥  
**cellabō yījīvu lila jēsinapāpa mentō**  
**vullamuna nunnahari vūrakē davvāya .... cellabō ..**  
**kannacōṭanē hari kalaḍannavāriki**  
**vinnacōṭanē viṣṇuḍu vivēkulaku**  
**vunnati goluvalēka voddanuṇḍagā gondaru**  
**minnumīda vedakēru mitimīṟa jadivi .... cellabō ..**  
**paṭṭinadē brahmamu paramārthavēttalaku**  
**tiṭṭulanā daivamu divyulakunu**  
**muṭṭi cēta mokkalēru mundaṭanē vuṇḍagānu**  
**baṭṭabayalu pākēru bahukarmavidula .... cellabō ..**  
**ūpirilō dēvuḍunnāḍu yōgīndrulaku**  
**dāpuna nunnāḍu haridāsulakunu**  
**yēpuna śrīvēṅkaṭēśu nēci śaraṇanalēka**  
**cāpalāna vedakēru sakaladēvatala .... cellabō ..**  
The sins committed by these Jivas with arrogance is so much  
(that) Hari who resides in the heart, became distant\!  
Wherever they see, there is Hari, for those who say that Hari exists  
Wherever they listen, it is ‘Vishnu’ for the learned  
Not worshipping Him in the higher sense, though He is within  
some seek Him in the sky, even after studying extensively\!  
Whatever they hold on to, it is Brahman, for the knowers of the supreme truth  
Even in a curse there is divinity for the divine\!  
Some do not bow down and touch, even when (HE is) present in front  
They seek all around, by performing various karmas  
In the breath itself God exists, for the Yogis  
Close by He exists for the Hari-daasas  
Without surrendering unto Lord SreeVenkateshwara  
some seek, with fickle-mindedness, all other Gods\!\!
