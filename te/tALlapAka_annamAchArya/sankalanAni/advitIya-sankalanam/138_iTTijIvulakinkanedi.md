**Iṭṭijīvulakiṅkanēdi**  
इट्टि जीवुल किङ्क नेदि वाटि  
दट्टमै देवुड नीवे दयजातुगाका ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
तनजन्मविदु(धु?) लनु दलचु नॊक्कॊकवेळ  
वॊनर मऱचु नट्टॆ वॊक्कॊकवेळ  
विनु बुराणकथलु विवरिञ्चि यॆकवेळ  
पॆनचि सन्देहमुलॆ पॆञ्चु नॊकवेळ ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
विसिगि संसारमन्दु विरतुडौ नॊकवेळ  
वॊसगि यन्दॆ मत्तुडौ नॊकवेळ  
पसिगॊनि यिन्द्रियालबण्टै वुण्डु नॊकवेळ  
मुसिपितो दैवानकु मॊक्कु नॊक्कवेळ ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
कोरि तपमुलु चेसि गुणिЕौ ता नॊकवेळ  
वूरके अलसिवुण्डु नॊक्कवेळ  
यीरीति श्रीवेङ्कटेश यॆदलो नी वुण्डगानु  
बीरान नीके मॊऱवॆट्टु नॊकवेळ ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
**iṭṭi jīvula kiṅka nēdi vāṭi**  
**daṭṭamai dēvuḍa nīvē dayajūtugākā .... iṭṭi ..**  
**tanajanmavidu(dhu?) lanu dalacu nokkokavēḷa**  
**vonara maṟacu naṭṭe vokkokavēḷa**  
**vinu burāṇakathalu vivariñci yokavēḷa**  
**penaci sandēhamule peñcu nokavēḷa .... iṭṭi ..**  
**visigi saṃsāramandu viratuḍau nokavēḷa**  
**vosagi yande mattuḍau nokavēḷa**  
**pasigoni yindriyālabaṇṭai vuṇḍu nokavēḷa**  
**musipitō daivānaku mokku nokkavēḷa .... iṭṭi ..**  
**kōri tapamulu cēsi guṇiyau tā nokavēḷa**  
**vūrakē alasivuṇḍu nokkavēḷa**  
**yīrīti śrīvēṅkaṭēśa yedalō nī vuṇḍagānu**  
**bīrāna nīkē moṟaveṭṭu nokavēḷa .... iṭṭi ..**  
For these kind of Jivas, where is the steadiness?  
Until You, O Lord, bestow Your grace abundantly\!  
Thinking about one's life duties at times  
Quickly forgetting the same at other times\!  
Ardently listening to the Puranic stores at times  
Nurturing only doubts (in the mind) at other times\!  
Having become vexed with Samsara, attaining dispassion at times  
Becoming intoxicated by the same at other times\!  
Getting attracted and becoming a slave of the senses at times  
Becoming fatigued and bowing down to the Lord at other times\!  
Wishfully engaging in penance and becoming good natured at times  
Becoming indolent at other times\!  
O Venkatesha, even when You reside within the heart  
feebly, unto You, we plead only at times\!\!
