**Cāladāharināma Saukhyāmṛtamu**  
चालदाहरिनाम सौख्यामृतमु दमकु  
चालदा हितवैन चवुलॆल्ल नॊसग ॥॥चालदा॥  
इदियॆकटि हरिनाम मिन्तैन जालदा  
चॆदर की जन्ममुल चॆऱलु विडिपिञ्च  
मदि नॊकटॆ हरिनाममन्त्र मदि चालदा  
पदिवेलु नरककूपमुल वॆडलिञ्च ॥॥चालदा॥  
कलदॊकटि हरिनामकनकाद्रि चालदा  
तॊलगुमनि दारिद्यदोषम्बु चॆऱच  
तॆलिवॊकटि हरिनामदीप मदि चालदा  
कलुषम्पु कठिन चीकटि पारद्रोल॥॥चालदा॥  
तगुवेङ्कटेशु कीर्तन मॊकटि चालदा  
जगमुलो कल्पभूजम्बुवलॆ नुण्ड  
सॊगिसि यी विभुनि दासुल करुण चालदा  
नगवु जापुलनु नुन्नत मॆपुडु जाप ॥॥चालदा॥  
**cāladāharināma saukhyāmṛtamu damaku**  
**cāladā hitavaina cavulella nosaga ....cāladā..**  
**idiyokaṭi harināma mintaina jāladā**  
**cedara kī janmamula ceṟalu viḍipiñca**  
**madi nokaṭe harināmamantra madi cāladā**  
**padivēlu narakakūpamula veḍaliñca.. ..cāladā..**  
**kaladokaṭi harināmakanakādri cāladā**  
**tolagumani dāridyadōṣambu ceṟaca**  
**telivokaṭi harināmadīpa madi cāladā**  
**kaluṣampu kaṭhina cīkaṭi pāradrōla....cāladā..**  
**taguvēṅkaṭēśu kīrtana mokaṭi cāladā**  
**jagamulō kalpabhūjambuvale nuṇḍa**  
**sogisi yī vibhuni dāsula karuṇa cāladā**  
**nagavu jūpulanu nunnata mepuḍu jūpa ....cāladā..**  
Does it not suffice, the pleasurable nectar of the name of Hari, for you?  
Does it not suffice, to bestow all benevolent wishes?  
This one name of Hari, is it not enough,  
to free us from the shackles of this birth?  
This one name of Hari, is it not enough, when present in the heart,  
to liberate us from even thousands of dungeons of hell?  
The one which is there, the golden mountain like name of Hari,  
is it not enough to abolish the curse of penury?  
The knowledge called the lamp of the name of Hari, is it not enough  
to drive away the imperfections of the deep darkness of ignorance?  
Just one benevolent Venkatesha Kirtana, does it not suffice,  
being the wish yielding tree in this world?  
Joyfully, the compassion of the Lord’s devotees, is it not enough  
to bestow eternal prosperity?
