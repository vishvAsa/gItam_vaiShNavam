**Cūḍarammā Satulāla**  
चूडरम्मा सतुलाल सोबान पाडरम्म  
कूडुन्नदि पति चूडिकुडुत नाञ्चारि ॥॥चूडरम्मा॥  
श्रीमहालक्ष्मि यट सिङ्गारालके मरुदु  
कामुनि तल्लियट चक्कदनालके मरुदु  
सोमुनि तोबुट्टवट सॊम्पु कळलके मरुदु  
कोमलाङ्गि ई चूडिकुडुत नाञ्चारि ॥॥चूडरम्मा॥  
कलशाब्धि कूतुरट गम्भीरालके मरुदु  
तलप लोकमातयट दय मरि एमरुदु  
जलज निवासिनियट चल्लदन मेमरुदु  
कॊलदि मीर ई चूडिकुडुत नाञ्चारि ॥॥चूडरम्मा॥  
अमर वन्दितयट अट्टे महिम एमरुदु  
अमृतमु चुट्टमट आनन्दालकेमरुदु  
तमितो श्री वेङ्कटेशु तानॆ वच्चि पॆण्ड्लाडे  
कॊमॆर वयस्सु ई चूडिकुडुत नाञ्चारि ॥॥चूडरम्मा॥  
**cūḍarammā satulāla sōbāna pāḍaramma**  
**kūḍunnadi pati cūḍikuḍuta nāñcāri ....cūḍarammā..**  
**śrīmahālakṣmi yaṭa siṅgārālakē marudu**  
**kāmuni talliyaṭa cakkadanālakē marudu**  
**sōmuni tōbuṭṭavaṭa sompu kaḷalakē marudu**  
**kōmalāṅgi ī cūḍikuḍuta nāñcāri ....cūḍarammā..**  
**kalaśābdhi kūturaṭa gambhīrālakē marudu**  
**talapa lōkamātayaṭa daya mari ēmarudu**  
**jalaja nivāsiniyaṭa calladana mēmarudu**  
**koladi mīra ī cūḍikuḍuta nāñcāri ....cūḍarammā..**  
**amara vanditayaṭa aṭṭē mahima ēmarudu**  
**amṛtamu cuṭṭamaṭa ānandālakēmarudu**  
**tamitō śrī vēṅkaṭēśu tāne vacci peṇḍlāḍē**  
**komera vayassu ī cūḍikuḍuta nāñcāri ....cūḍarammā..**  
Behold O women folk\! Sing the songs of benediction\!  
She is alongside Her Lord, this divine consort with lustrous hair\!  
As She Herself is Sree Mahalakshmi, can there be any dearth for adornments?  
As She is the mother of Manmatha, can there be any dearth for beauty?  
She being the co-born of the Moon, can there be any dearth for elegant grace?  
She is the one with a tender body, this divine consort with lustrous hair\!  
Since She is the daughter of the ocean, can Her sublimity ever fall short?  
She being the mother of all the worlds, can Her compassion ever fall short?  
As She resides the lotus, can Her cool nature ever fall short?  
The limitless one is She, this divine consort with lustrous hair\!  
Since She is the one worshipped even by divinities, can Her glory ever fall short?  
Since Amrutha is Her relative, can Her joy & bliss ever fall short?  
Eagerly, Lord Sree Venkatesha Himself came to wed Her,  
this young damsel is the divine consort with lustrous hair\!
