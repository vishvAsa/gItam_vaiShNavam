**Danujulu Ganiri Tatvamidi**  
दनुजुलु गनिरि तत्वमिदि  
वनजाक्षुनि कृप वसिञ्चनेला\!॥ दनु ॥  
हरि परमात्मु डनादि पुरुषुडु  
सुरलकु नरुलकु सुलभुडु  
हरि कोपमुतो नन्युल वरमुलु  
सरिगा वॆञ्चग जगमुललोन\!॥ दनु ॥  
वेदान्त विदुडु विष्णुडु कृष्णुडु  
श्रीदेविकि बति शेखरुडु  
पोदि बरस्तुति पुण्यमुकण्टे  
पादुग हरि दिट्टे पापमु मेलु\!॥ दनु ॥  
नारायणु डुन्नतु डच्युतु डुप  
कारुडु श्रीवॆङ्कटविभुडु  
चेरि यीतडे शिक्षिञ्च रक्षिञ्च  
ऒरदयिवमुलु वॊगि नितनि सरा\!॥ दनु ॥  
**danujulu ganiri tatvamidi**  
**vanajākṣuni kṛpa vasiñcanēlā\!.. danu ..**  
**hari paramātmu ḍanādi puruṣuḍu**  
**suralaku narulaku sulabhuḍu**  
**hari kōpamutō nanyula varamulu**  
**sarigā veñcaga jagamulalōna\!.. danu ..**  
**vēdānta viduḍu viṣṇuḍu kṛṣṇuḍu**  
**śrīdēviki bati śēkharuḍu**  
**pōdi barastuti puṇyamukaṇṭē**  
**pāduga hari diṭṭē pāpamu mēlu\!.. danu ..**  
**nārāyaṇu ḍunnatu ḍacyutu ḍupa**  
**kāruḍu śrīveṅkaṭavibhuḍu**  
**cēri yītaḍē śikṣiñca rakṣiñca**  
**oradayivamulu vogi nitani sarā\!.. danu ..**  
The demons propounded this philosophy,  
(which questions…….)  
“Why to live under the grace of the Lotus eyed one?”  
(Reply….)  
Hari is the supreme Lord, the ancient one  
For Devas and humans, the easily attainable one\!  
Hari’s anger and others boons,  
Can they ever be equated in this world?\!  
The one to be known through the Vedas, Vishnu\! Krishna\!  
He is the Lord and consort of Sree-Devi\!  
Than the mountain like merits gained by praising others,  
the sin accrued by the constant abuse of Hari is far better\!\!  
Narayana, the Highest of the highest, the indestructible one  
the all helpful Lord of Sree Venkata\!  
One the whole, He himself punishes and protects  
Can other Gods ever be equal to Him?\!\!
