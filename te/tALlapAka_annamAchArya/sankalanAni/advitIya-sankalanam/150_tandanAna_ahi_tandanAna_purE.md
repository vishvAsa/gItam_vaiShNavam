Tandanaa na ahi Tandanaa na pure\!  
Tandanaa na bhala Tandanaa na\!  
Brahman is One\! The Supreme Brahman is One\!  
Brahman is One\! The Supreme Brahman is One\!  
There is no disparity of high and low,  
for every one it is Sree Hari who is the inner self1  
The genus of all creatures is only one,  
Sree Hari is the supreme inner self of everyone\!  
The royal undisturbed sleep of the emperor is similar,  
to the sleep of an ordinary soldier\!  
The ‘high’ ground which a noble Brahmin treads,  
is similar to the ‘low’ ground where an untouchable lives\!  
The sexual pleasure of the demigods,  
and that of insects and animals, is same\!  
Day and night are same for the affluent,  
and so also for the destitute\!  
The hunger felt by those who can afford to buy good food,  
is same as that felt by one who lives on condemned food\!  
The breeze that carries foul smell,  
is also the one that carries fragrance\!  
The sun shines equally on an elephant,  
and on a street cur\!  
For the virtuous and the vicious alike,  
the Savior is only Sree Venkateshwara’s sacred name\!
