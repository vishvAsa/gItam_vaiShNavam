**Eṃta Sōdiñci Cūcinā**  
ऎन्त सोदिञ्चि चूचिना यॆन्नॆन्नि चदिविना  
वन्तलै ननी मूर्ति वॆस दॆलिसेमा ॥॥ ऎन्त ॥  
लोकमुलो समुद्रमु लोतु चॆप्पगरादट  
आकाश मिन्तन्तनि यनरादट  
मे कानि भूरेणुवुलु मिति वॆट्टगरादट  
श्रीकान्तुड नी महिम चॆप्पिचूप वशमा ॥॥ ऎन्त ॥  
अलगालि दॆच्चि मुडियगा गट्टगरादट  
वॆलय गालमु गण्टु वेयरादट  
कलय नलुदिक्कुल कड गानग रादट  
जलजाक्ष नीरूपु तलपोयगलना ॥॥ ऎन्त ॥  
केवलमैन नी माय गॆलवनेरादट  
भाविञ्चि मनसु जक्कबट्ट रादट  
देव यलमेल्मङ्गपतिवि नी शरणे गति  
श्री वेङ्कटेश निन्नु जेरि कॊल्ववशमा ॥॥ ऎन्त ॥  
**eṃta sōdiñci cūcinā yennenni cadivinā**  
**vantalai nanī mūrti vesa delisēmā .... eṃta ..**  
**lōkamulō samudramu lōtu ceppagarādaṭa**  
**ākāśa mintantani yanarādaṭa**  
**mē kāni bhūrēṇuvulu miti veṭṭagarādaṭa**  
**śrīkāntuḍa nī mahima ceppicūpa vaśamā .... eṃta ..**  
**alagāli decci muḍiyagā gaṭṭagarādaṭa**  
**velaya gālamu gaṇṭu vēyarādaṭa**  
**kalaya naludikkula kaḍa gānaga rādaṭa**  
**jalajākṣa nīrūpu talapōyagalanā .... eṃta ..**  
**kēvalamaina nī māya gelavanērādaṭa**  
**bhāviñci manasu jakkabaṭṭa rādaṭa**  
**dēva yalamēlmaṅgapativi nī śaraṇē gati**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭēśa ninnu jēri kolvavaśamā .... eṃta ..**  
How much ever one examines, how much ever one studies  
can one understand the glory of Your wonderful form?  
In this world, one cannot fathom the depth of the ocean  
The extent of the sky cannot be told  
The number of atoms of the earth cannot be counted  
O Lord of Sree, is it ever possible to explain Your glory\!  
It is not possible to tie up the air into a bundle  
One cannot obstruct the progress of time  
One cannot perceive the ends of the four directions  
O Lotus eyed Lord\! Can one ever imagine Your true form\!  
One cannot win over Your absolute Maya  
One cannot control the mind even upon resolving  
O consort of Alamelumanga\! Surrender unto You is the only means\!  
Sree Venkatesha\! Is it possible to reach unto You and worship You?\!
