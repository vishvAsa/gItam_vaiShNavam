**Edurulēka Carintu Reṭṭainā**  
ऎदुरुलेक चरिन्तु रॆट्टैना श्री वैष्णवुलु  
मदिञ्चि नेनुगुलकु मट्टुमेर वुन्नदा? ॥॥ऎदुरु॥  
भागीरथिलोन बापमुन्नदा पुण्य  
भोगपु वेदमुललो बॊङ्कु लुन्नवा?  
सागरपु टमृतपु चविलो जे दुन्नदा?  
आगति हरिदासुल कपराध मुन्नदा? ॥॥ऎदुरु॥  
आकसमुलोन नॆरु सन्दुन्नदा भूमि  
जेकॊनि रवि यॆदुट जीकटुन्नदा  
यीकड गामधेनुवु किय्यरानि वुन्नवा?  
कैकॊन्न प्रपन्नुलनु गरिहिञ्च नुन्नदा? ॥॥ऎदुरु॥  
हरिनाम मन्त्रमुलो नौगामुलुन्नवा?  
गुरुकृप गलिगिय गॊरतुन्नदा?  
सरवि श्री वेङ्कटेशु शरणन्नवारिकि  
विरसम्बु लन्दुलो वॆदकग गलदा? ॥॥ऎदुरु॥  
**edurulēka carintu reṭṭainā śrī vaiṣṇavulu**  
**madiñci nēnugulaku maṭṭumēra vunnadā? ....eduru..**  
**bhāgīrathilōna bāpamunnadā puṇya**  
**bhōgapu vēdamulalō boṅku lunnavā?**  
**sāgarapu ṭamṛtapu cavilō jē dunnadā?**  
**āgati haridāsula kaparādha munnadā? ....eduru..**  
**ākasamulōna neru sandunnadā bhūmi**  
**jēkoni ravi yeduṭa jīkaṭunnadā**  
**yīkaḍa gāmadhēnuvu kiyyarāni vunnavā?**  
**kaikonna prapannulanu garihiñca nunnadā? ....eduru..**  
**harināma mantramulō naugāmulunnavā?**  
**gurukṛpa galigiyu goratunnadā?**  
**saravi śrī vēṅkaṭēśu śaraṇannavāriki**  
**virasambu landulō vedakaga galadā? ....eduru..**  
Without any fear of opposition, they move about, the Sree Vaishnavas\!  
For the mighty elephant, is there any limit?  
Is there any sin in the pure Ganges?  
Are there any lies in the sacred Vedas?  
Is there any bitterness in the nectar born of the ocean?  
In this manner, is there any sin for the servants of Hari?  
Is there any breach in the expansive sky?  
Will there be any darkness in front of the sun?  
Is there anything that Kamadhenu cannot bestow?  
So too, can any blame befall those who willingly become Prappannas?  
In the pristine name of Hari, can there be any dualities?  
Even after begetting the grace of a Guru, can there be any shortcomings?  
For those who have surrendered to Sree Venkatapathi,  
can there be any ill-will even if you search?
