**Koluvuḍī Bhaktikoṇḍala**  
कॊलुवुडी भक्तिकॊण्डल कोनेटि  
निलयनि श्री निधियैन वानि ॥॥ कॊलु ॥  
आदि देवुनि अभवुनि साम  
वेदनाद विनोदुनि नॆर \-  
वादि जित प्रियनि निर्मल तत्त्व \-  
वादुल जीवनमैन वानि ॥॥ कॊलु ॥  
देवदेवुडैन दिव्युनि सर्व  
भावातीत स्वभावुनि  
श्रीवेङ्कटगिरि देवुडैन पर \-  
देवुनि भूदेव तत्परुनि ॥॥ कॊलु ॥  
**koluvuḍī bhaktikoṇḍala kōnēṭi**  
**nilayuni śrī nidhiyaina vāni .... kolu ..**  
**ādi dēvuni abhavuni sāma**  
**vēdanāda vinōduni nera \-**  
**vādi jita priyuni nirmala tattva \-**  
**vādula jīvanamaina vāni .... kolu ..**  
**dēvadēvuḍaina divyuni sarva**  
**bhāvātīta svabhāvuni**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭagiri dēvuḍaina para \-**  
**dēvuni bhūdēva tatparuni .... kolu ..**  
Worship with devotion, this lord residing on the hills, by the ‘Koneru’  
The one who is a treasure for Sree\!  
The primeval Lord, The unborn  
The enjoyer of the melody of Sama veda  
The one who loves those who have conquered the state of perfection  
The one who is but the way of life for the pious natured\!  
                                           (Worship with devotion)  
The Lord of lords, the supreme divinity,  
The one with a nature beyond all passion  
The lord of Sree Venkatagiri, the supreme Overlord  
The lord of the earth, the highest of the highest\!  
                                             (Worship with devotion)
