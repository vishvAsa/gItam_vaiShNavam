**Dēhinityuḍu Dēhamulu**  
देहिनित्युडु देहमुलु अनित्यालु  
ईवल नामनसा इदि मरुवकु मी ॥॥ देहि ॥  
गुदि पातचीर मानि कॊत्तचीर गट्टिनट्टु  
मुदिमेनु मानि देहिमॊगि गॊत्त मेनिमोचु  
अदन चम्पग लेवु आयधम्मु लीतनि  
कदिसि अग्निय नीरु गालिय चम्पगलेवु ॥॥ देहि ॥  
यीतडु नरकुवडडु यीतडग्नि कालडु  
यीतडु नीट मुनगडु यीतडु गालिबोडु  
चेतनुडै सर्वगतुडौ चॆलियिञ्चडेमिटनु  
यीतल ननादि यीतडिरुवु गदलडु ॥॥ देहि ॥  
चेरिकानरानिवाडु चिन्तिञ्चरानिवाडु  
भारपु विकाराल पासिन वाडी आत्म  
अरय श्री वेङ्कटेशुनाधीन मीतडनि  
सारमु तॆलियटे सत्यं ज्ञानमु ॥॥ देहि ॥  
**dēhinityuḍu dēhamulu anityālu**  
**īvala nāmanasā idi maruvaku mī .... dēhi ..**  
**gudi pātacīra māni kottacīra gaṭṭinaṭṭu**  
**mudimēnu māni dēhimogi gotta mēnimōcu**  
**adana campaga lēvu āyudhammu lītani**  
**kadisi agniyu nīru gāliyu campagalēvu .... dēhi ..**  
**yītaḍu narakuvaḍaḍu yītaḍagni kālaḍu**  
**yītaḍu nīṭa munagaḍu yītaḍu gālibōḍu**  
**cētanuḍai sarvagatuḍau celiyiñcaḍēmiṭanu**  
**yītala nanādi yītaḍiruvu gadalaḍu .... dēhi ..**  
**cērikānarānivāḍu cintiñcarānivāḍu**  
**bhārapu vikārāla pāsina vāḍī ātma**  
**araya śrī vēṅkaṭēśunādhīna mītaḍani**  
**sāramu teliyuṭē satyaṃ jñānamu .... dēhi ..**  
The soul is eternal, the body is impermanent  
O mind full of desires, forget this not\!  
Just as continuously discarding the old saree, and wearing a new one,  
discarding the old body, the soul quickly assumes a new one  
It cannot be killed by any weapon,  
nor by fire, water, air\!  
It cannot be cut, nor can it be burnt by fire  
It cannot be drowned in water, nor blown away by wind  
It is sentient, all pervading, unmoved by anything  
It is ancient and eternal\!  
It cannot be perceived, cannot be comprehended by thought  
It is free from all passions, the soul  
It is under the control of Lord Venkatesha  
To know this quintessence, is itself the Truth and Knowledge\!
