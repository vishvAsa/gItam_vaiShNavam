**Kaṇṭimi Nēḍide**  
कण्टिमि नेडिदॆ गरुडाचलपति  
यिण्टि वेलुपगु यीश्वरुडितडु ॥॥कण्टिमि॥  
श्री नरसिंहुडु चिन्मयकान्तुडु  
दानवान्तकुडु दयानिधि  
नाना महिमल नम्मिनवारिनि  
पूनुक काचे पोषकुडितडु ॥॥कण्टिमि॥  
देवादि देवुडु दिनकर तेजुडु  
जीवान्तरङ्गुडु श्रीविभुडु  
दैव शिखामणि तलचिन वारिनि  
सेवलुगॊनि काचे विभुडितडु ॥॥कण्टिमि॥  
परममूर्ति हरि प्रह्लाद वरदुडु  
करुणानिधि बुधकल्पमु  
परगु श्री वेङ्कटपति तनदासुल  
नरुदुग काचे अनन्तुडितडु ॥॥कण्टिमि॥  
**kaṇṭimi nēḍide garuḍācalapati**  
**yiṇṭi vēlupagu yīśvaruḍitaḍu ....kaṇṭimi..**  
**śrī narasiṃhuḍu cinmayakāntuḍu**  
**dānavāntakuḍu dayānidhi**  
**nānā mahimala namminavārini**  
**pūnuka kācē pōṣakuḍitaḍu ....kaṇṭimi..**  
**dēvādi dēvuḍu dinakara tējuḍu**  
**jīvāntaraṅguḍu śrīvibhuḍu**  
**daiva śikhāmaṇi talacina vārini**  
**sēvalugoni kācē vibhuḍitaḍu ....kaṇṭimi..**  
**paramamūrti hari prahlāda varaduḍu**  
**karuṇānidhi budhakalpamu**  
**paragu śrī vēṅkaṭapati tanadāsula**  
**naruduga kācē anantuḍitaḍu ....kaṇṭimi..**  
Today we beheld the lord of Garudachala\!  
Our family deity He is\! The Lord He is\!  
Sree Narasimha\! The lord of beatific effulgence  
the destroyer of demons, the ocean of compassion\!  
Those who believe in His various glories,  
He willingly protects them, this Supreme nourisher\!  
The Lord of lords, the one with the effulgence of the sun  
the in-dweller within Jivas, the Lord consort of Sree\!  
The crest jewel among Gods, those who contemplate on Him  
He accepts their worship and protects\! The Lord He is\!  
The supreme deity Hari, the one who blessed Prahalada  
the ocean of mercy, the wish yielding tree for the learned\!  
As the all pervading Sree Venkatapathi He protects  
His dasas uniquely\! The flawless one He is\!
