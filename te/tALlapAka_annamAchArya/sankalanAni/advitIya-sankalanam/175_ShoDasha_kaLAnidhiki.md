**ṣōḍaśa Kaḷānidhiki**  
षोडश कळानिधिकि षोडशोपचारमुलु  
जाडतोड निच्चलुनु समर्पयामि ॥॥ षोडश ॥  
अलरु विश्वात्मकुनकु आवाहनमिदॆ सर्व \-  
निलयनकु आसनमु नॆम्मिनिदे  
अल गङ्गाजनकुनकु अर्घ्यपाद्याचमनालु  
जलधि शायिकिनि मज्जनमिदॆ ॥॥ षोडश ॥  
वरपीताम्बरुनकु वस्त्रालङ्कारमिदॆ  
सरि श्रीमन्तुनकु भूषणमुलिवॆ  
धरणीधरुनकु गन्धपुष्प धूपमुलु  
तिरिमिदे कोटिसूर्यतेजुनकु दीपमु ॥॥ षोडश ॥  
अमृत मधनुनकु अदिवो नैवेद्यमु  
गमिचन्द्रनेत्रुनकु कप्पुरविडॆमु  
अमरिन श्री वेङ्कटाद्रिमीदि देवुनकु  
तमितो प्रदक्षिणालु दण्डमुलु इविगो ॥॥ षोडश ॥  
**ṣōḍaśa kaḷānidhiki ṣōḍaśōpacāramulu**  
**jāḍatōḍa niccalunu samarpayāmi .... ṣōḍaśa ..**  
**alaru viśvātmakunaku āvāhanamide sarva \-**  
**nilayunaku āsanamu nemminidē**  
**ala gaṅgājanakunaku arghyapādyācamanālu**  
**jaladhi śāyikini majjanamide .... ṣōḍaśa ..**  
**varapītāmbarunaku vastrālaṅkāramide**  
**sari śrīmantunaku bhūṣaṇamulive**  
**dharaṇīdharunaku gandhapuṣpa dhūpamulu**  
**tirimidē kōṭisūryatējunaku dīpamu .... ṣōḍaśa ..**  
**amṛta madhanunaku adivō naivēdyamu**  
**gamicandranētrunaku kappuraviḍemu**  
**amarina śrī vēṅkaṭādrimīdi dēvunaku**  
**tamitō pradakṣiṇālu daṇḍamulu ivigō .... ṣōḍaśa ..**  
To the Lord of sixteen divine attributes, I offer sixteen ways of obeisance\!  
With great care and devotion, I offer constantly my obeisances\!  
I welcome the Lord who is the indweller of the whole universe\!  
To the Lord who is all pervading I offer a throne humbly\!  
To the origin of the holy Ganges, I offer holy water as ‘arghya, padya and achamneya’\!  
To the one who is reclining on the ocean, I offer the auspicious bath\!  
To the Lord who is always in yellow silk robes, I offer “vastra” (cloth) as adornment\!  
To the most prosperous one, I offer ornaments\!  
To the one who lifted the earth, I offer sandal, flowers and incense\!  
To Him who is as brilliant as a million suns, I light this lamp of oil\!  
To Him who churned the ‘Amrutha’, I offer ‘Naivedyam’\!  
To the one who has the moon as eyes, I offer camphor Aarati\!  
To the Lord who adorns the Sree Venkatadri hill,  
eagerly I circumambulate, and prostrate before Him\!
