**Iṃdirāramaṇudecciyiyyarō**  
इन्दिरारमणुदॆच्चि यिय्यरो माकिटुवलॆ  
पॊन्दि यीतनि पूजिञ्च पॊद्दाय निपुडु ॥॥ इन्दिरा ॥  
धारुणि मैरावणु दण्डिञ्चि रामुदॆच्चि  
नेरुपुन मिञ्चिन अञ्जनीतनया  
फॆूर नागपाशमुल कॊट्टिवेसि यीतनि  
कारुण्य मन्दिनट्टि खगराज गरुडा ॥॥ इन्दिरा ॥  
नानादेवतलकु नरसिंहु कम्भमुलो  
पानिपट्टि चूपिनट्टि प्रह्लादुडा  
मानवुडै (डौ?) कृष्णमहिमल विश्वरूपु  
पूनि बण्डि नुञ्चुकॊन्न पोटुबण्टु अर्जुना ॥॥ इन्दिरा ॥  
श्रीवल्लभुनकु अशेष कैङ्कर्यमुल  
श्री वेङ्कटाद्रिवैन शेषमूरिती  
कैवसमैनयट्टि कार्तवीर्यार्जुनुडा  
देवुनि नीवेळ निट्टॆ तॆच्चि माकु निय्यरे ॥॥ इन्दिरा ॥  
**iṃdirāramaṇudecci yiyyarō mākiṭuvale**  
**pondi yītani pūjiñca poddāya nipuḍu .... iṃdirā ..**  
**dhāruṇi mairāvaṇu daṇḍiñci rāmudecci**  
**nērupuna miñcina aṃjanītanayā**  
**ghōra nāgapāśamula koṭṭivēsi yītani**  
**kāruṇya mandinaṭṭi khagarāja garuḍā .... iṃdirā ..**  
**nānādēvatalaku narasiṃhu kambhamulō**  
**pānipaṭṭi cūpinaṭṭi prahlāduḍā**  
**mānavuḍai (ḍau?) kṛṣṇamahimala viśvarūpu**  
**pūni baṇḍi nuñcukonna pōṭubaṇṭu arjunā .... iṃdirā ..**  
**śrīvallabhunaku aśēṣa kaiṅkaryamula**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭādrivaina śēṣamūritī**  
**kaivasamainayaṭṭi kārtavīryārjunuḍā**  
**dēvuni nīvēḷa niṭṭe tecci māku niyyarē .... iṃdirā ..**  
Bring back and give me the Lord of Indira\!  
It is now time for me to worship Him.  
Punishing Mairavana who was in the nether world and  
bringing back Rama with expertise, you O son of Anjana\!  
Rescuing from the dangerous clutches of the snakes,  
you who got the Lord blessing, O king of birds, Garuda\!  
Having shown to all Devas, the manifested form of  
Lord Narasimha from the pillar, you O Prahalada\!  
You who could perceive the glorious Universal form of  
Lord Krishna, O steadfast devotee Arjuna\!  
Performing endless servitude to the Lord of Sree,  
by becoming the Venkatadri Hill, you O Lord Sesha\!  
O one with a thousand hands, Kartiviryarjuna\!  
At this moment you must bring back to us our Lord\!\!
