**Vaiṣṇavulugāni Vāralevvaru**  
वैष्णवुलु गानि वारलॆव्वरु लेरु  
विष्णुप्रभाव मी विश्वमन्तय गान॥॥वैष्णवु॥  
अन्तय विष्णुमयम्बट मरि देव-  
तान्तरमुलु गलवननेला  
भ्रान्तिबॊन्दि यी भावमु भाविञ्चि  
नन्तने पुण्यलवुट दप्पॆदु गान ॥॥वैष्णवु॥  
यॆव्वरि गॊलिचिन नेमि गॊरत मरि  
यॆव्वरि दलचिन नेमि  
अव्वलिव्वल श्री हरि रूपुगानि वा \-  
रॆव्वरु लेरनि यॆरुकदोचिन जालु ॥॥वैष्णवु॥  
अति चञ्चलम्बै नयातुम गलिगिञ्चु \-  
कतमुन बहुचित्त गतुलै  
यितरुल गॊलिचिन यॆडयक यनाथ-  
पति तिरुवेङ्कटपति चेकॊनुगाक ॥॥वैष्णवु॥  
**vaiṣṇavulu gāni vāralevvaru lēru**  
**viṣṇuprabhāva mī viśvamantayu gāna....vaiṣṇavu..**  
**aṃtayu viṣṇumayambaṭa mari dēva-**  
**tāntaramulu galavananēlā**  
**bhrāntibondi yī bhāvamu bhāviñci**  
**nantanē puṇyalavuṭa dappedu gāna ....vaiṣṇavu..**  
**yevvari golicina nēmi gorata mari**  
**yevvari dalacina nēmi**  
**avvalivvala śrī hari rūpugāni vā \-**  
**revvaru lērani yerukadōcina jālu ....vaiṣṇavu..**  
**ati cañcalambai nayātuma galigiñcu \-**  
**katamuna bahucitta gatulai**  
**yitarula golicina yeḍayaka yanātha-**  
**pati tiruvēṅkaṭapati cēkonugāka ....vaiṣṇavu..**  
There is none who is not a Vaishnava\!  
Since the entire Universe is filled with Vishnu’s glorious power\!  
When everything is pervaded by Vishnu,  
why to say that there is distinction among Gods?  
Out of delusion, whatever form one conceives in the mind,  
finally, it will not prevent them from becoming meritorious\!  
Whosoever one worships, what is the shortcoming?  
whosoever one contemplates on?  
Here or there, everywhere, there is nothing that is not a  
manifestation of SreeHari\! If one understands this, it is enough\!  
Because of a very fickle mind,  
getting wavering thoughts, even if one worships others  
Lord TiruVenkatapathi, the refuge of the parentless  
without letting go, takes everyone unto His fold\!\!\!
