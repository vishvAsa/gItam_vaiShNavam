**Alamēlumaṅga**  
अलमेलुमङ्ग नी वभिनव रूपमु  
जलजाक्षु कन्नुलकु चवुलिच्चेवम्मा ॥॥अल॥  
गरुडाचलधीशु घनवक्षमुन नुण्डि  
परमानन्द सम्भरितवै  
नॆरतनमुलु चूपि निरन्तरमु नाथुनि  
हरुषिञ्चग जेसिति गदम्मा ॥॥अल॥  
शशिकिरणमुलकु चलुवल चूपुलु  
विशदमुगा मीद वॆदचल्लुचु  
रसिकतपॆम्पुन करगिञ्चि यॆप्पुडु नी  
वशमु चेसुकॊण्टि वल्लभु नोयम्मा ॥॥अल॥  
रट्टडि श्री वेङ्कट रायनिकि नीवु  
पट्टपुराणिवै परगुचु  
वट्टि माकु लिगरिञ्चु वलपुमाटल विभु-  
जट्टिगॊनि वुरमुन सतमैतिवम्मा ॥॥अल॥  
**alamēlumaṅga nī vabhinava rūpamu**  
**jalajākṣu kannulaku cavuliccēvammā ....ala..**  
**garuḍācaladhīśu ghanavakṣamuna nuṇḍi**  
**paramānanda sambharitavai**  
**neratanamulu cūpi nirantaramu nāthuni**  
**haruṣiñcaga jēsiti gadammā ....ala..**  
**śaśikiraṇamulaku caluvala cūpulu**  
**viśadamugā mīda vedacallucu**  
**rasikatapempuna karagiñci yeppuḍu nī**  
**vaśamu cēsukoṇṭi vallabhu nōyammā ....ala..**  
**raṭṭaḍi śrī vēṅkaṭa rāyaniki nīvu**  
**paṭṭapurāṇivai paragucu**  
**vaṭṭi māku ligariñcu valapumāṭala vibhu-**  
**jaṭṭigoni vuramuna satamaitivammā ....ala..**  
O Alamelumanga\! Your ever youthful form gives  
immense pleasure to the lotus eyes of His\!  
Residing on the majestic chest of the Lord of Garudachala,  
overflowing with blissful ecstasy,  
eternally showing Your feminine charms,  
You enchant Your Lord with happiness\!  
Your glances which are as cool as moonbeams,  
by casting them on Him abundantly,  
and melting His heart with increasing passion,  
You make Your Lord lovingly submissive\!  
For the amorous Lord Sree Venkataraya,  
You being the eternal reigning queen,  
with Your sweet love talks which are ever rejuvenating,  
You win Him over and reside on His chest eternally\!
