**Nīvēmi Sētuvayya Nīvu**  
नीवेमि सेतुवय्य नीवु दयानिधि वौदुवु  
भाविम्पलेनिवारि पापमिन्ते कानि ॥॥ नीवेमि ॥  
परमपदमॊसगि पापमडचेननि  
चरमश्लोकमुनन्दु चाटितिवि तॊलुतने  
निरति नीभूमिलोन नीवल्ल तप्पु लेदु  
परगनम्मनि वारि पापमिन्ते कानि ॥॥ नीवेमि ॥  
नी पादमुलकु नाकु नॆय्यमैन लङ्कॆनि  
येपुन द्वयारमुन निय्यकॊण्टिवि तॊलुत  
दापुगा नीवल्ल निङ्क तप्पु लेदु यॆञ्चिचूचि  
पैपैनम्मनिवारि पापमिन्ते कानि ॥॥ नीवेमि ॥  
बन्तिपुराणमुलनु भक्तसुलभुडननि  
अन्तरात्म वीमाट आडितिवि तॊलुतने  
इन्तट श्री वेङ्कटेश येमि सेतुवय्य नीवु  
पन्तान नम्मनिवारि पापमिन्ते कानि ॥॥ नीवेमि ॥  
**nīvēmi sētuvayya nīvu dayānidhi vauduvu**  
**bhāvimpalēnivāri pāpamintē kāni .... nīvēmi ..**  
**paramapadamosagi pāpamaḍacēnani**  
**caramaślōkamunandu cāṭitivi tolutanē**  
**nirati nībhūmilōna nīvalla tappu lēdu**  
**paraganammani vāri pāpamintē kāni .... nīvēmi ..**  
**nī pādamulaku nāku neyyamaina laṅkeni**  
**yēpuna dvayāramuna niyyakoṇṭivi toluta**  
**dāpugā nīvalla niṅka tappu lēdu yeñcicūci**  
**paipainammanivāri pāpamintē kāni .... nīvēmi ..**  
**bantipurāṇamulanu bhaktasulabhuḍanani**  
**aṃtarātma vīmāṭa āḍitivi tolutanē**  
**iṃtaṭa śrī vēṅkaṭēśa yēmi sētuvayya nīvu**  
**pantāna nammanivāri pāpamintē kāni .... nīvēmi ..**  
What more can You do, O Lord? You are the treasury of compassion!  
For those who are unable to comprehend, it is their ill-luck !  
“I shall bestow Parama Pada1 by destroying the sins”  
Thus You proclaimed in the ‘Charama Sloka’2 at the very beginning  
Eternally on this earth, from Your side there are no shortcomings  
It is the ill-luck of those who lack total faith in You!  
The loving relationship between Your feet and me, in a supreme manner  
You proclaimed it as the essence of the Dvayam3 at the very beginning  
If rightly appraised, from Your side there is no fault  
It is the ill-luck of those who do not believe!  
In the many Puranas, declaring “I am easily attainable by Bhaktas”  
O inner self, You spoke thus at the very beginning  
O Venkatesha! What more can You do?  
It is the ill-luck of the adamant people who lack faith!  
1.Parama Padam : The Supreme Realm  
2.Charama Sloka : The 66th verse of chapter 18 of Bagavad Gita  
3.Dvayam : The Dvaya mantra which shows the relationship between God  
and the Jiva to be that of a Lord and servant
