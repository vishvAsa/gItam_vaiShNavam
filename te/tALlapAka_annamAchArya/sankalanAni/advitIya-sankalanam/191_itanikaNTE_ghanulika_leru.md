**Itanikaṇṭe Ghanulika Lēru**  
इतनिकण्टॆ घनुलिक लेरु  
यितर देवतल यिन्दरिलोन ॥॥इतनि॥  
भूपति ईतडॆ पॊदिगि कॊलुवरो  
श्रीपति ईतडे चेकॊनरो  
येपुन बलुवुडु नीतडे चेररो  
पैपै वेङ्कटपति यैनाडु ॥॥इतनि॥  
मरुगुरुडितडे मति नम्मगदरो  
परमात्मुडितडे भाविञ्चरो  
करिवरदुडितडे गतियनि तलचरो  
परग श्री वेङ्कटपतियैनाडु ॥॥इतनि॥  
तल्लिय नितडे तण्ड्रिय नितडे  
वॆल्लिविरै यिक विडुवकुरो  
चल्लगा नितनि शरणनि ब्रतुकरो  
अल्लन श्री वेङ्कटहरि अयिनाडु ॥॥इतनि॥  
**itanikaṇṭe ghanulika lēru**  
**yitara dēvatala yindarilōna ....itani..**  
**bhūpati ītaḍe podigi koluvarō**  
**śrīpati ītaḍē cēkonarō**  
**yēpuna baluvuḍu nītaḍē cērarō**  
**paipai vēṅkaṭapati yaināḍu ....itani..**  
**maruguruḍitaḍē mati nammagadarō**  
**paramātmuḍitaḍē bhāviñcarō**  
**karivaraduḍitaḍē gatiyani talacarō**  
**paraga śrī vēṅkaṭapatiyaināḍu ....itani..**  
**talliyu nitaḍē taṇḍriyu nitaḍē**  
**vellivirai yika viḍuvakurō**  
**callagā nitani śaraṇani bratukarō**  
**allana śrī vēṅkaṭahari ayināḍu ....itani..**  
There is none who can excel Him in any aspect  
amongst all other deities\!  
He is the Lord of the earth, worship Him in total  
The consort of Sree is He, delight in Him  
Supremely strong one He is, reach unto Him  
High up there He has assumed the form of Venkatapathi\!  
The father of Love God He is, believe Him in your mind  
The supreme soul He is, contemplate on Him  
The savior of the elephant king He is, seek Him as your means  
He became the all-pervading Venkatapathi\!  
Mother He is, Father He is,  
know Him, and never let go  
Surrender unto Him and live life blessedly,  
Behold\! there He has assumed the form of Sree Venkatapathi\!
