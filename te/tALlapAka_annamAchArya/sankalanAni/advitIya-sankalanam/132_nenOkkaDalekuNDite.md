**Nēnokkaḍalēkuṇḍitē**  
ने नॊक्कड लेकुण्डिते नी कृपकु पात्र मेदि?  
पूनि नावल्लने कीर्ति पॊन्देवु नीवु\!॥ ने नॊक्कड॥  
अति मूढुललोन नग्रेसरुड नेनु;  
प्रति लेनि घनगर्व पर्वतमनु;  
तति पञ्चेन्द्रियमुलु धनवन्तुडनु नेनु;  
वॆतकि नावण्टिवानि विडुवग जॆल्लुना?॥ ने नॊक्कड॥  
महिलो संसारपु साव्रूज्य मेलेवाड नेनु;  
इहमुन कर्मवहि कॆक्किति नेनु;  
बहु योनिकूपसम्पद देलेवाड नेनु;  
वहिञ्चुक नावण्टि वानि दे नोपेना?॥ ने नॊक्कड॥  
भाविञ्चि नावण्टि नीचु बट्टि काचिनप्पुडुगा  
एवङ्क नीकीर्ति कडु नॆन्तुरु भुवि;  
नावल्ल नीकु पुण्यमु, नीवल्ल ने ब्रदुकुदु,  
श्री वेङ्कटेशुड\! इन्त चेरॆ सुम्मी मेलु.॥ ने नॊक्कड॥  
**nē nokkaḍa lēkuṇḍitē nī kṛpaku pātra mēdi?**  
**pūni nāvallanē kīrti pondēvu nīvu\!.. nē nokkaḍa..**  
**ati mūḍhulalōna nagrēsaruḍa nēnu;**  
**prati lēni ghanagarva parvatamanu;**  
**tati pañcēndriyamulu dhanavantuḍanu nēnu;**  
**vetaki nāvaṇṭivāni viḍuvaga jellunā?.. nē nokkaḍa..**  
**mahilō saṃsārapu sāmrājya mēlēvāḍa nēnu;**  
**ihamuna karmavahi kekkiti nēnu;**  
**bahu yōnikūpasampada dēlēvāḍa nēnu;**  
**vahiñcuka nāvaṇṭi vāni dē nōpēnā?.. nē nokkaḍa..**  
**bhāviñci nāvaṇṭi nīcu baṭṭi kācinappuḍugā**  
**ēvaṅka nīkīrti kaḍu nenturu bhuvi;**  
**nāvalla nīku puṇyamu, nīvalla nē bradukudu,**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍa\! iṃta cēre summī mēlu. ..nē nokkaḍa..**  
If I were not to exist, what would be the object of Your grace?  
It is because of me, You attain fame\!\!  
Amongst the highly ignorant, I am the first and foremost  
I am a matchless mountain of excessive pride  
I am affluent with the wealth of the five senses  
Is it right then to let go of one like me?  
On this earth, I am the one who reigns over the kingdom called Samsara  
Here I am bound by the debts of Karma  
I am one who goes through the births, born of many wombs  
Will You not bear with a worthy one like me?  
Only upon gracing a sinner like me,  
on this earth, You will attain fame\!  
Because of me You will earn merit, because of You I will survive  
SreeVenkatesha\! Like this we are mutually benefited\!\!
