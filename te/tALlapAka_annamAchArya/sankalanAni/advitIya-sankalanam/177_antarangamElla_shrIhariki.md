**Aṃtaraṅgamella Śrīhariki**  
अन्तरङ्गमॆल्ल श्रीहरिकि ऒप्पिञ्चकुण्टॆ  
विन्त विन्त विधमुल वीडुना बन्धमुलु ॥॥ अन्त ॥  
मनुजुडै फलमेदि मरिज्ञानि Еौदाका  
तनुवॆत्ति फलमेदि दयगलुगुदाका  
धनिकुडै फलमेदि धर्ममु सेयदाका  
पनिमालि मुदिसिते पासॆना भवमु ॥॥ अन्त ॥  
चदिविय फलमेदि शान्तमु कलुगुदाका  
पॆदवॆत्ति फलमेदि प्रियमाडु दाका  
मदिगल्गि फलमेदि माधवु दलचुदाका  
यॆदुट तानु राजैते एलुना परमु ॥॥ अन्त ॥  
पावनुडै फलमेदि भक्ति कलिगिन दाका  
जीविञ्चि फलमेदि चिन्त दीरुदाका  
वेवेल फलमेदि वेङ्कटेशुगन्नदाका  
भाविञ्चि ता देवुडैते प्रत्यक्षवौना ॥॥ अन्त ॥  
**aṃtaraṅgamella śrīhariki oppiñcakuṇṭe**  
**vinta vinta vidhamula vīḍunā bandhamulu .... aṃta ..**  
**manujuḍai phalamēdi marijñāni yaudākā**  
**tanuvetti phalamēdi dayagalugudākā**  
**dhanikuḍai phalamēdi dharmamu sēyudākā**  
**panimāli mudisitē pāsenā bhavamu .... aṃta ..**  
**cadiviyu phalamēdi śāntamu kalugudākā**  
**pedavetti phalamēdi priyamāḍu dākā**  
**madigalgi phalamēdi mādhavu dalacudākā**  
**yeduṭa tānu rājaitē ēlunā paramu .... aṃta ..**  
**pāvanuḍai phalamēdi bhakti kaligina dākā**  
**jīviñci phalamēdi cinta dīrudākā**  
**vēvēla phalamēdi vēṅkaṭēśugannadākā**  
**bhāviñci tā dēvuḍaitē pratyakṣamaunā .... aṃta ..**  
The mind, if not surrendered to Sree Hari,  
the varied attachments, will they let go?  
Being a human is of what use, until one become enlightened\!  
Possessing a body is of what use, until one becomes compassionate\!  
Being wealthy is of what use, until one becomes charitable\!  
Abstaining from work, if one grows old, can samsara be transgressed?  
Being learned is of what use, until one becomes tranquil\!  
Being able to speak is of what use, until one becomes sweet tongued\!  
Possessing a mind is of what use, until one thinks of Madhava\!  
Even if one becomes a king, can one rule over hereafter?  
Being pious is of what use, until one acquires true devotion\!  
Mere living is of what use, until one wins over worries\!  
What is the use of thousands upon thousands, until we perceive Venkatesha\!  
By merely thinking that one is divine, will God become manifest?
