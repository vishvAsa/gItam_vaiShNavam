**Koṇḍalalō Nelakonna**  
कॊण्डललो नॆलकॊन्न कोनेटि रायडु वाडु  
कॊण्डलन्त वरमुलु गुप्पॆडुवाडु ॥॥ कॊण्डललो ॥  
कुम्मर दासुडैन कुरुवरत्तिनम्बि  
इम्मन्न वरमुलॆल्ल इच्चिनवाडु  
दॊम्मुलु चेसिनयट्टि तॊण्डमान् जक्कुरवर्ति  
रम्मन्न चोटिकि वच्चि नम्मिन वाडु ॥॥ कॊण्डललो ॥  
अच्चपु वेडुकतो अनन्ताळुवारिकि  
मुच्चिलि वॆट्टिकि मन्नुमोचिन वाडु  
मच्चिक दॊलक तिरुमल नम्बि तोडुत  
इच्चनिच्च माटलाडि नच्चिनवाडु ॥॥ कॊण्डललो ॥  
कञ्चिलोन वुण्ड तिरुक्कच्चि नम्बि मीद करु  
णिञ्चि तव यॆडकु रप्पिञ्चिन वाडु  
ऎञ्चनॆक्कुडैन वेङ्कटेशुडु मनलकु  
मञ्चिवाडै करुण पालिञ्चिनवाडु ॥॥ कॊण्डललो ॥  
**koṇḍalalō nelakonna kōnēṭi rāyaḍu vāḍu**  
**koṇḍalanta varamulu guppeḍuvāḍu .... koṇḍalalō ..**  
**kummara dāsuḍaina kuruvarattinambi**  
**immanna varamulella iccinavāḍu**  
**dommulu cēsinayaṭṭi toṇḍamān jakkuravarti**  
**rammanna cōṭiki vacci nammina vāḍu .... koṇḍalalō ..**  
**accapu vēḍukatō anantāḷuvāriki**  
**muccili veṭṭiki mannumōcina vāḍu**  
**maccika dolaka tirumala nambi tōḍuta**  
**iccanicca māṭalāḍi naccinavāḍu ..koṇḍalalō ..**  
**kañcilōna vuṇḍa tirukkacci nambi mīda karu**  
**ṇiñci tava yeḍaku rappiñcina vāḍu**  
**eṃcanekkuḍaina vēṅkaṭēśuḍu manalaku**  
**mañcivāḍai karuṇa pāliñcinavāḍu ..koṇḍalalō ..**  
Residing in the hills, He is the Konetiraaya !  
He is the one who can bestow boons as huge as mountains!  
To ‘Kuruvaratti Nambi’ the potter,  
He bestowed all that he asked for!  
To the king ‘Tondamaan’ who was waging wars,  
He appeared wherever he had promised!  
To ‘Anantalvar’ with utmost delight,  
sharing his drudgery, He carried the sand weight!  
With ‘Tirumalanambi’, in the form of friendly gestures,  
He joyfully shared some banter and gossips!  
On ‘Tirukachhinambi’ who was in Kanchi,  
He showered His grace and made him come to His place!  
Lord Venkatesha cannot be measured by us,  
with His bounteous compassion, the benevolent Lord graces us!!  
1.Kuruvaratti Nambi was a potter by profession residing at Tirumala, and was the  
great devotee of the Lord.  
2.Tondaman was an emperor  
3.Anantalvar was a disciple of Ramanujacharya, who was sent to Tirumala by the  
Acharya to maintain a flower garden for Lord Venkateswara's daily worship.  
4.Tirumala Nambi was the maternal uncle of Ramanuja, who taught him the Ramayan  
at Tirumala.  
5.Tirukachhi Nambi (Kanchipurna) was a disciple of Yamunacharya, and it was he who  
instructed Ramanuja according to the Lord's wishes.
