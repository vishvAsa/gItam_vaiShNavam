**Iddarikiddarē Sari**  
इद्दरि किद्दरे सरि यीडुकु जोडुकु दगु  
गद्दरिकन्नुल जाड गलिगॆगा मनकु ॥॥ इद्दरि ॥  
पन्तपु चॆलिचन्नुलु (ल?) पसिडि कान्तुलकु  
कान्तुनि पीताम्बरपु कान्तुलु सरि  
दॊन्तुल चॆलिनीलपु तुरुमु कान्तुलकु  
वन्तुल मेनि नीलवर्णमु सरि ॥॥ इद्दरि ॥  
जलजाक्षि वॆललेनि जघनचक्रमुनकु  
चलमरिवलन केलिचक्रमु सरि  
कुलिकेटि यी यिन्ति कुत्तिक शङ्खमुनकु  
चलिवाय रमणुनि शङ्खमु सरि ॥॥ इद्दरि ॥  
कमलाक्षि श्री वेङ्कटपति गूडुटकु  
रमणु डण्टिन समरतुलु सरि  
तमितोडि निद्दरिकि तारुकाणलैनट्टि  
समुकपु मोहमुल सन्तसमुलु सरि ॥॥ इद्दरि ॥  
**iddari kiddarē sari yīḍuku jōḍuku dagu**  
**gaddarikannula jūḍa galigegā manaku .... iddari ..**  
**pantapu celicannulu (la?) pasiḍi kāntulaku**  
**kāntuni pītāmbarapu kāntulu sari**  
**dontula celinīlapu turumu kāntulaku**  
**vantula mēni nīlavarṇamu sari .... iddari ..**  
**jalajākṣi velalēni jaghanacakramunaku**  
**calamarivalana kēlicakramu sari**  
**kulikēṭi yī yinti kuttika śaṅkhamunaku**  
**calivāya ramaṇuni śaṅkhamu sari .... iddari ..**  
**kamalākṣi śrī vēṅkaṭapati gūḍuṭaku**  
**ramaṇu ḍaṇṭina samaratulu sari**  
**tamitōḍi niddariki tārukāṇalainaṭṭi**  
**samukapu mōhamula santasamulu sari .... iddari ..**  
They match each other\! They are perfect together in everyway\!  
We have clever eyes to perceive them\!  
For the golden radiance of His consort's breasts,  
the golden glow of the Lord's yellow silk robes are the perfect match\!  
For the thick blue radiance of the lady’s shining hair,  
the shine from His blue body is the perfect match\!  
For the precious beautifully rounded hips of the lotus eyed Goddess,  
the discus wielded by the Lord of Lakshmi is the perfect match\!  
For the conch like neck of the graceful lady,  
the conch wielded by the Lord of Lakshmi is the perfect match\!  
For the lotus eyed lady and Sree Venkatapathi to untie,  
the Lord's insatiable desire is the right match\!  
These are the examples proving that both are  
equal in passions, happiness, and are a ‘perfect match’\!
