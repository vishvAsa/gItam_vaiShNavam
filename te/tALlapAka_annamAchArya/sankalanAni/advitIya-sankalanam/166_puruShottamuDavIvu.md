**Puruṣōttamuḍavīvu**  
पुरुषोत्तमुड वीवु पुरुषाधमुड नेनु  
धरलोन नायन्दु मञ्चितनमेदि ॥॥ पुरुषोत्त ॥  
अनन्तापराधमुलु अटु नेनु सेसेवि  
अनन्तमैन दय अदि नीदि  
निनु नॆरुग कुण्डेटि नीचगुणमु नादि  
ननु नॆडय कुण्डेटि गुणमु नीदि ॥॥ पुरुषोत्त ॥  
सकल याचकमे सरुस नाकु पनि  
सकल रक्षकत्वमु सरि नी पनि  
प्रकटिञ्चि निन्नु दूरे पलुके नाकॆप्पुडुनु  
वॆकलिवै ननुकाचे विधमु नीदि ॥॥ पुरुषोत्त ॥  
नेरमिन्तय नादि नेरुपिन्तय नीदि  
सारॆकु अज्ञानि नेनु ज्ञानिवि नीवु  
ई रीति वेङ्कटेश इट्टॆ नन्नु नेलितिवि  
धारुणिलो निण्डॆनु प्रतापमु नीदि ॥॥ पुरुषोत्त ॥  
**puruṣōttamuḍa vīvu puruṣādhamuḍa nēnu**  
**dharalōna nāyandu mañcitanamēdi .... puruṣōtta ..**  
**anantāparādhamulu aṭu nēnu sēsēvi**  
**anantamaina daya adi nīdi**  
**ninu neruga kuṇḍēṭi nīcaguṇamu nādi**  
**nanu neḍaya kuṇḍēṭi guṇamu nīdi .... puruṣōtta ..**  
**sakala yācakamē sarusa nāku pani**  
**sakala rakṣakatvamu sari nī pani**  
**prakaṭiñci ninnu dūrē palukē nākeppuḍunu**  
**vekalivai nanukācē vidhamu nīdi .... puruṣōtta ..**  
**nēramintayu nādi nērupintayu nīdi**  
**sāreku ajñāni nēnu jñānivi nīvu**  
**ī rīti vēṅkaṭēśa iṭṭe nannu nēlitivi**  
**dhāruṇilō niṇḍenu pratāpamu nīdi .... puruṣōtta ..**  
Supreme most being You are\! The lowest of beings I am\!  
What good, in this world, do I possess?  
Innumerable sins are what I have committed,  
Limitless mercy is what You possess\!  
So lowly I am, I do not even know You,  
Your nature is such, You never let go of me\!  
Begging everything, such is my way,  
Bestowing eternal protection, that is Your way\!  
Though I abuse you openly, at all times,  
yet, Your way is such that you rescue me mercifully \!  
Heinous acts are what I commit, Perfect is what You are\!  
Eternally ignorant is what I am, All-Knower is what You are\!  
In this manner, O Venkatesha, you ruled over me,  
Your valor alone pervades this world \!\!
