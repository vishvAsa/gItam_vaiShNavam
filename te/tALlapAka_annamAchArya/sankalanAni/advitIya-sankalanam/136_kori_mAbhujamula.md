**Kōri Mābhujamula**  
कोरि माभुजमुल डागुलु मोचुकुन्नारमु  
धारुणिलोपल मम्मु दयजाचुगाक ॥॥ कोरि ॥  
सततमु हरिचेतिशङ्खचक्रमुले  
गतियै मापालनुण्डि काचुगाक  
अतिरयमैनट्टि अच्युतुनि आयधाले  
हितवुग मम्मु निट्टॆ येलुगाक गाक ॥॥ कोरि ॥  
कमलापति चेतुलगदापद्ममुलु  
ममकारमुतो ममु मन्निञ्चुगाक  
समरविजयमैन शार्ज्गादिशरमुलु  
तमितोड माकु दापुदण्डै वुण्डुगाक गाक ॥॥ कोरि ॥  
मुञ्चि श्रीवेङ्कटेशुडु मॊलगट्टिनकठारु  
अञ्चॆल माकॆल्ल रक्षै अमरुगाक  
अञ्च नीतनिकै दुवुलमरिनहस्तमुलु  
पञ्चल माकिट्टॆ वज्रपञ्जरालवुगाक गाक ॥॥ कोरि ॥  
**kōri mābhujamula ḍāgulu mōcukunnāramu**  
**dhāruṇilōpala mammu dayajūcugāka .... kōri ..**  
**satatamu haricētiśaṅkhacakramulē**  
**gatiyai māpālanuṇḍi kācugāka**  
**atirayamainaṭṭi acyutuni āyudhālē**  
**hitavuga mammu niṭṭe yēlugāka gāka .... kōri ..**  
**kamalāpati cētulagadāpadmamulu**  
**mamakāramutō mamu manniñcugāka**  
**samaravijayamaina śārjgādiśaramulu**  
**tamitōḍa māku dāpudaṇḍai vuṇḍugāka gāka .... kōri ..**  
**muñci śrīvēṅkaṭēśuḍu molagaṭṭinakaṭhāru**  
**aṃcela mākella rakṣai amarugāka**  
**aṃca nītanikai duvulamarinahastamulu**  
**pañcala mākiṭṭe vajrapañjarālavugāka gāka .... kōri ..**  
We are those who have yearned for, and have worn the impressions on our shoulders1  
May mercy be showered upon us, on this earth\!  
Eternally the Shanka-Chakra in Hari’s hands,  
being our only means, may they be by our side and protect us\!  
The speedy weapons of Achyutha,  
may they benevolently rule over us\!  
The mace and lotus in Kamala-Pathi’s hands  
affectionately, may they bless us\!  
The bow Sharga and others which are ever victorious in battle  
may they readily protect us closely\!  
The dagger that SreeVenkateshwara has tied upon His waist,  
may all these weapons protect all of us closely\!  
Those adorable hands of His which are adorned by these weapons  
may they closely surround us like diamond cages\!\!  
1.Refers to the wearing of the marks of the Conch and Discus upon the  
shoulders, signifying that one is a devotee of Vishnu.
