**Ādimūlamē Māku Aṃgarakṣa**  
आदिमूलमे माकु अङ्गरक्ष  
श्रीदेवुडे माकु जीवरक्ष॥॥आदि॥  
भूमिदेवि पतियैन पुरुषोत्तमुडॆ माकु  
भूमिपै नेड नुण्डिन भूमिरक्ष  
आमनि जलधिशायियैन देवुडे माकु  
सामीप्यमन्दुन्न जलरक्ष॥॥आदि॥  
व्रॆूयचु नग्निलो यज्ञमूर्तियैन देवुडु  
आयमुलु ताककुण्ड अग्निरक्ष  
वायसुतु नेलिनट्टि वनजनाभुडे माकु  
वायवन्दु कन्दकुण्ड वायरक्ष॥॥आदि॥  
पादमाकाशमुनकु पारजाचे विष्णुवे  
गादिलियै माकु आकाशरक्ष  
साधिञ्चि श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रि सर्वेश्वरुडॆ माकु  
सादरमु मीरिनट्टि सर्वरक्ष॥॥आदि॥  
**ādimūlamē māku aṃgarakṣa**  
**śrīdēvuḍē māku jīvarakṣa....ādi..**  
**bhūmidēvi patiyaina puruṣōttamuḍe māku**  
**bhūmipai nēḍa nuṇḍina bhūmirakṣa**  
**āmani jaladhiśāyiyaina dēvuḍē māku**  
**sāmīpyamandunna jalarakṣa....ādi..**  
**mrōyucu nagnilō yajñamūrtiyaina dēvuḍu**  
**āyamulu tākakuṇḍa agnirakṣa**  
**vāyusutu nēlinaṭṭi vanajanābhuḍē māku**  
**vāyuvandu kandakuṇḍa vāyurakṣa....ādi..**  
**pādamākāśamunaku pārajācē viṣṇuvē**  
**gādiliyai māku ākāśarakṣa**  
**sādhiñci śrīvēṅkaṭādri sarvēśvaruḍe māku**  
**sādaramu mīrinaṭṭi sarvarakṣa....ādi..**  
The Primal one (Adimoola) alone is our body protection  
The Lord of Sree alone is our life protection\!  
The consort of Bhudevi, the Supreme Being, wherever we are  
upon this earth, He is our earth protection\!  
The one who reclines on the vast ocean, that Lord alone  
who is closely present with us, is our water protection\!  
The Lord present within the fire as the sacrificial deity  
protecting our bodies from fire, He alone is our fire protection\!  
The Lord of the son of Wind God, the lotus naveled one alone  
protecting us from being weathered, He is our wind protection\!  
The one who extended His foot into the sky, that Vishnu alone,  
with total compassion, is our sky protection\!  
The Lord of Venkatadri, the Lord of all,  
with supreme benevolence , He is our all-round protection\!
