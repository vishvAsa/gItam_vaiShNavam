**Iṃdariki Na Bhayambuliccu**  
इन्दरिकि न भयम्बु लिच्चु चेयि  
कन्दुवगु मञ्चि बङ्गारु चेयि ॥॥इन्द॥  
वॆललेनि वेदमुलु वॆदकि तॆच्चिनचेयि  
चिलुकु गुब्बलि किन्द चेर्चु चेयि  
कलिकि यगु भूकान्त कौगलिञ्चिनचेयि  
वलनैन कॊनगोळ्ळ वाडिचेयि ॥॥इन्द॥  
तनिवोक बलिचेत दानमडिगिन चेयि  
ऒनरङ्ग भूदान मॊसगु चेयि  
मॊनसि जलनिधि यम्मुमॊनकु दॆच्चिन चेयि  
ऎनय नागेलु धरियिञ्चु चेयि ॥॥इन्द॥  
पुरसतुल मानमुलु पॊल्लसेसिन चेयि  
तुरगम्बुबरपॆडि दॊड्डचेयि  
तिरुवेङ्कटाचलधीशुडै मोक्षम्बु \-  
तॆरुवु प्राणुलकॆल्ल तॆलिपॆडि चेयि ॥॥इन्द॥  
**iṃdariki na bhayambu liccu cēyi**  
**kanduvagu mañci baṅgāru cēyi ....iṃda..**  
**velalēni vēdamulu vedaki teccinacēyi**  
**ciluku gubbali kinda cērcu cēyi**  
**kaliki yagu bhūkānta kaugaliñcinacēyi**  
**valanaina konagōḷḷa vāḍicēyi ....iṃda..**  
**tanivōka balicēta dānamaḍigina cēyi**  
**onaraṅga bhūdāna mosagu cēyi**  
**monasi jalanidhi yammumonaku deccina cēyi**  
**enaya nāgēlu dhariyiñcu cēyi ....iṃda..**  
**purasatula mānamulu pollasēsina cēyi**  
**turagambubarapeḍi doḍḍacēyi**  
**tiruvēṅkaṭācaladhīśuḍai mōkṣambu \-**  
**teruvu prāṇulakella telipeḍi cēyi ....iṃda..**  
The hand that grants boons to all,  
The divine hand which dazzles with golden hue\!  
The hand that searched and brought back the priceless Vedas  
The hand that held up and supported the hill  
The hands that have embraced the beautiful mother Earth  
The hands of His, with beautiful nails\!  
The hands that sought alms from Bali  
The hands that elegantly gave away gifts of land  
The hands that commanded the ocean with one arrow  
The hands that are armed with the plough\!  
The hands that played with the modesty of women (of Vrindavan)  
The powerful hands that control the horse  
The hands which show that the Lord of Tiru Venkatachala  
is for all aspirants of Moksha, the only refuge\!
