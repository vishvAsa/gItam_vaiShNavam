**Kallamāṭa Doḍḍamudra**  
कल्लमाट दॊड्डमुद्र कटकटा  
चॆल्लुबडिकल्ललु चॆप्पेरु लोकुलु ॥॥ कल्लमाड ॥  
यिप्पुडेले ब्रह्मदेवुडिट्टे वुण्डग मीदटि \-  
वॊप्पगुब्रह्मपट्टमु वॊकरिकि वॆच्चपॆट्टि  
अप्पटि मूडुमूर्तुलयन्दुलो नीतनि सरि \-  
चॆप्पबॊय्ये रीमाट चॆल्लुना लोकुलकु लोकुलु ॥॥ कल्लमाड ॥

चालि मूडुमूर्तुललो सरि यीतडण्टानु  
कूळलै याडेरुगाक कूडुना लोकुलकु लोकुलु ॥॥ कल्लमाड ॥  
घनुडीतनिपादमु गडिगॆ ब्रह्म देवुडु

**vonara mūḍumūrtulaṃ dokaḍu śrīvēṅkaṭēśu-**  
**ḍanumāṭa yidi tagavavunā lōkulaku lōkulu ..kallamāḍa**  
What a pity\! People are lying, and that lie is gaining value\!  
With authority, people are uttering only lies\!  
Even when the ruling Brahma is still existing,  
He (Vishnu) confers that very post of Brahma to another befitting one\!  
Yet people say “He is just one among the trinity”  
If they want to proclaim so to the world, is it right?  
Kailasa, Lord Rudra and the crores of other Brahmandas  
when Lord Vishnu has placed them within His stomach,  
Yet they say “He is just one among the trinity”  
Thus proclaim the wicked people\! Is it right to do so to the world?  
Lord Brahma washed the feet of this great Lord\!  
Touching His feet, Lord Shiva paid reverence by placing Him on his head\!  
Saying then, that Sree Venkatesha is just one among the trinity,  
and proclaiming so to the world, is it right??
