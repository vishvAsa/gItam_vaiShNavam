**Jōvacyutānanda Jōjō Mukunda**  
जोवच्युतानन्द जोजो मुकुन्द  
रावॆ परमानन्द रामगोविन्द  
नन्दु निण्टनु जेरि नयमु मीऱङ्ग  
चन्द्रवदनलु नीकु सेव चेयङ्ग  
वन्दमुग वारिण्ड्ल नाडुचुण्डग  
मन्दलकु दॊङ्ग मा मुद्दुरङ्ग  
पालवाराशिलो पवळिञ्चिनावु  
बालुगा मुनुल कभयमिच्चिनावु  
मेलुगा वसुदेवु कुदयिञ्चिनावु  
बालुडै यण्डि गोपालुडैनावु  
अट्टुगट्टिन मीग डट्टॆ तिन्नाडे  
पट्टि कोडलु मूतिपै रासिनाडे  
अट्टॆ तिनॆननि यत्तयडुग विन्नाडे  
गट्टिगा निदि दॊङ्ग कॊट्टुमन्नाडे  
गॊल्लवारिण्ड्लकुनु गॊब्बुनकु बोयि  
कॊल्ललुगा त्रावि कुण्डलनु नेयि  
चॆल्लुना मगवाण्ड्र जॆलिगि यी शायी  
चिल्लतनमुलु सेयजॆल्लुनटवोयि  
रेपल्लॆ सतुलॆल्ल गोपम्बुतोनु  
गोपम्म मी कॊडुकु मा यिण्ड्ललोनु  
मापुगाने वच्चि मा मानमुलनु  
नी पापडे चॆऱिचॆ नेमन्दुमम्म  
ऒकनि यालिनि दॆच्चि नॊकनि कडबॆट्टि  
जगडमुलु गलिपिञ्चि सतिपतुल बट्टि  
पगलु नलु जामुलनु बालुडैनट्टि  
मगनाण्ड्र जेपट्टि मदनुडै नट्टि  
अङ्गजुनि गन्न मा यन्न यिटु रारा  
बङ्गारु गिन्नॆलोन बालु पोसेरा  
दॊङ्ग नीवनि सतुलु पॊङ्गुचुन्नारा  
मुङ्गिट नाडरा मोहनाकार  
गोवर्धनं बॆल्ल गॊडुगुगा बट्टि  
कावरम्मुन नुन्न कंसुबडगॊट्टि  
नीवु मधुरापुरमु नेल जेपट्टि  
ठीवितो नेलिन देवकीपट्टि  
अङ्गुगा दाळ्ळपाकन्नमय्य चाल  
शृङ्गगा रचनगा जॆप्पॆ नी जोल  
सङ्गतिग सकल सम्पदल नीवेळ  
मङ्गळमु तिरुपट्ल मदन गोपाल

Jo Achyutanada, Jo Jo Mukunda\!  
Come over O supremely blissful one, Rama-Govinda\!  
Being the child of Nandas house, growing in beauty  
With beautiful moon-faced women serving you  
Happily playing in their houses,  
You thief among cowherds\! Our dear Ranga\!  
O one who reclines in the ocean of milk\!  
As a small child you blessed the sages,  
You who have dawned for the good of Vasudeva  
Being a young lad, you tended the cows as Gopala\!  
O one who ate butter from the hanging pots,  
Catching hold of a daughter-in-law you smeared it on her mouth  
You heard the mother-in-law ask ‘Who ate it?’  
You shouted loudly “Here is the thief, beat her” \!  
Going to the house of cowherds quickly,  
Drinking lots and lots of ghee from the pots  
Do you ever get tired of sporting with the menfolk?  
You can get away with playing any of your pranks\!  
The ladies of Repalle with anger (said)  
“O mother , your son, entering our houses  
at night time, with our modesty  
he (this child) played, what more can we say?”  
Placing one mans wife next to another man  
You instigated fights between man and wife  
During the four durations of the day, being a child  
You caught the menfolk in a spell, being a ‘Madana’  
O one who gave birth to Angaja1, come over here\!  
In a golden vessel, I offer you milk  
Puffed up in anger, the women are calling you as “thief”  
Play in the courtyard\! O handsome one\!  
Holding the entire Govardhana Hill as an umbrella  
Vanquishing the arrogant Kamsa  
You won over the land of Mathura city  
And ruled over it with royal majesty, O dear of Devaki\!  
In a very beautiful manner, Tallapaka Annayya  
Is singing this lullaby as a romantic poem.  
With these ‘all time treasures’ as its content  
Praises unto you, O Tirupati Madanagopala\!
