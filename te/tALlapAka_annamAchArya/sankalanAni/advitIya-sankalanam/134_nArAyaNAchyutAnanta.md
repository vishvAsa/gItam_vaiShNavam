**Nārāyaṇācyutānanta**  
नारायणाच्यु तानन्त गोविन्दा  
नेररादु विज्ञानमु नी विय्यक लेदु ॥॥ नारायणा ॥  
निगमान्तविदुलैते निन्नॆऱुगुदुरुगाक  
मृगसमानुलकेल मीमीदि भक्ति  
वॆगटुगा गृपतोड विश्वरूपु चूपिनावु  
पगटुदुर्योधनुडु बहुरूप मनडा ॥॥नारायणा ॥  
चिरपुण्युलैते मीसेवलु सेतुरुगाक  
दुरितचित्तुलकु मीत्रोव येटिकि  
गरिम ब्रत्यक्षमै कम्बमुलो गलिगिना  
हिरण्यकशिपुडु मि म्मॆञ्चि शरणनॆना ॥॥नारायणा ॥  
देवतलयिते मिम्मु दॆलियनेर्तुरुगाक  
येवल नसुरलु मिम्मॆऱिगेरा  
श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रिमीद सिरितो नी वुण्डगानु  
केवलसंसारु लॆऱिगियनु मऱवरा ॥॥ नारायणा ॥  
**nārāyaṇācyu tānanta gōvindā**  
**nērarādu vijñānamu nī viyyaka lēdu ..nārāyaṇā ..**  
**nigamāntavidulaitē ninneṟugudurugāka**  
**mṛgasamānulakēla mīmīdi bhakti**  
**vegaṭugā gṛpatōḍa viśvarūpu cūpināvu**  
**pagaṭuduryōdhanuḍu bahurūpa manaḍā .. nārāyaṇā..**  
**cirapuṇyulaitē mīsēvalu sēturugāka**  
**duritacittulaku mītrōva yēṭiki**  
**garima bratyakṣamai kambamulō galiginā**  
**hiraṇyakaśipuḍu mi mmeñci śaraṇanenā .. nārāyaṇā ..**  
**dēvatalayitē mimmu deliyanērturugāka**  
**yēvala nasuralu mimmeṟigērā**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭādrimīda siritō nī vuṇḍagānu**  
**kēvalasaṃsāru leṟigiyunu maṟavarā .. .. nārāyaṇā ..**  
Narayana\! Achyutha\! Anantha\! Govinda\!  
Do not find fault\! The special knowledge will not come if You do not bestow\!  
The knowers of the Vedas, they will be knowing You  
those who are like animals, why do they need Bhakti for You?  
Out of compassion, You showed the Divine form  
but Duryodhana proclaimed it as various ordinary forms\!\!  
The eternally meritorious ones will offer their servitude  
The evil minded, why do they need Your path?  
Even when from the pillar You emerged gloriously  
did Hiranyakashupu accept and surrender to You??  
The Demigods know and comprehend You  
How can the Asuras ever understand You?  
(So too) On SreeVenkatadri, though You are there with Sree  
the mere householders, even upon knowing You, will they not forget??  
The message is that unless God bestows His grace, one will not be devoted to God.
