**Pāḍaina Yerukatō Bandhamōkṣamulokka**  
पाडैन यॆरुकतो बन्धमोक्षमुलॊक्क  
गाडि गट्टुट तॆलिवि गानके कादा\!॥ पाडैन ॥  
भाविञ्चि निनु परब्रह्ममनि वेदमुलु  
वेवेलु विधुल मॊरवॆट्टगानु  
केवलपु निन्नु दक्किन दैवमुलगूर्चि  
सेविम्पुटिदि तप्पुसेयटे कादा\!॥ पाडैन ॥  
सरिलेनि निनु नुपनिषद्वाक्यमु लॆपरा  
त्परुडवनि नलुगडल बलुकगानु  
वरुसतो बॆक्कुदैवमुलु सङ्गडिनिन्नु  
तॊरल गॊलुचुट महाद्रोहमे कादा\!॥ पाडैन ॥  
ऎन्दुजाचिन पुराणेति हासमुलु नी  
चन्दमे यधिकमनि चाटगानु  
कन्दर्पजनक वेङ्कटगिरि स्वामि नी  
कन्दुवॆऱगनिदि यज्ञानमे कादा\!॥ पाडैन ॥  
**pāḍaina yerukatō bandhamōkṣamulokka**  
**gāḍi gaṭṭuṭa telivi gānakē kādā\!.. pāḍaina ..**  
**bhāviñci ninu parabrahmamani vēdamulu**  
**vēvēlu vidhula moraveṭṭagānu**  
**kēvalapu ninnu dakkina daivamulagūrci**  
**sēvimpuṭidi tappusēyuṭē kādā\!.. pāḍaina ..**  
**sarilēni ninu nupaniṣadvākyamu leparā**  
**tparuḍavani nalugaḍala balukagānu**  
**varusatō bekkudaivamulu saṅgaḍininnu**  
**torala golucuṭa mahādrōhamē kādā\!.. pāḍaina**  
**.**  
**eṃdujūcina purāṇēti hāsamulu nī**  
**candamē yadhikamani cāṭagānu**  
**kandarpajanaka vēṅkaṭagiri svāmi nī**  
**kanduveṟaganidi yajñānamē kādā\!.. pāḍaina**  
With misguided knowledge, if attachments and salvation  
are tied together to one yoke, is it not foolishness?  
Comprehending You as the Supreme Brahman, though the Vedas  
are proclaiming it in thousands of ways,  
yet grouping You , the pristine one, along with other deities  
and worshipping, is it not like committing a blunder?  
The Upanishidic statements are proclaiming You,  
the matchless one, as the Highest of the Highest\!  
Grouping You then in the same order as that of other Gods,  
and worshipping, is it not a great treachery\!  
In what ever manner perceived, though the Puranas and Itihaasas  
are declaring Your glory to be the Highest,  
O father of Kandarpa, O Lord of Venkatagiri,  
not knowing the path of devotion to You, is it not sheer ignorance\!\!
