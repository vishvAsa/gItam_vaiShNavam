**Iṭṭi Brāhmaṇya**  
इट्टि ब्राह्मण्य मॆक्कुडु यिन्निटिलोन  
दट्टमै तदिय्यलकॆ तगुनु ब्राह्मण्यमु ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
हरिगॊलिचेवारि कमरु ब्राह्मण्यमु  
परम वैष्णवमे ब्राह्मण्यमु  
हरिणोर्ध्वपुण्ड्रदेहुलौटे ब्राह्मण्यमु  
तिरुमन्त्रविधुलदे तेकुव ब्राह्मण्यमु ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
सतमै चक्राङ्कितुलचरिते ब्राह्मण्यमु  
पतिशरणागतिये ब्राह्मण्यमु  
वॆतलेनि वीरलसात्विकमे ब्राह्मण्यमु  
तति द्वयाधिकारुले तप्परु ब्राह्मण्यमु ॥॥ इट्टि ॥  
अञ्च बरमभागवताधीनमु ब्राह्मण्यमु  
पञ्चसंस्कारादुलदे ब्राह्मण्यमु  
यॆञ्चग श्री वेङ्कटेशुडितडे ब्रह्मण्युडु  
मुञ्चि यीतनिवारौटे मॊदलि ब्राह्मण्यमु ॥॥ इट्टि ॥

This kind of ‘Brahmanyamu’1 is the highest amongst all others  
Profound and befitting is this ‘Brahmanyamu’ for Tadeeyas (Sharanagathas)\!  
To those who worship Hari, it is agreeable, this ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Parama Vaishnavam2 is this ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Wearing Hari’s Urdhva Pundaras3 on the body is ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Chanting the ‘Tirumantra’4 dutifully is the resolute ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Eternally those who wear the marks of the Chakra, their conduct is ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Sharanagati5 unto the Lord alone is ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Those who remain sorrowless, their Saatvik6 nature is ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Those who are initiated into the Dvayam, they do not transgress this ‘Brahmanyamu’  
If rightly appraised, being subservient to ParamaBhagavathas7 is ‘Brahmanyamu’  
Getting initiated with Panchasamskara8 and the likes is ‘Brahmanyamu’  
If understood, Sree Venkatesha alone is the Supreme Brahman  
Totally becoming ‘His-own’ is the foremost ‘Brahmanyamu’  
1\. Brahmanyamu means the conduct of a true Brahmana  
2\. Parama Vaishnavam means the highest Vaishnava way of living  
3\. Urdhva Pundaras are naamams worn on the body, ie Tiruman-Sreechoornams  
4\. Tirumantra: “Om Namo Narayanaya”  
5\. Sharanagati means self-surrender  
6\. Mode of supreme goodness  
7\. Parama Bhagavathas are supreme devotees of Vishnu  
8\. Panchasamskara are the five purificatory rites performed during the initiation into  
SreeVaishnavism
