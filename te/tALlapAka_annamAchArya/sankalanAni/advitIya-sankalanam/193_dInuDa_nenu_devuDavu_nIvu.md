**Dīnuḍa Nēnu Dēvuḍavu Nīvu**  
दीनुड नेनु देवुडवु नीवु  
नी निज महिमे नॆरुपुट गाक ॥॥दीनुड॥  
मति जनन मॆरुग मरणं बॆरुगनु  
इतवुग निनुनिक नॆरिगेना  
क्षितिपुट्टिञ्चिन श्रीपतिविनीवे  
तति नापै दय दलतुवु गाक ॥॥दीनुड॥  
तलचपापमनि तलचपुण्यमनि  
तलपुन इक निनुतलचेना  
आलरिन नालो अन्तर्यामिवि  
कलुष मॆडय ननु गातुवु गाक ॥॥दीनुड॥  
तडव ना हेयमु तडव ना मलिनमु  
तडयक नी मेलु तडवेना  
विडुवलेनि श्रीवेङ्कट विभुडवु  
कडदाक निकगातुवुगाक ॥॥दीनुड॥  
**dīnuḍa nēnu dēvuḍavu nīvu**  
**nī nija mahimē nerupuṭa gāka ....dīnuḍa..**  
**mati janana meruga maraṇaṃ beruganu**  
**itavuga ninunika nerigēnā**  
**kṣitipuṭṭiñcina śrīpativinīvē**  
**tati nāpai daya dalatuvu gāka ....dīnuḍa..**  
**talacapāpamani talacapuṇyamani**  
**talapuna ika ninutalacēnā**  
**ālarina nālō aṃtaryāmivi**  
**kaluṣa meḍayu nanu gātuvu gāka ....dīnuḍa..**  
**taḍava nā hēyamu taḍava nā malinamu**  
**taḍayaka nī mēlu taḍavēnā**  
**viḍuvalēni śrīvēṅkaṭa vibhuḍavu**  
**kaḍadāka nikagātuvugāka ....dīnuḍa..**  
Lowly I am, Lordly you are\!  
Your true magnificence is all pervasive\!  
I am neither aware of birth , nor of death  
How can I ever know you?  
Creator of the earth, Sree-Pathi you are,  
At this moment, may your blessings be showered on me\!  
I don’t think of sins, and also of merits  
How can I even think of You?  
Present within me fully, the indweller you are,  
Whatever be my sins, may you protect me\!  
Hateful at times, sinful at times,  
how then can I spontaneously think of Your grace?  
You who never let go of me, Lord Venkatesha\!  
till the very end, may you protect me
