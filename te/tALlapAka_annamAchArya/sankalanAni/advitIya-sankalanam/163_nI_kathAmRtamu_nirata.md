**Nī Kathāmṛtamu Nirata**  
नी कथामृतमु निरत सेवन नाकु  
चेकॊनुट सकलसुखसेवनम्बटु गान ॥॥ नी कथा ॥  
इदियॆ मन्त्रराजमु नाकु नेप्रॊद्दु  
निदियॆ वेदसंहित पाठमु  
इदियॆ बहुशास्त्रमॆल्ल चदवुट नाकु  
इदियॆ सन्ध्य नाकिदियॆ जपमटु गान ॥॥ नी कथा ॥  
इदियॆ ब्रह्मविद्योपदेशमु नाकु  
इदियॆ दुःखविरहित मार्गमु  
इदियॆ बहुलोकहित भेषजमु नाकु  
इदियॆ वुपनिषद्वाक्य पद्धति गान ॥॥ नी कथा ॥  
इदियॆ दानफलमिय्यजालुनु नाकु  
इदियॆ तपविरहित कर्ममु  
इदियॆ तिरुवेङ्कटेश्वरुनि संस्मरण  
इदियॆ यिदियॆ यिन्नियनु नटु गान ॥॥ नी कथा ॥  
**nī kathāmṛtamu nirata sēvana nāku**  
**cēkonuṭa sakalasukhasēvanambaṭu gāna ..nī kathā**  
**idiye mantrarājamu nāku nēproddu**  
**nidiye vēdasaṃhita pāṭhamu**  
**idiye bahuśāstramella cadavuṭa nāku**  
**idiye sandhya nākidiye japamaṭu gāna .. nī kathā ..**  
**idiye brahmavidyōpadēśamu nāku**  
**idiye duḥkhavirahita mārgamu**  
**idiye bahulōkahita bhēṣajamu nāku**  
**idiye vupaniṣadvākya paddhati gāna .... nī kathā**  
**idiye dānaphalamiyyajālunu nāku**  
**idiye tapavirahita karmamu**  
**idiye tiruvēṅkaṭēśvaruni saṃsmaraṇa**  
**idiye yidiye yinniyunu naṭu gāna .... nī kathā**  
The nectar of your glorious legends is for me, eternal servitude\!  
Receiving it, is itself a worship totally pleasurable for me\!  
This itself is the highest mantra, for me, at all times  
This itself is a lesson of the Veda-Samhitas  
This itself is a study of all scriptures, for me  
This itself is ‘Sandhya’, for me, this itself is the ‘Japa’\!  
This itself is the initiation of Brahma Vidya (highest knowledge) for me  
This itself is the route without any sorrow  
This itself is the universally benevolent medicine, for me  
This itself is the way of the Upanishidic statements\!  
This itself can bestow the fruits of charity, for me  
This itself is the work without any penance  
This itself is ‘TiruVenkateshas’ remembrance  
This , and only This, is everything \!\!\!
