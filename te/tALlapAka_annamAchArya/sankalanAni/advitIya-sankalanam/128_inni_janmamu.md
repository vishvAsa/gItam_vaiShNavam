**Inni Janmamu**  
इन्नि जन्ममु लेटिकि हरिदासु  
लुन्न वूर दानुण्डिन जालु ॥॥ इन्नि ॥  
हरिभक्तुल यिण्टि यन्नमु गॊनुवारि  
वरुवुडै यण्डवलॆनन्न जालु  
परमभागवत भवनम्बुल जॆड्ड  
पुरुवु दानयि पॊडमिन जालु ॥॥ इन्नि ॥  
वासुदेवुनि भक्तवरुल दासुलु मुन्नु  
रोसिन यॆङ्गिलि रुचिगॊन्नजालु  
श्री सतीशुनि दलञ्चिनवारि दासान  
दासुडैवुण्ड दलचिन जालु ॥॥ इन्नि ॥  
श्रीवेङ्कटेशु जाचिनवारि श्रीपाद  
सेवकुडै यण्डजेजेरिन जालु  
ई विभुदासुल हितुल पादधूळि  
पावनमै सोकि ब्रदिकिन जालु ॥॥ इन्नि ॥  
**inni janmamu lēṭiki haridāsu**  
**lunna vūra dānuṇḍina jālu .... inni ..**  
**haribhaktula yiṇṭi yannamu gonuvāri**  
**varuvuḍai yuṇḍavalenanna jālu**  
**paramabhāgavata bhavanambula jeḍḍa**  
**puruvu dānayi poḍamina jālu .... inni ..**  
**vāsudēvuni bhaktavarula dāsulu munnu**  
**rōsina yeṅgili rucigonnajālu**  
**śrī satīśuni dalañcinavāri dāsāna**  
**dāsuḍaivuṇḍa dalacina jālu .... inni ..**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭēśu jūcinavāri śrīpāda**  
**sēvakuḍai yuṇḍajējērina jālu**  
**ī vibhudāsula hitula pādadhūḷi**  
**pāvanamai sōki bradikina jālu .... inni ..**  
These many births, of what use are they?  
If we reside in a town inhabited by Hari-daasas, that alone is sufficient\!  
Those who partake food from the house of Hari-bhaktas  
to think of being their servant, it is sufficient\!  
In the houses of Parama Bhagavathas  
even if one lives with low esteem, it is enough\!  
Those who are the servants of the devotees of Vasudeva  
even to taste their leftover food, it is enough\!  
Those who are the servants of the servants of the ones who contemplate on SreePathi  
to even think of becoming their servants, it is enough\!  
Those who have perceived Sree Venkatesha  
to even think of serving their respectable feet, it is enough\!  
Those well wishers who are the Daasas of this Lord  
touching the dust of their feet, becoming pious, and living thus is enough\!\!\!
