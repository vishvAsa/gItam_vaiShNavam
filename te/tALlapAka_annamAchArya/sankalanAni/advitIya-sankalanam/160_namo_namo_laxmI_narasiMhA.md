**Namō Namō Lakṣmī Narasiṃhā**  
नमो नमो लक्ष्मी नरसिंहा  
नमो नमो सुग्रीवनरसिंहा ॥॥ नमो ॥  
वरद सुलभ भक्तवत्सल नरसिंह  
नरमृगवेष श्री नरसिंहा  
परमपुरुष सर्व परिपूर्ण नरसिंह  
गिरिगुहवास सुग्रीवनरसिंहा ॥॥ नमो ॥  
भयहर प्रह्लादपालन नरसिंह  
नयनत्रयारविन्द नरसिंह  
जय जय सुरमुनिसंस्तुत नरसिंह  
क्रिया कलाप सुग्रीवनरसिंहा ॥॥ नमो ॥  
अति कृपानिलय मोहनरूप नरसिंह  
नत पितामह मुख्य नरसिंह  
सतत श्री वेङ्कटेश्वर दिव्य नरसिंह  
कितवारिभञ्जन सुग्रीवनरसिंहा ॥॥ नमो ॥  
**namō namō lakṣmī narasiṃhā**  
**namō namō sugrīvanarasiṃhā .... namō ..**  
**varada sulabha bhaktavatsala narasiṃha**  
**naramṛgavēṣa śrī narasiṃhā**  
**paramapuruṣa sarva paripūrṇa narasiṃha**  
**giriguhavāsa sugrīvanarasiṃhā .... namō ..**  
**bhayahara prahlādapālana narasiṃha**  
**nayanatrayāravinda narasiṃha**  
**jaya jaya suramunisaṃstuta narasiṃha**  
**kriyā kalāpa sugrīvanarasiṃhā .... namō ..**  
**ati kṛpānilaya mōhanarūpa narasiṃha**  
**nata pitāmaha mukhya narasiṃha**  
**satata śrī vēṅkaṭēśvara divya narasiṃha**  
**kitavāribhañjana sugrīvanarasiṃhā .... namō ..**  
Salutations to You, O Lakshmi Narasimha\!  
Salutations to You, O Sugreeva Narasimha\!  
One who bestows boons easily, who is affectionate towards his devotees, Narasimha\!  
The one who is in the form of man and beast, Narasimha\!  
The Supreme Purusha with completely perfect attributes, Narasimha\!  
The one who resides in the cave in the mountain, Sugreeva Narasimha\!  
The one who dispels fears, the one who blessed Prahalada, Narasimha\!  
With three lotus like eyes, Narasimha\!  
Victory unto You who is praised by Gods and sages, O Narasimha\!  
O one who ordains all deeds, Sugreeva Narasimha\!  
O abode of extreme compassion, O handsome one, Narasimha\!  
Even the great Brahma prostrates before You, O Narasimha\!  
Eternally You are SreeVenkateshwara, O divine Narasimha\!  
Destroyer of evil ones, O Narasimha\!
