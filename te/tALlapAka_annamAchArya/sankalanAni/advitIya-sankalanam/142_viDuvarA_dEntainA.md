**Viḍuvarā Dentainā**  
विडुवरा दॆन्तैना वॆऱ्ऱिवाडनैन नीकु  
कडवारु नव्वकण्डा गाचुको नन्नुनु ॥॥ विडुवरा ॥  
ज्ञानमु नेनॆऱग नज्ञानमु ने नॆऱगनु  
माननु विषयमुलु मरिगॆन्तैना  
नीनाममु नॊडिगि नीदासुड ननुकॊन्दु  
दीनिके वहिञ्चुकॊनि तिद्दुको नन्नुनु ॥॥ विडुवरा ॥  
अकर्ममु नॆऱगनु सुकर्ममु नॆऱगनु  
प्रकटसंसारमुपै पाटु माननु  
वॊकपनिवाडनै वूनि मुद्रधारिनैति  
मॊकमोडि यिन्दुके गोमुन नेलु नन्नुनु ॥॥ विडुवरा ॥  
वॆनक गाननु मुन्दु विचारिञ्चि काननु  
निनुपै देहधारिनै नीकु मॊक्केनु  
घनुड श्रीवेङ्कटेश कन्नुलॆदुट बडिति  
कनि पोविडुवरादु करुणिञ्चु नन्नुनु ॥॥ विडुवरा ॥  
**viḍuvarā dentainā veṟṟivāḍanaina nīku**  
**kaḍavāru navvakaṇḍā gācukō nannunu .... viḍuvarā ..**  
**jñānamu nēneṟaga najñānamu nē neṟaganu**  
**mānanu viṣayamulu marigentainā**  
**nīnāmamu noḍigi nīdāsuḍa nanukondu**  
**dīnikē vahiñcukoni tiddukō nannunu .... viḍuvarā ..**  
**akarmamu neṟaganu sukarmamu neṟaganu**  
**prakaṭasaṃsāramupai pāṭu mānanu**  
**vokapanivāḍanai vūni mudradhārinaiti**  
**mokamōḍi yindukē gōmuna nēlu nannunu ..viḍuvarā ..**  
**venaka gānanu mundu vicāriñci kānanu**  
**ninupai dēhadhārinai nīku mokkēnu**  
**ghanuḍa śrīvēṅkaṭēśa kannuleduṭa baḍiti**  
**kani pōviḍuvarādu karuṇiñcu nannunu ..viḍuvarā ..**  
Do not let go of me whatever it may be, I am madly devoted to You  
Lest others laugh at me, do protect me\!  
I do not know what knowledge is, what ignorance is  
I never let go of the material things, having become addicted to them  
But chanting Your name, I consider myself as Your Dasa  
For this reason, bearing with me, do correct my ways\!  
I do not know of inaction, nor of right action  
The struggles for samsara, I never let go  
But with one reason I have worn the Mudras1  
For this reason, do rule over me affectionately\!  
I do not think of the past, nor do I worry about the future  
But bearing this body, I bow down to You  
O magnanimous SreeVenkatesha\! I stood before You  
Having seen me, You should not let go\! Do shower Your compassion\!  
1.The permanent marks of Shanka-Chakra worn on the shoulders.
