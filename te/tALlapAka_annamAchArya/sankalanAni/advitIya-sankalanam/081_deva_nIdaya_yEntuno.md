**Dēva Nīdaya Yentunō**  
देव नीदय यॆन्तुनो दिव्यसुलभ मॆन्तुनो  
काविञ्चि गण्टगटुक काचुकुण्डे विदिवो ॥॥ देव ॥  
वॆदक नावसमा वेगुदाका निन्नु नेनु  
कदिसि नीमूर्ति कानवसमा  
यॆदुट शङ्खुचक्राल यॆट्टिदैवम नेननि  
पॊदुगुचु नीकुनीवे पॊडचूपेवुगाक ॥॥ देव ॥  
पॊगड नावसमा पुरुणिञ्चि नीगुणालु  
तगुल नावसमा नीतलपॆऱिगि  
निगिडि वेदशास्त्राल निन्नु नीवे चॆप्पुकॊनि  
पगटुमायज्ञानमु पापे विन्तेकाक ॥॥ देव ॥  
कॊलुव नावसमा गुऱुतॆऱिगि नीवॆण्ट  
चॆलगि नाचेतुल बूजिञ्चवसमा  
निलिचि श्रीवेङ्कटेश नीवे नायदलो नुण्डि  
मलसि पॆररेपुचु मन्निञ्चेवुगाक ॥॥ देव ॥  
**dēva nīdaya yentunō divyasulabha mentunō**  
**kāviñci gaṇṭagaṭuka kācukuṇḍē vidivō .... dēva ..**  
**vedaka nāvasamā vēgudākā ninnu nēnu**  
**kadisi nīmūrti kānavasamā**  
**yeduṭa śaṅkhucakrāla yeṭṭidaivama nēnani**  
**podugucu nīkunīvē poḍacūpēvugāka .... dēva ..**  
**pogaḍa nāvasamā puruṇiñci nīguṇālu**  
**tagula nāvasamā nītalapeṟigi**  
**nigiḍi vēdaśāstrāla ninnu nīvē ceppukoni**  
**pagaṭumāyajñānamu pāpē vintēkāka .... dēva ..**  
**koluva nāvasamā guṟuteṟigi nīveṇṭa**  
**celagi nācētula būjiñcavasamā**  
**nilici śrīvēṅkaṭēśa nīvē nāyadalō nuṇḍi**  
**malasi perarēpucu manniñcēvugāka .... dēva ..**  
O Lord\! How great Your compassion is\! How divinely affable You are\!  
You eagerly wait to protect us at all times\!  
Is it possible for me to search for You?  
Is it possible for me to behold Your form?  
Until You say ‘I am the Lord who wields the Shanka-Chakra’  
and manifest Yourself by your own will\!  
Is it possible for me to praise You? Or emulate your gunas?  
Can I reach You without knowing You in thoughts?  
Until You Yourself proclaim Your glories through the Veda-Shastras  
and destroy my illusion born of Maya\!  
Is it possible to worship You by comprehending You?  
Is it possible to move about with You and worship You with my hands?  
By being present firmly within my heart, O Sree Venkatesha,  
until you yourself incite me and grace me\!\!\!
