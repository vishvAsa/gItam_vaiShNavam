**Itani Kaṇṭe Mari**  
इतनि कण्टे मरिदैवमु कानमु यॆक्कड वॆदकिन नितडे  
अतिशयमगु महिमलतो वॆलसॆनु अन्निटिकाधारमुतानॆ ॥॥इतनि॥  
मदि जलधुल नॊकदैवमु वॆदकिन मत्स्यावतारम्बितडु  
अदिवो पाताळमन्दु वॆदकिते आदिकूर्म मी विष्णुडु  
पॊदुगॊनि यडवुल वॆदकि चूचिते भूवराहमनिकण्टिमि  
चॆदरक कॊण्डल गुहल वॆदकिते श्री नरसिंहम्बुन्नाडु ॥॥इतनि॥  
तॆलिसि भूनभोन्तरमुन वॆदकिन त्रिविक्रमाकृति निलिचिनदि  
पलुवीरुललो वॆदकि चूचितॆ परशुरामुडॊकडैनाडु  
तलपुन शिवुडुनु पार्वति वॆदकिन तारक ब्रह्ममु राघवुडु  
कॆलकुल नावुलमन्दल वॆदकिन कृष्णुडु रामुडुनैनाडु ॥॥इतनि॥  
पञ्चि असुरकान्तललो वॆदकिन बुद्धावतारम्बैनाडु  
मिञ्चिन कालमु कडपट वॆदकिन मीदटकल्क्यावतारमु  
अञ्चॆल जीवुललोपल वॆदकिन अन्तर्यामै मॆरसॆनु  
यॆञ्चुक इहमुन परमुन वॆदकिन अन्तर्यामै मॆरसॆनु  
यॆञ्चुक इहमुन परमुन वॆदकिन यीतडे श्रीवेङ्कटविभुडु ॥ ।इतनि॥  
**itani kaṇṭē maridaivamu kānamu yekkaḍa vedakina nitaḍē**  
**atiśayamagu mahimalatō velasenu anniṭikādhāramutāne.. itani..**  
**madi jaladhula nokadaivamu vedakina matsyāvatārambitaḍu**  
**adivō pātāḷamandu vedakitē ādikūrma mī viṣṇuḍu**  
**podugoni yaḍavula vedaki cūcitē bhūvarāhamanikaṇṭimi**  
**cedaraka koṇḍala guhala vedakitē śrī narasiṃhambunnāḍu. ..itani.**  
**telisi bhūnabhōntaramuna vedakina trivikramākṛti nilicinadi**  
**paluvīrulalō vedaki cūcite paraśurāmuḍokaḍaināḍu**  
**talapuna śivuḍunu pārvati vedakina tāraka brahmamu rāghavuḍu**  
**kelakula nāvulamandala vedakina kṛṣṇuḍu rāmuḍunaināḍu. .itani.**  
**pañci asurakāntalalō vedakina buddhāvatārambaināḍu**  
**miñcina kālamu kaḍapaṭa vedakina mīdaṭakalkyāvatāramu**  
**aṃcela jīvulalōpala vedakina aṃtaryāmai merasenu**  
**yeñcuka ihamuna paramuna vedakina yītaḍē śrīvēṅkaṭavibhuḍu . itani..**  
We cannot see another God like Him\! Wherever we seek, He alone is\!  
Having manifested with increasing glory, He is the support for all\!  
If one seeks the Lord in the ocean of thoughts, He is the Matsya Avatara\!  
If one looks for Him in the nether worlds, He is Adi Kurma, this Vishnu\!  
If one looks for Him in the forests with intent, He will be seen as Bhu Varaha\!  
If one resolutely looks for Him in caves and mountains, He is there as Sree Narasimha\!  
If one understands and seeks Him in the vast universes, He is there in the form of Trivikrama\!  
If one looks for Him amongst the mighty, Parashurama is one among them\!  
In the thoughts of Lord Shiva and Parvathi, He exists as Lord Raghava of the Taraka mantra\!  
If one looks for Him amongst the herds of cows, He is there as Krishna and Balarama\!  
If one looks for Him amongst the women of the Asura clan, He is there as Lord Buddha\!  
If one looks for Him at the end of the cycle of time, He is the Kalki Avatara\!  
If one seeks Him within the Jivas, He shines as the Antaryami\!  
If one seeks Him here and hereafter, He exists as Lord Sree Venkata\!
