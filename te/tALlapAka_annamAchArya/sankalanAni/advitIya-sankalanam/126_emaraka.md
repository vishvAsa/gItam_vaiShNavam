**Ēmaraka**  
एमरक तलचरो यिदे चालु  
कामिञ्चिनवि यॆल्ल गक्कुननॆ कलुगु ॥॥ एमरक ॥  
दुरितमुलॆल्ल दीरु दुःखमुलॆल्ल नणगु  
हरि यनि वॊक माटु अन्नाजालु  
सुरलु पूजिन्तुरु सिरुलॆल्ल जेरुनु  
मरुगुरुनि नाममटु पेरुकॊन्नजालु ॥॥ एमरक ॥  
भवमुलन्निय बाय परमु निहमुजेरु  
आवल नारायण यन्नाजालु  
भुवियॆल्ला दानेलु पुण्यमुलन्निय जेरु  
तविलि गोविन्दुनात्म दरचिन जालु ॥॥ एमरक ॥  
आनन्दमु गलुगु नज्ञानमॆल्ल बाय  
आनुक श्री वेङ्कटेश यन्नजालु  
यी नॆपान नारदादु लिन्दरु निन्दकु साक्षि  
दानवारि मन्त्र जपतपमे चालु ॥॥ एमरक ॥  
**ēmaraka talacarō yidē cālu**  
**kāmiñcinavi yella gakkunane kalugu .... ēmaraka**  
**duritamulella dīru duḥkhamulella naṇagu**  
**hari yani voka māṭu annājālu**  
**suralu pūjinturu sirulella jērunu**  
**maruguruni nāmamaṭu pērukonnajālu .... ēmaraka**  
**bhavamulanniyu bāyu paramu nihamujēru**  
**āvala nārāyaṇa yannājālu**  
**bhuviyellā dānēlu puṇyamulanniyu jēru**  
**tavili gōvindunātma daracina jālu .... ēmaraka**  
**ānandamu galugu najñānamella bāyu**  
**ānuka śrī vēṅkaṭēśa yannajālu**  
**yī nepāna nāradādu lindaru nindaku sākṣi**  
**dānavāri mantra japatapamē cālu .... ēmaraka**  
Without forgetting, do think (of Him). This alone suffices\!  
All that one desires will be instantly bestowed\!  
All sins get absolved, all sorrows are suppressed  
uttering even once, the name of ‘Hari’, is enough\!  
Even the demigods will revere, all riches accumulate  
mentioning the name of the father of Madana is enough\!  
All sorrows will go, the supreme will be attained  
saying ‘Narayana’ alone is enough\!  
One can rule over the earth, all merits can be obtained  
calling out ‘Govinda’ from our inner self is enough\!  
Bliss will be obtained, ignorance will go  
‘Sree Venkatesha’ if we say, it is enough  
For this, Narada and the others are the proof  
Mantra, Japa, and Tapa1 of Vishnu alone is enough\!\!\!  
1\. Mantras are incantations, Japa is chanting, Tapa is meditation
