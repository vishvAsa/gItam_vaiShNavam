**Maṅgaḷamu Gōvindunaku**  
मङ्गळमु गोविन्दुनकु जय \- मङ्गळमु गरुडध्वजुनकु  
मङ्गळमु सर्वात्मकुनकु धर्म स्वरूपुनकू जय जय॥॥मङ्ग॥  
आदिकि नादैनदेवुन कच्युतुन कम्भोज नाभुन  
कादिकूर्मम्बैन जगदाधार मूर्तिकिनि  
वेदरक्षकुनकुनु सन्तत वेदमार्ग विहारुनकु बलि  
भेदिकिनि सामादिगान प्रियविहारुनकु॥॥मङ्ग॥  
हरिकि परमेश्वरुनकु श्रीधरुनकुनु कालान्तकुनकुनु  
परम पुरुषोत्तमुनकु बहुबन्ध दूरुनकुनु  
सुरमुनि स्तोत्रुनकु देवा सुरगण श्रेष्टुनकु गरुणा  
करुनकुनु कात्यायनीनुत कलित नामुनकु॥॥मङ्ग॥  
पङ्कजासन वरदुनकु भवपङ्क विच्छेदुनकु भवुनकु  
शङ्करुन कव्यक्तुनकु नाश्चर्य रूपुनकु  
वेङ्कटाचल वल्लभुनकु विश्वमूर्तिकि नीश्वरुनकुनु  
पङ्कजाकुचकुम्भ कुङ्कुम पङ्कलोलुनकु जयजय॥॥मङ्ग॥

Benedictions to Lord Govinda, Victory to the Lord with the Garuda flag\!  
Benedictions to the indweller of all, Victory to the embodiment of Dharma\!  
To the Lord who is the origin of all origins, to Achyutha, the lotus naveled one,  
to the Lord who supports the world as the Adi Kurma,  
to the one who protected the Vedas, the one who joyously treads the path of the Vedas,  
to the Lord who vanquished king Bali, to the enjoyer of the Sama-gaana,  
many benedictions\!  
To Hari, the Supreme Lord, to Sreedhara, to the one who is above time,  
to the Supreme Purushottama, to the one who cuts off various attachments,  
to the one praised by the sages, to the best one among divinities,  
to the compassionate one, to the one whose name is praised by Goddess Parvathi,  
many benedictions\!  
To the one who blessed Brahma, to the one who destroys the mire of existence,  
to the one who is not known even by Brahma and Shiva, to that marvelous form,  
to the Lord of Venkatachala, to the Universal deity, to the Supreme ruler,  
to the one who is enamored by the vermillion from the beautiful breasts of Lakshmi,  
many praises and benedictions\!\!
