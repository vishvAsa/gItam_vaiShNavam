**Ekkuva Kulajuḍaina**  
ऎक्कुव कुलजुडैन हीनकुलजाडैन  
निक्कमॆरिगिन महानित्युडे घनुडु ॥॥ ऎक्कुव ॥  
वेदमुलु चदिवियनु विमुखुडै हरिभक्ति  
यादरिञ्चनि सोमयाजिकण्टॆ  
येदियनु लेनि कुलहीनुडैननु विष्णु  
पादमुलु सेविञ्चु भक्तुडे घनुडु ॥॥ ऎक्कुव ॥  
परममगु वेदान्त पठन दॊरिकिय सदा  
हरिभक्तिलेनि सन्यासिकण्टॆ  
सरवि मालिन यन्त्यजाति कुलजुडैन  
नरसिविष्णुनि वॆदकु नातडे घनुडु ॥॥ ऎक्कुव ॥  
विनिय जदिवियनु श्री विभुनि दासुडुगाक  
तनुवु वेपुचुनुण्डु तपसिकण्टॆ  
ऎनलेनि तिरुवेङ्कटेशु प्रसादान्न  
मनुभविञ्चिन यातडप्पुडे घनुडु ॥॥ ऎक्कुव ॥  
**ekkuva kulajuḍaina hīnakulajūḍaina**  
**nikkamerigina mahānityuḍē ghanuḍu ..ekkuva ..**  
**vēdamulu cadiviyunu vimukhuḍai haribhakti**  
**yādariñcani sōmayājikaṇṭe**  
**yēdiyunu lēni kulahīnuḍainanu viṣṇu**  
**pādamulu sēviñcu bhaktuḍē ghanuḍu .... ekkuva..**  
**paramamagu vēdānta paṭhana dorikiyu sadā**  
**haribhaktilēni sanyāsikaṇṭe**  
**saravi mālina yantyajāti kulajuḍaina**  
**narasiviṣṇuni vedaku nātaḍē ghanuḍu .... ekkuva**  
**viniyu jadiviyunu śrī vibhuni dāsuḍugāka**  
**tanuvu vēpucunuṇḍu tapasikaṇṭe**  
**enalēni tiruvēṅkaṭēśu prasādānna**  
**manubhaviñcina yātaḍappuḍē ghanuḍu .... ekkuva**  
Whether of a high caste, or of a low caste  
the one who knows the truth, he alone is the great one\!  
A Somayaji1 well versed in the Vedas, but averse  
to Hari-Bhakti, better than him is  
the great one who has nothing, and is of a low caste  
but worships the feet of Lord Vishnu\!  
A Sanyasi who has access to the study of the great Vedanta,  
but does not have constant Hari-Bhakti, better than him is  
the great one who is disorderly and is of the lowest caste  
but closely seeks Vishnu\!  
An Ascetic who having heard and studied, yet who does not become  
a Hari-daasa but who tortures his body, better than him is  
the one who has partaken and relished the prasada of TiruVenkatesha\!  
Such a person becomes great at once\!  
1\. Somayaji is one who performs Yagnas or sacrifices
