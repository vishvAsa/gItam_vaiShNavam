**Ēmisētu Ninduku Mandēmaina**  
एमिसेतु निन्दुकु मन्देमैन बोयरादा  
सामज गुरुड नीतो सङ्गमॊल्लदेटिके?॥एमि॥  
मायल संसारमु मरिगिन कर्ममु  
यी यॆड निनु मरुग देटिको हरि  
कायज केलिपै दमि गलिगिन मनसु  
कायजुतण्ड्रि नीपै गलुगु ददेटिके?॥एमि॥  
नाटकपु गककमु नमकमिनट्टि बदुकु  
येटिकि नी भक्ति नम्मदेले हरि  
गूट बडे पदवुलु गोरेटि जीवुडु  
कूटुवैन निजमुक्ति गोरिदि(डि)दि येटिकी?॥एमि॥  
पाप पुण्यमुलकॆ पालुबड्ड नेनु  
येपुन नीपाल जिक्कि नेलको हरि  
श्रीपति नालोनि श्री वेङ्कटेशुड  
नी पेरि वाड नाकु निण्डु मायलेटिकि?॥एमि॥  
**ēmisētu ninduku mandēmaina bōyarādā**  
**sāmaja guruḍa nītō saṅgamolladēṭikē?..ēmi..**  
**māyala saṃsāramu marigina karmamu**  
**yī yeḍa ninu maruga dēṭikō hari**  
**kāyaja kēlipai dami galigina manasu**  
**kāyajutaṇḍri nīpai galugu dadēṭikē?..ēmi..**  
**nāṭakapu gakakamu namakaminaṭṭi baduku**  
**yēṭiki nī bhakti nammadēlē hari**  
**gūṭa baḍē padavulu gōrēṭi jīvuḍu**  
**kūṭuvaina nijamukti gōridi(ḍi)di yēṭikī?..ēmi..**  
**pāpa puṇyamulake pālubaḍḍa nēnu**  
**yēpuna nīpāla jikki nēlakō hari**  
**śrīpati nālōni śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍa**  
**nī pēri vāḍa nāku niṇḍu māyalēṭiki?..ēmi..**  
What can I do for this? Will You not give me some medicine?  
Why do I never seek Your company, O father of Manmatha\!  
The karmas have become addicted to the Samsara which is full of Maya,  
Likewise, why do I not get addicted to You, O Hari?  
The mind which is eager to indulge in bodily pleasures,  
Why does it not feel the same eagerness for You, O father of Manmatha\!  
The life which believes in the deceptive wealth of gold,  
Why does it never believe in devotion for You, O Hari?  
The Jiva which desires impermanent posts,  
Why does it never desire the true eternal salvation?  
Me who is engaged in both sinful and virtuous deeds  
Why do I never come into Your fold, O Hari?  
Sree-Pathi\! O one who resides within\! Sree Venkatesha\!  
When I possess (the power of) Your name, why this strong maya for me?
