**Vāḍē Veṅkaṭēśuḍanē Vāḍē Vīḍu**  
वाडे वॆङ्कटेशुडने वाडे वीडु  
वाडि चुट्टुकैदुव वलचेतिवाडु ॥॥ वाडे ॥  
कारिमारसुतुनि चक्कनि माटलकु चॊक्कि  
चूरगा वेदालगुट्टु चूपिनवाडु  
तीरनि वेडुकतो तिरुमङ्गैयाळुवारि  
आरडि मुच्चिमिकूटिकि आसपड्डवाडु ॥॥ वाडे ॥  
पॆरियाळुवारिबिड्ड पिसिकि पै वेसिन  
विरुल दण्डल मॆडवेसिनवाडु  
तरुणि चेयिवेसिन, दग्गरि, बुजमु चाचि  
परवशमै चॊक्कि पायलेनि वाडु ॥॥ वाडे ॥  
पामरुल दनमीदि पाटलॆल्ला पाडुमण्टा  
भूमिकॆल्ला नोर नूरि पोसिनवाडु  
मामकूतुरलमेलुमङ्ग नाञ्चारियदानु  
गीमुगाने वेङ्कटगिरि नुण्डेवाडु ॥॥ वाडे ॥

**pāmarula danamīdi pāṭalellā pāḍumaṇṭā**  
**bhūmikellā nōra nūri pōsinavāḍu**  
**māmakūturalamēlumaṅga nāñcāriyudānu**  
**gīmugānē vēṅkaṭagiri nuṇḍēvāḍu .... vāḍē ..**  
He alone is the one who is called as Venkatesha\!  
He is the one holding the sharp weapon- the discus\!  
Immensely liking the beautiful words of the son of Kari and Mara1  
He is the one who readily revealed the secrets of Vedas to him\!  
With great joy, Tirumangai Alwars food offerings  
which were procured by looting, He desired to partake\!  
The garland of flowers worn by Peri Alwars daughter2  
that crumpled garland, He wore around His neck\!  
When she offered her hand, he extended His arms in embrace  
with delightful ecstasy, and remained inseparable (from her)\!  
To make even ignorant ones sing His praises,  
He filled the earth with His grace\!  
Along with His consort Alamelumanga  
He alone is the one who made Venkatagiri His abode\!\!\!  
1.Nammalwar is the son of Kari and Mara  
2.Goda devi
