**Kaṇṭigaṇṭi Niluvu Cakkanimēnu**  
कण्टिगण्टि निलुवु चक्कनिमेनु दण्डलुनु  
नण्टुजापुलनु जाचे नव्वुमोमुदेवुनि॥॥कण्टि॥  
कनकपु बादमुलु गज्जॆलु नन्दॆलुनु  
घनपीताम्बरमु पैकट्टु कटारि  
मॊनसि यॆड्डाणपु मॊगपुल मॊलनूलु  
ऒनर नाभीकमल मुदरबन्धमुलु॥॥कण्टि॥  
गरिम वरदहस्त कटिहस्तमुलुनु  
सरस नॆत्तिन शङ्खचक्रहस्तमुलु  
उरमु पै कौस्तुभ मॊप्पगु हारमुलु  
... ... ... ... .... .... ....॥कण्टि॥  
कट्टिन कण्टसरुल घनभुजकीर्तुलु  
कट्टाणिमुत्याल सिङ्गारनाममु  
नॆट्टन श्रीवेङ्कटेश नीकु गर्णपत्रमुलु-  
नट्टॆ सिरसुमीद नमरे किरीटमु ॥॥कण्टि॥  
**kaṇṭigaṇṭi niluvu cakkanimēnu daṇḍalunu**  
**naṇṭujūpulanu jūcē navvumōmudēvuni....kaṇṭi..**  
**kanakapu bādamulu gajjelu nandelunu**  
**ghanapītāmbaramu paikaṭṭu kaṭāri**  
**monasi yoḍḍāṇapu mogapula molanūlu**  
**onara nābhīkamala mudarabandhamulu....kaṇṭi..**  
**garima varadahasta kaṭihastamulunu**  
**sarasa nettina śaṅkhacakrahastamulu**  
**uramu pai kaustubha moppagu hāramulu**  
**... ... ... ... .... .... ......kaṇṭi..**  
**kaṭṭina kaṇṭasarula ghanabhujakīrtulu**  
**kaṭṭāṇimutyāla siṅgāranāmamu**  
**neṭṭana śrīvēṅkaṭēśa nīku garṇapatramulu-**  
**naṭṭe sirasumīda namarē kirīṭamu ....kaṇṭi..**  
I saw\! I saw\! The tall beautiful body decorated with garlands\!  
The one who casts mesmerizing looks, the Lord with the smiling face\!  
(I saw)  
The golden feet, and the dazzling anklets with small bells  
The golden couloured silk robes, the dagger tied over it\! (I saw)  
The beautiful waist belt and the clasping waist thread  
For the stomach with a lotus like navel, a befitting belt\! (I saw)  
The great Varada Hasta and the Kati Hasta\!  
The hands that have upheld the Shanka-Chakra  
The Kaustubha on the chest, and the befitting garlands (I saw)  
——————————-------——————————————  
The closely tied necklaces, the great shoulder ornaments  
The beautiful Tirunamam seeming like a string of pure pearls  
Sree Venkatesha\! For You there are these attractive ear-rings  
Likewise, the befitting crown on Your head\! (I saw)
