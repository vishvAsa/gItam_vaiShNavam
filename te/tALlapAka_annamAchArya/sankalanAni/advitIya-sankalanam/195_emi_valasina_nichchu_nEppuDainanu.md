**Ēmi Valasina Niccu Neppuḍainanu**  
एमि वलसिन निच्चु नॆप्पुडैननु  
एमऱक कॊलचिन नीतडे दैवमु ॥॥एमि॥  
घनमुगा निन्दऱिकि गन्नु लिच्चु गाळ्ळिच्चु  
पनिसेय जेतु लिच्चु बलियडै  
तनु गॊलुवुमनि चित्तमु लिच्चु गरुणिञ्चि  
वॊनर लोकानकॆल्ल नॊक्कडे दैवमु ॥॥एमि॥  
मच्चिक तनु गॊलुव मनसिच्चु माटलिच्चु  
कुच्चितमुलेनि कॊडुकुल निच्चुनु  
चॊच्चिन चोटे चॊच्चि शुभ मिच्चु सुख मिच्चु  
निच्चलु लोकानकॆल्ल निजमैन दैवमु ॥॥एमि॥  
पन्तमाडि कॊलिचिन ब्राण मिच्चु प्राय मिच्चु  
यॆन्तटि पदवुलैन निट्टॆ यिच्चु  
विन्तविन्त विभवाल वेङ्कटेशु डिदे मा-  
यन्तरङ्गमुन नुण्डे अरचेति दैवमु ॥॥एमि॥  
**ēmi valasina niccu neppuḍainanu**  
**ēmaṟaka kolacina nītaḍē daivamu ....ēmi..**  
**ghanamugā nindaṟiki gannu liccu gāḷḷiccu**  
**panisēya jētu liccu baliyuḍai**  
**tanu goluvumani cittamu liccu garuṇiñci**  
**vonara lōkānakella nokkaḍē daivamu ....ēmi..**  
**maccika tanu goluva manasiccu māṭaliccu**  
**kuccitamulēni koḍukula niccunu**  
**coccina cōṭē cocci śubha miccu sukha miccu**  
**niccalu lōkānakella nijamaina daivamu ....ēmi..**  
**pantamāḍi kolicina brāṇa miccu prāya miccu**  
**yentaṭi padavulaina niṭṭe yiccu**  
**vintavinta vibhavāla vēṅkaṭēśu ḍidē mā-**  
**yantaraṅgamuna nuṇḍē aracēti daivamu ....ēmi..**  
He bestows at any time whatever is necessary,  
if we worship Him without forgetting\! He alone is the Lord\!  
Magnanimously He gives everyone eyes and limbs,  
He gives hands with strength to engage in work  
Compassionately He gives the heart for the purpose of worshipping Him  
He alone is the Lord for the entire world\!  
To worship Him fondly, He gives the mind and speech  
He is the one who gives good guileless progeny  
Having entered within us, He bestows auspiciousness and happiness  
Eternally for all the worlds, He is the true Lord\!  
If we worship Him assiduously, He bestows life and youth  
The highest post, He easily bestows\!  
He is Lord Venkatesha, who is full of glories  
He is the easily attainable Lord who resides in our innermost hearts\!
