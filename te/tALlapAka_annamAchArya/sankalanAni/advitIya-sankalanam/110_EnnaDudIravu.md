**Ennaḍudīravu**  
ऎन्नडुदीरवु यी पनुलु  
पन्निन नीमाय बहुळम्बाय ॥॥ ऎन्नडु ॥  
पॆक्कुमतम्बुल पॆद्दलु नडचिरि  
वॊक्कसम्मतै वॊडबडरु  
पॆक्कु देवतलु पेरुवाडॆदरु  
तक्कक घनुलमु तामेयनुचु ॥॥ ऎन्नडु ॥  
पलिकॆटि चदुवुलु बहुमार्गम्बुलु  
कलसि येकवाक्यत गादु  
चल वादम्बुलु जनुलुनु मानरु  
एलु तर्कम्बुलॆ पचरिञ्चेरु ॥॥ ऎन्नडु ॥  
शरणागतुलकु श्री वेङ्कटेश्वर  
तिरमुग नीवे तीर्चितिवि  
परमवैष्णवुलु पट्टिरि व्रतमु  
यिरुवुग नाचार्यु लॆरुगुदुरु ॥॥ ऎन्नडु ॥  
**ennaḍudīravu yī panulu**  
**pannina nīmāya bahuḷambāya .... ennaḍu ..**  
**pekkumatambula peddalu naḍaciri**  
**vokkasammatai voḍabaḍaru**  
**pekku dēvatalu pēruvāḍedaru**  
**takkaka ghanulamu tāmēyanucu .... ennaḍu ..**  
**palikeṭi caduvulu bahumārgambulu**  
**kalasi yēkavākyata gādu**  
**cala vādambulu janulunu mānaru**  
**ēlu tarkambule pacariñcēru .... ennaḍu ..**  
**śaraṇāgatulaku śrī vēṅkaṭēśvara**  
**tiramuga nīvē tīrcitivi**  
**paramavaiṣṇavulu paṭṭiri vratamu**  
**yiruvuga nācāryu leruguduru .... ennaḍu ..**  
These kinds of deeds are never diminishing,  
Your pervasive Maya is ever increasing\!  
Having tread the path of various doctrines  
yet they do not prevail upon one thing.  
They use the names of various Gods,  
but still proclaim that they themselves are Gods\!  
Having studied the very many paths,  
they do not proclaim one common statement.  
People do not give up their rigid arguments,  
yet they speak about logical reasoning\!  
For Sharanagathas, O Sree Venkateshwara,  
You alone clear the overpowering doubts\!  
This is the vow being followed by Parama Vaishnavas,  
and only the Acharyas understand it for certain\!  
Annamacharya here upholds the path of Sharanagathi, saying that  
it is the path followed by Parama Vaishnavas and also by the  
Acharyas. And that only they are capable of comprehending its true  
glory\!
