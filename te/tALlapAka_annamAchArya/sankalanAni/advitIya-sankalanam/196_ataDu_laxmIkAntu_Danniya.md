**Ataḍu Lakṣmīkāntu Ḍanniyu**  
अतडु लक्ष्मीकान्तु डन्निय नॊसगुगाक  
यितरुलवेडुकॊण्टे नेमि गलदु? ॥॥अतडु॥  
मोदमुतो नॊकमानि मॊदलबोसिन नीरु  
पादुकॊनि कॊनकॆक्कि फलिञ्चुगाक  
आदिमूलमगु हरि नातुम सेविञ्चक  
ये दैवमुलगन्ना नेमि सॆलवु? ॥॥अतडु॥  
तल्लि भुजिञ्चिन वॆल्ला तगु गर्भमु शिशुवु  
कॆल्लगाग बरिणाम मिच्चुगाक  
उल्लमुलोपलि हरि कॊसगनि पूजलॆल्ला  
वॆल्लि जिन्तपण्टिवलॆ वृथा वृथा \! ॥॥अतडु॥  
ओलिनॆन्त जारिपड्ड नूरके यॆव्वरिकैना  
नेले याधारमै निलुचुगाक,  
तालिमि श्री वेङ्कटेशु दलचक तलचिना  
पालिञ्च नातडेकाक परुलकु वशमा? ॥॥अतडु॥  
**ataḍu lakṣmīkāntu ḍanniyu nosagugāka**  
**yitarulavēḍukoṇṭē nēmi galadu? ....ataḍu..**  
**mōdamutō nokamāni modalabōsina nīru**  
**pādukoni konakekki phaliñcugāka**  
**ādimūlamagu hari nātuma sēviñcaka**  
**yē daivamulagannā nēmi selavu? ....ataḍu..**  
**talli bhujiñcina vellā tagu garbhamu śiśuvu**  
**kellagāga bariṇāma miccugāka**  
**ullamulōpali hari kosagani pūjalellā**  
**velli jintapaṇṭivale vṛthā vṛthā \! ....ataḍu..**  
**ōlinenta jāripaḍḍa nūrakē yevvarikainā**  
**nēlē yādhāramai nilucugāka,**  
**tālimi śrī vēṅkaṭēśu dalacaka talacinā**  
**pāliñca nātaḍēkāka parulaku vaśamā? ....ataḍu..**  
He is the Lord of Lakshmi, who can bestow everything\!  
Pleading with others is of what use?  
Only by joyously watering the base of a plant, the  
water makes way to the top and bears fruit,  
So too, without serving Hari, the primal cause (Adimoola)  
worshipping other Gods is of what use?  
Just as the food that the mother consumes, to the  
baby in her womb, it reaches and nourishes,  
So too, without offering worship to Hari, who is present within,  
all goes wasteful, as a tamarind washed away in floods\!  
From wherever one slips and falls, whoever it may be  
the ground remains the final support,  
So too, whether you think or not, Sree Venkatesha  
is the only savior, Can anybody else be??  
**Purport:**  
Just as watering the root of the plant is the only beneficial act, rather than watering each  
flower separately, so too, only serving the supreme origin of all creation, Lord Vishnu, is  
beneficial\!  
Just as the baby in the womb can be fed only through the mother, so too, there is no use  
trying to worship all other Gods who are sustained by Lord Sree Hari\!
