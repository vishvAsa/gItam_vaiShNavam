**Purport:**  
“AA Brahma bhuvanan lokaha punaravartinorjuna”  
‘All the worlds down from the realm of Brahma are subject  
to return, O Arjuna’  
All the worlds down from the realm of Brahma are destructible  
during the dissolution of the entire creation. These worlds re-emerge  
from the body of the Supreme Lord Narayana during the next creative  
cycle. Those who attain such impermanent worls are subject to return.  
The rulers of such worlds like Brahma, Indra and others are themselves  
subject to return and so they are in no position to grant salvation to  
anybody.  
“Maam upetya tu Kaunteya Punar janma na Vidhyathe”  
“But on reaching Me, there is no return”  
The Supreme Lord Narayana is the Overlord of the entire Universe.  
He is the one who sends forth the multitude of Universes from His  
body. He creates the various Gods like Brahma and others. It is He  
who sustains the entire Universe and also it is into Him that all worlds  
merge at the time of dissolution. However, He Himself remains  
unaffected through all this and remain as the Sovereign master whose  
sport is creation, sustentation and dissolution. He remains in the same  
unchanging true form at all times and is immutable. For this reason,  
there is no destruction in case of those who reach Him. He is the sole  
Lord capable of bestowing Moksha\!  
Thus, He Himself advices  
“Manmanabhava madbhakto madyagi mam namaskuru  
Mamevaisyasi satyam te pratijane priyoasi me”  
Focus your mind on Me, Be My devotee, Be My worshipper,  
prostrate before Me. To Me alone you shall come. I promise you  
truly, for you are dear to me”  
The Supremely compassionate Lord, out of utmost love for His dear  
devotee Arjuna, declares that one can attain the highest only by  
worshipping Him. Out of his overflowing compassion, He promises  
the devotee that by worshipping Him, one can attain Him speedity.  
Annamacharya has composed his kirtana out of his deep knowledge  
of Ramanyjacharya's Gita Bhasya. These verses quoted here find  
their true elaborate meanings in the divine work of Ramanuja.  
Annamayya here sings the essence\!

Chanda-Prachanda, Jaya-Vijaya and the others,  
only after worshipping them first, is the worship of Hari\!  
Consecrating Kurma, Bhumi and others devoutly,  
worshipping appropriately, important ones like Dharma, Adharma, and  
others like the ones who bear the Chamaras,  
only then follows the worship of Hari\!  
Garuda, Vishwaksena, the great Sesha,  
those other blessed ones who massage the feet of the Lord,  
the adorning weapons like Shanka, Chakra, Gada, Sharga and others,  
only after worshipping them joyously, is the worship of Hari\!  
The ever resplendent crown, the bead necklaces, the Kousthubha,  
the fine silk robes and other such adornments,  
worshipping them first through various courtesies, and then submitting  
the various offerings, follows the Pooja of Lord Venkateshwara\!  
The appropriate method of the Deity worship of Lord Vishnu has been  
elaborately described in ‘Nitya’ written by Sri Ramanujacharya.  
Annamacharya here sings the essence of the following verse which  
salutes the nityas and muktas along with Lord Vishnu at Vaikuntam.

I salute Lord Narayana in the form of a Tortoise bearing on its back the entire  
universe. I pay salutation to the fourteen worlds of the universe including the earth.  
I salute the eternal Vaikunta, the mantap studded with jewels, the baseof the four  
pillars of the mantap representing Dharma, Jnana, Vairagya, Aishwarya; the body of  
the pillars representing Adharma, Ajnana, Avairagya, Anaishwarya; Sesha within the  
mantap; the Lotus flower in the middle of Sesha, the eight ladies standing on the eight  
petals, offering the service of Chamara (Fanning) to the lord; The supreme Lord who  
is seated at the centre of the lotus along with His consorts \- Sri, Bhu, Neela; the  
Acharyas on the left side; the dress and ornaments worn by the Lord such as silk  
cloth, crown, ear rings, waist belt; the weapons of the Lord such as the Conch,  
Discuss, Mace, Bow, Sword, Lotus; the Paduka of the Lord; Vainateya (Garuda),  
Vishwaksena; Guards at the door way such as Chanda-Prachanda, Bhadra-Subhadra,  
Dhatra-Vidhatra; Jaya-Vijaya guarding the doors of Vaikuntam in the four directions;  
the other armed guards protecting the eight directions of Vaikuntam, the other  
devotees of Lord Vishnu such as Nityas and Muktas. I salute the auspicious form of  
Lord Sriman Narayana with all his Paraphernalia.
