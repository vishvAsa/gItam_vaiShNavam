**Dācukō Nī Pādālaku**  
दाचुको नी पादालकु तगने जेसिन पूजलिवि  
पूचि नीकीरिति रूप पुष्पमु लिवि अय्या ॥॥ दाचुको ॥  
वॊक्क सङ्कीर्तने चालु वॊद्दिकै मम्मु रक्षिञ्चग  
तक्किनवि भण्डारान दाचि वुण्डनी  
वॆक्कसमगु नी नाममु वॆल सुलभमु फलमधिकमु  
दिक्कै नन्नेलितिविक अवि तीरनि नाधनमय्या ॥॥ दाचुको ॥  
ना नालिकपै नुण्डि नाना सङ्कीर्तनलु  
पूनि नाचे निन्नु पॊगडिञ्चितिवि  
वे नामाल वॆन्नुडा विनुतिञ्च नॆन्तवाड  
कानिम्मनि ना कीपुण्यमु गट्टिति विन्ते अय्या ॥॥ दाचुको ॥  
यीमाट गर्वमुगादु नी महिमे कॊनियाडितिगानि  
चेमुञ्चि नास्वातन्त्र्यमु चॆप्पिन वाडगानु  
नेमान पाडेवाडनु नेरमु लॆञ्चकु मी  
श्रीमाधव नी दासुड श्रीवेङ्कटेशुडवय्या अय्या ॥॥ दाचुको ॥  
**dācukō nī pādālaku taganē jēsina pūjalivi**  
**pūci nīkīriti rūpa puṣpamu livi ayyā ..dācukō ..**  
**vokka saṅkīrtanē cālu voddikai mammu rakṣiñcaga**  
**takkinavi bhaṇḍārāna dāci vuṇḍanī**  
**vekkasamagu nī nāmamu vela sulabhamu phalamadhikamu**  
**dikkai nannēlitivika avi tīrani nādhanamayyā ..dācukō ..**  
**nā nālikapai nuṇḍi nānā saṅkīrtanalu**  
**pūni nācē ninnu pogaḍiñcitivi**  
**vē nāmāla vennuḍā vinutiñca nentavāḍa**  
**kānimmani nā kīpuṇyamu gaṭṭiti vintē ayyā ..dācukō ..**  
**yīmāṭa garvamugādu nī mahimē koniyāḍitigāni**  
**cēmuñci nāsvātantryamu ceppina vāḍagānu**  
**nēmāna pāḍēvāḍanu nēramu leñcaku mī**  
**śrīmādhava nī dāsuḍa śrīvēṅkaṭēśuḍavayyā ayyā.. dācukō**  
Preserve these offerings unto Your feet, performed by me as Your worship  
These blossoms are but flowers representing Your fame\!  
One Sankirtana suffices, to protect us with ease,  
let the remaining ones be preserved in the Bhandaram\!  
Satisfying is Your name, low in price but high in merits  
Since You are the only means of protection, this is my limitless wealth\!  
Through the means of my tongue, in very many sankirtanas,  
You had yourself praised by me\!  
O one with a thousand names\! O Vennuda\! Who am I to praise You?  
Taking pity, unto me You Yourself bestowed this fortune\!  
This I am not saying out of pride, I have only extolled Your glories,  
not saying anything independently out of my own free will\!  
I am a follower of the rules, please do not find fault  
O Sri Madhava\! I am your servant and you ‘Sri Venkatesha’\!
