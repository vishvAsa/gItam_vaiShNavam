**Mūḍē Māṭalu**  
मूडे माटलु मूडुमूण्ड्लु तॊम्मिदि  
वेडुकॊनि चदुवरो वेदान्त रहस्यमु ॥॥मूडे॥  
जीवस्वरूपमु चिन्तिञ्चि ऎन्त तानु  
देवुनि वैभवमु तॆलिसि  
भाविञ्चि प्रकृति सम्पद इदि यॆरुगुटे  
वेवेल विधमुल वेदान्त रहस्यमु ॥॥मूडे॥  
तनलोनि ज्ञानमु तप्पकुण्डा तलपोसि  
पनितोड नन्दुवल्ल भक्ति निलिपि  
मनिकिगा वैराग्यमु मरुवकुण्डुटे  
विनवलसिनयट्टि वेदान्त रहस्यमु॥॥मूडे॥  
वेडुकतो नाचार्य विश्वासमु गलिगि  
जाडल शरणागति साधनमुतो  
कूडि श्रीवेङ्कटेश्वरु गॊलिचि दासुडौटे  
वीडनि ब्रह्मानन्द वेदान्त रहस्यमु॥॥मूडे॥  
**mūḍē māṭalu mūḍumūṇḍlu tommidi**  
**vēḍukoni caduvarō vēdānta rahasyamu ....mūḍē..**  
**jīvasvarūpamu cintiñci eṃta tānu**  
**dēvuni vaibhavamu telisi**  
**bhāviñci prakṛti sampada idi yeruguṭē**  
**vēvēla vidhamula vēdānta rahasyamu ....mūḍē..**  
**tanalōni jñānamu tappakuṇḍā talapōsi**  
**panitōḍa nanduvalla bhakti nilipi**  
**manikigā vairāgyamu maruvakuṇḍuṭē**  
**vinavalasinayaṭṭi vēdānta rahasyamu....mūḍē..**  
**vēḍukatō nācārya viśvāsamu galigi**  
**jāḍala śaraṇāgati sādhanamutō**  
**kūḍi śrīvēṅkaṭēśvaru golici dāsuḍauṭē**  
**vīḍani brahmānanda vēdānta rahasyamu....mūḍē..**  
Only three words\! Three threes are nine\!  
With utmost delight, study this secret of Vedanta\!  
Contemplating about the nature of the self,  
and comprehending the glory of the Lord  
realizing this as the wealth of Prakriti,  
this itself, in thousands of ways, is the secret of Vedanta\!  
Relying on the knowledge that lies within oneself,  
continuing to perform work daily and yet engaging in Devotion,  
being wise and never forgetting to be dispassionate,  
this itself is the secret of Vedanta that needs to be heard and followed\!  
With utmost fervor, placing total faith in Acharya,  
here itself performing ‘Sharanagati’, also,  
worshipping Sree Venkateswara, becoming His dasa,  
this itself is the unchanging supremely blissful secret of Vedanta\!
