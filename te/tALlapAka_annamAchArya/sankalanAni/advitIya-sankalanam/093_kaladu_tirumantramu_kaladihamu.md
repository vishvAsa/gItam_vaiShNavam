**Kaladu Tirumantramu Kaladihamu**  
कलदु तिरुमन्त्रमु कलदिहमु बरमु  
कलिमि गलुगु माकु गडमे लेदु\!॥ कलदु ॥  
कमलाक्ष नीवु माकु गलिगियण्डग भूमि  
नमरलेनिदॊकटि नव्वललेदु  
नॆमकि नालुकमीद नीनाममु मॆलगग  
तमितो बरुल वेड दा जोटु लेदु\!॥ कलदु ॥  
शौरि नी चक्रमु नाभुजमु मीदनुण्डगानु  
मोरसपु बग लेदु यॆदुरू लेदु  
चेरुव नी सेव नाचेतुलपै नुण्डगानु  
तीरनि कर्मपु वॆट्टिदिनमू लेदु\!॥ कलदु ॥  
अच्चुत नीपै भक्तियातुमलो नुण्डगानु  
रच्चल बुट्टिन यपराधिमू लेदु  
निच्चलु श्री वॆङ्कटेश नीशरणागतुण्डगा  
विच्चिन विरे कानि विचारमे लेदु\!॥ कलदु ॥  
**kaladu tirumantramu kaladihamu baramu**  
**kalimi galugu māku gaḍamē lēdu\!.. kaladu ..**  
**kamalākṣa nīvu māku galigiyuṇḍaga bhūmi**  
**namaralēnidokaṭi navvalalēdu**  
**nemaki nālukamīda nīnāmamu melagaga**  
**tamitō barula vēḍa dā jōṭu lēdu\!.. kaladu ..**  
**śauri nī cakramu nābhujamu mīdanuṇḍagānu**  
**mōrasapu baga lēdu yedurū lēdu**  
**cēruva nī sēva nācētulapai nuṇḍagānu**  
**tīrani karmapu veṭṭidinamū lēdu\!.. kaladu ..**  
**accuta nīpai bhaktiyātumalō nuṇḍagānu**  
**raccala buṭṭina yaparādhimū lēdu**  
**niccalu śrī veṅkaṭēśa nīśaraṇāgatuṇḍagā**  
**viccina virē kāni vicāramē lēdu\!.. kaladu ..**  
It is there, the Tirumantra, for here and hereafter\!  
There shall be prosperity for us, never any obstacles\!  
O lotus eyed one\! When You are there for us,  
there is nothing in the other worlds that will not be found here\!  
If your name ruminates upon the tongue,  
there is no necessity to worship others eagerly\!  
O Showri\! If the symbol of your Discus is upon my shoulder,  
nobody can avenge or confront me\!  
If my hands are ever engaged in serving You,  
I will never be enslaved by the limitless Karmas\!  
O Achyutha\! If devotion to You is present within my ‘self’,  
there will never be any faults born out of disputes\!  
Sree Venkatesha\! When Sharanagati unto you is ever present,  
it bestows a joyful state, without any sorrow\!
