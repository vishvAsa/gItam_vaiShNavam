**Gatulanni Khilamaina**  
गतुलन्नि खिलमैन कलियग मन्दुनु  
गति यीतडे चूपॆ घन गुरुदैवमु ॥॥ गतु ॥  
यीतनि करुणनेका यिल वैष्णवुल मैति -  
मीतनिवल्लने कण्टि मीतिरुमणि  
यीतडेका वुपदेश मिच्चॆ नष्टाक्षरि मन्त्र-  
मीतडे रामानुजुलु यिहपरदैवमु ॥॥ गतु ॥  
वॆलयिञ्चॆ नीतडेका वेदपु रहस्यमुलु  
चलिमि नीतडे चूपॆ शरणागति  
निलिपिना ईतडेका निजमुद्रधारणमु  
मलसि रामानुजुले माटलाडे दैवमु ॥॥ गतु ॥  
नियममु लीत डेका निलिपॆ ब्रपन्नुलकु  
दयतो मोक्षमु चूपॆ दग नीतडे  
नयमै श्री वेङ्कटेशुनग मॆक्के वाकिटनु  
दमजाची मम्मु निट्टे तल्लि तण्ड्रि दैवमु ॥॥ गतु ॥  
**gatulanni khilamaina kaliyuga mandunu**  
**gati yītaḍē cūpe ghana gurudaivamu .... gatu ..**  
**yītani karuṇanēkā yila vaiṣṇavula maiti -**  
**mītanivallanē kaṇṭi mītirumaṇi**  
**yītaḍēkā vupadēśa micce naṣṭākṣari mantra-**  
**mītaḍē rāmānujulu yihaparadaivamu .... gatu ..**  
**velayiñce nītaḍēkā vēdapu rahasyamulu**  
**calimi nītaḍē cūpe śaraṇāgati**  
**nilipinā ītaḍēkā nijamudradhāraṇamu**  
**malasi rāmānujulē māṭalāḍē daivamu .... gatu ..**  
**niyamamu līta ḍēkā nilipe brapannulaku**  
**dayatō mōkṣamu cūpe daga nītaḍē**  
**nayamai śrī vēṅkaṭēśunaga mekkē vākiṭanu**  
**damajūcī mammu niṭṭē talli taṇḍri daivamu .... gatu ..**  
In the evil ways of Kaliyuga,  
he showed ‘The path’, the great divine guru!  
Isn’t it out of his compassion, here we are Vaishnavas,  
Because of him, we could find the Tiruman1,  
It is he who initiated us into the Ashtakshari mantra  
He is Ramanuja, the God of here and hereafter!2  
Isn’t he the one who revealed the Vedic secrets  
With benevolence he showed the path of Sharanagati  
Isn’t he the one who established the real Mudhra dharana3  
In total, Ramanuja is a ‘Speaking God’!  
Isn’t he the one who laid the rules for Prapannas4  
With compassion, he is the one who showed us Moksha.  
Having beneficially climbed Lord Sree Venkatesha’s hill,  
he blesses us at the doorway5, as mother, father and God!  
1.Sacred mud worn on the forehead as Naamam  
2.When Ramanuja visited Tirumala, the Lord bestowed upon him both the  
kingdoms- the spiritual as well as the material. Thus he is called as  
‘Ubhaya Vibhuti Pati’ or ‘The God of here and hereafter’!  
3.The method of Samashrayanam or Panchasamskara  
4.Prapannas are those who have performed Sharanagathi  
5.The Ramanuja shrine is at the doorway of the Tirumala temple
