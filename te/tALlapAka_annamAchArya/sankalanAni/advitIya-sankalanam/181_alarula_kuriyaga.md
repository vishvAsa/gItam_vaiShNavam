**Alarula Kuriyaga**  
अलरुल कुरियग नाडॆनदे  
अलकल कुलुकुल नलमेल्मङ्ग ॥॥अल॥  
अरविरि सॊबगुल नतिवलु मॆच्चग  
अरतॆर मरगुन नाडॆनदे  
वरुसग पूर्वदुवाळपु तिरुपुल  
हरिकरगिम्पुचु नलमेल्मङ्ग ॥॥अल॥  
मट्टपु मलपुल मट्टॆल कॆलपुल  
तट्टॆडि नडपुल दाटॆनदे  
पॆट्टिन वज्रपु पॆण्डॆपुतळुकुलु  
अट्टिटु चिम्मुचु नलमेल्मङ्ग ॥॥अल॥  
चिन्दुल पाटल सिरिपॊलयाटल  
अन्दॆल व्रॆूतल नाडॆनदे  
कन्दुव तिरु वेङ्कटपति मॆच्चग  
अन्दपु तिरुपुल नलमेल्मङ्ग ॥॥अल॥  
**alarula kuriyaga nāḍenadē**  
**alakala kulukula nalamēlmaṅga ....ala..**  
**araviri sobagula nativalu meccaga**  
**aratera maraguna nāḍenadē**  
**varusaga pūrvaduvāḷapu tirupula**  
**harikaragimpucu nalamēlmaṅga ....ala..**  
**maṭṭapu malapula maṭṭela kelapula**  
**taṭṭeḍi naḍapula dāṭenadē**  
**peṭṭina vajrapu peṇḍeputaḷukulu**  
**aṭṭiṭu cimmucu nalamēlmaṅga ....ala..**  
**cindula pāṭala siripolayāṭala**  
**aṃdela mrōtala nāḍenadē**  
**kanduva tiru vēṅkaṭapati meccaga**  
**aṃdapu tirupula nalamēlmaṅga ....ala..**  
Sprinkling her beauty around, She danced\!  
With graceful movements, Alamelumanga\!  
With the charm of a half bloomed flower, when women around praised Her,  
behind the translucent screens, She danced.  
Stepping with the speed of lightning,  
Melting the heart of Hari, Alamelumanga\! (She danced)  
With Her proportionate postures, with rubies in Her toe-rings  
She crossed over thresholds,  
With the shimmer of diamonds of Her anklets  
spreading around, Alamelumanga\! (She danced)  
With Jig and songs, playfully,  
with jingling anklets, She danced\!  
The passionate Lord Tiru Venkatapathi appreciating,  
swirling gracefully, Alamelumanga\! (She danced)
