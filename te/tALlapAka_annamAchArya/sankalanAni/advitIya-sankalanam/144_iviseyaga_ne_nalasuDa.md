**Ivisēyaga Nē Nalasuḍa**  
इवि सेयग ने नलसुड यॆटुवलॆ मोक्षं बडिगॆदनु  
विवरमुतोडुत नीवु सुलभुडवु विष्णुड निन्ने कॊलिचॆदगाक ॥॥ इवि ॥  
जपयज्ञ दानकर्मम्बुलु यॆञ्चग जिरकालफलम्बुलु  
यॆपुडु बुण्यतीर्थस्नानमुलु यिल पापविमोचनमुलु  
अपरिमित देवतान्तरभजनलु आयालोकप्राप्तुलु  
वुपवासादिनियमव्रतमुलु तपोनिष्ठकु गारणम्बुलु ॥॥ इवि ॥  
रविचन्द्रग्रहताराबलमुलु भुविलो गाम्यफलमुलु  
तविलिनपञ्चेन्द्रियनिग्रहम्बुलु तनुधरुलकु दुर्लभमुलु  
अविरळधर्मार्थकामम्बुलु मऱियैश्वर्यमुलकु मूलमुलु  
अवलग्रहणकालानुष्ठानमु लधिकफलम्बुलु आशामयमु ॥॥ इवि ॥  
परग सप्तसन्तानब्राह्मणतर्पणमुलु ख्यातिसुकृतमुलु  
अरय बुत्रदार क्षेत्रसङ्ग्रह मन्दरिकिनि संसारभोगमु  
हरि नरहरि श्रीवेङ्कटेश्वरा अखिलमु नॊसगॆडि दातवु  
सरुगन नीवे दयतो रक्षिञ्चजालुदु वेकालमुनु मम्मुनु ॥॥ इवि ॥  
ivi sēyaga nē nalasuḍa yeṭuvale mōkṣaṃ baḍigedanu  
vivaramutōḍuta nīvu sulabhuḍavu viṣṇuḍa ninnē kolicedagāka. ivi  
japayajña dānakarmambulu yeñcaga jirakālaphalambulu  
yepuḍu buṇyatīrthasnānamulu yila pāpavimōcanamulu  
aparimita dēvatāntarabhajanalu āyālōkaprāptulu  
vupavāsādiniyamavratamulu tapōniṣṭhaku gāraṇambulu. ivi  
ravicandragrahatārābalamulu bhuvilō gāmyaphalamulu  
tavilinapañcēndriyanigrahambulu tanudharulaku durlabhamulu  
aviraḷadharmārthakāmambulu maṟiyaiśvaryamulaku mūlamulu  
avalagrahaṇakālānuṣṭhānamu ladhikaphalambulu āśāmayamu. ivi  
paraga saptasantānabrāhmaṇatarpaṇamulu khyātisukṛtamulu  
araya butradāra kṣētrasaṅgraha mandarikini saṃsārabhōgamu  
hari narahari śrīvēṅkaṭēśvarā akhilamu nosageḍi dātavu  
sarugana nīvē dayatō rakṣiñcajāludu vēkālamunu mammunu. ivi  
To do all these, I am very indolent\! How can I even seek moksha?  
But, since You are the easily attainable one, O Vishnu, I worship You alone\!  
Japa, Yagna, Daana1, if rightly appraised, give eternal fruits  
Bathing in sacred teerthas frees one of all sins  
By unending worship of various Gods, one attains the respective worlds  
Fasting and other such austere practices are the cause of devout penance  
The effects of the sun, moon, planets, stars, bestow the object of desire upon this earth  
Trying to control the five senses, for one with a physical body, is very difficult  
Constantly engaging in Dharma, Artha, Kaama2, is the means for material wealth  
Performing the rituals during the eclipses bestow fruits born of desires  
Offering ‘Tarpanas’ to Brahmanas bestows fame and fortune  
Becoming attracted to children, wife, lands, is for everyone the pleasure of samsara  
Hari\! Nara-Hari\! SreeVenkateshwara\! You alone bestow everything\!  
Out of compassion, You should protect us at all times\!  
The purport is that Surrendering unto Sree Venkatesha will easily bestow all fruits that are  
obtained by various other means which are stringent and difficult.  
1.Japa is meditation, Yagna is sacrifice, Daana is charity  
2.Dharma is righteousness, Artha is material wealth, Kaama is desire
