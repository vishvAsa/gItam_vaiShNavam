**parusamokkaṭē kādā**  
परुसमॊक्कटे कादा पयिडिगाजेसेदि  
अरयलोहमॆट्टुन्ना अन्दुकेमी ॥॥ वरुस ॥  
वनजनाभुनि भक्तिवदलकुण्डिन जालु  
मनसु ऎन्दु तिरिगिना मरियेमि  
मॊनसि मुद्रलु भुजमुल नुण्डिते जालु  
तनुवॆन्त हेयमैना दानिकेमि ॥॥ वरुस ॥  
श्रीकान्तुनाममु जिह्वदगिलिते जालु  
ए कुलजुडैनानु हीनमेमि  
साकारुडैन हरि शरणुजॊच्चिन जालु  
चेकॊनिपापमुलॆन्नि चेसिनानेमि ॥॥ वरुस ॥  
जीवुडॆट्टुन्नानेमि जीवुनिलो अन्तरात्म  
श्रीवेङ्कटेशुनिका चिन्तयेमि  
येवलन परमैन यिहमैन माकु जालु  
कैवशमाय नीतडु कडमलिङ्केला ॥॥ वरुस ॥  
**parusamokkaṭē kādā payiḍigājēsēdi**

**manasu eṃdu tiriginā mariyēmi**  
**monasi mudralu bhujamula nuṇḍitē jālu**  
**tanuventa hēyamainā dānikēmi .... varusa ..**  
**śrīkāntunāmamu jihvadagilitē jālu**  
**ē kulajuḍainānu hīnamēmi**  
**sākāruḍaina hari śaraṇujoccina jālu**  
**cēkonipāpamulenni cēsinānēmi .... varusa ..**  
**jīvuḍeṭṭunnānēmi jīvunilō aṃtarātma**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭēśunikā cintayēmi**  
**yēvalana paramaina yihamaina māku jālu**  
**kaivaśamāya nītaḍu kaḍamaliṅkēlā .... varusa ..**  
Isnt the process of Alchemy the only one which can turn any metal into gold?  
Whatever the metal is how does it matter?  
If we do not let go of ‘Bhakti’ for the lotus naveled one, it is enough  
wherever the mind roams, how does it matter?  
If the marks of the mudras1 are present on my shoulders, it is enough  
however lowly the body is, how does it matter?  
If SreeKanthas2 name is present upon the tongue, it is enough  
whatever caste one belongs to, how is it inferior?  
If one surrenders to the manifest Lord Hari, it is enough  
how many ever sins one commits, how does it matter?  
However the Jiva is, how does it matter?  
When SreeVenkatesha is the inner most self, what is the worry?  
Whether it is here or hereafter, how does it matter  
when we have attained Him, what else is lacking?\!  
1.Mudras are the permanent marks of the Conch and Discus worn on the  
shoulders  
2.SreeKantha: The consort of Sree (Vishnu)
