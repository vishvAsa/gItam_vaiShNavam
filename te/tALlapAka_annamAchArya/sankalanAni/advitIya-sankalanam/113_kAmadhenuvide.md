**Kāmadhēnuvidē**  
कामधेनु विदे कल्प वृक्ष मिदे  
प्रामण्यमुगल प्रपन्नुलकु ॥॥ काम ॥  
हरिनामजपमे आभरणम्बुलु  
परमात्मुनिनुति परिमळमु  
धरणिधरु पादसेवे भोगमु  
परमं बॆरगिन प्रपन्नुलकु ॥॥ काम ॥  
देवुनिध्यानमु दिव्यान्नम्बुलु  
श्रीविभुभक्ते जीवनमु  
आविफ्णुकैङ्कर्यमे संसारमु  
पापनुलगु यी प्रपन्नुलकु ॥॥ काम ॥  
येपुन श्रीवॆङ्कटेशुडे सर्वमु  
दापै यितनिवन्दनमे विधि  
कापुग शरणागतुले चुट्टालु  
पैपयि गॆलचिन प्रपन्नुलकु ॥॥ काम ॥

**paramātmuninuti parimaḷamu**

**śrīvibhubhaktē jīvanamu**  
**āviphṇukaiṅkaryamē saṃsāramu**  
**pāpanulagu yī prapannulaku .... kāma ..**  
**yēpuna śrīveṅkaṭēśuḍē sarvamu**  
**dāpai yitanivandanamē vidhi**  
**kāpuga śaraṇāgatulē cuṭṭālu**  
**paipayi gelacina prapannulaku .... kāma ..**  
Kamadhenu1 this is\! Kalpavruksha2 this is\!  
For the Prapannas3 who rely upon authority\!  
The chanting of Hari’s names itself are the adornments,  
Praising Paramatma4 is the fragrance that emanates from them,  
Servitude offered to the feet of Dharanidhara5 is their pleasure,  
for Prapannas who know the supreme\!  
Contemplation on the Lord is like divine food,  
Devotion to the Lord of Sree is but the living,  
Kainkaryam6 to Vishnu is but their Samsara,  
for the Prapannas who are pious\!  
SreeVenkatesha is ‘Everything’  
Obeisances unto Him is the duty,  
Protectively, Sharanagathas alone are the relatives,  
for the Prapannas who win over easily\!\!  
1.Kamadhenu is the wish yielding cow  
2.Kalpavruksha is the wish yielding tree  
3.Prapannas are those who have performed Sharanagati unto the  
Lord  
4.Paramatma means the Supreme Soul, ie the Lord  
5.Dharanidhara is the one who held up the earth, ie Lord Varaha or  
Vishnu  
6.Kainkaryam means to offer servitude unto the Lord
