**Aniśamu Dalacarō**  
अनिशमु दलचरो अहोबलं  
अनन्त फलदं अहोबलम् ॥॥ अनि ॥  
हरि निजनिलयं अहोबलं  
हर विरिञ्चि नुत महोबलं  
अरुणमणिशिखर महोबलं  
अरि दैत्यहरण महोबलम् ॥॥ अनि ॥  
अतिशय शुभदं अहोबलं  
अतुल मनोहर महोबलं  
हत दुरितचयं अहोबलं  
यतिमत सिद्धं अहोबलम् ॥॥ अनि ॥  
अगु श्रीवेङ्कट महोबलं  
अगम्यमसुरुल कहोबलं  
अगपडु पुण्युल कहोबलं  
अगकुलराजं अहोबलम् ॥॥ अनि ॥  
**aniśamu dalacarō ahōbalaṃ**  
**ananta phaladaṃ ahōbalaṃ .... ani ..**  
**hari nijanilayaṃ ahōbalaṃ**  
**hara viriñci nuta mahōbalaṃ**  
**aruṇamaṇiśikhara mahōbalaṃ**  
**ari daityaharaṇa mahōbalaṃ .... ani ..**  
**atiśaya śubhadaṃ ahōbalaṃ**  
**atula manōhara mahōbalaṃ**  
**hata duritacayaṃ ahōbalaṃ**  
**yatimata siddhaṃ ahōbalaṃ .... ani ..**  
**agu śrīvēṅkaṭa mahōbalaṃ**  
**agamyamasurula kahōbalaṃ**  
**agapaḍu puṇyula kahōbalaṃ**  
**agakularājaṃ ahōbalaṃ .... ani ..**  
Always contemplate on Ahobalam\!  
The bestower of endless merits, Ahobalam\!  
The true abode of Hari, Ahobalam  
Praised Lord Shiva and Brahma, Ahobalam  
With peaks decorated by the rays of the rising sun, Ahobalam  
Destroyer of the entire demon clan, Ahobalam\!  
Bestower of supreme auspiciousness, Ahobalam  
Immeasurably magnificent, Ahobalam  
Destroyer of sinful ways, Ahobalam  
Yati-Matham1 is established here, Ahobalam\!  
That Sree Venkata Ahobalam\!  
Unperceivable by the wicked, Ahobalam  
Perceived by the meritorious- Ahobalam  
The king of all mountains – Ahobalam\!  
1\. The philosophy of Ramanuja is known as Yati Matham because he is  
known as Yatiraja, that is ‘the king among ascetics’.
