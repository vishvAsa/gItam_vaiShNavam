**Anniṭikekkuḍuyīvi**  
अन्निटि कॆक्कुडुयिवि हरियिच्चेदि  
मन्निञ्चुनातनिकण्टॆ मऱिलेरु दॊरलु ॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
तगुब्रह्मलोकमुदाका नॆक्किचूचिन  
मगुड बुट्टेलोकाले मनुजुलकु  
तॆगि यिच्चे यिन्द्रादिदेवतल वरमुलु  
यॆगुवदिगुवलनु यीसन्दिवे ॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
मायलोन बुट्टेदि मायलोन बॆरिगेदि  
कायदारुलकु नॆल्ला गलिगिनदे  
सेयरानिपुण्यमॆल्ला जेसि गडिञ्चुकॊनेदि  
चायल बहुरूपपुसंसारमे ॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
चॆडनिवैकुण्ठ मिच्चु जेटुलेनिवर मिच्चु  
वॆडमाय बॆडबापु विष्णुडीतडे  
यॆडयक श्रीवेङ्कटेशुडै वुन्नाडु वीडॆ  
जडिय कितडे काचु शरणण्टे जालुनु ॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
**anniṭi kekkuḍuyivi hariyiccēdi**  
**manniñcunātanikaṇṭe maṟilēru doralu .... anni ..**  
**tagubrahmalōkamudākā nekkicūcina**  
**maguḍa buṭṭēlōkālē manujulaku**  
**tegi yiccē yindrādidēvatala varamulu**  
**yeguvadiguvalanu yīsandivē .... anni ..**  
**māyalōna buṭṭēdi māyalōna berigēdi**  
**kāyadārulaku nellā galiginadē**  
**sēyarānipuṇyamellā jēsi gaḍiñcukonēdi**  
**cāyala bahurūpapusaṃsāramē .... anni ..**  
**ceḍanivaikuṇṭha miccu jēṭulēnivara miccu**  
**veḍamāya beḍabāpu viṣṇuḍītaḍē**  
**yeḍayaka śrīvēṅkaṭēśuḍai vunnāḍu vīḍe**  
**jaḍiya kitaḍē kācu śaraṇaṇṭē jālunu .... anni ..**  
What Hari gives is higher than the rest\!  
There is no other ruling deity than Him, who is all forgiving\!  
Even after attaining Brahma-loka, if one understands  
it is again a return to the mortal world for humans\!  
Akin to this, the profusely bestowed boons by Indra and other demigods  
is but reaching higher worlds and coming back down\!  
Being born in Maya, growing in Maya  
for those who bear the mortal body, this is but common  
By doing immeasurable number of meritorious deeds,  
all that one gains is the manifold samsara again\!  
Bestowing the eternal Vaikunta, bestowing benevolent boons  
the one who dispels the powerful Maaya is Lord Vishnu alone\!  
He Himself exists here as Venkateshwara  
without hesitation He alone protects, when one surrenders\!\!
