**Teravakupativaitivi**  
तॆऱवकुबतिवै तिवि गाक  
गऱिसिञ्चिनगडुघनमा नीवु॥॥तॆऱ॥  
चूचिनवॆल्ला सॊम्पुमुत्यमुलु  
पूचिनपुव्वुलॆ पुन्नमुलु  
लाचिनवॆल्ला लागवेगमुलु  
यी चॆलि सॊबगुल कॆदुरा नीवु ॥॥तॆऱ॥  
पलिकिनवॆल्ला पसनि वज्रमुलु  
निलुवुनदुरमे नीलमुलु  
कलिगिनपुलकलॆ कनकपु मॊलकलु  
जलजलोचनकु सरिया नीवु ॥॥तॆऱ॥  
कौगिटिवॆल्ला गन्तुनिरचनलु  
मूगिनरति निनु मुञ्चॆ निदॆ  
चेगल चिगुरुल श्रीवेङ्कटपति  
वी गेटिरमणिकि विन्ता नीवु ॥॥तॆऱ॥  
**teṟavakubativai tivi gāka**  
**gaṟisiñcinagaḍughanamā nīvu....teṟa..**  
**cūcinavellā sompumutyamulu**  
**pūcinapuvvule punnamulu**  
**lācinavellā lāgavēgamulu**  
**yī celi sobagula kedurā nīvu ....teṟa..**  
**palikinavellā pasani vajramulu**  
**niluvunaduramē nīlamulu**  
**kaliginapulakale kanakapu molakalu**  
**jalajalōcanaku sariyā nīvu ....teṟa..**  
**kaugiṭivellā gantuniracanalu**  
**mūginarati ninu muñce nide**  
**cēgala cigurula śrīvēṅkaṭapati**  
**vī gēṭiramaṇiki vintā nīvu ....teṟa..**  
Since You are the Lord of Sree,  
You became the supremely great one, is it not?  
Whatever you behold in Her are perfect pearls,  
She is like the full bloomed flowers, like the full moon\!  
Her ever pervading beauty is all attractive,  
Are You a match for Her beauty\!  
All the words She speaks are like tough diamonds,  
Her tresses are like blue sapphire,  
Her goose flesh is like golden saplings,  
Are You a match for this lotus eyed goddess?  
Her embraces are enticed by Manmatha,  
She floods you with amorous love making\!  
O capable Lord Sree Venkatapathi,  
are You a match for this proud lady?
