**Iṃduku Dhṛvādu Liṭu Sākṣi**  
इन्दुकु धृवादु लिटु साक्षि  
चॆन्दि नम्मवो जीवुड नीवु\!॥ इन्दुकु ॥  
कॊण्डलवण्टिवि फॆूरपापमुलु  
खण्डिञ्चुनु हरि घन नामजपमु  
निन्दिञ्चुनु मति नित्यानन्दमु  
पण्डिञ्चुनपुडॆ परम पदम्बु\!॥ इन्दुकु ॥  
जलधुलवण्टिवि जनन बन्धमुलु  
तॊलगिञ्चुनु हरितूरिन भक्तिदि  
वॆलिगिञ्चुनु घन विज्ञानम्बुलु  
चॆलगिञ्चुनु बहु सिरिसम्पदलु\!॥ इन्दुकु ॥  
तोवरानि बहु दुःखमु लणचुनु  
श्रीवेङ्कटपति जेरिन शरणमु  
पावनम्बुगा बचरिञ्चु गुणमु  
कैवसमगु लोकमु लॆल्लानु\!॥ इन्दुकु ॥  
**iṃduku dhṛvādu liṭu sākṣi**  
**cendi nammavō jīvuḍa nīvu\!.. iṃduku ..**  
**koṇḍalavaṇṭivi ghōrapāpamulu**  
**khaṇḍiñcunu hari ghana nāmajapamu**  
**nindiñcunu mati nityānandamu**  
**paṇḍiñcunapuḍe parama padambu\!.. iṃduku ..**  
**jaladhulavaṇṭivi janana bandhamulu**  
**tolagiñcunu haritūrina bhaktidi**  
**veligiñcunu ghana vijñānambulu**  
**celagiñcunu bahu sirisampadalu\!.. iṃduku ..**  
**tōvarāni bahu duḥkhamu laṇacunu**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭapati jērina śaraṇamu**  
**pāvanambugā bacariñcu guṇamu**  
**kaivasamagu lōkamu lellānu\!.. iṃduku ..**  
For this, Dhruva and others are the proof  
You believe in this truly, O Jiva\!  
Mountains like are the heinous sins,  
chanting the glorious names of Hari cuts them asunder\!  
The mind may detest, but only upon being  
constantly blissful, one attains Paramapada\!  
Oceans like are the attachments due to birth,  
Bhakti for Hari drives them away\!  
It enlightens with profound knowledge,  
and bestows wealth and prosperity\!  
The various overpowering sorrows are suppressed  
by reaching and surrendering unto SreeVenkatapathi\!  
Conducting oneself in a pious manner,  
enables one to win over the worlds\!\!
