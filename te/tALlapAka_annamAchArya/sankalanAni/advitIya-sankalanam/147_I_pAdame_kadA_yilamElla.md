**Ī Pādamē Kadā Yilamella**  
ई पादमे कदा यिलमॆल्ल गॊलिचिनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यिन्दिरा हस्तमुल कितवैनदि ॥॥ ई पादमे ॥  
ई पादमे कदा इन्दरुनु म्रॊक्कॆडिदि  
ई पादमे कदा यी गगनगङ्ग पुट्टिनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यॆलमि पॆम्पॊन्दिनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यिन्निटिकि यॆक्कुडैनदि ॥॥ ई पादमे ॥  
ई पादमे कदा यिभराजु दलचिनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यिन्द्रादुलॆल्ल वॆदकिनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यी ब्रह्म कडिगिनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यॆगसि ब्रह्माण्डमण्टिनदि ॥॥ ई पादमे ॥  
ई पादमे कदा यिहपरमु लॊसगॆडिदि  
ई पादमे कदा यिल नहल्यकु कोरिकैनदि  
ई पादमे कदा यीक्षिम्प दुर्लभमु  
ई पादमे कदा यी वेङ्कटाद्रिपै निरवैनदि ॥॥ ई पादमे ॥  
**ī pādamē kadā yilamella golicinadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yindirā hastamula kitavainadi ..ī pādamē**  
**ī pādamē kadā iṃdarunu mrokkeḍidi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yī gaganagaṅga puṭṭinadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yelami pempondinadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yinniṭiki yekkuḍainadi ..ī pādamē**  
**ī pādamē kadā yibharāju dalacinadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yindrādulella vedakinadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yī brahma kaḍiginadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yegasi brahmāṇḍamaṇṭinadi ..ī pādamē**  
**ī pādamē kadā yihaparamu losageḍidi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yila nahalyaku kōrikainadi**  
**ī pādamē kadā yīkṣimpa durlabhamu**  
**ī pādamē kadā yī vēṅkaṭādripai niravainadi ..ī pādamē**  
This is the foot that measured the whole Universe,  
These are the feet that befit the hands of Lakshmi\!  
These are the feet unto which everybody prostrates,  
these are the feet from where Ganga took birth,  
these are the feet that sustain the earth,  
these are the feet that are higher than the highest\!  
These are the feet worshipped by the elephant King,  
these are the feet sought by Indra and others,  
these are feet that Brahma worshipped,  
these are the feet that covered the entire Brahmanda\!  
These are the feet that bestow ‘here’ and ‘hereafter’  
these are the feet that satisfied Ahalya’s wishes,  
these are the feet which are difficult to behold,  
these are the feet sanctifying Sree Venkatadri\!
