**Ataḍē Parabrahmamataḍē**  
अतडे परब्रह्ममतडे लोकनायकुडु  
अतनि कण्टे मरि अधिकुलु वेरय्या ॥॥ अतडे ॥  
कमलवासिनि लक्ष्मि कलदा यॆव्वरिकैना  
कमलनाभुनि ऒक्कनिके काक  
कमलुजुडैन ब्रह्म कलडा यॆव्वनि नाभि \-  
नमर वन्द्युडु मा हरिकेकाक ॥॥ अतडे ॥  
अन्दरु नुण्डॆडि भूमि अन्युलकु गलदा  
अन्दपु गोविन्दुनिके आलायगाक  
चॆन्दिन भागीरथी श्रीपादाल गलदा  
मन्दर धरुडयिन माधवुनिकि गाक ॥॥ अतडे ॥  
निच्चलु अभयमिच्चे नेरुपु यॆन्दुगलदा  
अच्चुगा नारायणुनियन्देकाक  
रच्चल शरणागतरक्षण मॆन्दु गलदा  
तच्चिन श्री वेङ्कटाद्रि दैवानिकेकाक ॥॥ अतडे ॥  
**ataḍē parabrahmamataḍē lōkanāyakuḍu**  
**atani kaṇṭē mari adhikulu vērayyā .... ataḍē ..**  
**kamalavāsini lakṣmi kaladā yevvarikainā**  
**kamalanābhuni okkanikē kāka**  
**kamalujuḍaina brahma kalaḍā yevvani nābhi \-**  
**namara vandyuḍu mā harikēkāka .... ataḍē ..**  
**aṃdaru nuṇḍeḍi bhūmi anyulaku galadā**  
**aṃdapu gōvindunikē ālāyagāka**  
**cendina bhāgīrathī śrīpādāla galadā**  
**mandara dharuḍayina mādhavuniki gāka ..ataḍē ..**  
**niccalu abhayamiccē nērupu yendugaladā**  
**accugā nārāyaṇuniyandēkāka**  
**raccala śaraṇāgatarakṣaṇa mendu galadā**  
**taccina śrī vēṅkaṭādri daivānikēkāka .. ataḍē ..**  
He alone is the Parabrahman\! The Lord of the worlds\!  
There is nobody greater than Him\!  
Lakshmi who resides in the lotus, is she there for anybody else  
but for the lotus naveled Lord alone\!  
Brahma who is born of the lotus, is he in anybody else’s navel  
but for our Hari, who is revered by all Gods\!  
The earth on which everybody exists, is she there for anybody else  
but for the handsome Govinda, whose wife she is\!  
Is Bhageerathi at anybody else’s auspicious feet  
but for Madhava, who is the one who held up the Mandara mountain\!  
Who else has the skill to bestow refuge at all times  
but for Narayana, who possess it\!  
Where else is there protection for Sharanagathas  
but with the Lord of SreeVenkatadri\!
