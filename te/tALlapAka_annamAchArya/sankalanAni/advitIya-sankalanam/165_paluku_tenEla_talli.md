**Paluku Tēnela Talli**  
पलुकु तेनॆल तल्लि पवळिञ्चॆनु  
कलिकितनमुल विभुनि कलसिनदिगान ॥॥ पलुकु ॥  
निगनिगनि मोमुपै नॆरुलु गॆलकुलु चॆदर  
पगलैनदाका चॆलि पवळिञ्चॆनु  
तॆगनि परिणतुलतो तॆल्लवारिन दाक  
जगदेकपति मनसु जट्टिगॊनॆ गान ॥॥ पलुकु ॥  
कॊङ्गु जारिन मॆरगु गुब्बलॊलयग तरुणि  
बङ्गारु मेडपै पवळिञ्चॆनु  
चॆङ्गलुव कनुगॊनल सिङ्गारमुलु दॊलक  
अङ्गजगुरुनितोड अलसिनदि गान ॥॥ पलुकु ॥  
मुरिपॆम्पु नटनतो मुत्यालमलगुपै  
परवशम्बुन तरुणि पवळिञ्चॆनु  
तिरुवेङ्कटाचलाधिपुनि कौगिट गलसि  
अरविरै नुनुचॆमट नण्टिनदि गान ॥॥ पलुकु ॥  
**paluku tēnela talli pavaḷiñcenu**  
**kalikitanamula vibhuni kalasinadigāna .... paluku ..**  
**niganigani mōmupai nerulu gelakulu cedara**  
**pagalainadākā celi pavaḷiñcenu**  
**tegani pariṇatulatō tellavārina dāka**  
**jagadēkapati manasu jaṭṭigone gāna .... paluku ..**  
**koṅgu jārina meragu gubbalolayaga taruṇi**  
**baṅgāru mēḍapai pavaḷiñcenu**  
**ceṅgaluva kanugonala siṅgāramulu dolaka**  
**aṃgajagurunitōḍa alasinadi gāna .... paluku ..**  
**muripempu naṭanatō mutyālamalagupai**  
**paravaśambuna taruṇi pavaḷiñcenu**  
**tiruvēṅkaṭācalādhipuni kaugiṭa galasi**  
**aravirai nunucemaṭa naṇṭinadi gāna .... paluku ..**  
The mother with honey sweet speech, is resting\!  
As she with Her feminine charms spent the night with Her Lord\!  
With curls in her hair disheveled on Her shiny brow,  
till late in the day she is resting\!  
Matching with His unending passion till the break of the dawn,  
winning over the heart of the supreme Lord, she is resting\!  
With her displaced veil revealing the beauty of Her breasts,  
in the golden mansion, she is resting\!  
From the corner of Her lotus bud eyes, with grace overflowing  
after an exhausting time with Her Lord \- the father of Manmatha, she is resting\!  
On the pearly bed, with joyful ecstacy,  
with a contended look, She is resting\!  
As She was embraced strongly by Her Lord, Sree Venkatesha,  
With blossoms of sweat all over, she is resting\!
