**Valenanu Vāridē Vaiṣṇavamu**  
वलॆननु वारिदे वैष्णवमु यिदि  
वलपु देनॆवो वैष्णवमु ॥॥ वलॆ ॥  
कोरिक लुडुगुचु गुऱि निन्निटिपै  
वैराग्यमॆपो वैष्णवमु  
सारॆकु कोपमु चलमुनु तनलो  
वारिञ्चुटवो वैष्णवमु ॥॥ वलॆ ॥  
सुडिगॊनु देहपु सुखदुःखमुलो  
वडि चॊरनिदॆ पो वैष्णवमु  
मुडिवडि यिन्द्रियमुल किङ्करुडै  
वडबड निदॆ पो वैष्णवमु ॥॥ वलॆ ॥  
वुदुटुन तन सकलोपायम्बुलु  
वदलुट पो निजवैष्णवमु  
यॆदुटनु श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरु नाममु  
वदनमु चेर्चुट वैष्णवमु ॥॥ वलॆ ॥  
**valenanu vāridē vaiṣṇavamu yidi**  
**valapu dēnevō vaiṣṇavamu .... vale ..**  
**kōrika luḍugucu guṟi ninniṭipai**  
**vairāgyamepō vaiṣṇavamu**  
**sāreku kōpamu calamunu tanalō**  
**vāriñcuṭavō vaiṣṇavamu .... vale ..**  
**suḍigonu dēhapu sukhaduḥkhamulō**  
**vaḍi coranide pō vaiṣṇavamu**  
**muḍivaḍi yindriyamula kiṅkaruḍai**  
**vaḍabaḍa nide pō vaiṣṇavamu .... vale ..**  
**vuduṭuna tana sakalōpāyambulu**  
**vadaluṭa pō nijavaiṣṇavamu**  
**yeduṭanu śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaru nāmamu**  
**vadanamu cērcuṭa vaiṣṇavamu .... vale ..**  
Whosoever desires, it is theirs, this Vaishnavism\!  
Honey like adorable Vaishnavism\!  
Giving up all desires, keeping You (the Lord) as the aim,  
attaining Vairagya, is Vaishnavism\!  
Anger and fickleness that occur within us from time to time  
suppressing them, is Vaishnavism\!  
The whirlpools of pleasures and pains of the material body  
not being drawn into them, is Vaishnavism\!  
Getting entangled by becoming a slave of the senses  
not to tremble when being overpowered by them, is Vaishnavism\!  
To quickly let go of all other means  
is truly the Vaishnavism\!  
The one who is in front of us, SreeVenkateshwara  
reciting His name is Vaishnavism\!
