**Ārūpamunakē Hari**  
आरूपमुनके हरि नेनु मॊक्कॆदनु  
चेरि विभीषणुनि शरणागतुडनि चेकॊनि सरि गाचितिवि ॥॥ आरूप ॥  
पाललोचनुडु ब्रह्मय निन्द्रुडु  
सोलि अग्नियनु सूर्य चन्द्रुलुनु  
नीलो नुण्डग नॆरि गनॆ किरीटि  
मूलभूतिवगु मूर्तिवि गान ॥॥ आरूप ॥  
अनन्त शिरसुल अनन्त पदमुल  
अनन्त नयनमुल अनन्त करमुल  
घन नीरूपमु कनुगॊनॆ किरीटि  
अनन्त मूरिति वन्निट गान ॥॥ आरूप ॥  
जगमु लिन्नियनु सकल मुनीन्द्रुलु  
अगु श्री वेङ्कट नाथुड निन्ने  
पॊगडग किरीटि पॊडगनॆ नीरूपु  
अगणित महिमुड वन्निट गान ॥॥ आरूप ॥  
**ārūpamunakē hari nēnu mokkedanu**  
**cēri vibhīṣaṇuni śaraṇāgatuḍani cēkoni sari gācitivi .. .. ārūpa**  
**phālalōcanuḍu brahmayu nindruḍu**  
**sōli agniyunu sūrya candrulunu**  
**nīlō nuṇḍaga neri gane kirīṭi**  
**mūlabhūtivagu mūrtivi gāna . .. ārūpa ..**  
**ananta śirasula ananta padamula**  
**ananta nayanamula ananta karamula**  
**ghana nīrūpamu kanugone kirīṭi**  
**ananta mūriti vanniṭa gāna .... ārūpa ..**  
**jagamu linniyunu sakala munīndrulu**  
**agu śrī vēṅkaṭa nāthuḍa ninnē**  
**pogaḍaga kirīṭi poḍagane nīrūpu**  
**agaṇita mahimuḍa vanniṭa gāna .... ārūpa ..**  
I offer my salutations to that form of Yours, O Hari\!  
Accepting Vibheeshana as a Sharanagatha, You bestowed protection\!  
Lord Shiva, Lord Brahma, Indra  
Agni-deva, the sun and the moon,  
all these Gods, Arjuna realized that they exist within You,  
since You are the Lord who is the Primal cause\!  
Innumerable heads, innumerable feet,  
innumerable eyes, innumerable hands  
This magnificent form of Yours Arjuna perceived,  
since You are the Lord who is limitless\!  
All the worlds, all the enlightened sages,  
when they praised You, O Lord of Sree Venkata,  
so also did Arjuna praise Your form,  
since You are the Lord who has innumerable glories\!
