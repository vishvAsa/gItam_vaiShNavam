**Dhruvavaradā Saṃstutavaradā**  
ध्रुववरदा संस्तुतवरदा  
नवमैनयार्तुनि नन्नु गाववे ॥॥ ध्रुव ॥  
करिराजवरदा काकासुरवरदा  
शरणागतविभीषणवरदा  
सिरुल वेदालु निन्नु जॆप्पगा विनीनि  
मरिगि मऱगुचॊच्चे मम्मु गाववे ॥॥ ध्रुव ॥  
अक्रूरवरदा अम्बरीषवरदा  
शत्रादिदिविजनिचयवरदा  
विक्रमिञ्चि यिन्निटा नीवे घनमनि नीकु  
चक्रधर शरणण्टि सरि गाववे ॥॥ ध्रुव ॥  
द्रौपदीवरदा तग नर्जुनवरदा  
श्रीपति प्रह्लादशिशुवरदा  
येपुन श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रि निटु नेनु नागुरुडु  
चूपगा गॊलिचे नच्चुग गाववे ॥॥ ध्रुव ॥  
**dhruvavaradā saṃstutavaradā**  
**navamainayārtuni nannu gāvavē .... dhruva ..**  
**karirājavaradā kākāsuravaradā**  
**śaraṇāgatavibhīṣaṇavaradā**  
**sirula vēdālu ninnu jeppagā vinīni**  
**marigi maṟagucoccē mammu gāvavē .... dhruva ..**  
**akrūravaradā aṃbarīṣavaradā**  
**śatrādidivijanicayavaradā**  
**vikramiñci yinniṭā nīvē ghanamani nīku**  
**cakradhara śaraṇaṇṭi sari gāvavē .... dhruva ..**  
**draupadīvaradā taga narjunavaradā**  
**śrīpati prahlādaśiśuvaradā**  
**yēpuna śrīvēṅkaṭādri niṭu nēnu nāguruḍu**  
**cūpagā golicē naccuga gāvavē .... dhruva ..**  
O one who blessed Dhruva\! O protector of those who praise You\!  
The distressed one now is me, do protect\!  
O savior of the elephant\! O one who blessed Kaakasura\!  
O one who blessed Vibheeshana, who had surrendered  
The priceless Vedas- as they praise You, listening to them  
we seek your refuge, do protect us\!  
O one who blessed Akrura\! O one who protected Ambareesha\!  
O one who blessed Indra and other divinities\!  
Comprehending Your valor and greatness amongst all others  
O one who wields the Chakra weapon, I surrender unto You, do protect me\!  
O protector of Draupadi\! O one who blessed Arjuna\!  
O Lord of Sree, who protected the child Prahalada  
The pristine SreeVenkatadri Hill, as to me my Guru showed  
I worshipped\! Pleasingly do protect me\!\!
