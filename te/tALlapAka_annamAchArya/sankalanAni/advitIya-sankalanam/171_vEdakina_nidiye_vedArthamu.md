**Vedakina Nidiyē Vēdārthamu**  
वॆदकिन निदिये वेदार्थमु  
मॊदलु तुदलु हरिमूलम्बु ॥॥ वॆदकिन ॥  
मुनुकॊनि अवयवमुलु यॆन्नैना  
पनिवडि शिरसे प्रधानमु  
यॆनलेनि सुरलु यॆन्दरु कलिगिन  
मुनपटि हरिये मूलम्बु ॥॥ वॆदकिन ॥  
मोवनि यिन्द्रियमुलु यॆन्नैना  
भावपु मनसे प्रधानमु  
यीवल मतमुलु यॆन्निकलिगिना  
मूवुरलो हरि मूलम्बु ॥॥ वॆदकिन ॥  
यॆरवगु गुणमुलु यॆन्नि कलिगिना  
परम ज्ञानमु प्रधानमु  
यिरवुग श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरु नाममे  
सरवि मन्त्रमुल सारम्बु ॥॥ वॆदकिन ॥  
**vedakina nidiyē vēdārthamu**  
**modalu tudalu harimūlambu .... vedakina ..**  
**munukoni avayavamulu yennainā**  
**panivaḍi śirasē pradhānamu**  
**yenalēni suralu yendaru kaligina**  
**munapaṭi hariyē mūlambu .... vedakina ..**  
**mōvani yindriyamulu yennainā**  
**bhāvapu manasē pradhānamu**  
**yīvala matamulu yennikaliginā**  
**mūvuralō hari mūlambu .... vedakina ..**  
**yeravagu guṇamulu yenni kaliginā**  
**parama jñānamu pradhānamu**  
**yiravuga śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaru nāmamē**  
**saravi mantramula sārambu .... vedakina ..**  
If understood, this itself is the teaching of the Vedas  
At the beginning and at the end, Hari alone is the origin\!  
Amongst all the organs present in the body,  
for executing work, the Head is the important one\!  
Amongst the numberless Gods,  
Hari, who existed from before, is alone the origin\!  
Amongst all the functional organs,  
the thoughtful mind is the important one\!  
Here, how many ever doctrines exist,  
amongst the Trinity, Hari alone is the origin\!  
How many ever intellectual traits one has,  
the supreme Knowledge is the important one\!  
The name of Sree Venkateshwara, which is with us,  
that alone is the essence of all mantras\!
