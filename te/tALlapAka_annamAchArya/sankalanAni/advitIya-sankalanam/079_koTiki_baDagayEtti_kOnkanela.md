**Kōṭiki Baḍagayetti Koṅkanēla**  
कोटिकि बडगयॆत्ति कॊङ्कनेल  
यीटुलेनॊ पद मॆक्कि यिक नेल चिन्त ॥॥ कोटिकि ॥  
पॆट्टिनदि नॊसलनु पॆद्द पॆद्द तिरुमणि  
कट्टिनदि मॊल जिन्न कौपीनमु  
पट्टिनदि श्रीहरिपादपद्म मूलमु  
यॆट्टयिना माकु मेले यिक नेल चिन्त ॥॥ कोटिकि ॥  
चिक्कि ना लोनैनदि श्रीवैष्णवधर्ममु  
तॊक्किनदि भवमुल तुदिपदमु  
यॆक्किनदि हरिभक्ति यिदि पट्ट पेनुग  
यॆक्कुव कॆक्कुवे काक यिक नेल चिन्त ॥॥ कोटिकि ॥  
चित्तमुलो निण्डिनदि श्रीपतिरूपमु  
हत्तिनदि वैराग्य मात्मधनमु  
यॆत्तल जाचिन माकु निदिवो श्रीवेङ्कटेशु \-  
डॆत्ति ममु गावगानु यिक नेल चिन्त ॥॥ कोटिकि ॥  
**kōṭiki baḍagayetti koṅkanēla**  
**yīṭulēno pada mekki yika nēla cinta .... kōṭiki ..**  
**peṭṭinadi nosalanu pedda pedda tirumaṇi**  
**kaṭṭinadi mola jinna kaupīnamu**  
**paṭṭinadi śrīharipādapadma mūlamu**  
**yeṭṭayinā māku mēlē yika nēla cinta .... kōṭiki ..**  
**cikki nā lōnainadi śrīvaiṣṇavadharmamu**  
**tokkinadi bhavamula tudipadamu**  
**yekkinadi haribhakti yidi paṭṭa pēnuga**  
**yekkuva kekkuvē kāka yika nēla cinta .... kōṭiki ..**  
**cittamulō niṇḍinadi śrīpatirūpamu**  
**hattinadi vairāgya mātmadhanamu**  
**yettala jūcina māku nidivō śrīvēṅkaṭēśu \-**  
**ḍetti mamu gāvagānu yika nēla cinta .... kōṭiki ..**  
Having attained the highest, why fear?  
Having reached the state that is eternal, why worry?  
Having worn the Tiruman on the forehead prominently,  
Having tied a small loin cloth over the waist,  
Having caught hold of SreeHari’s supreme lotus feet,  
Whatever happens to us is for the good, why worry at all?  
Having obtained and having imbibed fully the SreeVaishnava Dharma1  
Having trampled over the end of the cycles of birth and death,  
Having mounted onto the royal elephant called ‘Hari-Bhakti’,  
Is it not higher than the highest? Why worry at all?  
Having the glorious form of Sree-pathi within our hearts,  
Having embraced the wealth of the soul called ‘Vairagya’2  
Wherever we see, there is SreeVenkatesha,  
who uplifts and protects us\! Why then worry at all?  
1.The philosophy of Ramanuja  
2.Dispassion
