**Sarvōpāyamula Jagati**  
सर्वोपायमुल जगति ना कितडे  
वुर्वीधरुडु पुरुषोत्तमुं डितडे ॥॥ सर्वो ॥  
सकल गङ्गानदि तीर्थस्नानफलमु लिवि  
स्वामिपुष्करिणिजलमे नाकु  
सकल पुण्यक्षेत्रावासयात्र लिवि  
सरि वेङ्कटाचलविहार मिदिये  
सकल वेदाध्यनशास्त्रपाठम्बु लिवि  
शौरिसङ्कीर्तनं बिदिये नाकु  
सकल कर्मानुष्ठानमुल यितनि किच्चट  
जातुपडि कैङ्कर्य मिदिये ॥॥ सर्वो ॥  
उपवासतपमु लिवि यितनिप्रसादम्बु \-  
लॊगि भुजिम्पुटे नाकु दिनदिनम्बु  
जप रहस्योपदेशम्बु लितनि पाद \-  
जलजम्बुलु शरणनेटिसेव यॆकटे  
उपमिम्प बुण्यपुरुषल दर्शनमु नाकु \-  
नॊगि निचटि बहुवृक्षदर्शनम्बु  
यॆपुडु बुण्य कथाश्रवणम्बु लिच्चोटि  
यॆन्नगल बहुपक्षिकलकलम्बु ॥॥ सर्वो ॥  
तलपुगल योगम्बुलन्दु श्रीवैष्णवुल  
दगुलु संवास सहयोगम्बु  
वॆलय निण्डुमहोत्सवम्बु लिन्निय नितनि \-  
विभवम्बु लॆसगु तिरुनाळ्ळु नाकु  
Lord Vishnu, the Supreme overlord of the entire universe, is the primal  
Lord who is the origin of all including Brahma, Isha and the host of other  
divinities. All Gods come into existance by His will alone.  
Being the sole Lord, He is the one who bestows the post of Brahma to  
a deserving one. Even when the Brahma of the present creative cycle  
exists, He conferrs the next post of Brahma to another beffitting one.  
At the time of dissolution, all worlds merge back into Him. He protects  
the entire cosmos within His stomach. Even the higher worlds like  
Brahmaloka and Kailasa are absorbed into Him. He protects Lord  
Brahma and Lord Shiva by placing them within His Stomach.  
When such is the case, it is a crime to consider Lord Narayana as just  
one among the trinity. He is the soverign ruler of all, the seat of origin  
and dissolution, and the base for preservation. He is the one who  
bestows the post of Brahma and Shiva to deserving ones and so He  
remains as their Overlord. He is that Lord who has never had and will  
never ever have an equal. He is the Lord even to great ones like Brahma  
and Isha. Lord Narayana dwells in Brahma and Shiva as their inner  
soul and shows them the way. Thus they, Brahma and Isha, become  
the creator & destroyer respectively. The vedas clearly extol the  
transcendent supremacy of Lord Narayana. Annamacharya here  
proclaims Lord Vishnu's soverignity and supremacy.  
Many Scriptures mention the Avatara of Vishnu in between Brahma and  
Shiva. Lord Vishnu manifests as one among the trinity only because He  
takes up the task of protecting His devotees unto Himself. For this reason  
the scriptures mention Him to be the preserver, inbetween the creator  
Brahma and the destroyer Shiva. Those who ignore this reason think  
that Vishnu is placed between Brahma and Shiva because He is equal  
to them\! This is a wrong understanding because, the one who manifests  
as Vishnu among the trinity is the Supreme Overlord Narayana Himself,  
who is the creator of Brahma and Shiva also. It is He who exists as  
their inner self and it is He who bestows upon them their respective  
posts. He cannot be considered as just one among the trinity. It would  
also be wrong to hold that the consort of Sree is the Supreme self  
different from the Vishnu of the trinity. Having created Brahma and Shiva  
to perform the task of creation and destruction respectively, the Supreme  
Lord Narayana, the consort of Sree, Himself occupies the position of  
Vishnu – the preserver. The reality is that He, Lord Sriman Narayana, is  
the sole Overlord, even to Brahma and Shiva\!

चॆलगि यिटु देवताप्रार्थनिन्तय नाकु  
श्रीवेङ्कटेश्वरुनि शरणागति  
अलरु ना सम्पदलु यितनि पट्टपुराणि \-  
अलुमेलुमङ्ग कडकण्टिचूपु ॥॥ सर्वो ॥  
**sarvōpāyamula jagati nā kitaḍē**  
**vurvīdharuḍu puruṣōttamuṃ ḍitaḍē .... sarvō ..**  
**sakala gaṅgānadi tīrthasnānaphalamu livi**  
**svāmipuṣkariṇijalamē nāku**  
**sakala puṇyakṣētrāvāsayātra livi**  
**sari vēṅkaṭācalavihāra midiyē**  
**sakala vēdādhyanaśāstrapāṭhambu livi**  
**śaurisaṅkīrtanaṃ bidiyē nāku**  
**sakala karmānuṣṭhānamula yitani kiccaṭa**  
**jātupaḍi kaiṅkarya midiyē .... sarvō ..**  
**upavāsatapamu livi yitaniprasādambu \-**  
**logi bhujimpuṭē nāku dinadinambu**  
**japa rahasyōpadēśambu litani pāda \-**  
**jalajambulu śaraṇanēṭisēva yokaṭē**  
**upamimpa buṇyapuruṣala darśanamu nāku \-**  
**nogi nicaṭi bahuvṛkṣadarśanambu**  
**yepuḍu buṇya kathāśravaṇambu liccōṭi**  
**yennagala bahupakṣikalakalambu .... sarvō ..**  
**talapugala yōgambulandu śrīvaiṣṇavula**  
**dagulu saṃvāsa sahayōgambu**  
**velaya niṇḍumahōtsavambu linniyu nitani \-**  
**vibhavambu lesagu tirunāḷḷu nāku**  
**celagi yiṭu dēvatāprārthanintayu nāku**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭēśvaruni śaraṇāgati**  
**alaru nā sampadalu yitani paṭṭapurāṇi \-**  
**alumēlumaṅga kaḍakaṇṭicūpu .... sarvō ..**  
The means for everything in this world, to me, is He  
The supporter of the entire universe, the supreme person, is He\!  
Like a holy bath in Ganga and other sacred rivers,  
is the water of Swami Pushkarini, for me\!  
Like a pilgrimage to various sacred places,  
is the pleasurable walk on Venkatachala hill\!  
Like a study of Vedas , shastras, and other scriptures,  
is for me rendering Sowri’s Sankirtana\!  
Offering all our daily duties and ordained deeds unto Him,  
is but His Kainkarya \!  
Fastings and penances are but His prasadam,  
that I relish day after day\!  
Like all Japas and sacred incantations,  
is surrendering unto His lotus –feet\!  
Like seeing holy people, is for me,  
the sight of the host of trees\!  
Like constantly listening to the glorious pastimes of His,  
is the sweet chirping of birds here\!  
Amongst the desirable Yogas is,  
associating and living with SreeVaishnavas\!  
All these glorious festivities here, are like  
auspicious days that bestow His grace\!  
Like the worship of all devatas, is for me,  
performing Sharanagathi unto SreeVenkateshwara\!  
An ever-growing wealth of mine, is His reigning queen,  
Alamelumanga’s one compassionate glance from the corner of Her eyes\!
