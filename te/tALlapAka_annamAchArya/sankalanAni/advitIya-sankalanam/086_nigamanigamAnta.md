**Nigamanigamānta**  
निगम निगमान्त वर्णित मनोहर रूप  
नगराजधरुड श्री नारायणा ॥॥निग॥  
दीपिञ्चु वैराग्य दिव्य सौख्यं बिय्य-  
नोपक कदा नन्नु नॊडबरुचु  
पैपैनॆ संसार बन्धमुल गट्टेवु  
ना पलुकु चॆल्लुना नारायणा ॥॥निग॥  
चीकाकु पडिन नाचित्त शान्तमु सेय \-  
लेकका नीवु बहुलील नन्नु  
काकु सेसॆदवु बहु कर्ममुल बडुवारु  
ना कॊलदि वारला नारायणा ॥॥निग॥  
विविध निर्भन्धमुल वॆडलद्रोयक नन्नु  
भवसागरमुल दड बड जेतुरा  
दिविजेन्द्रवद्य श्री तिरुवेङ्कटाद्रीश  
नवनीतचोर श्री नारायणा ॥॥निग॥  
**nigama nigamānta varṇita manōhara rūpa**  
**nagarājadharuḍa śrī nārāyaṇā ....niga..**  
**dīpiñcu vairāgya divya saukhyaṃ biyya-**  
**nōpaka kadā nannu noḍabarucu**  
**paipaine saṃsāra bandhamula gaṭṭēvu**  
**nā paluku cellunā nārāyaṇā ....niga..**  
**cīkāku paḍina nācitta śāntamu sēya \-**  
**lēkakā nīvu bahulīla nannu**  
**kāku sēsedavu bahu karmamula baḍuvāru**  
**nā koladi vāralā nārāyaṇā ....niga..**  
**vividha nirbhandhamula veḍaladrōyaka nannu**  
**bhavasāgaramula daḍa baḍa jēturā**  
**divijēndravadya śrī tiruvēṅkaṭādrīśa**  
**navanītacōra śrī nārāyaṇā ....niga..**  
O one whose bewitching form is praised by the Vedas and Vedanta\!  
O Lord who lifted up the mountain\! Sree Narayana\!  
The bright divine happiness of Vairagya, since You  
do not wish to bestow, You make me consent  
and bind me again and again in the attachments of samsara\!  
Will my words ever prevail, O Narayana\!  
To my mind which is under turmoil, since You do not wish  
to bestow peace, by Your various Leelas You make me feel fatigued\!  
Those who undergo various karmas,  
are they comparable to me, O Narayana\!  
Instead of destroying all my various strong attachments,  
You overwhelm me in the ocean of existence\!  
O one who is revered by Indra and other divinities,  
O Lord Sree TiruVenkatesha\! O butter thief, Narayana\!
