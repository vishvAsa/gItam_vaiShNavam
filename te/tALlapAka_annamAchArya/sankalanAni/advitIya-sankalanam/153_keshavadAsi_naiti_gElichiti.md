**Kēśavadāsi Naiti Geliciti**  
केशवदासि नैति गॆलिचिति नन्निटानु  
यी शरीरपु नेरालिकनेल वॆदक ॥॥ केशव ॥  
निच्चलु कोरिकलिय्य नीनाममे चालु  
तॆच्चि पुनीतुचेय नीतीर्थमे चालु  
पच्चिपापालणच नी प्रसादमे चालु  
यॆच्चु कॊन्दु वुपायालु इकनेल वॆदक ॥॥ केशव ॥  
घनुनि चेयगनु नी कैङ्कर्यमे चालु  
मॊनसि रक्षिञ्चनु नी मुद्रले चालु  
मनिषि कावग तिरुमणि लाञ्छनमे चालु  
यॆनसॆनु दिक्कुदॆस इकनेल वॆदक ॥॥ केशव ॥  
नॆलवैन सुखमिय्य नी ध्यानमे चालु  
अल दापुदण्डकु नी यर्चने चालु  
यिलपै श्रीवेङ्कटेश यिन्निटा माकु कलवु  
यॆलमि नितरमुलु यिकनेल वॆदक ॥॥ केशव ॥  
**kēśavadāsi naiti geliciti nanniṭānu**  
**yī śarīrapu nērālikanēla vedaka .... kēśava ..**  
**niccalu kōrikaliyya nīnāmamē cālu**  
**tecci punītucēya nītīrthamē cālu**  
**paccipāpālaṇaca nī prasādamē cālu**  
**yeccu kondu vupāyālu ikanēla vedaka .... kēśava ..**  
**ghanuni cēyaganu nī kaiṅkaryamē cālu**  
**monasi rakṣiñcanu nī mudralē cālu**  
**maniṣi kāvaga tirumaṇi lāñchanamē cālu**  
**yenasenu dikkudesa ikanēla vedaka .... kēśava ..**  
**nelavaina sukhamiyya nī dhyānamē cālu**  
**ala dāpudaṇḍaku nī yarcanē cālu**  
**yilapai śrīvēṅkaṭēśa yinniṭā māku kalavu**  
**yelami nitaramulu yikanēla vedaka .... kēśava ..**  
Keshava’s servant I became, I won over everything\!  
Why should I ponder over the sins of my body?  
To fulfill wishes eternally, Your name alone is enough  
To purify, Your teertha alone is enough  
To destroy heinous sins, Your prasadam is enough  
Why should I search for any other means?  
To make me exalted, Your Kainkaryam (servitude) is enough  
To rescue me, wearing Your mudras alone is enough  
To protect the human body, wearing the mark of the Tiruman alone is enough  
These being everywhere, why should I seek in any other direction?  
To bestow eternal happiness, Your contemplation alone is enough  
For saving me ‘here’ and ‘hereafter’, Your worship alone is enough  
On this earth, Sree Venkatesha, in all these ways you exist for us  
Being totally satisfied, why should I search for anything else??
