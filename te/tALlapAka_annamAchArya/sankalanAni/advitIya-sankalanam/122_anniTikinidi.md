**Anniṭikinidi**  
अन्निटिकि निदि परवौषधमु  
वॆन्नुनि नाममे विमलौषधमु ॥॥ अन्निटि ॥  
चित्तशान्तिकिनि श्रीपति नाममॆ  
हत्तिन निज दिव्यौषधमु  
मॊत्तपु बन्धविमोचनम्बुनकु  
चित्तजगुरुडे सिद्धौषधमु ॥॥ अन्निटि ॥  
परिपरि विधमुल भवरोगमुलकु  
हरि पादजलमे Еौषधमु  
दुरितकर्ममुल दॊलगिञ्चुटकुनु  
मुरहरु पूजे मुख्यौषधमु ॥॥ अन्निटि ॥  
यिल निहपरमुल निन्दिराविभुनि  
नलरि भजिम्पुटॆ Еौषधमु  
कलिगिन श्रीवेङ्कटपति शरणमॆ  
निलिचिन माकिदि नित्यौषधमु ॥॥ अन्निटि ॥  
**anniṭiki nidi paramauṣadhamu**  
**vennuni nāmamē vimalauṣadhamu .... anniṭi ..**  
**cittaśāntikini śrīpati nāmame**  
**hattina nija divyauṣadhamu**  
**mottapu bandhavimōcanambunaku**  
**cittajaguruḍē siddhauṣadhamu .... anniṭi ..**  
**paripari vidhamula bhavarōgamulaku**  
**hari pādajalamē yauṣadhamu**  
**duritakarmamula dolagiñcuṭakunu**  
**muraharu pūjē mukhyauṣadhamu .... anniṭi ..**  
**yila nihaparamula nindirāvibhuni**  
**nalari bhajimpuṭe yauṣadhamu**  
**kaligina śrīvēṅkaṭapati śaraṇame**  
**nilicina mākidi nityauṣadhamu .... anniṭi** ..  
For everything, this is the Supreme medicine\!  
The name of ‘Vennudu’1 is the pristine medicine\!  
For peace of mind, Sree-Pathi’s name alone  
is the true divine medicine\!  
For total release from all bondage,  
the father of Manmatha is the remedial medicine\!  
For the various aliments of Samsara,  
the Hari Pada-Jala2 is the medicine\!  
For driving away our evil Karmas,  
the worship of Mura-Hara is the important medicine\!  
On this earth, for now and hereafter, joyously praising  
the Lord of Lakshmi, is ‘The’ medicine\!  
Surrendering unto the manifest God Sree-Pathi  
this alone is for us the eternal medicine\!\!\!  
1.Vennudu is Vishnu  
2.Hari Pada-Jala is the water that has been used to worship Hari’s feet
