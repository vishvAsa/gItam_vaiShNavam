**Dēvadēvaṃ Bhajē**  
देवदेवं भजे दिव्यप्रभावं  
रावणासुरवैरि रणपुङ्गवम् ॥॥ देव ॥  
राजवरशेखरं रविकुल सुधाकरं  
आजानुबाहुं नीलाभ्रकायं  
राजारि कोदण्ड राज दीक्षागुरुं  
राजीवलोचनं रामचन्द्रम् ॥॥ देव ॥  
नीलजीमुत सन्निभ शरीरं घन वि \-  
शालवक्षं विमल जलजनाभं  
तालाहिनगहरं धर्मसंस्थापनं  
भूललनाधिपं भोगिशयनम् ॥॥ देव ॥  
पङ्कजासन विनुत परमनारायणं  
शङ्करार्जित जनकचाप दळनं  
लङ्का विशोषणं लालित विभीषणं  
वॆङ्कटेशं साधु विबुध विनुतम् ॥॥ देव ॥  
**dēvadēvaṃ bhajē divyaprabhāvaṃ**  
**rāvaṇāsuravairi raṇapuṅgavaṃ .... dēva ..**  
**rājavaraśēkharaṃ ravikula sudhākaraṃ**  
**ājānubāhuṃ nīlābhrakāyaṃ**  
**rājāri kōdaṇḍa rāja dīkṣāguruṃ**  
**rājīvalōcanaṃ rāmacandraṃ .... dēva ..**  
**nīlajīmuta sannibha śarīraṃ ghana vi \-**  
**śālavakṣaṃ vimala jalajanābhaṃ**  
**tālāhinagaharaṃ dharmasaṃsthāpanaṃ**  
**bhūlalanādhipaṃ bhōgiśayanaṃ .... dēva ..**  
**paṅkajāsana vinuta paramanārāyaṇaṃ**  
**śaṅkarārjita janakacāpa daḷanaṃ**  
**laṅkā viśōṣaṇaṃ lālita vibhīṣaṇaṃ**  
**veṅkaṭēśaṃ sādhu vibudha vinutaṃ .... dēva ..**  
Praises to the Lord of Gods, the divinely glorious one\!  
To the enemy of the demon Ravana, to the lion in the battlefield\!  
To the prominent one among kings, the moon of the sun-clan,  
the one with long beautiful arms upto the knee, the one with a body like the blue ocean,  
the one who wields the bow of the enemy of kings (Parashurama), the king initiated one  
the one with lotus eyes, Lord Ramachandra\!  
To the one with a body like that of a blue cloud,  
the one with a great broad chest, the one with a navel of pure lotus,  
............................................. , the protector of Dharma,  
the Lord of the one born of the earth (Sita), the one who reclines on the serpent\!  
To the one praised by Brahma, that Parama Narayana\!  
The one who broke the bow of Janaka which was obtained from Shankara,  
the one who conquered Lanka, the one adored by Vibheeshana,  
Lord Venkatesha, who is praised by sages and learned ones\!
