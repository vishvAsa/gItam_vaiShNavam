**Itara Meraga Gati Yidiye Śaraṇyamu**  
इतर मॆरग गति यिदियॆ शरण्यमु  
सतत पूर्णुनिकि शरण्यमु ॥॥इतर॥  
सर्व लोकमुल साक्षै काचिन  
सर्वेश्वरुनकु शरण्यमु  
उर्विकि मिण्टिकि नॊक्कट बॆरगिन  
सार्वभौमुनकु शरण्यमु ॥॥इतर॥  
श्रीकान्त नुरमु चॆङ्गट निलिपिन  
साकारुनकुनु शरण्यमु  
पैकॊनि वॆलिगॆटि परं ज्योतियै (Еौ)  
सौकुमारुनकुनु शरण्यमु ॥॥इतर॥  
तग निहपरमुलु दासुल कॊसगॆटि  
जगदीश्वरुनकु शरण्यमु  
नगु श्री वॆङ्कट नाधुड नीकु  
सगुण मूर्ति यिदॆ शरण्यमु ॥॥इतर॥  
**itara meraga gati yidiye śaraṇyamu**  
**satata pūrṇuniki śaraṇyamu ....itara..**  
**sarva lōkamula sākṣai kācina**  
**sarvēśvarunaku śaraṇyamu**  
**urviki miṇṭiki nokkaṭa beragina**  
**sārvabhaumunaku śaraṇyamu ....itara..**  
**śrīkānta nuramu ceṅgaṭa nilipina**  
**sākārunakunu śaraṇyamu**  
**paikoni veligeṭi paraṃ jyōtiyai (yau)**  
**saukumārunakunu śaraṇyamu ....itara..**  
**taga nihaparamulu dāsula kosageṭi**  
**jagadīśvarunaku śaraṇyamu**  
**nagu śrī veṅkaṭa nādhuḍa nīku**  
**saguṇa mūrti yide śaraṇyamu ....itara..**  
I know not any other thing, the means is only this, Surrender unto Him\!  
The eternally perfect one, Surrender unto Him\!  
The one who is the witness of all worlds, the Protector,  
the Lord of all, Surrender unto Him\!  
The one who measured the earth and sky in one step,  
the emperor of the universe, Surrender unto Him\!  
The one who placed Goddess Sree closely onto His chest,  
the one who possess a true form, Surrender unto Him\!  
High above, the one who shines brilliantly as the Supreme effulgence,  
the eternally youthful one, Surrender unto Him\!  
The one who blesses His daasas here and hereafter,  
the Lord of all the worlds, Surrender unto Him\!  
O Lord Sree Venkatanatha, O Saguna deity,  
unto You I surrender\!
