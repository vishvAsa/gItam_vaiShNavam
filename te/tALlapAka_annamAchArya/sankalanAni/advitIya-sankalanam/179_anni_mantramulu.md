**Anni Mantramulu**  
अन्नि मन्त्रमुलु इन्दे आवहिञ्चॆनु  
वॆन्नतो नाकु कलिगॆ वेङ्कटेशुमन्त्रमु ॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
नारदुडु जपियिञ्चॆ नारायणमन्त्रमु  
चेरे प्रह्लादुडु नारसिंहमन्त्रमु  
कोरि विभीषणुडु चेकॊनॆ राममन्त्रमु  
वेरॆ नाकुगलिगॆ वेङ्कटेशुमन्त्रमु॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
रङ्गगु वासुदेवमन्त्रमु ध्रुवुडुजपियिञ्चॆ  
अङ्गविञ्चॆ कृष्टमन्त्रमु अर्जुनुडु  
मुङ्गिट विष्टुमन्त्रमु मॊगि शुकुडु पठिञ्चॆ  
विङ्गडमैनाकु नब्बॆ वेङ्कटेशुमन्त्रमु ॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
इन्नि मन्त्रमुलकॆल्ल इन्दिरानाथुडे गुरि  
पन्निनदिदिये परब्रह्ममन्त्रमु  
नन्नु कावगलिगॆपो नाकु गुरुडिय्यगानु  
वॆन्नॆल वण्टिदि श्री वेङ्कटेशुमन्त्रमु॥॥ अन्नि ॥  
**anni mantramulu iṃdē āvahiñcenu**  
**vennatō nāku kalige vēṅkaṭēśumantramu .... anni ..**  
**nāraduḍu japiyiñce nārāyaṇamantramu**  
**cērē prahlāduḍu nārasiṃhamantramu**  
**kōri vibhīṣaṇuḍu cēkone rāmamantramu**  
**vēre nākugalige vēṅkaṭēśumantramu.... anni ..**  
**raṅgagu vāsudēvamantramu dhruvuḍujapiyiñce**  
**aṃgaviñce kṛṣṭamantramu arjunuḍu**  
**muṅgiṭa viṣṭumantramu mogi śukuḍu paṭhiñce**  
**viṅgaḍamaināku nabbe vēṅkaṭēśumantramu .... anni ..**  
**inni mantramulakella iṃdirānāthuḍē guri**  
**panninadidiyē parabrahmamantramu**  
**nannu kāvagaligepō nāku guruḍiyyagānu**  
**vennela vaṇṭidi śrī vēṅkaṭēśumantramu.... anni ..**  
All mantras are inherently possessed by this  
Easily I obtained this Venkatesha mantra\!  
Chanted by Narada, the Narayana mantra  
Attained by Prahlada, the Narasimha mantra  
Desired and attained by Vibheeshana, the Rama mantra  
Specially, I got this Venkatesha mantra\!  
The glorious Vasudeva mantra that Dhruva chanted,  
The Krishna mantra in which Arjuna excelled in delight,  
The important Vishnu mantra which Shuka endeavored and recited  
With grace I imbibed this Venkatesha mantra\!  
For all these mantras, the Lord of Lakshmi is the aim,  
This is the joining link, the ParaBrahma mantra\!  
This which could protect me, as it was bestowed by my Guru  
Soothing Like moonlight is this Venkatesha mantra\!\!
