O people, do not be fooled by the pleasurable times!
For the heart to rejoice, worship the Lord,
with the Dhanurmasa rituals!
Promptly during the early hours of the day,
the knowers of the Veda wake up,
perform the duties according to the injunctions of the Agamas,
and perform the Sandhyavandana, according to their bodily gunas!
The Lord who reclines on the pure ocean,
worship Him devoutly,
without breaking the routine,
of the Dhanurmasa rituals, O great ones!
Offering beautiful silk robes
sweet scented flowers
in a very fine manner, to Venkatapathi, along with the Naivedyam
and also with unique incense, lighting many lamps
with betel-leaf offerings
worship in a beautiful manner
the Lord of all entirety
with the Dhanurmasa rituals, O great ones!
नित्युलु मुक्तुलु निर्मल चित्तुलु
Nityulu Muktulu Nirmala Cittulu
नित्युलु मुक्तुलु निर्मल चित्तुलु निगमान्तविदुलु वैष्णवुलु
सत्यमु वीरल शरणनि ब्रदुकरॊ साटिकि बॆनगक जडुलाल!॥ नित्युलु ॥
सकलोपाय शून्युलु सम्यज् ज्ञान पूर्णुलु
अकलङ्कुलु शङ्खचक्रलाञ्छनुलन्निट बूज्युलु वैष्णवुलु
वॊकटि गोररु वॊरुल गॊलुवरु वॊल्लरु ब्रह्मादि पट्टमुलु
अकटा वीरलसरि यन बापं बाऱुमतम्बुल पूतकोकिल्!॥ नित्युलु ॥
मन्त्रान्तर साधनान्तरम्बुलु मानिन पुण्युलु विरक्तुलु
यन्त्रपुमायल बॊरलु परुलकु यॆन्तैना मॊक्करु वैष्णवुलु
तन्त्रपु कामक्रोध विदूरुलु तमनिज धर्ममु वदलरु
जन्त्रपु संसारुलतो वीरल सरियनि यॆञ्चग बापमय्य! ॥ नित्युलु ॥
तप्परु तमपट्टिन व्रतमॆप्पुडु दैवमॊक्कडे गतियनुचु
वॊप्पगु तम पातिव्रत्यम्बुन नुन्दुरु सुखमुन वैष्णवुलु
कप्पिन श्रीवेङ्कटपतिदासुलु कर्मविदूरुलु सात्विकुलु
चॆप्पकु डितरुल सरिगा वीरिकि सेविञ्चगने धन्युडनैति!॥ नित्युलु ॥

The Nityas, the Muktas, the pure hearted ones,
the knowers of the Vedas, the Vaishnavas!
Truly surrender unto them and live!
Do not equate yourselves with them, O irrational fools!
Those who do not resort to any other means,
who are endowed fully with right knowledge,
who are flawless, who wear the symbols of Shanka-Chakra,
who are revered everywhere, who do not desire anything,
who do not worship others, who do not even desire the post of
Brahma and other gods, they are the Vaishnavas!
Beware! Equating them with those of the six-mathas of
superficial knowledge is a sin!
Those who give up all other mantras and sadhanas,
who are meritorious, who are dispassionate,
who do not believe in the influence of Yantras,
who never bow down to others at any cost,
who stay away from the sway of Kama and Krodha,
who never let go of their true dharma, they are the Vaishnavas!
Comparing them to ordinary householders is a great sin!
Those who never go against their vows,
who agree that God is the only means,
who remain chaste, who are ever happy,
the devotees of the all pervading Lord Sree Venkatapathi,
who are not bound by Karmas, the Satvik ones,
they are the Vaishnavas!
Do not ever say that they are equaled by anybody!
By serving them I am blessed!
