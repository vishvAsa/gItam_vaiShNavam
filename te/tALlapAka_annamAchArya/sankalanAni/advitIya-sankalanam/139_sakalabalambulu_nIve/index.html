Sakalabalambulu Nīvē
सकलबलम्बुलु नीवे सर्वेश्वर नाकु
अकलङ्कम्बगुसुखमे अन्निट निदे नाकु ॥॥ सकल ॥
पॊन्दुग जक्राङ्कितमे बुजबल मिदॆ नाकु
अन्दिनहरि नीचिन्ते आत्मबलमु नाकु
सन्दडि बेरुबलमु केशवनाममु नाकु
यिन्दुनु नन्दुनु भवभय मिक ले दिदे नाकु नाकु ॥॥ सकल ॥
अङ्गपुदिरुमणु लिवि पञ्चाङ्गबलमु नाकु
सङ्गति नीपै पाटलॆ स्वरबलि मिदे नाकु
रङ्गुग नी गुणरासुले रासिबलमु नाकु
यिङ्गितमुग निहपरमुल कॆदुरे दिदे नाकु ॥॥ सकल ॥
कनुगॊनु नीविग्रहमे ग्रहबलि मिदॆ नाकु
विनु नीदासुलसेवे वॆनुबलि मिदॆ नाकु
तनरिन श्रीवेङ्कटपति दैवबलमु नाकु
घनमे चॆप्पग निन्तट गलिगॆबो यिदे नाकु ॥॥ सकल ॥

You alone are the all round strength for me, O Lord of all!
The pristine joy is only this, for me!
The beautiful mark of the Chakra is the ‘Bhuja-bala’1 for me
O Hari! Your thoughts alone are the ‘Atma-bala’2 for me
Joyously, Keshava’s name alone is the ‘root-strength’ for me
Now and hereafter, the fear of Samsara is never there for me!!
On the body, the marks of the Tiruman alone are the ‘Panchanga-balamu’3 for me
Songs on You are the ‘voice strength’ for me
The effulgence of Your abundant qualities alone are the ‘Rasi-balamu’4 for me
For this reason, ‘here’ and ‘hereafter’, where are any obstacles for me?!
Beholding Your Vigraham5 alone is the ‘Graha-bala’6 for me
Servitude to Your daasas is the ‘back-bone strength’ for me
The ever exalted SreeVenkatapathi is the ‘Daiva-balamu’7 for me
To be able to say all this, is a great privilege for me!!
1.Arm strength
2.Soul strength
3.Strength of the five sense organs
4.Strength of Astrological signs
5.Worshippable form
6.Planetary strength
7.Divine strength
