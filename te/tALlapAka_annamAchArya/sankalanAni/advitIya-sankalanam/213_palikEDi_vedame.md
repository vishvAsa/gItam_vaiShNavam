**Palikeḍi Vēdamē**  
पलिकॆडि वेदमे प्रमाणमु  
तलचिन वारिकि तत्वमु सुण्डी॥॥पलिकॆडि॥  
नरहरि यजाण्ड नायकुडु  
सुरलित निने संस्तुञ्चिरि  
पुरुषोत्तमुडु इदे भूमिकि  
ई हरि कॊलवनिवारु असुरुलु सुण्डी॥॥पलिकॆडि॥  
कमलारमणुडे घनुडु  
अमरुले म्रॊक्कुदुरातनिकि  
विमतुल तुनिमे विष्णुडु  
तमि पूजिञ्चगा तगु गतिलेदु॥॥पलिकॆडि॥  
भावज जनकुडु ब्रह्ममु  
त्रोवगा कॊलिचिरि शुकादुलु  
श्री वेङ्कटपति चॆलुवुडु  
दैवशिखामणि तलचरा विबुडु॥॥पलिकॆडि॥  
**palikeḍi vēdamē pramāṇamu**  
**talacina vāriki tatvamu suṇḍī....palikeḍi..**  
**narahari yajāṇḍa nāyakuḍu**  
**suralita ninē saṃstuñciri**  
**puruṣōttamuḍu idē bhūmiki**  
**ī hari kolavanivāru asurulu suṇḍī....palikeḍi..**  
**kamalāramaṇuḍē ghanuḍu**  
**amarulē mrokkudurātaniki**  
**vimatula tunimē viṣṇuḍu**  
**tami pūjiñcagā tagu gatilēdu....palikeḍi..**  
**bhāvaja janakuḍu brahmamu**  
**trōvagā koliciri śukādulu**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭapati celuvuḍu**  
**daivaśikhāmaṇi talacarā vibuḍu....palikeḍi..**  
The words proclaimed by the Vedas are the authority\!  
For those who understand and accept it, it is the eternal truth\!  
Lord Nara-Hari is the sole ruler of the cosmic egg,  
all the divinities praise this Lord alone\!  
He is the Purushottama for this earth,  
those who do not worship this Lord Hari are but demoniac in nature\!  
The consort of Lakshmi is the supremely great one,  
the celestial divinities themselves offer their salutations to Him\!  
Vishnu is the one who cuts asunder the evil ones,  
there is no other appropriate means other than worshipping Him\!  
The father of Manmatha is the Supreme Brahman\!  
Taking Him alone as the means, sage Suka and others worshipped Him\!  
Sree Venkatapathi is the true friend,  
the learned ones seek this crest jewel among Gods\!  
Annamacharya sings here the essence of the works of Ramanujacharya like  
The Sree-Bhasya, The Vedanta Sara, The Vedartha Samgraha etc, to prove that the  
Vedas are the supreme authority, and thus Lord Vishnu, having been glorified by the  
Vedas, qualifies as the Supreme Brahman.
