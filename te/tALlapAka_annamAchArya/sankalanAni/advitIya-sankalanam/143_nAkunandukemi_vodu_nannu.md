**Nākunandukēmi Vōdu Nannu**  
नाकु नन्दु केमि वोदु नन्नु नीवेमि चूचेवु  
नी करुण गलिगिते निञ्चि चूपवय्य ॥॥ नाकु ॥  
फॆूरमैन देहपु दुर्गुणमेमि गलिगिन  
अरसि ब्रकृतिबोयि अडुगवय्य  
नेरनिनाजन्ममुतो (लो?) नेरुपेमिगल्ला नन्नु  
धारुणि बुट्टिञ्चिनविधात नडुगवय्य ॥॥ नाकु ॥  
पञ्चेन्द्रियमुललोनि पापमेमि गलिगिना  
अञ्चॆलगामुनिबोयि अडुगवय्य  
मुञ्चिननाकर्ममुलो मोसमेमि गलिगिना  
मञ्चितनान जेयिञ्चेमाय नडुगवय्य ॥॥ नाकु ॥  
अन्निटा ना वॆनकटि अपराध मेमिगल्ला  
मन्निञ्चि नागुरुजाचि मानवय्य  
मिन्नक श्री वेङ्कटेश मीदिपनुलेमिगल्ला  
निन्नु जाचुकॊनि नन्नु नीवे येलवय्य ॥॥ नाकु ॥  
**nāku nandu kēmi vōdu nannu nīvēmi cūcēvu**  
**nī karuṇa galigitē niñci cūpavayya .... nāku ..**  
**ghōramaina dēhapu durguṇamēmi galigina**  
**arasi brakṛtibōyi aḍugavayya**  
**nēraninājanmamutō (lō?) nērupēmigallā nannu**  
**dhāruṇi buṭṭiñcinavidhāta naḍugavayya .... nāku ..**  
**pañcēndriyamulalōni pāpamēmi galiginā**  
**aṃcelagāmunibōyi aḍugavayya**  
**muñcinanākarmamulō mōsamēmi galiginā**  
**mañcitanāna jēyiñcēmāya naḍugavayya .... nāku ..**  
**anniṭā nā venakaṭi aparādha mēmigallā**  
**manniñci nāgurujūci mānavayya**  
**minnaka śrī vēṅkaṭēśa mīdipanulēmigallā**  
**ninnu jūcukoni nannu nīvē yēlavayya .... nāku ..**  
None of these comes from me. Why do You look at me?  
If You have compassion for me, do bestow it fully\!  
Whatever be the vices of this heinous body  
do ask the ‘Prakriti’ about it\!  
The birth which I do not know about, if there are any virtues,  
do ask the Creator who created me on this earth\!  
Whatever be the sins associated with the five sense organs,  
do ask the handsome Kama Deva about it\!  
Whatever deceit exists in all my actions, do ask the ‘Maaya’  
which lures us with seeming goodness, into committing them\!  
If there are any sins that I have committed in the past,  
forgive me, and seeing my Guru, do pardon me fully\!  
Sree Venkatesha\! Whatever be the deeds in future,  
seeing Yourself (within me), do rule over me\!\!
