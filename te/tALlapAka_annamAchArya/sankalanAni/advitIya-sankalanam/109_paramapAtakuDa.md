**Paramapātakuḍa**  
परमपातकुड भवबन्धुड श्री \-  
हरि निनु दलच ने नरुहुडना ॥॥ परम ॥  
अपवित्रुड ने नमंळुड गडु \-  
नपगत पुण्युड नलसुडनु  
कपटकलुष परिकर हृदयड ने \-  
नपवर्गमुनकु नरुहुडना ॥॥ परम ॥  
अतिदुष्णुड ने नधिक दूषितुड  
हतविवेकमति नदयडनु  
पतिलेनि रमापति मिमुदल चले-  
नतुलगतिकि ने नरुहुडना ॥॥ परम ॥  
अनुपम विषय पराधीनुड ने \-  
ननन्त मोहभयातुरुड  
विनुतिम्पङ्ग दिरु वेङ्कटेश घनु-  
लनघुलु गाक ने नरुहुडना ॥॥ परम ॥  
**paramapātakuḍa bhavabandhuḍa śrī \-**  
**hari ninu dalaca nē naruhuḍanā .... parama ..**  
**apavitruḍa nē namaṃḷuḍa gaḍu \-**  
**napagata puṇyuḍa nalasuḍanu**  
**kapaṭakaluṣa parikara hṛdayuḍa nē \-**  
**napavargamunaku naruhuḍanā .... parama ..**  
**atiduṣṇuḍa nē nadhika dūṣituḍa**  
**hatavivēkamati nadayuḍanu**  
**patilēni ramāpati mimudala calē-**  
**natulagatiki nē naruhuḍanā .... parama ..**  
**anupama viṣaya parādhīnuḍa nē \-**  
**nananta mōhabhayāturuḍa**  
**vinutimpaṅga diru vēṅkaṭēśa ghanu-**  
**lanaghulu gāka nē naruhuḍanā .... parama ..**  
I am the highest sinner, caught in this material existence\!  
O Sree Hari, am I qualified to think of You?  
Impure I am, inauspicious I am  
I am meritorious only in the bad way, ignorant I am  
My heart is full of deceit and sin,  
Am I eligible for salvation?  
Extremely wicked and highly blameworthy I am,  
I lack wisdom and am uncompassionate  
O Lord of Lakshmi, You who has no other Overlord  
without thinking of You, am I eligible for the supreme abode?  
I am controlled by many material desires,  
I am gripped by limitless fear and passion  
O Lord TiruVenkatesha\! But for the sinless and  
great ones, am I eligible to praise You?
