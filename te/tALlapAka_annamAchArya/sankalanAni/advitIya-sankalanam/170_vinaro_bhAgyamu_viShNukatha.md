**Vinarō Bhāgyamu Viṣṇukatha**  
विनरो भाग्यमु विष्णुकथ  
वॆनुबलमिदिवो विष्णुकथ ॥॥ विनरो ॥  
आदि नुण्डि सन्ध्यादि विधुललो  
वेदम्बयिनदि विष्णुकथ  
नादिञ्चीनिदे नारदादुलचे  
वीधि वीधुलने विष्णुकथ ॥॥ विनरो ॥  
वदलक वेदव्यासुलु नुडिविन  
विदित पावनमु विष्णुकथ  
सदनम्बैनदि सङ्कीर्तनयै  
वॆदकिनचोटने विष्णुकथ ॥॥ विनरो ॥  
गॊल्लॆतलु चल्ललु गॊनकॊनि चिलकग  
वॆल्लि विरियायॆ विष्णुकथ  
यिल्लिदे श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरु नाममु  
वॆल्लिगॊलिपॆ नी विष्णुकथ ॥॥ विनरो ॥  
**vinarō bhāgyamu viṣṇukatha**  
**venubalamidivō viṣṇukatha .... vinarō ..**  
**ādi nuṇḍi sandhyādi vidhulalō**  
**vēdambayinadi viṣṇukatha**  
**nādiñcīnidē nāradādulacē**  
**vīdhi vīdhulanē viṣṇukatha .... vinarō ..**  
**vadalaka vēdavyāsulu nuḍivina**  
**vidita pāvanamu viṣṇukatha**  
**sadanambainadi saṅkīrtanayai**  
**vedakinacōṭanē viṣṇukatha .... vinarō ..**  
**golletalu callalu gonakoni cilakaga**  
**velli viriyāye viṣṇukatha**  
**yillidē śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaru nāmamu**  
**velligolipe nī viṣṇukatha .... vinarō ..**  
O listen\! It is a fortune, the Vishnu Katha\!  
Support like a backbone, is this, Vishnu Katha\!  
From the primeval times, in rituals like Sandhya,  
akin to the Vedas, is Vishnu Katha\!  
Sung by sages like Narada,  
in streets and lanes, is Vishnu Katha\!  
Constantly praised by VedaVyasa,  
is this famed , pristine, Vishnu Katha\!  
Eternally present as Sankirtana,  
wherever searched for, is Vishnu Katha\!  
When cowherd lasses churned the curds,  
it emerged, the Vishnu Katha\!  
In this manner, Sree Venkateshwaras sacred name,  
reveals this Vishnu Katha\!
