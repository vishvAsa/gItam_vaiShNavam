**Sakalasaṅgrahamu Sakala Sañcayamu**  
सकलसङ्ग्रहमु सकल सञ्चयमु  
अकृतसुकृत मिदि हरिनामम् ॥॥सकल॥  
सकल वेदशास्त्रमुलसार मिदि  
सकलमन्त्र राजम्बु निदि  
सकलपुराण रसमुलमधुर मिदि  
अकुटिलपावनं हरिनामम् ॥॥सकल॥  
सकलतत्व संशयखण्डन मिदि  
सकलकर्मनिश्चयमु निदि  
सकल विधिरहस्यप्रधान मिदि  
अकारणहितं हरिनामम् ॥॥सकल॥  
सकल देवतास्वामिप्रियं बिदि  
सकललोक रक्षणमु निदि  
प्रकटं वेङ्कटपतिनामाङ्कित-  
मकिञ्चनधनं हरिनामम् ॥॥सकल॥  
**sakalasaṅgrahamu sakala sañcayamu**  
**akṛtasukṛta midi harināmaṃ ....sakala..**  
**sakala vēdaśāstramulasāra midi**  
**sakalamantra rājambu nidi**  
**sakalapurāṇa rasamulamadhura midi**  
**akuṭilapāvanaṃ harināmaṃ ....sakala..**  
**sakalatatva saṃśayakhaṇḍana midi**  
**sakalakarmaniścayamu nidi**  
**sakala vidhirahasyapradhāna midi**  
**akāraṇahitaṃ harināmaṃ ....sakala..**  
**sakala dēvatāsvāmipriyaṃ bidi**  
**sakalalōka rakṣaṇamu nidi**  
**prakaṭaṃ vēṅkaṭapatināmāṅkita-**  
**makiñcanadhanaṃ harināmaṃ ....sakala..**  
An assemblage of everything, a union of everything,  
a naturally bestowed reward, is the name of Hari\!  
This is the quintessence of the entire Veda-Shastras,  
this is the highest of all mantras,  
this is the sweet elixir of all the Puranas,  
the flawless and pristine one, the name of Hari\!  
This is the one that cuts asunder the doubts about the eternal truths,  
this is the one that ordains all Karmas,  
this is the important one amongst all esoteric deeds,  
this is the unconditionally benevolent one, the name of Hari\!  
This is the one that pleases all Gods,  
this is the protector of all the worlds,  
this is the one that is revealed by the name of Venkatapathi,  
the wealth of the destitute, is this name of Hari\!
