**Poḍagaṇṭimayya Mimmu**  
पॊडगण्टिमय्य मिम्मु पुरुषोत्तमा मम्मु  
नॆडयकवय्य कोनेटिरायडा ॥॥ पॊडगण्टि ॥  
कोरि मम्मु नेलिनट्टि कुलदैवमा! चाला  
नेरचि पॆद्दलिच्चिव निधानमा  
गारविञ्चि दप्पिदीर्चु कालमेघमा! माकु  
चेरुव चित्तमुलोनि श्रीनिवासुडा! ॥॥ पॊडगण्टि ॥  
भाविम्पकैवसमैन पारिजातमा! मम्मु  
चेवदेर गाचिनट्टि चिन्तामणी  
काविञ्चि कोरिकलिच्चे कामधेनुवा! मम्मु  
तावै रक्षिञ्चेटि धरणीधरा! ॥॥ पॊडगण्टि ॥  
चॆडनीक ब्रतिकिञ्चे सिद्धमन्त्रमा! रोगा -  
लडचि रक्षिञ्चे दिव्यौषधमा!  
बडिबायक तिरिगे प्राणबन्धुडा! मम्मु  
गडियिञ्चिवट्टि श्री वेङ्कटनाधुडा ॥॥ पॊडगण्टि ॥  
**poḍagaṇṭimayya mimmu puruṣōttamā mammu**  
**neḍayakavayya kōnēṭirāyaḍā .... poḍagaṇṭi ..**  
**kōri mammu nēlinaṭṭi kuladaivamā! cālā**  
**nēraci peddalicciva nidhānamā**  
**gāraviñci dappidīrcu kālamēghamā! māku**  
**cēruva cittamulōni śrīnivāsuḍā! .... poḍagaṇṭi ..**  
**bhāvimpakaivasamaina pārijātamā! mammu**  
**cēvadēra gācinaṭṭi cintāmaṇī**  
**kāviñci kōrikaliccē kāmadhēnuvā! mammu**  
**tāvai rakṣiñcēṭi dharaṇīdharā! .... poḍagaṇṭi ..**  
**ceḍanīka bratikiñcē siddhamantramā! rōgā -**  
**laḍaci rakṣiñcē divyauṣadhamā!**  
**baḍibāyaka tirigē prāṇabandhuḍā! mammu**  
**gaḍiyiñcivaṭṭi śrī vēṅkaṭanādhuḍā .... poḍagaṇṭi ..**  
We beheld your presence, O Purushottama!  
Do not let go of us, O Konetiraayaa!1  
You ruled over us lovingly by your own wish, O family deity!  
You are the real treasure our fore-fathers bestowed upon us after realizing!  
You are the dark blue cloud who quenches our thirst in a loving manner!  
You are Srinivasa, seated in our minds!  
You are the Parijatha2 which we have gained by mere thought!  
You are the Chintamani3 who protects us to our hearts' content!  
You are the Kamadhenu4 who blesses us and grants desired boons!  
Being our only refuge, You protect us, O Dharanidhara5!  
O life saving powerful incantation,  
O disease destroying divine medication,  
O inseparable soul – relation,  
You won over us, O Lord Sree Venkatanatha!!  
1.Konetiraya means ‘The Lord of the Koneru’. Koneru is the holy Pushkarini tank.  
2.The Parijatha flower  
3.The wish fulfilling gem  
4.The wish yielding cow  
5.The Lord who bore the earth
