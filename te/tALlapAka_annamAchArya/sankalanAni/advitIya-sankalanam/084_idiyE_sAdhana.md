**Idiye Sādhana**  
इदियॆ साधन मिहपरमुलकुनु  
पदिलमु मापालि परमपु नामम् ॥॥इदियॆ॥  
कलिदोषहरमु कैवल्यकरमु  
अलरिन मा श्रीहरि नामं  
सुलभमु सौख्यमु शोभन तिलकमु  
पलुमारुनु श्रीपतिनामम् ॥॥इदियॆ॥  
पापनाशनमु बन्ध विमोक्षमु  
पैपैनिदि भूपति नामं  
स्थापितधन मिदि सर्व रक्षकमु  
दापि रमिदि माधवनामम् ॥॥इदियॆ॥  
नेममु दीममु नित्य कर्ममिदि  
दोमटि गोविन्दुनि नामं  
हेममु शरणमु यिन्निट माकुनु  
येमेर श्री वेङ्कटेश्वर नामम् ॥॥इदियॆ॥  
**idiye sādhana mihaparamulakunu**  
**padilamu māpāli paramapu nāmaṃ ....idiye..**  
**kalidōṣaharamu kaivalyakaramu**  
**alarina mā śrīhari nāmaṃ**  
**sulabhamu saukhyamu śōbhana tilakamu**  
**palumārunu śrīpatināmaṃ ....idiye..**  
**pāpanāśanamu bandha vimōkṣamu**  
**paipainidi bhūpati nāmaṃ**  
**sthāpitadhana midi sarva rakṣakamu**  
**dāpi ramidi mādhavanāmaṃ ....idiye..**  
**nēmamu dīmamu nitya karmamidi**  
**dōmaṭi gōvinduni nāmaṃ**  
**hēmamu śaraṇamu yinniṭa mākunu**  
**yēmēra śrī vēṅkaṭēśvara nāmaṃ ....idiye..**  
This alone is the means for here and hereafter  
It is safe and secure for us, this Name-Supreme\!  
Destroyer of the sins of Kali, bestower of eternal beatitude  
is the splendid name of our Sree Hari\!  
The affable one, the bestower of well being, the propitious mark  
if repeatedly chanted, this name of Sree-Pathi\!  
The annihilator of sins, the one which releases from bondage,  
high above it is, the name of Bhu-Pathi\!  
The proven established wealth is this, the all-protector is this,  
if ardently sought, this name of Madhava\!  
The one to be followed with diligence, the divine bait, the daily ritual,  
the nourishing name of Govinda\!  
The precious refuge amongst all, is for us,  
the omnipotent name of Sree Venkateshwara\!
