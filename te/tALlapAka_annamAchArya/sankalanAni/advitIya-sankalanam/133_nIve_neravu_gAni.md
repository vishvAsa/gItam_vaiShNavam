**Nīvē Nēravu Gāni**  
नीवे नेरवु गानि, निन्नु पण्डिञ्चेमु नेमु.  
दैवमा\! नीकण्टॆ नी दासुले नेर्परुलु\!॥ नीवे ॥  
वट्टि भक्ति नीमीद वळुकु वेसि निन्नु  
पट्टि तॆच्चि मदिलोन पॆट्टुकॊण्टिमि;  
पुट्टॆडु तुलसि नी पादमुलपै पॆट्टि  
जट्टिकॊनिरि मोक्षमु, जाणलु नी दासुलु\!॥ नीवे ॥  
नीवु निर्मिञ्चिनवॆ नीके समर्पण सेसि,  
सोवल नीकृप यॆल्ल चूरगॊण्टिमि;  
भाविं चॊक म्रॊक्कु म्रॊक्कि भारमु नीपै वेसिरि,  
पावनपु नी दासुले पन्तपु चतुरुलु\!॥ नीवे ॥  
चॆरुवुल नीळ्ळु तॆच्चि चेरॆडु नीपै चल्लि,  
वरमु वडसितिमि वलसिनट्टु;  
इरवै श्रीवेङ्कटेश\! इटुवण्टि विद्यलने  
दरि चेरि मिञ्चिरि, नी दासुले पो घनुलु\!॥ नीवे ॥  
**nīvē nēravu gāni, ninnu paṇḍiñcēmu nēmu.**  
**daivamā\! nīkaṇṭe nī dāsulē nērparulu\!.. nīvē ..**  
**vaṭṭi bhakti nīmīda vaḷuku vēsi ninnu**  
**paṭṭi tecci madilōna peṭṭukoṇṭimi;**  
**puṭṭeḍu tulasi nī pādamulapai peṭṭi**  
**jaṭṭikoniri mōkṣamu, jāṇalu nī dāsulu\!.. nīvē ..**  
**nīvu nirmiñcinave nīkē samarpaṇa sēsi,**  
**sōvala nīkṛpa yella cūragoṇṭimi;**  
**bhāviṃ coka mrokku mrokki bhāramu nīpai vēsiri,**  
**pāvanapu nī dāsulē pantapu caturulu\!.. nīvē ..**  
**ceruvula nīḷḷu tecci cēreḍu nīpai calli,**  
**varamu vaḍasitimi valasinaṭṭu;**  
**iravai śrīvēṅkaṭēśa\! iṭuvaṇṭi vidyalanē**  
**dari cēri miñciri, nī dāsulē pō ghanulu\!.. nīvē ..**  
You do not know of it\! We reap rich harvests out of You\!  
O Lord\! Your devotees are more tactful than You\!  
We enchanted You with our mere devotion  
caught hold of You and placed You within our hearts  
Placing but a small measure of Tulasi at Your feet  
we buy Moksha\! So clever are Your devotees\!  
That which You alone created, offering the same unto You  
easily we stole all Your limitless grace\!  
Merely bowing down in salutations just once, we place all our burdens upon You  
Your pious devotees are obstinately cleverer\!  
Fetching water from the river, sprinkling a fistful on You  
we get whatever we seek\!  
SreeVenkatesha\! In these kind of arts, we excel\!  
Your devotees are truly great\!\!
