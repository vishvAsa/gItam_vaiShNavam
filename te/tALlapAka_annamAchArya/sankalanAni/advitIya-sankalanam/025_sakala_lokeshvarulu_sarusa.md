**Sakala Lōkēśvarulu Sarusa**  
सकल लोकेश्वरुलु सरुस चेकॊनुवाडु  
अकलङ्कमुग पुष्प यागम्बु ॥॥ सकल ॥  
विविध मन्त्रमुलतो वेदफॆूषमुलतो  
अवल तिरुवामुडिय अङ्गनल आटतो  
कविवन्दिनुतुलतो कम्म पूजलतोड -  
नवधरिञ्ची पुष्प यागम्बु ॥॥ सकल ॥  
कप्पुरपुटारतुल घनचन्दनमु तोड  
तॆप्पल धूपमुल तिरुवन्दि कापुतो  
ऒप्पु(ग) पण्यारमुलु वॊगि पॆक्कुवगलतो  
अप्पडन्दी पुष्प यागम्बु ॥॥ सकल ॥  
तगु छत्र चामर ताम्बुलमुलतोड  
पगटुतो नीरीति पदिपूजलन्दुकॊनि  
जिगिमीरॆ चूडरे श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरुनि  
अगणितम्बगु पुष्प यागम्बु ॥॥ सकल ॥  
**sakala lōkēśvarulu sarusa cēkonuvāḍu**  
**akalaṅkamuga puṣpa yāgambu .... sakala ..**  
**vividha mantramulatō vēdaghōṣamulatō**  
**avala tiruvāmuḍiyu aṃganala āṭatō**  
**kavivandinutulatō kamma pūjalatōḍa -**  
**navadhariñcī puṣpa yāgambu .... sakala ..**  
**kappurapuṭāratula ghanacandanamu tōḍa**  
**teppala dhūpamula tiruvandi kāputō**  
**oppu(ga) paṇyāramulu vogi pekkuvagalatō**  
**appaḍandī puṣpa yāgambu .... sakala ..**  
**tagu chatra cāmara tāmbulamulatōḍa**  
**pagaṭutō nīrīti padipūjalandukoni**  
**jigimīre cūḍarē śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaruni**  
**agaṇitambagu puṣpa yāgambu .... sakala ..**  
He, the Lord of all the worlds, receives gracefully,  
in a pristine manner, the Pushpayagam1!  
Along with various mantras, along with the Veda Goshas  
with the recitation of Tiruvaimozi2, along with dancing damsels  
along with praises sung by poets, along with pleasing worship,  
He is adorned by the Pushpayagam!  
Along with camphor arathi, along with pure sandal paste,  
along with plentiful incense, along with the Tirumandikapu3  
along with agreeable Paniyarams4, with duly offered abundant luxuries,  
the Almighty receives the Pushpayagam!  
Along with the umbrellas, with the fans, with beetle leaf offerings,  
joyously receiving in this manner the various kinds of worship,  
Behold! He excelled in brilliance, this Lord Sree Venkateshwara,  
in the glorious Pushpayagam!  
1.10th day of Brahmotsavam  
2.Nammalwar's work of one thousand Prabandhams  
3.Waving of the lamp  
4.Food offerings
