**Telisinaṃ Deliyaṇḍu Teliyani**  
तॆलिसिनं दॆलियण्डु तॆलियनि वारलु  
दॊलगुण्डु ब्रह्मादुलॆ यॆरुङ्गुदुरु\!॥तॆलिसि॥  
वरदं डखिल देवतलकु वन्द्युडु  
गरडुं डसुरलकु गण्टकुण्डु  
परमात्मुं डम्बुज भव शिवादुलकु  
बरुलकॆल्ल मुव्वुरिलो नॊकण्डु \!॥तॆलिसि॥  
देवुण्डु सनकादि मुनुलकुनु बर  
दैव मखिल वेदमुलकुनु  
कैवल्यमॊसङ्गु घन निधि विधिकि  
महाविधि जडुलकु यादवकुलुण्डु \!॥तॆलिसि॥  
आद्युं डचलुण्डु महाभूत मितं ड  
भेद्युं डसाध्युण्डु भीकरुण्डु  
सद्यः फलदुण्डु सकल मुनुलकुनु  
वेद्युं डितण्डॆवो वेङ्कट विभुडु \!॥तॆलिसि॥  
**telisinaṃ deliyaṇḍu teliyani vāralu**  
**dolaguṇḍu brahmādule yeruṅguduru\!..telisi..**  
**varadaṃ ḍakhila dēvatalaku vandyuḍu**  
**garaḍuṃ ḍasuralaku gaṇṭakuṇḍu**  
**paramātmuṃ ḍambuja bhava śivādulaku**  
**barulakella muvvurilō nokaṇḍu \!..telisi..**  
**dēvuṇḍu sanakādi munulakunu bara**  
**daiva makhila vēdamulakunu**  
**kaivalyamosaṅgu ghana nidhi vidhiki**  
**mahāvidhi jaḍulaku yādavakuluṇḍu \!..telisi..**  
**ādyuṃ ḍacaluṇḍu mahābhūta mitaṃ ḍa**  
**bhēdyuṃ ḍasādhyuṇḍu bhīkaruṇḍu**  
**sadyaḥ phaladuṇḍu sakala munulakunu**  
**vēdyuṃ ḍitaṇḍevō vēṅkaṭa vibhuḍu \!..telisi..**  
Even upon knowing, He will not be known\! Those who do not know,  
move away\! Only Brahma and other such beings will know\!  
He is the bestower of boons to all other deities,  
object of reverence for Garuda, destroyer of the demons,  
the supreme God even to Brahma and Shiva \!  
But for others, He is just one among the trinity\!  
He is the Lord for Sanaka and other sages  
For the entire Vedas, He alone is the Supreme Lord  
He is the Supreme treasure bestowed by salvation\! For fate itself, He is  
the Supreme fate\! But for the ignorant He is just one of the Yadava clan\!  
The Primal one, the Unwavering, the Supreme Spirit  
The Indestructible, the Extraordinary, the Terrifying one\!  
The one who bestows fruits instantly, for all sages He alone  
is the object of Knowledge, this Venkata Vibhudu\!\!\!
