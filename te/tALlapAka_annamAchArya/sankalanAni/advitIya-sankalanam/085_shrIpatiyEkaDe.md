**Śrīpatiyokaḍē**  
श्रीपति यॆकडे शरणमु माकुनु  
तेप यितडे मऱि तॆरगेदि ॥॥श्रीपति॥  
आसलु मिगुला नातुम नुन्नवि  
यीसुलेनि सुख मॆक्कडिदि  
चेसिन पापमु चेतुल नुन्नदि  
मोसपोनि गति मुन्दर नेदि ॥॥श्रीपति॥  
कोपमु गॊन्दुल गुणमुल नुन्नदि  
येपुन निजसुख मिक नेदि  
दीपनाग्नितो दिरिगेटि देहमु  
पैपै विरतिकि बट्टेदि ॥॥श्रीपति॥  
पञ्चेन्द्रियमुल पालिटि बदुकिदि  
यिञ्चुक निलुकड कॆड येदि  
यॆञ्चग श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरु डॊकडे  
पञ्चिन विधुलनु पालिञ्चुगाक ॥॥श्रीपति॥  
**śrīpati yokaḍē śaraṇamu mākunu**  
**tēpa yitaḍē maṟi teragēdi ....śrīpati..**  
**āsalu migulā nātuma nunnavi**  
**yīsulēni sukha mekkaḍidi**  
**cēsina pāpamu cētula nunnadi**  
**mōsapōni gati mundara nēdi ....śrīpati..**  
**kōpamu gondula guṇamula nunnadi**  
**yēpuna nijasukha mika nēdi**  
**dīpanāgnitō dirigēṭi dēhamu**  
**paipai viratiki baṭṭēdi ....śrīpati..**  
**pañcēndriyamula pāliṭi badukidi**  
**yiñcuka nilukaḍa keḍa yēdi**  
**yeñcaga śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaru ḍokaḍē**  
**pañcina vidhulanu pāliñcugāka ....śrīpati..**  
The Lord of Sree alone is the refuge for us\!  
The means is He alone, what else is?  
There are many desires within oneself,  
where then is the pleasure without any jealousy?  
The sins committed are in ones own hands,  
which then is the path devoid of any deceit?  
Anger is hidden within ones own nature  
where then is the true happiness?  
The body moves about carrying the fires of passion,  
how then, can one adhere to dispassion?  
The life is under the sway of the five senses,  
how can there be even a little steadiness?  
If understood rightly, Sree Venkatesha alone  
rules over all the ordained duties\!
