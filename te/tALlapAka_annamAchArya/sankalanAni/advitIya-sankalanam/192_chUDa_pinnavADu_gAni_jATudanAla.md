**Cūḍa Pinnavāḍu Gāni Jūṭudanāla**  
चूड पिन्नवाडु गानि जाटुदनाल नॆक्कुडु  
येड चूचिना तानु यी विट्ठलुण्डे ॥॥चूड॥  
गोवुललो विहरिञ्चे गोविन्दुडु पॆक्कु  
वावुल गोपिकलकु वल्लभुडु  
चेवमीरि मुद्दुल श्रीकृष्णुडु  
येवल्लजाचिनानु यी विट्ठलुडे ॥॥चूड॥  
रासक्रीडललोनि राजीवाक्षुडु  
आस पदारुवेल पॆण्ड्लाडिनवाडु  
बासतो रुक्मिणी सत्यभामासमेतुडु  
यी सुद्दुल वॆलसी नी विट्ठलुडे ॥॥चूड॥  
विन्त मधुराङ्गनल वेडुककाडु  
चॆन्त कॊलनि सतुल सिग्गुलवाडु  
कान्त लक्ष्मीपति श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरुडु  
यिन्तटा विनोदिञ्ची नी विठलुडे ॥॥चूड॥  
**cūḍa pinnavāḍu gāni jūṭudanāla nekkuḍu**  
**yēḍa cūcinā tānu yī viṭṭhaluṇḍē ....cūḍa..**  
**gōvulalō vihariñcē gōvinduḍu pekku**  
**vāvula gōpikalaku vallabhuḍu**  
**cēvamīri muddula śrīkṛṣṇuḍu**  
**yēvallajūcinānu yī viṭṭhaluḍē ....cūḍa..**  
**rāsakrīḍalalōni rājīvākṣuḍu**  
**āsa padāruvēla peṇḍlāḍinavāḍu**  
**bāsatō rukmiṇī satyabhāmāsamētuḍu**  
**yī suddula velasī nī viṭṭhaluḍē ....cūḍa..**  
**vinta madhurāṅganala vēḍukakāḍu**  
**centa kolani satula siggulavāḍu**  
**kānta lakṣmīpati śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaruḍu**  
**yintaṭā vinōdiñcī nī viṭhaluḍē ....cūḍa..**  
To look at, He is a mere child\! Yet His mischievous qualities are great\!  
Wherever we see, there He is, this Vittala\!  
He moves about with the cows, this Govinda\!  
Related to the Gopis in many ways, He is their Vallabha\!  
Exceedingly lovable is this Sree Krishna\!  
Wherever we see, there He is, this Vittala\!  
In His amorous plays, He is the lotus eyed one\!  
With ardor, He married sixteen thousand wives  
this one who is bound in love with Rukmini-satyabhama\!  
In this manner, He shone splendidly, this Vittala\!  
The one who delighted with beautiful damsels  
The one who put women to shame by the riverside  
The consort of Lakshmi, SreeVenkatesha  
Everywhere He sported, this Vittala\!\!
