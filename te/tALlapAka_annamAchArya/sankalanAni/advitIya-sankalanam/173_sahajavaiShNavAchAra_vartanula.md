**Sahajavaiṣṇavācāra Vartanula**  
सहजवैष्णवाचार वर्तनुल  
सहवासमॆ मा सन्ध्य ॥॥ सहज ॥  
अतिशयमगु श्रीहरि सङ्कीर्तन  
सततम्बुनु मा सन्ध्य  
मतिरामानुजमतमे माकुनु  
चतुरतमॆरसिन सन्ध्य ॥॥ सहज ॥  
परम भागवतपद सेवनमे  
सरवि नॆन्न मा सन्ध्य  
सिरिवरुमहिमलु चॆलुवॊन्दग वे \-  
सरक विनुटे मा सन्ध्य ॥॥ सहज ॥  
मन्तुकॆक्क तिरुमन्त्रपठनमे  
सन्ततमुनु मा सन्ध्य  
कन्तु गुरुडु वेङ्कटगिरिरायनि  
सन्तर्पणमे मा सन्ध्य ॥॥ सहज ॥  
**sahajavaiṣṇavācāra vartanula**  
**sahavāsame mā sandhya .... sahaja ..**  
**atiśayamagu śrīhari saṅkīrtana**  
**satatambunu mā sandhya**  
**matirāmānujamatamē mākunu**  
**caturatamerasina sandhya .... sahaja ..**  
**parama bhāgavatapada sēvanamē**  
**saravi nenna mā sandhya**  
**sirivarumahimalu celuvondaga vē \-**  
**saraka vinuṭē mā sandhya .... sahaja ..**  
**mantukekka tirumantrapaṭhanamē**  
**santatamunu mā sandhya**  
**kantu guruḍu vēṅkaṭagirirāyani**  
**santarpaṇamē mā sandhya .... sahaja ..**  
Those who follow the Vaishnava code of conduct innately,  
their association itself is our Sandhya\!  
Passionately singing Sankirtanas of SreeHari,  
is always our Sandhya\!  
Understanding the philosophy of Ramanuja is our  
glowing intellectual Sandhya\!  
Serving the feet of Parama Bhagavathas,  
habitually, is our Sandhya\!  
Listening to the glories of the Lord of Sree,  
joyously and untiringly, is itself our Sandhya\!  
Chanting the glorious ‘Tirumantra’,  
constantly, is our Sandhya\!  
The father of Manmatha, the Lord of Venkata hill,  
pleasing Him only, is our Sandhya\!
