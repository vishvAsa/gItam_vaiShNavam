**Cēri Yaśōdaku Śiśuvitaḍu**  
चेरि यशोदकु शिशु वितडु  
धारुणि ब्रह्मकुतण्ड्रिय नितडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
सॊलसि चूचिननु सूर्यचन्द्रुलनु  
ललि वॆदचल्लॆडु लक्ष्मणुडु  
निलिचिन निलुवुन निखिलदेवतल  
कलिगिञ्चु सुरलगनिवो यितडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
माटलाडिननु मरि यजाण्डमुलु  
कोटुलु वॊडमेटि गुणराशि  
नीटगुनूर्पुल निखिलवेदमुलु  
चाटुव नूरेटि समुद्र मितडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
मुङ्गिट पॊलसिन मोहन मात्मुल  
पॊङ्गिञ्चे घनपुरुषुडु  
सङ्गति मावण्टि शरणागतुलकु  
नङ्गमु श्री वेङ्कटाधिपु डितडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
**cēri yaśōdaku śiśu vitaḍu**  
**dhāruṇi brahmakutaṇḍriyu nitaḍu .... cēri ..**  
**solasi cūcinanu sūryacandrulanu**  
**lali vedacalleḍu lakṣmaṇuḍu**  
**nilicina niluvuna nikhiladēvatala**  
**kaligiñcu suralaganivō yitaḍu .... cēri ..**  
**māṭalāḍinanu mari yajāṇḍamulu**  
**kōṭulu voḍamēṭi guṇarāśi**  
**nīṭagunūrpula nikhilavēdamulu**  
**cāṭuva nūrēṭi samudra mitaḍu .... cēri ..**  
**muṅgiṭa polasina mōhana mātmula**  
**poṅgiñcē ghanapuruṣuḍu**  
**saṅgati māvaṇṭi śaraṇāgatulaku**  
**naṅgamu śrī vēṅkaṭādhipu ḍitaḍu .... cēri ..**  
He is the baby of Yashoda, and at the same time He is  
the father of Brahma, who is the creator of the earth\!  
Even with a casual look, He sprinkles the effulgence  
of sun and moon, Such are His glances\!  
From His tall stature, He creates all the Devathas  
Such a mine of Gods He is\!  
When He speaks, crores of Universes emerge  
Such an assemblage of attributes He is\!  
From His exhalation of pure breaths spring the Vedas  
Such an expansive ocean He is\!  
He is the supremely attractive form in front of us  
He is the great Purusha who makes the Jivas swell with joy  
For Sharanagathas like us who have reached unto Him  
He is the only means, this Lord of Venkata\!
