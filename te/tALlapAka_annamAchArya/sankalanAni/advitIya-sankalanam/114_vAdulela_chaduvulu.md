**Vādulēla Caduvulu**  
वादुलेल चदुवुलु वारु चॆप्पिनवे कावा  
वादुलेल मीमाट वारिकण्टे नॆक्कुडा ॥॥ वादुलेल ॥  
नालुगुवेदालब्रह्म नलि नॆव्वनिसुतुडु  
वालिनपुराणालव्यासु डॆव्वनिदासुडु  
लील रामायणपुवाल्मीकि वसिष्टुलु  
आलकिं चॆव्वनि गॊल्चि रातडे पो देवुडु ॥॥ वादुलेल ॥  
भारत मॆव्वनि कथ भागवतमु चॆप्पिन \-  
धीरुडैनशुकुडु येदेवुनिकिङ्करुडु  
सारपुशास्त्रालु चूचि सन्यसिञ्चि नुडिगेटि \-  
नारायणनामपुनाथुडे पो देवुडु ॥॥ वादुलेल ॥  
विष्णुवाज्ञयमनि चॆप्पे विधिसङ्कल्प मेडदि  
विष्णुमाययनि चॆप्पे विश्वमन्ता नॆव्वनिदि  
""विष्णुमयं सर्व’’ मने वेदवाक्य मॆव्वनिदि  
विष्णुवु श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रिविभुडे आ देवुडु ॥॥ वादुलेल ॥

**bhārata mevvani katha bhāgavatamu ceppina \-**

**sārapuśāstrālu cūci sanyasiñci nuḍigēṭi \-**  
**nārāyaṇanāmapunāthuḍē pō dēvuḍu .... vādulēla**  
**viṣṇuvājñayamani ceppē vidhisaṅkalpa mēḍadi**  
**viṣṇumāyayani ceppē viśvamantā nevvanidi**  
**"viṣṇumayaṃ sarva’’ manē vēdavākya mevvanidi**  
**viṣṇuvu śrīvēṅkaṭādrivibhuḍē ā dēvuḍu .... vādulēla**  
Why any arguments, when these people have only spoken the scriptures  
Why any arguments? Is your word more authoritative than theirs?  
The Brahma of the four Vedas, whose son is he?  
Vyasa who authored the Puranas, whose Daasa is he?  
Valmiki and Vasista of the glorious Ramayana, having comprehended,  
the one to whom they offered their worship, He alone is the Supreme Lord\!  
Whose glory did the ‘Bhaaratha’1 speak of? The one who spoke the Bhagavatha  
the great Suka, unto which deity did he offer servitude?  
For those who examine all the shastras, renounce and then live by it,  
the Lord by the name of Narayana, He alone is the Supreme God\!  
That Vishnu-Agnya2 which is called destiny and fate, from where does it originate?  
That Vishnu-Maaya3 which is called as the Universe, whose possession is it?  
The words of the Veda that declare “Vishnu mayam sarvam”, whose words are they?  
That Vishnu, Lord SreeVenkateshwara, alone is that supreme Lord\!\!  
**Purport** : Annamacharya declares here that the supremacy of Lord Narayana has been  
established in the scriptures and also by great sages like Vyasa, Valmiki, Shuka , Vasista and  
others who composed many works purely for the sake of imparting the true essence of the  
Vedas. Thus, having the injunctions of the Vedas as the prime support, their words stand as the  
authority\!  
1.Mahabharatha  
2.The will of Lord Vishnu  
3.The Triguna Maaya of Vishnu, by which the Universe is created
