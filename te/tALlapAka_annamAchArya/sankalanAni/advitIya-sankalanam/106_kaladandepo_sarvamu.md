**Kaladandēpō Sarvamu**  
कलदन्देपो सर्वमु गलदु कामितार्थमुनु गलदु  
कलदु गलदु शरणागतुलकु हरिकैङ्कर्यम्बुन मोक्षमु गल दु ॥॥ कल ॥  
आकाशम्बुन मोक्षमु वॆदकिन नन्दु लोपला लेदु  
पैकॊनि तानॆन्त वॆदकि चूचिना पाताळम्बुन लेदु  
यीकड धरलो मूलमूललनु यॆन्दु वॆदकिना लेदु  
श्रीकान्तुनि मति जिन्तिञ्चि यासल जिक्कक तॊलगिन नन्त कलदु ॥॥ कल ॥  
कोटि जन्ममुलु यॆत्तिन मुक्तिकि कान मॊदलेमिय गनरादु  
वाटपु संसारमुलो गर्मपु वार्धि यीदिना गनरादु  
कूटुवतो स्वर्गादिलोकमुल गोरि वॆदकिना गनरादु  
गाटपु केशवभक्ति गलिगिते कैवल्यमु मतिगान वच्चुनु ॥॥ कल ॥  
सकलशास्त्रमुलु चदिविना बरमु चक्कटि मार्गमु दॊरकदु  
विकटापुल वेल्पुल नॆन्दरि गडुवॆदकि कॊलचिना दॊरकदु  
अकलङ्कुडु श्री वेङ्कटगिरिपति अन्तरङ्गमुन नुन्नाडनुचुनु  
प्रकटमुगा गरुडानति इच्चिन परमु सुत्तानमु तनलो दॊरकु ॥॥ कल ॥  
**kaladandēpō sarvamu galadu kāmitārthamunu galadu**  
**kaladu galadu śaraṇāgatulaku harikaiṅkaryambuna mōkṣamu galadu .... kala ..**  
**ākāśambuna mōkṣamu vedakina nandu lōpalā lēdu**  
**paikoni tānenta vedaki cūcinā pātāḷambuna lēdu**  
**yīkaḍa dharalō mūlamūlalanu yendu vedakinā lēdu**  
**śrīkāntuni mati jintiñci yāsala jikkaka tolagina nanta kaladu .... kala ..**  
**kōṭi janmamulu yettina muktiki kāna modalēmiyu ganarādu**  
**vāṭapu saṃsāramulō garmapu vārdhi yīdinā ganarādu**  
**kūṭuvatō svargādilōkamula gōri vedakinā ganarādu**  
**gāṭapu kēśavabhakti galigitē kaivalyamu matigāna vaccunu .... kala ..**  
**sakalaśāstramulu cadivinā baramu cakkaṭi mārgamu dorakadu**  
**vikaṭāpula vēlpula nendari gaḍuvedaki kolacinā dorakadu**  
**akalaṅkuḍu śrī vēṅkaṭagiripati aṃtaraṅgamuna nunnāḍanucunu**  
**prakaṭamugā garuḍānati iccina paramu suttānamu tanalō doraku .... kala ..**  
Everything is there in it, even the realization of desires is there\!  
It is there\! It is there\! For Sharanagathas, Moksha is there in the service of Hari\!  
If we search for moksha in the sky, it is not even there  
Even upon searching deeply, it is not there in the nether worlds  
Here on earth, even upon searching in every nook and corner, it is not there  
Only upon contemplating on the Lord of Sree, and driving away all desires, it is there\!  
Even upon taking birth crores of times, we cannot find the beginning or end of Moksha  
In the attractive Samsara, even if we swim in the sea of Karmas, we cannot find it  
Even upon collectively searching at ‘Swarga’ and other worlds, it cannot be found  
When there is intense devotion for Keshava, eternal beatitude can be found in the mind\!  
Even upon studying the various Shastras, we do not get the path to the supreme  
Even upon searching and worshipping many other inferior Gods, it cannot be got  
Saying that the pristine Lord of SreeVenkatagiri resides within, as revealed by Guru,  
one can find the right knowledge and the Supreme within oneself\!
