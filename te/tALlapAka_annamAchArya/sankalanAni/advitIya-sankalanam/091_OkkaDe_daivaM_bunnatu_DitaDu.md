**Okkaḍē Daivaṃ Bunnatu Ḍitaḍu**  
ऒक्कडे दैवं बुन्नतु डितडु  
तक्किन तलपुलु तप्पु दॆरुवुलु\!॥ ऒक्कडे ॥  
पुरुषुल कॆल्ला बुरुषोत्तमुडु  
सुरेन्द्रादुलकु सुरेन्द्रुडु  
हरुनिकि नजुनिकि नव्वलि मूरिति  
हरि यितडे पर मात्मुडु\!॥ ऒक्कडे ॥  
वेदान्तम्बुल वेद्युडीतडु  
आदिकि नादिय नन नितडे  
मेदिनि नरुलकु मेदि नीश्वरुडु  
येदॆस जाचिन नीश्वरु डितडे\!॥ ऒक्कडे ॥  
यॆक्कुवुलकॆल्ल नॆक्कुव यीतडु  
मक्कुव मरुनिकि मरुडितडु  
यिक्कड श्री वेङ्कटेशुडै मिगुल  
पक्कन निदिवो प्रत्यक्षमितडु\!॥ ऒक्कडे ॥  
**okkaḍē daivaṃ bunnatu ḍitaḍu**  
**takkina talapulu tappu deruvulu\!.. okkaḍē ..**  
**puruṣula kellā buruṣōttamuḍu**  
**surēndrādulaku surēndruḍu**  
**haruniki najuniki navvali mūriti**  
**hari yitaḍē para mātmuḍu\!.. okkaḍē ..**  
**vēdāntambula vēdyuḍītaḍu**  
**ādiki nādiyu nana nitaḍē**  
**mēdini narulaku mēdi nīśvaruḍu**  
**yēdesa jūcina nīśvaru ḍitaḍē\!.. okkaḍē ..**  
**yekkuvulakella nekkuva yītaḍu**  
**makkuva maruniki maruḍitaḍu**  
**yikkaḍa śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍai migula**  
**pakkana nidivō pratyakṣamitaḍu\!.. okkaḍē ..**  
He is the one and only Lord, the highest one He is\!  
Other thoughts lead to the wrong ways\!  
For all beings, He is the Supreme Being  
For Indra and other heavenly beings, He is the supreme Lord  
Even above Shiva and Brahma, He is the supreme deity  
He is Hari\! The supreme soul\!  
He is the one to be know from the Vedanta  
He is the one who is to be referred to as the Origin of all origins  
For the earthly beings, He is the Lord of the Earth  
In which ever manner you perceive, He alone is the Lord\!  
Among the exalted, He is the most exalted  
For Manmatha the love-god, He himself is the ‘Manmatha’  
Here, Sree Venkatesha, the supreme one,  
has manifested Himself within reach\!
