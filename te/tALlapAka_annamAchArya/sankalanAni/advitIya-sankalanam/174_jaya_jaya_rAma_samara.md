**Jaya Jaya Rāma Samara**  
जय जय राम समर विजय राम  
भयहर निज भक्त पारीण रामा ॥॥ जय ॥  
जलधि बन्धिञ्चिन सौमित्रि रामा  
सॆलविल्लु विरचिन सीतारामा  
अल सुग्रीवु नेलिन अयोध्य रामा  
कलिगि यज्ञमु काचॆ कौसल्य रामा ॥॥ जय ॥  
अरि रावणान्तक आदित्यकुल रामा  
गुरु वौनुलनु गाचे कोदण्ड रामा  
धर नहल्य पालिटि दशरथ रामा  
हरुराणि नुतुल लोकाभि रामा ॥॥ जय ॥  
अति प्रतापमुल मायामृगान्तक रामा  
सुत कुशलव प्रिय सुगुण रामा  
वितत महिमल श्री वेङ्कटाद्रि रामा  
मतिलोन बायनि मनुवंश रामा ॥॥ जय ॥  
**jaya jaya rāma samara vijaya rāma**  
**bhayahara nija bhakta pārīṇa rāmā .... jaya ..**  
**jaladhi bandhiñcina saumitri rāmā**  
**selavillu viracina sītārāmā**  
**ala sugrīvu nēlina ayōdhya rāmā**  
**kaligi yajñamu kāce kausalya rāmā .... jaya ..**  
**ari rāvaṇāntaka ādityakula rāmā**  
**guru maunulanu gācē kōdaṇḍa rāmā**  
**dhara nahalya pāliṭi daśaratha rāmā**  
**harurāṇi nutula lōkābhi rāmā .... jaya ..**  
**ati pratāpamula māyāmṛgāntaka rāmā**  
**suta kuśalava priya suguṇa rāmā**  
**vitata mahimala śrī vēṅkaṭādri rāmā**  
**matilōna bāyani manuvaṃśa rāmā .... jaya ..**  
Praises unto Him, the victor in the battlefield, Rama\!  
The one who vanquishes fear and protects devotees, Rama\!\!  
The one who took the ocean into captivity, Soumitri Rama  
The one who broke the reputed bow, Sita Rama  
The one who reigned over Sugriva, Ayodhya Rama  
The one who guarded the yagna ritual, Kausalya Rama\!  
The one who killed the enemy Ravana, Aditya kula Rama  
The one who is the savior of sages, Kodanda Rama  
The one who blessed Ahalya, Dasharatha Rama  
The one who is praised eternally by the consort of Shiva, that Lokabhi Rama\!  
The valiant one who killed the magical deer, Rama  
The one loved by Kusha and Lava, Suguna Rama  
The performer of great miracles, Sri Venkatadri Rama  
The one who is eternally inseparable from our thoughts, Manu Vamsha Rama\!\!
