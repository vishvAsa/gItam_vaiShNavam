**Ē Kulajuḍēmi Yevvaḍainanēmi**  
ए कुलजुडेमि यॆव्वडैननेमि  
आकड नातडॆ हरिनॆऱिगिनवाडु जीवुडु ॥॥ ए कुलजु ॥  
परगिन सत्यसम्पन्नुडैन वाडे  
परनिन्दसेय तत्परुडु कानि वाडु  
अरुदैन भूतदयानिधि यगुवाडे  
परुलु तानेयनि भाविञ्चुवाडु ॥॥ ए कुलजु ॥  
निर्मलुडै यात्मनियति गलुगुवाडे  
धर्मतत्पर बुद्धि तगिलिनवाडु  
कर्ममार्गमुलु कडवनिवाडे  
मर्ममै हरिभक्ति मऱवनिवाडु ॥॥ ए कुलजु ॥  
जगतिपै हितमुगा चरियिञ्चुवाडे  
पगलेक मतिलोन ब्रदिकिनवाडु  
तॆगि सकलमु नात्म तॆलिसिनवाडे  
तगिलि वॆङ्कटेशु दासुडयिनवाडु ॥॥ ए कुलजु ॥  
**ē kulajuḍēmi yevvaḍainanēmi**  
**ākaḍa nātaḍe harineṟiginavāḍu jīvuḍu .... ē kulaju ..**  
**paragina satyasampannuḍaina vāḍē**  
**paranindasēya tatparuḍu kāni vāḍu**  
**arudaina bhūtadayānidhi yaguvāḍē**  
**parulu tānēyani bhāviñcuvāḍu .... ē kulaju ..**  
**nirmaluḍai yātmaniyati galuguvāḍē**  
**dharmatatpara buddhi tagilinavāḍu**  
**karmamārgamulu kaḍavanivāḍē**  
**marmamai haribhakti maṟavanivāḍu .... ē kulaju ..**  
**jagatipai hitamugā cariyiñcuvāḍē**  
**pagalēka matilōna bradikinavāḍu**  
**tegi sakalamu nātma telisinavāḍē**  
**tagili veṅkaṭēśu dāsuḍayinavāḍu .... ē kulaju ..**  
To what caste one belongs, how does it matter? Whoever it is, how does it matter?  
He should be the one who realized Hari\!  
(He should be…….)  
The one who possess the treasure of Truth,  
who does not engage in decrying others,  
who has the unique quality of compassion for all living creatures,  
who sees all others as himself\!  
The one who is pure and shows self restraint,  
who remains steadfast to righteousness,  
who never transgresses the path of Karma,  
who never forgets profound devotion to Hari\!  
The one who lives in the world with benevolence,  
who lives with a mind free from malice,  
who knows everything as the ‘Self’,  
who remains a steadfast servant of Sree Venkatesha\!
