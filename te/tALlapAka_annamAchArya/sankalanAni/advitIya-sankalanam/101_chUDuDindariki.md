**Cūḍuḍindariki**  
चूडु डिन्दरिकि सुलभुडु हरि \-  
तोडु नीडयगु दॊरमुनि यितडु ॥॥ चूडु ॥  
कैवल्यमुनकु गनकपु ता पल-  
त्रोवै श्रुतुलकु दुदि पदमै  
पावन मॊकरूपमै विरजकु  
नावै यन्नाडिदॆ यितडु ॥॥ चूडु ॥  
कापाडग लोकमुलकु सूज्ञान \-  
दीपमै जगतिकि तेजमै  
पापा लडपग भवपयोधुलकु  
तॆपै यन्नाडिदे यितडु ॥॥ चूडु ॥  
करुणानिधि रङ्गपतिकि गाञ्ची \-  
वरुनकु वेङ्कटगिरिपतिकि  
निरति नहोबलनृ केसरिकि त-  
त्परुडगु शठगोप मुनि यितडु ॥॥ चूडु ॥  
**cūḍu ḍindariki sulabhuḍu hari \-**  
**tōḍu nīḍayagu doramuni yitaḍu .... cūḍu ..**  
**kaivalyamunaku ganakapu tā pala-**  
**trōvai śrutulaku dudi padamai**  
**pāvana mokarūpamai virajaku**  
**nāvai yunnāḍide yitaḍu .... cūḍu ..**  
**kāpāḍaga lōkamulaku sūjñāna \-**  
**dīpamai jagatiki tējamai**  
**pāpā laḍapaga bhavapayōdhulaku**  
**tepai yunnāḍidē yitaḍu .... cūḍu ..**  
**karuṇānidhi raṅgapatiki gāñcī \-**  
**varunaku vēṅkaṭagiripatiki**  
**nirati nahōbalanṛ kēsariki ta-**  
**tparuḍagu śaṭhagōpa muni yitaḍu .... cūḍu ..**  
Look\! He is easily approachable by all\!  
Hari is the constant companion of this Lordly Sage\!  
Being the golden paved path to eternal beatitude,  
being the final authority of the Vedas,  
being the sole personification of purity,  
being the boat to Viraja, here he is\!  
To save the worlds, being a brilliant lamp of true knowledge,  
and filling the worlds with effulgence,  
To absolve the sins of the ocean of Samsara,  
being the raft, here he is\!  
The abode of compassion the Lord of Srirangam,  
Lord Varada of Kanchi, Lord of the Venkata hill  
eternally, Lord Narasimha of Ahobilam,  
these being his supreme goal, Here he is, Sri Satakopa Muni\!  
This is a Sankirtana on Sri Adi Van Satakopa Yateendra Mahadesikan,  
the first Jeer of Ahobilam mutt, who was also Annamacharyas  
preceptor.
