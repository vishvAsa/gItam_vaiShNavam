**Manujuḍai Puṭṭi Manujuni**  
मनुजुडै पुट्टि मनुजुनि सेविञ्चि  
अनुदिनमुनु दुःख मन्दनेला ॥॥ मनु ॥  
जुट्टॆडु कडुपुकै चॊरनिचोट्लुचॊच्चि  
पट्टॆडु कूटिकै बतिमालि  
पुट्टिन चोटिके पॊरलि मनसुवॆट्टि  
वट्टि लम्पटमु वदलनेरडु गान ॥॥ मनु ॥  
अन्दरिलो पुट्टि अन्दरिलो पॆरिगि  
अन्दरि रूपमु लटु तानै  
अन्दमैन श्री वेङ्कटाद्रीशु सेविञ्चि  
अन्दरानि पदमु अन्दॆनटुगान ॥॥ मनु ॥  
**manujuḍai puṭṭi manujuni sēviñci**  
**anudinamunu duḥkha mandanēlā .... manu ..**  
**juṭṭeḍu kaḍupukai coranicōṭlucocci**  
**paṭṭeḍu kūṭikai batimāli**  
**puṭṭina cōṭikē porali manasuveṭṭi**  
**vaṭṭi lampaṭamu vadalanēraḍu gāna .... manu ..**  
**aṃdarilō puṭṭi aṃdarilō perigi**  
**aṃdari rūpamu laṭu tānai**  
**aṃdamaina śrī vēṅkaṭādrīśu sēviñci**  
**aṃdarāni padamu aṃdenaṭugāna .... manu ..**  
Being born as a human, serving another human\!  
Why endure that pain constantly?  
For a small stomach, going to forbidden places,  
pleading for a handful of food \!  
We become slaves of the mind, for the sake of this world,  
as we cannot let go of our attachments \!  
Being born within everybody, growing up within them,  
HE assumes all forms\!  
It is only by serving glorious Lord of SreeVenkata hill,  
That the unattainable can be attained\!
