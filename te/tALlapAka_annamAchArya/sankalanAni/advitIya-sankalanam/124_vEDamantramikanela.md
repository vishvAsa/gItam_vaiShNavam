**Veḍamantramikanēla**  
वॆडमन्त्रमिकनेल वेरु वॆल्लङ्कुलुनेल  
पुडमि धरुडु माकु भुवनौषधमु ॥॥ वॆडमन्त्र ॥  
हरि यच्युतायण्टे नणुगु बापमुलु  
नरसिंह यनि यण्टे नाटिन दुःखमुलु मानु  
पुरुषोत्तमा यण्टे बुण्ड्लुबूचुलु मानु  
परवौषध मीतडे पाटिम्प माकु ॥॥ वॆडमन्त्र ॥  
वासुदेव यनि यण्टे वदलु बन्धमु लॆल्ला  
वासिकि गृष्णायण्टे वन्दल रोगालु मानु  
श्री सतीश यनि यण्टे चिन्त लिन्नियनु मानु  
गासि दीर नित डेपो घन दिव्यौषधमु ॥॥ वॆडमन्त्र ॥  
गोविन्दा यनि यण्टे गूडुनु सम्पदलु  
यीवल माधव यण्टे निहमु बरमु जेरु  
देवनारायण यण्टे देहमु सुखियै यण्डु  
श्री वेङ्कटेशुडे माकु सिद्धौषधमु ॥॥ वॆडमन्त्र ॥  
**veḍamantramikanēla vēru vellaṅkulunēla**  
**puḍami dharuḍu māku bhuvanauṣadhamu .. veḍamantra ..**  
**hari yacyutāyaṇṭē naṇugu bāpamulu**  
**narasiṃha yani yaṇṭē nāṭina duḥkhamulu mānu**  
**puruṣōttamā yaṇṭē buṇḍlubūculu mānu**  
**paramauṣadha mītaḍē pāṭimpa māku ..veḍamantra ..**  
**vāsudēva yani yaṇṭē vadalu bandhamu lellā**  
**vāsiki gṛṣṇāyaṇṭē vandala rōgālu mānu**  
**śrī satīśa yani yaṇṭē cinta linniyunu mānu**  
**gāsi dīra nita ḍēpō ghana divyauṣadhamu .. veḍamantra ..**  
**gōvindā yani yaṇṭē gūḍunu sampadalu**  
**yīvala mādhava yaṇṭē nihamu baramu jēru**  
**dēvanārāyaṇa yaṇṭē dēhamu sukhiyai yaṇḍu**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍē māku siddhauṣadhamu .. veḍamantra**  
Why any other mantra? Why any other means?  
The Lord who lifted the Earth, He alone is the Universal medicine\!  
“Hari\! Achyuta\!” if we say, all sins are suppressed  
“Narasimha” if we say, the deep rooted sorrows will go  
“Purushottama” if we say, all bodily sores, and fears will heal  
He alone is the supreme medicine, if we follow\!  
“Vasudeva” if we say, all attachments are gone  
“Krishna” if we say, hundreds of diseases will heal  
“Sree-Sati Isha” if we say, all worries will vanish  
Totally He alone is our great divine medicine\!  
“Govinda” if we say, wealth accumulates  
“Madhava” if we say here, we can reach the Supreme  
“Deva\! Narayana\!” if we say, the body shall remain comfortable  
Sree Venkatesha alone is for us the remedial medicine\!\!\!
