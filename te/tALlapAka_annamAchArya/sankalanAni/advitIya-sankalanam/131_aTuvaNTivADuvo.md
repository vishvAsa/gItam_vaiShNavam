**Aṭuvaṇṭivāḍuvō**  
अटुवण्टिवाडुवो हरिदासुडु  
अटमटालु विडिचि नातडे सुखि ॥॥ अटुवण्टि ॥  
तिट्टेटि माटलुनु दीविञ्चे माटलुनु  
अट्टे सरेनि तलचिनातडे सुखि  
पट्टि चम्पेवेळनु पट्टमुगट्टेवेळ  
अट्टु निट्टु चलिञ्चनि यातडे सुखि ॥॥ अटुवण्टि ॥  
चेरिपञ्चदारिडिन जेदु दॆच्चिपॆट्टिनानु  
आरगिञ्चि तनिवॊन्दे यतडे सुखि  
तेरकाण्ड्लजाचिन तॆगरानि चुट्टमुल  
नारय सरिगाजाचे यातडे सुखि ॥॥ अटुवण्टि ॥  
पॊन्दि पुण्यमु वच्चिन पॊरिबापमु वच्चिन  
नन्दलि फलमॊल्लनि यातडे सुखि  
विन्दुगा श्री वेङ्कटाद्रिविभुनि दासुलजेरि  
अन्दरानि पदमन्दि नातडे सुखि ॥॥ अटुवण्टि ॥  
**aṭuvaṇṭivāḍuvō haridāsuḍu**  
**aṭamaṭālu viḍici nātaḍē sukhi .... aṭuvaṇṭi**  
**tiṭṭēṭi māṭalunu dīviñcē māṭalunu**  
**aṭṭē sarēni talacinātaḍē sukhi**  
**paṭṭi campēvēḷanu paṭṭamugaṭṭēvēḷa**  
**aṭṭu niṭṭu caliñcani yātaḍē sukhi .... aṭuvaṇṭi**  
**cēripañcadāriḍina jēdu deccipeṭṭinānu**  
**āragiñci tanivondē yataḍē sukhi**  
**tērakāṇḍlajūcina tegarāni cuṭṭamula**  
**nāraya sarigājūcē yātaḍē sukhi .... aṭuvaṇṭi**  
**pondi puṇyamu vaccina poribāpamu vaccina**  
**nandali phalamollani yātaḍē sukhi**  
**vindugā śrī vēṅkaṭādrivibhuni dāsulajēri**  
**aṃdarāni padamandi nātaḍē sukhi .... aṭuvaṇṭi**  
This is how a Hari-daasa should be\!  
One who lets go of dualities, he alone is happy\!  
Words of castigation, words of benediction  
one who takes them as equal, he alone is happy\!  
At times of threat for life, at times of attaining reward  
one who remains unmoved, he alone is happy\!  
When given sweet sugar, or when given something bitter  
one who eats and feels satisfied, he alone is happy\!  
Upon meeting a stranger, or a close relative  
one who sees them as equal, he alone is happy\!  
Upon attaining merits, upon getting sins  
one who does not accept the fruits thereof, he alone is happy\!  
Having joyously associated with the servants of the Lord of Sree,  
one who considers that state to be the highest, he alone is happy\!
