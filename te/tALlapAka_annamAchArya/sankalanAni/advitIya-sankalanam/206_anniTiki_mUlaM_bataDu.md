**Anniṭiki Mūlaṃ Bataḍu**  
अन्निटिकि मूलं बतडु  
वॆन्नुनि कण्टॆनु वेल्पुलु लेरु \!॥अन्नि॥  
पञ्च भूतमुल प्रपञ्च मूलमु  
मुञ्चिन ब्रह्मकु मूलमु  
पॊञ्चिन जीवुल पुट्टुगु मूलमु  
यॆञ्चग दैवमु यितडे काडा \!॥अन्नि॥  
वॆनुकॊनि पॊगडॆटि वेदाल मूलमु  
मुनुल तपमुलकु मूलमु  
घन यज्ञादुल कर्मपु मूलमु  
यॆनलेनि दैवमितडे काडा \!॥अन्नि॥  
अगपडि सुरलकु नमृत मूलमु  
मुगुरु मूर्तुलकु मूलमु  
नगु श्री वॆङ्कट नाथुडे मूलमु  
यॆगुव लोकपति यितडे काडा \!॥अन्नि॥  
**anniṭiki mūlaṃ bataḍu**  
**vennuni kaṇṭenu vēlpulu lēru \!..anni..**  
**pañca bhūtamula prapañca mūlamu**  
**muñcina brahmaku mūlamu**  
**poñcina jīvula puṭṭugu mūlamu**  
**yeñcaga daivamu yitaḍē kāḍā \!..anni..**  
**venukoni pogaḍeṭi vēdāla mūlamu**  
**munula tapamulaku mūlamu**  
**ghana yajñādula karmapu mūlamu**  
**yenalēni daivamitaḍē kāḍā \!..anni..**  
**agapaḍi suralaku namṛta mūlamu**  
**muguru mūrtulaku mūlamu**  
**nagu śrī veṅkaṭa nāthuḍē mūlamu**  
**yeguva lōkapati yitaḍē kāḍā \!..anni..**  
For everything, He is the origin  
But for Vennudu1, there are no other Gods\!  
For the five elements and the whole universe, being the origin,  
For the Primal Brahma, being the origin,  
For the birth of all Jivas, being the origin,  
To seek, is ‘He’ not the God\!  
For the Vedas that constantly praise(Him), being the origin,  
For the penance of the sages, being the basis,  
For the various works such as Yagnas, being the object of worship  
Is ‘He’ not the matchless God\!  
For the Devathas to perceive the amrutha, being the cause,  
For the divine trinity, being the Primal Cause\!  
He who is all this, that Sree Venkateshwara, is ‘The Origin’  
Is He not the Lord of the higher worlds\!  
         1\. Vennudu is Lord Krishna
