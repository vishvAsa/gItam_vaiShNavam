**Kaliyugambunaku Galadidiyē**  
कलियगम्बुनकु गलदिदिये  
वॆलसिन पञ्चमवेदमॆ कलिगॆ॥॥कलि॥  
बोधल हरि नुति पॊडमॆनु शूद्र  
स्साधनि कलिदोषमु मान्प  
राधामाधव रचन सकलजन  
साधुवेदमे जगमुन गलिगॆ ॥॥कलि॥  
परमगु वेदमु बहुळमु चदिविय  
हरिनॆरिगिन वा ररुदनुचु  
तिरुवामुडियै दिव्यमन्त्रमै  
वॆलसिन (?) पञ्चम वेदमॆ कलिगॆ॥॥कलि॥  
बिङ्कपु मनुजुलु पॆक्कुलु चदिविय  
सङ्कॆदीरदॆच्चट ननुचु  
सङ्कीर्तनमे सकललोकमुल  
वॆङ्कटेश्वरुनि वेदमॆ कलिगॆ॥॥कलि॥  
**kaliyugambunaku galadidiyē**  
**velasina pañcamavēdame kalige....kali..**  
**bōdhala hari nuti poḍamenu śūdra**  
**ssādhani kalidōṣamu mānpa**  
**rādhāmādhava racana sakalajana**  
**sādhuvēdamē jagamuna galige ....kali..**  
**paramagu vēdamu bahuḷamu cadiviyu**  
**harinerigina vā rarudanucu**  
**tiruvāmuḍiyai divyamantramai**  
**velasina (?) pañcama vēdame kalige....kali..**  
**biṅkapu manujulu pekkulu cadiviyu**  
**saṅkedīradeccaṭa nanucu**  
**saṅkīrtanamē sakalalōkamula**  
**veṅkaṭēśvaruni vēdame kalige....kali..**  
This is there for us in Kaliyuga\!  
It has manifested as the ‘Panchama Veda’\!  
It filled the mind with the knowledge of Hari  
to prevent the lowly practices of the evil Kali-yuga\!  
Preaching Radha-Madhava’s tattva  
it appeared in the world as the benevolent Veda\!  
Even after studying extensively the supreme Vedas  
those who can know Hari are rare\!  
So the Sankirtana appeared in the form of the Tiruvaimozi  
as the Divya-mantra and as the Panchama-Veda\!  
Even after studying greatly, the doubts of the rigid people  
can never be clarified\!  
So the Sankirtana filled all the worlds  
and appeared as Sree Venkateshwara’s Veda\!
