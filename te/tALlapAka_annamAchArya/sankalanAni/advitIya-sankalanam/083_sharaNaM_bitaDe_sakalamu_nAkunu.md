**Śaraṇaṃ Bitaḍē Sakalamu Nākunu**  
शरणं बितडे सकलमु नाकुनु  
वॆरपुन मनसा वॆतकवॊ यितनि ॥॥शरणं॥  
अभयं बॊसगेटि यतडॆव्वडु मुनु  
इभ रक्षकु डतडॆव्वडु  
उभय विभूतुल कॊडयं डॆव्वडु  
प्रभुवतडे ना पालि देवुडु॥॥शरणं॥  
शरणागतुलकु सरिदापॆव्वडु  
यिरवुग श्रीपति यॆव्वडु  
अरि दुष्ट दैत्य हन्तकु डॆव्वडु  
वरमुनु नतडॆ ना पालि देवुडु ॥॥शरणं॥  
आदि शङ्ख चक्रायधु डॆव्वडु  
ये दॆस बूर्णुडॆव्वडु  
वेदमयडु श्री वेङ्कटपतियै  
पादाय निदॆ ना पालिदेवुडु ॥॥शरणं॥  
**śaraṇaṃ bitaḍē sakalamu nākunu**  
**verapuna manasā vetakavo yitani ....śaraṇaṃ..**  
**abhayaṃ bosagēṭi yataḍevvaḍu munu**  
**ibha rakṣaku ḍataḍevvaḍu**  
**ubhaya vibhūtula koḍayaṃ ḍevvaḍu**  
**prabhuvataḍē nā pāli dēvuḍu....śaraṇaṃ..**  
**śaraṇāgatulaku saridāpevvaḍu**  
**yiravuga śrīpati yevvaḍu**  
**ari duṣṭa daitya hantaku ḍevvaḍu**  
**varamunu nataḍe nā pāli dēvuḍu ....śaraṇaṃ..**  
**ādi śaṅkha cakrāyudhu ḍevvaḍu**  
**yē desa būrṇuḍevvaḍu**  
**vēdamayuḍu śrī vēṅkaṭapatiyai**  
**pādāya nide nā pālidēvuḍu ....śaraṇaṃ..**  
He alone is the refuge and everything for me  
Wisely O mind, search for Him\!  
The one who bestows refuge, who is He?  
Previously the savior of the elephant, who is He?  
The ultimate Lord of the Ubhaya Vibhutis, who else is He?  
He alone is the Lord, and my worshippable God\!  
The one who is close to Sharanagatas, who is He?  
The eternal Lord of Sree, who else is He?  
The destroyer of the evil demons, who is He?  
As a boon to me, He alone is my Lord\!\!  
The primal one who wields the Shanka-Chakra weapons, who is He?  
The one who is perfect is every respect, who else is He?  
The one who fills the Vedas, SreeVenkatapathi,  
forever, He alone is my Lord\!\!\!
