**Marali Marali Jayamaṅgaḷamu**  
मरलि मरलि जयमङ्गळमु  
सॊरदिनिच्चलुनु शुभमङ्गळमु ॥॥मरलि॥  
कमला रमणिकि कमलाक्षुनकुनु  
ममतल जयजयमङ्गळमु  
अमरजननिकिनिअमरवन्द्युनकु  
सुमुहूर्तमुतो शुभमङ्गळमु ॥॥मरलि॥  
जलधिकन्यकुनु जलधिशायिकिनि  
मलयचुनु शुभमङ्गळमु  
कलिमिकान्त काकलिकि विभुनिकिनि  
सुळुवुल यारति शुभमङ्गळमु ॥॥मरलि॥  
चित्तजु तल्लिकि श्री वेङ्कटपतिकि  
मत्तिल्लिन जय मङ्गळमु  
यित्तल नत्तल यिरुवुरकौगिटि  
जॊत्तुल रतुलकु शुभमङ्गळमु ॥॥मरलि॥  
**marali marali jayamaṅgaḷamu**  
**soradiniccalunu śubhamaṅgaḷamu ....marali..**  
**kamalā ramaṇiki kamalākṣunakunu**  
**mamatala jayajayamaṅgaḷamu**  
**amarajananikiniamaravandyunaku**  
**sumuhūrtamutō śubhamaṅgaḷamu ....marali..**  
**jaladhikanyakunu jaladhiśāyikini**  
**malayucunu śubhamaṅgaḷamu**  
**kalimikānta kākaliki vibhunikini**  
**suḷuvula yārati śubhamaṅgaḷamu ....marali..**  
**cittaju talliki śrī vēṅkaṭapatiki**  
**mattillina jaya maṅgaḷamu**  
**yittala nattala yiruvurakaugiṭi**  
**jottula ratulaku śubhamaṅgaḷamu ....marali..**  
Again and again, victory and benedictions to You\!  
Constantly and eternally, propitious benedictions to You\!  
To the lotus lady and to the lotus eyed Lord,  
affectionately, many benedictions\!  
To the divine mother and to the Lord praised by the divinities,  
at this auspicious hour, many benedictions\!  
To the daughter of the ocean, to the Lord who reclines on the ocean,  
we offer many benedictions\!  
To the Goddess of wealth, and to Her Lord,  
with the waving of lamps, many auspicious benedictions\!  
To the mother of Manmatha, and to Sree Venkatapathi,  
over and over again, many benedictions\!  
For their loving embraces and amorous love making,  
for now and ever, many propitious benedictions\!
