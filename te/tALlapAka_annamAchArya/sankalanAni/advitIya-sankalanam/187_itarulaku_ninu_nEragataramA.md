**Itarulaku Ninu Neragataramā**  
इतरुलकु निनु नॆऱगतरमा  
सतत सत्यव्रतुलु सम्पूर्णमोहविर-  
हितुलॆऱुगुदुरु निनु निन्दिरारमण ॥॥इत॥  
नारीकटाक्षपटुनाराचभयरहित \-  
शूरुलॆऱुगुदुरु निनु चूचेटि चूपु  
फॆूरसंसार सङ्कुलपरिच्छेदुलगु-  
धीरुलॆऱुगुदुरु नीदिव्यविग्रहमु ॥॥इत॥  
रागभोगविदूररञ्जितात्मुलु महा-  
भागुलॆरुगुदुरु निनु प्रणतिञ्चु विधमु  
आगमोक्तप्रकारभिगम्युलु महा-  
योगुलॆऱुगुदुरु नीवुण्डेटिवुनिकि ॥॥इत॥  
परमभागवत पदपद्मसेवानिजा-  
भरणुलॆऱुगुदुरु नी पलिकेटिपलुकु  
परगुनित्यानन्दपरिपूर्ण मानस-  
स्थिरुलॆऱुगुदुरु निनु तिरुवेङ्कटेश ॥॥इत॥  
**itarulaku ninu neṟagataramā**  
**satata satyavratulu sampūrṇamōhavira-**  
**hituleṟuguduru ninu nindirāramaṇa ....ita..**  
**nārīkaṭākṣapaṭunārācabhayarahita \-**  
**śūruleṟuguduru ninu cūcēṭi cūpu**  
**ghōrasaṃsāra saṅkulaparicchēdulagu-**  
**dhīruleṟuguduru nīdivyavigrahamu ....ita..**  
**rāgabhōgavidūrarañjitātmulu mahā-**  
**bhāguleruguduru ninu praṇatiñcu vidhamu**  
**āgamōktaprakārabhigamyulu mahā-**  
**yōguleṟuguduru nīvuṇḍēṭivuniki ....ita..**  
**paramabhāgavata padapadmasēvānijā-**  
**bharaṇuleṟuguduru nī palikēṭipaluku**  
**paragunityānandaparipūrṇa mānasa-**  
**sthiruleṟuguduru ninu tiruvēṅkaṭēśa ....ita..**  
Is it possible for others to know You?  
Those who constantly adhere to the vow of truthfulness, those who  
are totally dispassionate, only they can know You, O Lord of Lakshmi\!  
Those who do not get swayed by the charms of women, the fearless  
and the courageous, they alone can know how to perceive You\!  
Those brave ones who break the shackles of the frightful Samsara,  
only they can know Your Divya Vigraham\!  
Those who are far from desires and pleasures, those elevated souls,  
those great devotees, they alone know how to praise You\!  
Those great Yogis who follow the injunctions of the Agamas  
only they can realize Your presence\!  
Those true gem like people who worship the lotus feet  
of Parama Bhagavathas, they alone know Your divine speech\!  
Those resolute ones who are eternally supremely blissful, those  
completely perfect ones, only they can perceive You, O TiruVenkatesha\!
