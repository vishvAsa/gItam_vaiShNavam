**Itaḍokaḍē Sarvēśvaruḍu**  
इतडॊकडे सर्वेश्वरुडु  
सितकमलाक्षुडु श्री वेङ्कटेशुडु ॥॥इत॥  
परमयोगुलकु भावनिधानमु  
अरय निन्द्रादुल कैश्वर्यमु  
गरिम गॊल्लॆतल कौगिटि सौख्यमु  
सिरुलॊसगे ई श्री वेङ्कटेशुडु ॥॥इत॥  
कलिगि यशोदकु कन्नमाणिकमु  
तलचिन करिकिनि तगुदिक्कु  
अलद्रौपदिकिनि आपद्भन्दुडु  
चॆलरेगिन ई श्री वेङ्कटेशुडु ॥॥इत॥  
तगिलिन मुनुलकु तपमुल सत्फलमु  
मुगुरु वेल्पुलकु मूलमु  
ऒगि नलमेल्मङ्ग कॊनरिन पति यितडु  
जिगिमिञ्चिन ई श्री वेङ्कटेशुडु ॥॥इत॥  
**itaḍokaḍē sarvēśvaruḍu**  
**sitakamalākṣuḍu śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍu ....ita..**  
**paramayōgulaku bhāvanidhānamu**  
**araya nindrādula kaiśvaryamu**  
**garima golletala kaugiṭi saukhyamu**  
**sirulosagē ī śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍu ....ita..**  
**kaligi yaśōdaku kannamāṇikamu**  
**talacina karikini tagudikku**  
**aladraupadikini āpadbhanduḍu**  
**celarēgina ī śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍu ....ita..**  
**tagilina munulaku tapamula satphalamu**  
**muguru vēlpulaku mūlamu**  
**ogi nalamēlmaṅga konarina pati yitaḍu**  
**jigimiñcina ī śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍu ....ita..**  
He alone is the Supreme Lord of all\!  
The lotus eyed Sree Venkateshwara\!  
He is the treasure in the thoughts of exalted Sages,  
For heavenly beings like Indra , He is immense wealth,  
For the blessed cowherds, He is like the comfort of an embrace,  
The one who bestows all round prosperity, is He, Lord Venkateshwara\!  
Being there for mother Yashoda, He is her precious gem  
To the elephant king who contemplated on Him, He became the only means  
Previously, to the troubled Draupadi, He was the Soul-savior  
This valiant Lord, Sree Venkateshwara\!  
He is the benevolent result of hermits’ penance  
He is the origin of the divine trinity  
He is the compatible consort to Alamelumanga  
He is the supremely effulgent Lord Venkateshwara\!
