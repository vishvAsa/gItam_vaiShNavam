**Nadulollavu Nasnānamu**  
नदुलॊल्लवु नास्नानमु कडु \-  
सदरमु नाकीस्नानमु ॥॥ नदु ॥  
इरुवङ्कल नीयेचिन मुद्रलु  
धरियिञ्चुटॆ ना स्नानमु  
धरपै नीनिज दासुल दासुल \-  
चरण धूळि ना स्नानमु ॥॥ नदु ॥  
तलपुलोन निनुदलचिन वारल  
दलचुटी ना स्नानमु  
वलनुग निनु गनुवारल श्री पाद \-  
जलमु ले ना स्नानमु ॥॥ नदु ॥  
परम भगवत पादाम्बुजमुल \-  
दरुशनमे ना स्नानमु  
तिरुवेङ्कटगिरि देव नी कथा \-  
स्मरणमे ना स्नानमु ॥॥ नदु ॥  
**nadulollavu nasnānamu kaḍu \-**  
**sadaramu nā kīsnānamu .... nadu ..**  
**iruvaṅkala nīyēcina mudralu**  
**dhariyiñcuṭe nā snānamu**  
**dharapai nīnija dāsula dāsula \-**  
**caraṇa dhūḷi nā snānamu .... nadu ..**  
**talapulōna ninudalacina vārala**  
**dalacuṭī nā snānamu**  
**valanuga ninu ganuvārala śrī pāda \-**  
**jalamu lē nā snānamu .... nadu ..**  
**parama bhagavata pādāmbujamula \-**  
**daruśanamē nā snānamu**  
**tiruvēṅkaṭagiri dēva nī kathā \-**  
**smaraṇamē nā snānamu .... nadu ..**  
A bath in the rivers is not acceptable\!  
Very satisfying is for me ‘this’ kind of bath\!  
Wearing Your divine mudras on both shoulders  
is alone my bath\!  
On this earth, those true servants of Your servants,  
touching the dust of their feet, is my bath\!  
Thinking of those who contemplate on You,  
this alone is my bath\!  
Those who can perceive You with devotion,  
partaking their Sree-pada Jala1 is my bath\!  
The mere sight of the lotus feet of Parama-Bhagavathas  
is alone my bath\!  
O Tiruvenkatagiri Deva\! Thinking about Your glories  
is alone my bath\!  
1.Sree pada jala is the water that has been used to wash the feet of  
devotees
