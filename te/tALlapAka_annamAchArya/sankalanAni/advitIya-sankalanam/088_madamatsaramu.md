**Madamatsaramu**  
मदमत्सरमु लेक मनसु पेदैपो  
पदरिन यासलवाडु वो वैष्णवुडु ॥॥मद॥  
इट्टुनट्टु दिरिगाडि येमैना जॆडनाडि  
पॆट्टरण्टा बोयरण्टा बॆक्कुलाडि  
यॆट्टिवारिनैना दूरि यॆव्वरिनैना जेरि  
वट्टि यासलबडनि वाडुवो वैष्णवुडु ॥॥मद॥  
गडनकॊरकु जिक्कि कौमुक विद्यल जॊक्कि  
निडिवि नेमैना गनि निक्कि निक्कि  
वॊडलिगुणमुतोड वुदुट विद्यल जाल  
वडदाकि बडलनि वाडुवो वैष्णवुडु ॥॥मद॥  
आवल वॊरुल जॆडनडग विनिविनि  
चेवमीरि यॆव्वरिनि जॆडनाडक  
कोविदु श्री वेङ्कटेशु गॊलिचि पॆद्दलकृप  
वाविवर्तनगल वाडुवो वैष्णवुडु ॥॥मद॥  
**madamatsaramu lēka manasu pēdaipō**  
**padarina yāsalavāḍu vō vaiṣṇavuḍu ....mada..**  
**iṭṭunaṭṭu dirigāḍi yēmainā jeḍanāḍi**  
**peṭṭaraṇṭā bōyaraṇṭā bekkulāḍi**  
**yeṭṭivārinainā dūri yevvarinainā jēri**  
**vaṭṭi yāsalabaḍani vāḍuvō vaiṣṇavuḍu ....mada..**  
**gaḍanakoraku jikki kaumuka vidyala jokki**  
**niḍivi nēmainā gani nikki nikki**  
**voḍaliguṇamutōḍa vuduṭa vidyala jāla**  
**vaḍadāki baḍalani vāḍuvō vaiṣṇavuḍu ....mada..**  
**āvala vorula jeḍanaḍaga vinivini**  
**cēvamīri yevvarini jeḍanāḍaka**  
**kōvidu śrī vēṅkaṭēśu golici peddalakṛpa**  
**vāvivartanagala vāḍuvō vaiṣṇavuḍu ....mada..**  
One whose mind is free from ego and envy,  
One who reproves all desires, he alone is a Vaishnava\!  
One who does not wander about, who denounces everything,  
who has no complaints that he has not been offered anything to eat or drink,  
who does not mingle with anyone and everyone,  
who is not attracted to ephemeral desires, he alone is a Vaishnava\!  
One who does not get caught in money earning spree, one who does not enjoy lustful ways  
who does not aspire for everything that he sees,  
who does not expend his energies in bodily pleasures and enjoyment,  
he alone is a Vaishnava\!  
Even after having repeatedly heard others speak ill of someone,  
one who does not indulge in the same,  
one who worships Sree Venkatesha, and who by the grace of enlightened ones  
has acquired good conduct, he alone is a Vaishnava\!
