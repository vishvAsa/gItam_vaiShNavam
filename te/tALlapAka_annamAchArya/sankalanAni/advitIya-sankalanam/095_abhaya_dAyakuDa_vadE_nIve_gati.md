**Abhaya Dāyakuḍa Vade Nīvē Gati**  
अभय दायकुड वदॆ नीवे गति  
यिभ रक्षक ननु निपुडु गाववे\!॥ अभय ॥  
भयहर दैतेय भञ्जन केशव  
जय जय नृसिंह सर्वेश्वरा  
नियतमु माकिदॆ नी पादमुले गति  
क्रियगा मम्मेलि किङ्क लुडुपवे\!॥ अभय ॥  
बन्ध विमोचन पाप विनाशन  
सिन्धुर वरदाश्रितरक्षा  
कन्धर वर्णुड गति नी नाममॆ  
अन्ध कारमुल नणचि मनुपवे\!॥ अभय ॥  
दैव शिखामणि तत चक्रायध  
श्री वॆङ्कटगिरि श्री रमणा  
सावधान नी शरण्यमे गति  
वेवेलकु ना विन्नप मिदिये\!॥ अभय ॥  
**abhaya dāyakuḍa vade nīvē gati**  
**yibha rakṣaka nanu nipuḍu gāvavē\!.. abhaya ..**  
**bhayahara daitēya bhañjana kēśava**  
**jaya jaya nṛsiṃha sarvēśvarā**  
**niyatamu mākide nī pādamulē gati**  
**kriyagā mammēli kiṅka luḍupavē\!.. abhaya ..**  
**bandha vimōcana pāpa vināśana**  
**sindhura varadāśritarakṣā**  
**kandhara varṇuḍa gati nī nāmame**  
**aṃdha kāramula naṇaci manupavē\!.. abhaya ..**  
**daiva śikhāmaṇi tata cakrāyudha**  
**śrī veṅkaṭagiri śrī ramaṇā**  
**sāvadhāna nī śaraṇyamē gati**  
**vēvēlaku nā vinnapa midiyē\!.. abhaya ..**  
O one who grants protection\! You are the only means  
O savior of the elephant king, do save me now\!  
O destroyer of fears, O vanquisher of demons, O Keshava\!  
Glory unto you O Narasimha, O lord of all\!  
Eternally for us, your feet alone are the means,  
Do rule over and release us from all suffering\!  
O reliever of attachments, O destroyer of sins  
O rescuer of the elephant, O protector of Sharanagatas  
O one with the colour of a cloud, Your name alone is the means,  
Dispelling the ignorance, do save me\!  
O Lord of all divinities, O wielder of the Chakra weapon  
O Lord of Venkatagiri, O Lord of Sree\!  
O Lord, for me Your refuge alone is the means,  
for thousands of times, this alone is my request\!
