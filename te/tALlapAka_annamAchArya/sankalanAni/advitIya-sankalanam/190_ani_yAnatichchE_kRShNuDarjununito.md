**Ani Yānaticce Kṛṣṇuḍarjununitō**  
अनि यानतिच्चॆ कृष्णुडर्जुनुनितो  
विनि यातनि भजिञ्चु विवेकमा ॥॥अनि॥  
भूमिलोनु चॊच्चि सर्वभूत प्राणुलनॆल्ल  
दीमपान मोचेटि देवुड नेनु  
कामिञ्चि सस्यमुलु कलिगिञ्चि चन्द्रुडनै  
तेमल पण्डिञ्चेटि देवुड नेनु ॥॥अनि॥  
दीपनाग्निनै जीवदेहमुल अन्नमुलु  
तीपुल नरगिञ्चेटि देवुड नेनु  
एपुन निन्दरिलोनि हृदयमुलोन नुन्दु  
दीपिन्तु तलपु मरपै देवुड नेनु ॥॥अनि॥  
वेदमु लन्निटिचेत वेदान्तवेत्तलचे  
अदि ने नॆरगतगिन आ देवुडनु  
श्रीदेवितो गूडि श्री वेङ्कटाद्रि मीद  
पादैन देवुडनु भाविञ्च नेनु ॥॥अनि॥  
**ani yānaticce kṛṣṇuḍarjununitō**  
**vini yātani bhajiñcu vivēkamā ....ani..**  
**bhūmilōnu cocci sarvabhūta prāṇulanella**  
**dīmapāna mōcēṭi dēvuḍa nēnu**  
**kāmiñci sasyamulu kaligiñci candruḍanai**  
**tēmala paṇḍiñcēṭi dēvuḍa nēnu ....ani..**  
**dīpanāgninai jīvadēhamula annamulu**  
**tīpula naragiñcēṭi dēvuḍa nēnu**  
**ēpuna nindarilōni hṛdayamulōna nundu**  
**dīpintu talapu marapai dēvuḍa nēnu ....ani..**  
**vēdamu lanniṭicēta vēdāntavēttalacē**  
**adi nē neragatagina ā dēvuḍanu**  
**śrīdēvitō gūḍi śrī vēṅkaṭādri mīda**  
**pādaina dēvuḍanu bhāviñca nēnu ....ani..**  
In this manner, Krishna advised Arjuna\!  
Listen and worship Him, O intellect\!  
“Descending onto the earth, sustaining all creatures and living beings,  
with fortitude, the Lord I am\!  
Out of love, nurturing the crops as the moon,  
sprinkling cool moon-beams, the Lord I am\!”  
“As the bright fire within the bodies of the Jivas  
digesting the food consumed, the Lord I am\!  
In full glory, residing in the hearts of everybody  
shining brightly as ‘the thought’ and ‘the oblivion’, the Lord I am”  
“Through the Vedas, and by the knowledgeable Vedantins  
the Primal one to be known, that Lord I am\!  
Together with SreeDevi, permanently residing at SreeVenkatadri,  
know me to be that Worshipable Lord\!\!”
