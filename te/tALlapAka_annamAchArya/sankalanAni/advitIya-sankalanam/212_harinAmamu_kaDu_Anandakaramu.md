**Harināmamu Kaḍu Ānandakaramu**  
हरिनाममु कडु आनन्दकरमु  
मरगवो, मरुगवो, मरुगवो मनसा ॥॥हरि॥  
नळिनाक्षुनि श्रीनाममु  
कलिदोष हरमु कैवल्यमु  
फलसारमु बहु बन्ध मोचनमु  
तलचवो, तलचवो, तलचवो मनसा ॥॥हरि॥  
नगधरुनाममु, नरक हरणमु  
जगदेक हितमु सम्मतमु  
सगुण निर्गुणमु, साक्षात्कारमु  
पॊगडवो, पॊगडवो, पॊगडवो मनसा ॥॥हरि॥  
कडगि श्री वेङ्कटपति नाममु  
बडि बडिसे सम्पत्करमु  
अडियालम्बिल अति सुखमूलमु  
तडववो, तडववो, तडववो मनसा ॥॥हरि॥  
**harināmamu kaḍu ānandakaramu**  
**maragavō, marugavō, marugavō manasā ....hari..**  
**naḷinākṣuni śrīnāmamu**  
**kalidōṣa haramu kaivalyamu**  
**phalasāramu bahu bandha mōcanamu**  
**talacavō, talacavō, talacavō manasā ....hari..**  
**nagadharunāmamu, naraka haraṇamu**  
**jagadēka hitamu sammatamu**  
**saguṇa nirguṇamu, sākṣātkāramu**  
**pogaḍavō, pogaḍavō, pogaḍavō manasā ....hari..**  
**kaḍagi śrī vēṅkaṭapati nāmamu**  
**baḍi baḍisē sampatkaramu**  
**aḍiyālambila ati sukhamūlamu**  
**taḍavavō, taḍavavō, taḍavavō manasā ....hari..**  
The name of ‘Hari’ is supremely blissful\!  
Take shelter, take shelter, take shelter O mind\!  
The divine name of the Lotus eyed one,  
the destroyer of the sins of ‘kali’, the beatitude bestowing one  
the fruitful one, the one which delivers from many attachments  
Contemplate, contemplate, contemplate O mind\!  
The name of the one who held up the mountain, the destroyer of hell  
the only one which is benevolent for the entire world, the agreeable one  
the one with attributes beyond the gunas, the manifested one  
Praise, praise, praise O mind\!  
The purifying name of Sree Venkateshwara,  
the one which instantly bestows prosperity,  
the great support here, the origin of supreme bliss,  
Seek, seek, seek O mind\!\!
