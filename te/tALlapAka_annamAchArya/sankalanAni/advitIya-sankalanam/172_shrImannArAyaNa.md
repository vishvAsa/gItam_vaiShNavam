**śrīmannārāyaṇa**  
श्रीमन्नारायण श्रीमन्नारायण  
श्रीमन्नारायण नी श्रीपादमे शरणु ॥॥श्रीमन्नारायण॥  
कमलासतीमुखकमल कमलहित  
कमलप्रिय कमलेक्षण  
कमलासनहित गरुडगमन श्री  
कमलनाभ नी पदकमलमे शरणु ॥॥श्रीमन्नारायण॥  
परमयोगिजन भागधेय श्री  
परम पुरुष परात्परा  
परमात्मा परमाणुरूप श्री  
तिरुवेङ्कटगिरि देवा शरणु ॥॥श्रीमन्नारायण॥  
**śrīmannārāyaṇa śrīmannārāyaṇa**  
**śrīmannārāyaṇa nī śrīpādamē śaraṇu ...śrīmannārāyaṇa..**  
**kamalāsatīmukhakamala kamalahita**  
**kamalapriya kamalēkṣaṇa**  
**kamalāsanahita garuḍagamana śrī**  
**kamalanābha nī padakamalamē śaraṇu ...śrīmannārāyaṇa..**  
**paramayōgijana bhāgadhēya śrī**  
**parama puruṣa parātparā**  
**paramātmā paramāṇurūpa śrī**  
**tiruvēṅkaṭagiri dēvā śaraṇu ....śrīmannārāyaṇa..**  
Sriman Narayana, Sriman Narayana  
Sriman Narayana, our refuge is at your divine feet\!  
O one who is the pleasure giver for Kamala-Sati’s lotus like face,  
O one who loves the lotus, O lotus eyed one\!  
O one who sustains Brahma, O one who moves mounted on Garuda  
O one with a lotus navel\! Our refuge is at your lotus-feet\!  
O one who is the destiny for the exalted Yogis,  
O Parama Purusha\! O one who is higher than the highest\!  
O Supreme self, also the one in the most subtle form,  
O Lord of Tiru Venkatagiri\! You are ‘THE’ refuge\!
