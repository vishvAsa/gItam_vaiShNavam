**Mēdinijīvulagāva**  
मेदिनि जीवुलगाव मेलुकोवय्या  
नीदये माकॆल्ल रक्ष निद्रमेलुकोवय्या ॥॥ मेदिनि ॥  
तगु गोपिकल कन्नु दामरलु विकसिञ्चॆ  
मिगुल सूर्यनेत्रुड मेलुकोवय्या  
तॆगुव राक्षसुलने तिमिरमु विरियग  
नॆगडिन परञ्ज्योति निद्र मेलुकोवय्या ॥॥ मेदिनि ॥  
घनदुरितपु नल्लगलुवल विकसिञ्चॆ  
मिनुकु शशिवर्णुड मेलुकोवय्या  
पनिवडि वेदालने पक्षुलॆल्लाबलुकग  
जनक याश्रितपारिजात मेलुकोवय्या ॥॥ मेदिनि ॥  
वरलक्ष्मी कुचचक्रवाकमु लॊण्डण्टिराय  
मॆलय दोषारहित मेलुकोवय्या  
पॊरसि नीवु नित्य भोगमुलु भोगिञ्च  
निरति श्री वॆङ्कटेश नेडु मेलुकोवय्या ॥॥ मेदिनि ॥  
**mēdini jīvulagāva mēlukōvayyā**  
**nīdayē mākella rakṣa nidramēlukōvayyā .... mēdini ..**  
**tagu gōpikala kannu dāmaralu vikasiñce**  
**migula sūryanētruḍa mēlukōvayyā**  
**teguva rākṣasulanē timiramu viriyaga**  
**negaḍina parañjyōti nidra mēlukōvayyā .... mēdini ..**  
**ghanaduritapu nallagaluvala vikasiñce**  
**minuku śaśivarṇuḍa mēlukōvayyā**  
**panivaḍi vēdālanē pakṣulellābalukaga**  
**janaka yāśritapārijāta mēlukōvayyā .... mēdini ..**  
**varalakṣmī kucacakravākamu loṇḍaṇṭirāya**  
**melayu dōṣārahita mēlukōvayyā**  
**porasi nīvu nitya bhōgamulu bhōgiñca**  
**nirati śrī veṅkaṭēśa nēḍu mēlukōvayyā .... mēdini .**  
Wake up\! To protect these Jivas on earth\!  
Your compassion is the protection for us. Wake up O Lord\!  
The lotus eyes of the Gopis are opening,  
Do wake up, O Lord ‘Surya-Netra’1\!  
As the stubborn demoniac darkness is spreading  
O ever increasing supreme effulgence, do wake up from Your sleep\!  
The great sins are blooming like dark water lilies  
O shining moon-complexioned one, do wake up\!  
The birds called ‘Vedas’ are eagerly calling  
O father of the worlds\! O Parijatha of the seekers\! Wake up\!  
Having been awakened by the touch of Lakshmi’s perfect  
Chakravaka2 like breasts, O flawless one, do wake up\!  
As you enjoy the eternal pleasures,  
O Lord Sree Venkatesha\! Do wake up today\!  
1.The one with Sun as the eyes  
2.Chakravaka is a beautiful bird
