**Telisinavāriki Dēvuṇḍitaḍē**  
तॆलिसिनवारिकि देवुण्डितडे  
वलवनि दुष्टुल वादमुलेल ॥॥तॆलि॥  
पुरुषुललोपल पुरुषोत्तमुडु  
नरुललोन नरनारायणुडु  
परदैवमुलकु परमेश्वरुडु  
वरुसमूढुल कॆव्वरोयितडु ॥॥तॆलि॥  
पलुब्रह्मलकुनु परब्रह्ममु  
मलयनीशुलकु महेशुडितडु  
इलनात्मललो निटुपरमात्मुडु  
खलुलकॆट्लुण्डुनो कानमु यितडु ॥॥तॆलि॥  
वेदम्बुललो वेदान्तवेद्युडु  
सोदिञ्चकरिगाचुचो नादिमूलमु  
यीदॆस श्री वेङ्कटेशुडिन्दरिकि  
गादिलिमतुलनु गैकॊनडितडु ॥॥तॆलि॥  
**telisinavāriki dēvuṇḍitaḍē**  
**valavani duṣṭula vādamulēla ....teli..**  
**puruṣulalōpala puruṣōttamuḍu**  
**narulalōna naranārāyaṇuḍu**  
**paradaivamulaku paramēśvaruḍu**  
**varusamūḍhula kevvarōyitaḍu ....teli..**  
**palubrahmalakunu parabrahmamu**  
**malayunīśulaku mahēśuḍitaḍu**  
**ilanātmalalō niṭuparamātmuḍu**  
**khalulakeṭluṇḍunō kānamu yitaḍu ....teli..**  
**vēdambulalō vēdāntavēdyuḍu**  
**sōdiñcakarigācucō nādimūlamu**  
**yīdesa śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍindariki**  
**gādilimatulanu gaikonaḍitaḍu ....teli..**  
For those who are learned, He is ‘THE’ Lord,  
With the evil who cavil, why argue?  
Within the beings, He is the Supreme Being  
Within (Naras) humans, He is ‘Narayana’  
For the supreme Gods, He is the Supreme Overlord\!  
To the utterly ignorant, what could He be?  
For the innumerable Brahmas\*, He is the ‘Supreme Brahman’  
For the numerous Shivas\* of the mountains, He is the ‘Maheswara’  
For these numerous individual selves here, He is the Supreme self  
For the lowly sinners, what He is, is beyond our comprehension\!  
He is the one to be known from the Vedas and Vedanta  
He is the ‘Adimoola’ who saved the pleading elephant\!  
In this manner, is Sree Venkatesha for everybody  
Ignorant minds, He will not accept\!  
\* A plural is used here because a new Shiva and a new Brahma appear  
in each creative cycle and there are an infinite number of them. The  
use of plural thus shows the impossibility of identity between these  
numerous deities and the Lord who is unique. The Lord is thus different  
from all other Gods and is superior to them.
