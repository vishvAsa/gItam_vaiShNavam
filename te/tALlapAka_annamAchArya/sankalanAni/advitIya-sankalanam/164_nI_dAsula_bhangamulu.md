**Nī Dāsula Bhaṅgamulu**  
नी दासुल भङ्गमुलु नीवु जुतुरा  
एदेनि चूचेवु नीकु ऎच्चरिञ्चवलॆना ॥॥ नी दासुल ॥  
पालसमुद्रमु मीद पव्वळिञ्चिनट्टि नीकु  
बेललै सुरलु मॊरवॆट्टिन यट्टु  
वेळतो मा मनवुलु विन्नविञ्चितिमि नीकु  
एल निद्दिरिञ्चेवु मम्मिट्टे रक्षिञ्चरादा ॥॥ नी दासुल ॥  
द्वारका नगरमुलो तग नॆत्तमाडे नीकु  
बीरान द्रौपदि मॊरवॆट्टिनयट्टु  
फॆूरपु राजसभल कुन्दि विन्नविञ्चितिमि  
येरीति पराकु नीकु इङ्का रक्षिञ्चरादा ॥॥ नी दासुल ॥  
ऎनसि वैकण्ठमुलो इन्दिरकूडुन्न नीकु  
पॆनगि गजमु मॊरवॆट्टिन यट्टु  
चनवुतो मा कोरिकॆ सारॆ विन्नविञ्चितिमि  
विनि श्री वेङ्कटेशुण्ड वेग रक्षिञ्चरादा ॥॥ नी दासुल ॥  
**nī dāsula bhaṅgamulu nīvu juturā**  
**ēdēni cūcēvu nīku eccariñcavalenā .... nī dāsula ..**  
**pālasamudramu mīda pavvaḷiñcinaṭṭi nīku**  
**bēlalai suralu moraveṭṭina yaṭṭu**  
**vēḷatō mā manavulu vinnaviñcitimi nīku**  
**ēla niddiriñcēvu mammiṭṭē rakṣiñcarādā ...nīdāsula .**  
**dvārakā nagaramulō taga nettamāḍē nīku**  
**bīrāna draupadi moraveṭṭinayaṭṭu**  
**ghōrapu rājasabhala kundi vinnaviñcitimi**  
**yērīti parāku nīku iṃkā rakṣiñcarādā .... nīdāsula**  
**enasi vaikaṇṭhamulō iṃdirakūḍunna nīku**  
**penagi gajamu moraveṭṭina yaṭṭu**  
**canavutō mā kōrike sāre vinnaviñcitimi**  
**vini śrī vēṅkaṭēśuṇḍa vēga rakṣiñcarādā ..nī dāsula**  
Humiliation of your servants, are you merely going to watch?  
What is it that you witness? Do you need to be prompted?  
For you who are reclining on the milky ocean,  
just as the helpless Devas sought your help,  
so too we have pleaded with you, very much in time\!  
Why is it that you are still asleep? Can’t you rescue us just now?  
To you who were playing dice at Dwaraka,  
just as the distressed Draupadi cried out for help,  
so too we, suffering in the courts of wicked kings, have sought your help\!  
Why this indifference from You? Can’t you save us just now?  
To you who were at Vaikunta, in the joyous company of Lakshmi,  
just as the battling elephant sought your help,  
so too we, with total faith, have expressed our desire\!  
Listening, O Sree Venkatesha\! Won’t you speedily rescue us?
