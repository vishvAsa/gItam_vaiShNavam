**Garuḍādri Vēdādri Kalimi**  
गरुडाद्रि वेदाद्रि कलिमि ईपॆ  
सिरुलॊसगी चूडरो चिन्तामणि ईपॆ ॥॥ ईपॆ ॥  
पालजलधि पुट्टिन पद्मालय ईपॆ  
लालित श्री नारसिंहु लक्ष्मि ईपॆ  
मेलिमि लोकमातयै मिञ्चिन मगुव ईपॆ  
ई लील लोकमुलेले इन्दिर ईपॆ ॥॥ ईपॆ ॥  
घन सम्पदलॊसगु कमलाकान्त ईपॆ  
मनसिजुगनिन रमासति ईपॆ  
अनिशमु बायनि महा हरिप्रिय ईपॆ  
धन धान्य रूपपु श्री तरुणि ईपॆ॥॥ ईपॆ ॥  
रच्चल वॆलसिनट्टि रमावनित ईपॆ  
मच्चिक गल अलमेल्मङ्ग ईपॆ  
इच्चटि वेङ्कटाद्रि नी अहोबलमु नन्दु  
निच्चलु तावुकॊनिन निधानमु ईपॆ॥॥ ईपॆ ॥  
**garuḍādri vēdādri kalimi īpe**  
**sirulosagī cūḍarō cintāmaṇi īpe .... īpe ..**  
**pālajaladhi puṭṭina padmālaya īpe**  
**lālita śrī nārasiṃhu lakṣmi īpe**  
**mēlimi lōkamātayai miñcina maguva īpe**  
**ī līla lōkamulēlē iṃdira īpe .... īpe ..**  
**ghana sampadalosagu kamalākānta īpe**  
**manasijuganina ramāsati īpe**  
**aniśamu bāyani mahā haripriya īpe**  
**dhana dhānya rūpapu śrī taruṇi īpe.... īpe ..**  
**raccala velasinaṭṭi ramāvanita īpe**  
**maccika gala alamēlmaṅga īpe**  
**iccaṭi vēṅkaṭādri nī ahōbalamu nandu**  
**niccalu tāvukonina nidhānamu īpe.... īpe ..**  
The prosperity of Garudadri, and Vedadri She is\!  
Bestower of treasures, Chintamani She is\!  
Born from the milky ocean, the one who resides in the Lotus She is  
One who is caressed by Narasimha, Lakshmi She is  
The supreme most mother of the worlds, the glorious lady She is  
In this manner, the one who reigns over the worlds, Indira She is\!  
One who bestows great wealth, lotus lady She is  
Mother of Manmatha, Goddess Rama She is  
Eternally inseparable from Him, the great love of Hari She is  
The embodiment of wealth and prosperity, Goddess Sree She is\!  
The one who manifested before everybody, Goddess Rama She is  
The one who is supremely affectionate, Alamelumanga She is\!  
Here, in Venkatadri and in Ahobilam,  
eternally all pervading, a treasure She is\!\!
