**Ipuḍiṭu Kalagaṇṭi**  
इप्पुडिटु कलगण्टि नॆल्ललोकमुलकु  
नप्पडगु तिरुवेङ्कटाद्रीशु गण्टि ॥॥ इपुडिटु ॥  
अतिशयम्बैन शेषाद्रि शिखरमु गण्टि  
प्रतिलेनि गोपुर प्रभलु गण्टि  
शतकोटि सूर्यतेजमुलु वॆलुगग गण्टि  
चतुरास्यु बॊडगण्टि चय्यन मेलुकण्टि ॥॥ इपुडिटु ॥  
कनकरत्न कवाटकान्तु लिरुगड गण्टि  
घनमैन दीप सङ्घमुलु गण्टि  
अनुपम मणिमयमगु किरीटमु गण्टि  
कनकाम्बरमु गण्टि गक्कन मेलु कण्टि ॥॥ इपुडिटु ॥  
अरुदैन शङ्खचक्रादु लिरुगड गण्टि  
सरिलेनि अभय हस्तमु गण्टि  
तिरुवेङ्कटाचलाधिपुनि जाडग गण्टि  
हरि गण्टि गुरु गण्टि नन्तट मेलुकण्टि ॥॥ इपुडिटु ॥  
**ippuḍiṭu kalagaṇṭi nellalōkamulaku**  
**nappaḍagu tiruvēṅkaṭādrīśu gaṇṭi .... ipuḍiṭu .**  
**atiśayambaina śēṣādri śikharamu gaṇṭi**  
**pratilēni gōpura prabhalu gaṇṭi**  
**śatakōṭi sūryatējamulu velugaga gaṇṭi**  
**caturāsyu boḍagaṇṭi cayyana mēlukaṇṭi .... ipuḍiṭu .**  
**kanakaratna kavāṭakāntu lirugaḍa gaṇṭi**  
**ghanamaina dīpa saṅghamulu gaṇṭi**  
**anupama maṇimayamagu kirīṭamu gaṇṭi**  
**kanakāmbaramu gaṇṭi gakkana mēlu kaṇṭi ... ipuḍiṭu ..**  
**arudaina śaṅkhacakrādu lirugaḍa gaṇṭi**  
**sarilēni abhaya hastamu gaṇṭi**  
**tiruvēṅkaṭācalādhipuni jūḍaga gaṇṭi**  
**hari gaṇṭi guru gaṇṭi nantaṭa mēlukaṇṭi .... ipuḍiṭu**  
I had a vision just now\! In which I beheld,  
The Overlord of all worlds, the Lord of TiruVenkatadri\!  
I saw the marvelous peak of Seshadri Hill  
The unmatched glow of the gopura I saw  
The light like that of the brilliance of crores of suns I saw  
Lord Brahma I saw, with a startle I woke up\!  
The dazzle of the golden doors embedded with gems, from both sides I saw  
The union of many brilliant lamps I saw  
The crown studded with matchless gems I saw  
The golden hued sky I saw, I woke up suddenly\!  
The unique Shanka-Chakra on either side I saw  
The matchless Abhaya Hastam I saw  
I could perceive the Lord of TiruVenkatachala  
Hari I saw, my guru I saw\! Just then I woke up\!
