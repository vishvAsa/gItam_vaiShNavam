**Arasi Nannu Gācina**  
अरसि नन्नु गाचिनातनिकि शरणु  
परमु निहमु नेले पतिकिनि शरणु ॥॥अरसि॥  
वेदमुलु दॆच्चिनट्टि विभुनिकि शरणु  
अदिमूलमण्टे वच्चिनतनिकि शरणु  
येदॆसा तानैयन्न यीतनिकि शरणु  
श्रीदेवि मगडैन श्रीपतिकि शरणु ॥॥अरसि॥  
अन्दरिकि प्राणमैन अतनिकि शरणु  
मुन्दु मूडु मूर्तुल मूर्तिकि शरणु  
दिन्दुपडि देवतल देवुनिकि शरणु  
अन्दि मिन्नु नेलनेकमैनतनिकि शरणु॥॥अरसि॥  
ताने चैतन्यमैन दैवानकु शरणु  
नानाब्रह्माण्डालनाथुनिकि शरणु  
आनुक श्री वेङ्कटाद्रि यन्दुनुण्डि वरमुलु  
दीनुल किन्दरिकिच्चे देवुनिकि शरणु ॥॥अरसि॥  
**arasi nannu gācinātaniki śaraṇu**  
**paramu nihamu nēlē patikini śaraṇu ....arasi..**  
**vēdamulu deccinaṭṭi vibhuniki śaraṇu**  
**adimūlamaṇṭē vaccinataniki śaraṇu**  
**yēdesā tānaiyunna yītaniki śaraṇu**  
**śrīdēvi magaḍaina śrīpatiki śaraṇu ....arasi..**  
**aṃdariki prāṇamaina ataniki śaraṇu**  
**mundu mūḍu mūrtula mūrtiki śaraṇu**  
**dindupaḍi dēvatala dēvuniki śaraṇu**  
**aṃdi minnu nēlanēkamainataniki śaraṇu....arasi..**  
**tānē caitanyamaina daivānaku śaraṇu**  
**nānābrahāmāṇḍālanāthuniki śaraṇu**  
**ānuka śrī vēṅkaṭādri yandunuṇḍi varamulu**  
**dīnula kindarikiccē dēvuniki śaraṇu ....arasi..**  
The one who protects me closely, unto Him I surrender  
The one who rules over ‘here and hereafter’, unto that Lord I surrender\!  
The one who retrieved the Vedas, unto that God I surrender  
The one who appeared upon calling out for ‘Adimoola’, unto Him I surrender  
The one who Himself is present in all directions, unto Him I surrender  
The one who is the consort of Sree Devi, unto that Sreepathi I surrender\!  
The one who is the ‘life-force’ for everyone, unto Him I surrender  
The primal one who existed even before the trinity, unto that deity I surrender  
The one who is the Lord for all these Devathas, unto that God I surrender  
The one who could reach earth and sky at once, unto Him I surrender\!  
The one who Himself is ‘pure consciousness’, unto that divinity I surrender  
The one who is the Lord of the innumerable universes, unto that Lord I surrender  
After which, residing at Sree Venkatadri Hill,  
the Lord who bestows boons to the helpless, unto that Lord I surrender\!
