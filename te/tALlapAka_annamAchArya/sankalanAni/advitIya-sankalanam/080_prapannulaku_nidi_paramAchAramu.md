**Prapannulaku Nidi Paramācāramu**  
प्रपन्नुलकु निदि परमाचारमु  
विपरीताचारमु विडुवग वलय ॥॥ प्रपन्नु ॥  
भगवदपचारमु भागवतापचारमु  
दगुलक देवतान्तरमु मानि  
नगधरु शरणमु नम्मि याचार्युनि  
बगि वायनिदे परमवैष्णवमु ॥॥ प्रपन्नु ॥  
दुरहङ्कारमु दःखमु सुखमुनु  
बॊरयक प्राकृतुल पॊन्त बोवक  
दरिशनाभिमानान धर्ममु वदलक  
परिशुद्धि नुण्डुटे परमवैष्णवमु ॥॥ प्रपन्नु ॥  
उपायान्तरमु लॊल्लक भक्ति चेपट्टि  
यॆपुडू दीर्थप्रसादेच्छतोड  
निपुणत श्रीवेङ्कट निलयडॆ गतियनि  
प्रपत्ति गलुगुटे परमवैष्णवमु ॥॥ प्रपन्नु ॥  
**prapannulaku nidi paramācāramu**  
**viparītācāramu viḍuvaga valayu .... prapannu ..**  
**bhagavadapacāramu bhāgavatāpacāramu**  
**dagulaka dēvatāntaramu māni**  
**nagadharu śaraṇamu nammi yācāryuni**  
**bagi vāyanidē paramavaiṣṇavamu .... prapannu ..**  
**durahaṅkāramu daḥkhamu sukhamunu**  
**borayaka prākṛtula ponta bōvaka**  
**dariśanābhimānāna dharmamu vadalaka**  
**pariśuddhi nuṇḍuṭē paramavaiṣṇavamu .... prapannu ..**  
**upāyāntaramu lollaka bhakti cēpaṭṭi**  
**yepuḍū dīrthaprasādēcchatōḍa**  
**nipuṇata śrīvēṅkaṭa nilayuḍe gatiyani**  
**prapatti galuguṭē paramavaiṣṇavamu .... prapannu ..**  
For Prapannas, this is the highest way of life.  
Perverse ways must be definitely shunned.  
Not committing Bhagavath apachara and Bh  
**ā**gavatha apachara,  
Abstaining from differentiating between deities  
Believing in the refuge of the one who lifted the mountain (Lord Vishnu)  
Not caviling against our Acharyas, is itself the highest Vaishnavism\!  
Not coming in contact with arrogance, sorrow or joy  
Not seeking the company of the materialistic ones  
Out of pride of knowledge, not letting go of Dharma  
Being pure, is itself the highest Vaishnavism\!  
Having true devotion, not accepting any other means,  
Always having a desire to partake Tirtha and Prasadam  
Wisely taking the Lord of Sree Venkata as the sole means,  
and performing Prapatti, is alone the highest Vaishnavism\!\!
