**Ihamēgāni Ika Paramēgāni**  
इहमेगानि इक परमेगानि  
बहुळमै हरि नीपै भक्ते चालु॥॥इहमे॥  
ऎन्दु जनिञ्चिन नेमि यॆच्चोट नुन्ननेमि  
कन्दुव नी दास्यमु गलिगिते चालु  
अन्दि स्वर्गमेगानि अल नरकमेगानि  
अन्दपु नी नाममु ना कब्बुटे चालु॥॥इहमे॥  
दॊरयैन जालुगडु तुच्छपुबण्टैन जालु  
करिगि निन्नु दलच गलिगिते चालु  
परुलु मॆच्चिन मेलु पम्मि दूषिञ्चिन मेलु  
हरि नी सेवापरुडौटे चालु॥॥इहमे॥  
इल चदुवुलुरानी इटु राक माननी  
तलपु नी पादमुल तगुले चालु  
यॆलमि श्री वेङ्कटेश येलितिवि नन्नु निट्टॆ  
चलपट्टि नाकु नी शरणमे चालु॥॥इहमे॥  
**ihamēgāni ika paramēgāni**  
**bahuḷamai hari nīpai bhaktē cālu....ihamē..**  
**eṃdu janiñcina nēmi yeccōṭa nunnanēmi**  
**kanduva nī dāsyamu galigitē cālu**  
**aṃdi svargamēgāni ala narakamēgāni**  
**aṃdapu nī nāmamu nā kabbuṭē cālu....ihamē..**  
**dorayaina jālugaḍu tucchapubaṇṭaina jālu**  
**karigi ninnu dalaca galigitē cālu**  
**parulu meccina mēlu pammi dūṣiñcina mēlu**  
**hari nī sēvāparuḍauṭē cālu....ihamē..**  
**ila caduvulurānī iṭu rāka mānanī**  
**talapu nī pādamula tagulē cālu**  
**yelami śrī vēṅkaṭēśa yēlitivi nannu niṭṭe**  
**calapaṭṭi nāku nī śaraṇamē cālu....ihamē..**  
Whether here, or whether hereafter,  
In many ways, O Hari, devotion for you is enough\!  
Where we are born, where we live, how does it matter?  
A token of servitude to you, if possessed, is enough\!  
Be it heaven, or be it hell, how does it matter?  
Your glorious name, if acquired, is enough\!  
Whether a ruler, or a lowly slave, how does it matter?  
Being overcome by emotion, if I think about you, it is enough\!  
If praised by others, or spurned by others, how does it matter?  
O Hari\! Offering service unto you is enough\!  
Whether I am learned, or I am not, how does it matter?  
If my thoughts reach unto your holy feet, it is enough\!  
Joyously, Sree Venkatesha, as you have reigned over me  
constantly for me, your refuge alone is enough\!
