Annicōṭlabaramātma
अन्निचोट्लबरमात्म नीवु
यिन्निरूपुल भ्रमयिन्तुवुगा ॥॥ अन्नि ॥
पालजलनिधि नुण्डि बदरीवनान नुण्डि
आलयमै गयलो ब्रयाग नुण्डि
भूलोकनिधियै पुरुषोत्तमान नुण्डि
वेलसङ्ख्यलरूपै विच्चेतुगा ॥॥ अन्नि ॥
वुत्तर मधुरलो नयोध्यलोपल नुण्डि
सत्तैननन्दव्रजान नुण्डि
चित्तगिञ्चि पञ्चवटि सिंहाद्रिलोन नुण्डि
वत्तुगा लोकमुलु पावनमु सेयगनु ॥॥ अन्नि ॥
कैवल्यमुन नुण्डि कमलजलोकान
मोवग श्रीरङ्गमुन नुण्डि
यीवल नावल नुण्डि यी वेङ्कटाद्रि पै
नीवे नीवे वच्चि नॆलकॊण्टिगा ॥॥ अन्नि ॥
annicōṭlabaramātma nīvu
yinnirūpula bhramayintuvugā …. anni ..
pālajalanidhi nuṇḍi badarīvanāna nuṇḍi
ālayamai gayalō brayāga nuṇḍi
bhūlōkanidhiyai puruṣōttamāna nuṇḍi
vēlasaṅkhyalarūpai viccētugā …. anni ..
vuttara madhuralō nayōdhyalōpala nuṇḍi
sattainanandavrajāna nuṇḍi
cittagiñci pañcavaṭi siṃhādrilōna nuṇḍi
vattugā lōkamulu pāvanamu sēyaganu …. anni ..
kaivalyamuna nuṇḍi kamalajalōkāna
mōvaga śrīraṅgamuna nuṇḍi
yīvala nāvala nuṇḍi yī vēṅkaṭādri pai
nīvē nīvē vacci nelakoṇṭigā …. anni ..
At all these places, O Lord, You alone are there!
In all these manifested forms, You fascinate us!
From the milky ocean, form the gardens of Badari,
from Gaya Your abode, from Prayaag, becoming the treasure
of this world, from the highest position of Purushottama,
You emerged in thousands of forms!
From Mathura, and from Ayodhya
from the blissful Nanda Vraja,
manifesting from Panchavati, and from Simhadri
You appeared to sanctify the worlds!
From eternal beatitude, sustaining Brahma-loka
and also from Sree Rangam,
from here and from there, and from everywhere
You and You alone came as the Lord to reside on this Venkatadri Hill!
