**Chī Chī Vō Jīvuḍā**  
छी छी वो जीवुडा चिन्तिञ्चुको जीवुडा  
आ चन्दान कीचन्दमु अरुहमा जीवुडा ॥॥ छी छी ॥  
वुत्तमुडयिन हरिवुण्डेटि यातुमलोन  
तत्तरपु गान्तलनु तलपोसेदा  
वॊत्ति नारायणनाम मुच्चरिञ्चेनोरनु  
रुत्तयधरपुदोलु रुचिगॊनेदा ॥॥ छी छी ॥  
पॊन्दुगा गोविन्दुनि बूजिञ्चेटि चेतलनु  
सन्दडि जन्नुलमीद जाचबॊय्येदा  
कन्दुव देवुनिरूपु कनुगॊने चूपुलु  
मुन्दनि मर्मपुटङ्गमुल मीद बॆट्टॆदा ॥॥ छी छी ॥  
वेवेलु विष्णकथलु विनियेटि चॆवुलनु  
आवटिञ्चि याडसुद्दुलालकिञ्चेदा  
श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरुनि सेविञ्चे यी जीवनमु  
भाविञ्चक विषयालपालु सेसेदा ॥॥ छी छी ॥  
**chī chī vō jīvuḍā cintiñcukō jīvuḍā**  
**ā candāna kīcandamu aruhamā jīvuḍā .... chī chī ..**  
**vuttamuḍayina harivuṇḍēṭi yātumalōna**  
**tattarapu gāntalanu talapōsēdā**  
**votti nārāyaṇanāma muccariñcēnōranu**  
**ruttayadharapudōlu rucigonēdā .... chī chī ..**  
**pondugā gōvinduni būjiñcēṭi cētalanu**  
**sandaḍi jannulamīda jācaboyyēdā**  
**kanduva dēvunirūpu kanugonē cūpulu**  
**mundani marmapuṭaṅgamula mīda beṭṭedā .... chī chī .**  
**vēvēlu viṣṇakathalu viniyēṭi cevulanu**  
**āvaṭiñci yāḍasuddulālakiñcēdā**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaruni sēviñcē yī jīvanamu**  
**bhāviñcaka viṣayālapālu sēsēdā .... chī chī ..**  
Fie upon You O Jiva\! Analyze carefully O Jiva\!  
For something like that, is this befitting, O Jiva\!  
In the mind where the highest Lord Hari resides,  
is it right to think about women in a sensual way?  
With the mouth that pronounces the name of Narayana,  
is it right to taste the lips of a woman which is but mere flesh\!  
With the hands that joyously worship Lord Govinda,  
is it right to caress the breasts of women?  
The eyes that eagerly behold the beautiful form of the Lord,  
is it right to use them to see the private parts?  
The ears which listen to the glories of Lord Vishnu thousands of times,  
is it right to use them to hear mere gossips of women?  
This life which is for the service of Lord Sree Venkateshwara,  
thoughtlessly, why waste it indulging in material pleasures?
