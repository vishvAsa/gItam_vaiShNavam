**Kalige Mākunidi Kaivalyaṃ**  
कलिगॆ माकु निदि कैवल्यं  
कलकालमु हरिकथाश्रवणम् ॥॥ कलिगॆ ॥  
अचिन्त्य मद्भुत मानन्दं  
प्रचुरं दिव्यं पावनं  
सुचरित्रं श्रुति शोभितं  
अचलं बिदिवो हरिकीर्तनम् ॥॥ कलिगॆ ॥  
निरतं नित्यं निखिल शुभकरं  
दुरितं हर भवदूरं  
परम मङ्गळं भावातीतं  
करिवरदं निजकैङ्कर्यम् ॥॥ कलिगॆ ॥  
सुलभं सुकरं शोकनाशनं  
फलदं ललितं भयहरणं  
कलितं श्री वेङ्कटपति शरणं  
जलजोदर निच्च स्तोत्रम् ॥॥ कलिगॆ ॥  
**kalige māku nidi kaivalyaṃ**  
**kalakālamu harikathāśravaṇaṃ .... kalige ..**  
**acintya madbhuta mānandaṃ**  
**pracuraṃ divyaṃ pāvanaṃ**  
**sucaritraṃ śruti śōbhitaṃ**  
**acalaṃ bidivō harikīrtanaṃ .... kalige ..**  
**nirataṃ nityaṃ nikhila śubhakaraṃ**  
**duritaṃ hara bhavadūraṃ**  
**parama maṅgaḷaṃ bhāvātītaṃ**  
**karivaradaṃ nijakaiṅkaryaṃ .... kalige ..**  
**sulabhaṃ sukaraṃ śōkanāśanaṃ**  
**phaladaṃ lalitaṃ bhayaharaṇaṃ**  
**kalitaṃ śrī vēṅkaṭapati śaraṇaṃ**  
**jalajōdara nicca stōtraṃ .... kalige ..**  
Bestowed upon us is this beatitude,  
the constant hearing of the stories of Hari\!  
Free from worries, extraordinary, blissful  
the famed, divine, pious  
the blessed story, glorified by the Vedas,  
the constant one is this, the praising of Lord Hari\!  
Eternal, forever, the all benevolent,  
destroyer of sins, remover of difficulties  
supremely auspicious, beyond all mundane passions,  
the savior of the elephant, the true servitude\!  
Easy, beneficent, destroyer of sorrows,  
fruitful, tender, remover of all fears,  
teaching surrender unto Venkatapathi  
this is the eternal verse praising the Lord with lotus navel\!
