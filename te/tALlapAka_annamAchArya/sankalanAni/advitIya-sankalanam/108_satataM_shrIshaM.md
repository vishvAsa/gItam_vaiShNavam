**Satataṃ Śrīśaṃ**  
सततं श्रीशं  
हितं परात्पर मीडे ॥॥ सततम् ॥  
गदाधरं मेघगम्भीरनि \-  
नदं परमोन्नत शुभदं  
मृदु तरगमनं मेदिनीधरं  
हृदय निलय महमीडे ॥॥ सततम् ॥  
नन्दकधरं जनार्दनं गो \-  
विन्दं चारु मुकुन्दं  
नन्द गोपवर नन्दन कन्दं  
यिन्दुर विनयन मीडे ॥॥ सततम् ॥  
गरुडगमन मुरगशयन मधिकं  
परमपदेशं पावनं  
तिरुवेङ्कट गिरि देव मतुलं म-  
हिरमणं स्धिर मीडे ॥॥ सततम् ॥  
**satataṃ śrīśaṃ**  
**hitaṃ parātpara mīḍē .... satataṃ ..**  
**gadādharaṃ mēghagambhīrani \-**  
**nadaṃ paramōnnata śubhadaṃ**  
**mṛdu taragamanaṃ mēdinīdharaṃ**  
**hṛdaya nilaya mahamīḍē .... satataṃ ..**  
**nandakadharaṃ janārdanaṃ gō \-**  
**vindaṃ cāru mukundaṃ**  
**nanda gōpavara nandana kandaṃ**  
**yindura vinayana mīḍē .... satataṃ ..**  
**garuḍagamana muragaśayana madhikaṃ**  
**paramapadēśaṃ pāvanaṃ**  
**tiruvēṅkaṭa giri dēva matulaṃ ma-**  
**hiramaṇaṃ sdhira mīḍē .... satataṃ ..**  
Eternally unto the Lord of Sree,  
the doer of good, the Supreme being, I surrender\!  
The wielder of the mace, the one with a voice like thunder  
the bestower of supreme auspiciousness,  
the one who moves gently, the Lord who bore the earth,  
the one who resides in the heart, unto Him I surrender\!  
The wielder of Nandaka, Janardhana\!  
Govinda, the attractive one, Mukunda\!  
The one who blessed the Gopis of Nanda, that dear one of Nanda  
the one with sun and moon as eyes, unto Him I surrender\!  
The one who moves mounted on Garuda, the one who reclines on the serpant,  
the superior one, the Supreme Lord of Parama pada, the propitious one,  
That Lord of TiruVenkatagiri, the matchless one, the Lord of Sesha  
the eternal one, unto Him I surrender\!
