**Nirmalulu Vīru**  
निर्मलुलु वीरु नित्यसुखुलु  
कर्मदूरुलु विष्णुकैङ्कर्यपरुलु ॥॥ निर्मलुलु ॥  
परमज्ञानसम्पन्नुलगु वारिकिनि  
अरुदुगा दिचटिमहादैश्वर्यमु  
हरिकृपाधनमु चेनब्बिन महात्मुलकु  
सरकुगाकुण्डु पञ्चल निधानमुलु ॥॥ निर्मलुलु ॥  
बलिमिनि वेदान्तपट्टभद्रुलकु निल  
बलुराज्यपदवु लिरुगडबण्ट्लु  
ललिमीरि हरिचक्रलाञ्छनपु शूरुलकु  
यॆलमि विजयमुलॆल्ल निण्टिलो नुण्डु ॥॥ निर्मलुलु ॥  
यीवलनु श्रीवेङ्कटेशुशरणागतुल-  
कावटिञ्चिन पुण्य मडुवुगादु  
दैवज्ञुलयिन या धर्मस्वरूपुलकु  
वेवेलु भोगमुलु वॆनुतगुलुचुण्डु ॥॥ निर्मलुलु ॥  
**nirmalulu vīru nityasukhulu**  
**karmadūrulu viṣṇukaiṅkaryaparulu .... nirmalulu ..**  
**paramajñānasampannulagu vārikini**  
**arudugā dicaṭimahādaiśvaryamu**  
**harikṛpādhanamu cēnabbina mahātmulaku**  
**sarakugākuṇḍu pañcala nidhānamulu .... nirmalulu ..**  
**balimini vēdāntapaṭṭabhadrulaku nila**  
**balurājyapadavu lirugaḍabaṇṭlu**  
**lalimīri haricakralāñchanapu śūrulaku**  
**yelami vijayamulella niṇṭilō nuṇḍu .... nirmalulu ..**  
**yīvalanu śrīvēṅkaṭēśuśaraṇāgatula-**  
**kāvaṭiñcina puṇya maḍuvugādu**  
**daivajñulayina yā dharmasvarūpulaku**  
**vēvēlu bhōgamulu venutagulucuṇḍu .... nirmalulu ..**  
Pure are these people, eternally joyful\!  
Unbounded by karmas are those who offer ‘Kainkaryam’1 to Vishnu\!  
For them who are endowed with ‘Parama-Jnana’2  
it is not rare to find here the supreme wealth\!  
Those great beings who are blessed with the wealth of Hari’s grace  
they do not care for the available material wealth\!  
Those resolute ones who are learned in Vedanta  
take the many Kingly posts to be nothing but shackles\!  
To those great ones who wear the marks of Hari’s Chakra3 with exceeding joy  
all victories remain happily in their houses\!  
Here Sharanagathas who have surrendered unto SreeVenkatesha  
the merits they have earned can never be destroyed\!  
For those embodiments of Dharma who are divine  
thousands of comforts will closely follow\!\!  
The message is that those who offer servitude to Lord Vishnu and perform Sharanagati unto  
Him, they are blessed with all prosperity, they alone are divine\!  
1.Servitude  
2.Supreme knowledge  
3.Wearing of the permanent marks of the Conch and Discus (mudras)
