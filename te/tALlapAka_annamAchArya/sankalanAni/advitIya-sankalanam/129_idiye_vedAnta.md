**Idiyē Vēdānta**  
इदिये वेदान्त मिन्दुकण्टॆ लेदु  
इदिये श्री वेङ्कटेशुनि मतमु ॥॥ इदिये ॥  
विरतिये लाभमु विरतिये सौख्यमु  
विरतियेपो विज्ञानमु  
विरतिचे घनुलैरि वॆनक वारॆल्ल  
विरति बॊन्दकुन्न वीडदु भवमु ॥॥ इदिये ॥  
चित्तमे पापमु चित्तमे पुण्यमु  
चित्तमे मोक्षसिद्धियनु  
चित्तमु वलॆने श्रीहरि निलुचुनु  
चित्तशान्ति लेक चेरदु परमु ॥॥ इदिये ॥  
ऎन्त चदिविना ऎन्त वॆदकिना  
यिन्तकण्टॆ मरियिक लेदु  
यिन्तट श्रीवेङ्कटेशु दासुलौट  
यॆन्तवारिकैन यिदिये तॆरुवु ॥॥ इदिये ॥  
**idiyē vēdānta mindukaṇṭe lēdu**  
**idiyē śrī vēṅkaṭēśuni matamu .... idiyē ..**  
**viratiyē lābhamu viratiyē saukhyamu**  
**viratiyēpō vijñānamu**  
**viraticē ghanulairi venaka vārella**  
**virati bondakunna vīḍadu bhavamu .... idiyē ..**  
**cittamē pāpamu cittamē puṇyamu**  
**cittamē mōkṣasiddhiyunu**  
**cittamu valenē śrīhari nilucunu**  
**cittaśānti lēka cēradu paramu .... idiyē ..**  
**eṃta cadivinā eṃta vedakinā**  
**yintakaṇṭe mariyika lēdu**  
**yintaṭa śrīvēṅkaṭēśu dāsulauṭa**  
**yentavārikaina yidiyē teruvu .... idiyē ..**  
This is the Vedanta, there is nothing else\!  
This itself is SreeVenkatesha’s religion\!  
Detachment itself is gainful, detachment itself is comforting  
detachment itself is the special knowledge\!  
By being detached, earlier many have become great  
without being detached, sorrows will not let go\!  
Mind itself is sinful, mind itself is meritorious  
Mind itself is the (cause of) attainment of Moksha\!  
As ones mind is, so is Hari perceived  
without peace of mind, the supreme cannot be reached\!  
How much ever one studies, how much ever one searches  
there is nothing else other than this\!  
For all this, being Daasas of SreeVenkatesha  
is the only way, for whoever it may be\!
