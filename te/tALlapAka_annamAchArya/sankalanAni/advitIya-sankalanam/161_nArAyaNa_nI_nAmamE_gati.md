**Nārāyaṇa Nī Nāmame Gati**  
नारायण नी नाममॆ गति यिक  
कोरिकलु नाकु कॊनसागुटकु ॥॥ नारा ॥  
पैपै मुन्दट भवजलधि  
दापु वॆनक चिन्ताजलधि  
चापलमु नडुम संसार जलधि  
तेप येदि इवि तॆग नीदुटकु ॥॥ नारा ॥  
पण्डॆनु ऎडम पापपुराशि  
अण्डनु गुडिनि पुण्यपु राशि  
कॊण्डनु नडुम त्रिगुणराशि \- यिवि  
निण्ड कुडुचुटकु निलुकड येदि ॥॥ नारा ॥  
किन्दि लोकमुलु कीडु नरकमुलु  
अन्देटि स्वर्गालवॆ मीद  
चॆन्दि अन्तरात्म श्रीवेङ्कटेश नी \-  
यन्दॆ परमपद मवल मरेदि ॥॥ नारा ॥  
**nārāyaṇa nī nāmame gati yika**  
**kōrikalu nāku konasāguṭaku .... nārā ..**  
**paipai mundaṭa bhavajaladhi**  
**dāpu venaka cintājaladhi**  
**cāpalamu naḍuma saṃsāra jaladhi**  
**tēpa yēdi ivi tega nīduṭaku .... nārā ..**  
**paṇḍenu eḍama pāpapurāśi**  
**aṃḍanu guḍini puṇyapu rāśi**  
**koṇḍanu naḍuma triguṇarāśi \- yivi**  
**niṇḍa kuḍucuṭaku nilukaḍa yēdi .... nārā ..**  
**kindi lōkamulu kīḍu narakamulu**  
**aṃdēṭi svargālave mīda**  
**cendi aṃtarātma śrīvēṅkaṭēśa nī \-**  
**yande paramapada mavala marēdi .... nārā ..**  
Narayana\! Your name alone is the means, now  
for my wishes to become fulfilled\!  
Over and over again, in front is the ocean of sorrow  
Near and behind is the ocean of worries  
Amidst the fickleness is the ocean of samsara  
Which is the raft for me to crossover these?  
Onto the left is the pile of growing sins,  
Close by, in the shrine, is the abundance of virtue  
Midway on the Hill is the heap of Trigunas  
To savor in total, where is the determination?  
Below are the worlds which are hells of evil  
Reachable are the heavens which are up there  
When Sree Venkatesha is your inner self,  
where in the outside is the supreme abode?
