**Nityapūjalivivō Nēricina**  
नित्यपूजलिविवो नेरिचिन नोहो  
प्रत्यक्षमैनट्टि परमात्मुनिकि ॥॥ नित्य ॥  
तनुवे गुडियट तलये शिखरमट  
पॆनुहृदयमे हरि पीठमट  
कनुगॊन चूपुले घनदीपमुलट  
तन लोपलि अन्तर्यामिकिनि ॥॥ नित्य ॥  
पलुके मन्त्रमट पादैन नालिके  
कलकलमनु पिडि घण्टयट  
नलुवैन रुचुले नैवेद्यमुलट  
तलपु लोपल नुन्न दैवमुनकु ॥॥ नित्य ॥  
गमन चेष्टले अङ्गरङ्गगतियट  
तमिगल जीवुडे दासुडट  
अमरिन वूर्पुले आलवट्टमुलट  
क्रममुतो श्री वेङ्कटरायनिकि ॥॥ नित्य ॥  
**nityapūjalivivō nēricina nōhō**  
**pratyakṣamainaṭṭi paramātmuniki .... nitya ..**  
**tanuvē guḍiyaṭa talayē śikharamaṭa**  
**penuhṛdayamē hari pīṭhamaṭa**  
**kanugona cūpulē ghanadīpamulaṭa**  
**tana lōpali aṃtaryāmikini .... nitya ..**  
**palukē mantramaṭa pādaina nālikē**  
**kalakalamanu piḍi ghaṇṭayaṭa**  
**naluvaina ruculē naivēdyamulaṭa**  
**talapu lōpala nunna daivamunaku .... nitya ..**  
**gamana cēṣṭalē aṃgaraṅgagatiyaṭa**  
**tamigala jīvuḍē dāsuḍaṭa**  
**amarina vūrpulē ālavaṭṭamulaṭa**  
**kramamutō śrī vēṅkaṭarāyaniki .... nitya ..**  
These are the daily obeisance, which I have learnt  
For the Lord, who has manifested\!  
The body is itself the shrine, the head the adorning pinnacle,  
the large heart is itself the seat for Sree Hari,  
the vision of the eyes are the bright lamps,  
for the Lord who resides within\!  
Speech itself is the incantation, the firm tongue's  
resonant sounds are like the ringing of bells,  
the varied tastes are the food offerings,  
to the Lord who is ever present in our thoughts \!  
The walk itself is like taking the Lord on a procession,  
the eager Jiva is itself the ‘dasa’  
the process of breathing is itself the waving of the fan,  
in this manner is the constant worship of Sree Venkataraya\!  
This is a description of Maanasika Pooja.
