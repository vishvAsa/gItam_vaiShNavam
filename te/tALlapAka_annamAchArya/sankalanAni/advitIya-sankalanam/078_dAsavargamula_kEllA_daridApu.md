**Dāsavargamula Kellā Daridāpu**  
दासवर्गमुल कॆल्ला दरिदापु मीरे कान  
वासिकि नॆक्किञ्चरादा वसुधलो मम्मुनु ॥॥ दास ॥  
सेनाधिपति नीवु चेरि विन्नविञ्चरादा  
श्रीनाथुनिकि नेमुसेसे विन्नपमु  
आनुक भाष्यकारुलु अट्टॆ मीरु जेयरादा  
मानक विन्नपमु मा मनवि चनवुलु ॥॥ दास ॥  
वेययनोळ्ळ भोगि नीवु विन्नपमु सेयरादा  
वेयेसि माविन्नपालु विष्णुनिकिनि  
आयितमै गरुडड अट्टॆ मीरु जेयरादा  
येयॆड विन्नपमु मा केमि वलसिनानु ॥॥ दास ॥  
देवुलम्म यिन्दिर मादिक्कै विन्नविञ्चरादा  
श्रीवेङ्कटपतिकि चित्तमन्दुनु  
आवेळ शेषाचलम अट्टॆ मीरु जेयरादा  
यीवेळ माविन्नपमु लीडेरॆ निकनु ॥॥ दास ॥  
**dāsavargamula kellā daridāpu mīrē kāna**  
**vāsiki nekkiñcarādā vasudhalō mammunu .... dāsa ..**  
**sēnādhipati nīvu cēri vinnaviñcarādā**  
**śrīnāthuniki nēmusēsē vinnapamu**  
**ānuka bhāṣyakārulu aṭṭe mīru jēyarādā**  
**mānaka vinnapamu mā manavi canavulu .... dāsa ..**  
**vēyayanōḷḷa bhōgi nīvu vinnapamu sēyarādā**  
**vēyēsi māvinnapālu viṣṇunikini**  
**āyitamai garuḍaḍa aṭṭe mīru jēyarādā**  
**yēyeḍa vinnapamu mā kēmi valasinānu .... dāsa ..**  
**dēvulamma yindira mādikkai vinnaviñcarādā**  
**śrīvēṅkaṭapatiki cittamandunu**  
**āvēḷa śēṣācalama aṭṭe mīru jēyarādā**  
**yīvēḷa māvinnapamu līḍēre nikanu .... dāsa ..**  
For all those who belong to the clan of Dasas, since you are the ones who  
are very close, will you not elevate us to a higher state, in this world?  
O Senadipathi\!1 Will you not approach on our behalf,  
the Lord of Sree, with our requests?  
After which, O Bhasyakarulu2, will you not do the same,  
without fail, presenting our requests and pleadings?  
O thousand mouthed Sesha\! Will you not put forth  
thousands of our requests to Lord Vishnu?  
Readily O Garuda, will you not do the same,  
pleading for our requests and desires?  
O Goddess Indira\! Being our refuge, will You not appeal  
to SreeVenkatapathi, so that it reaches unto His heart?  
At that very time, O Seshachala hill, will you not do the same?  
Now our requests will be duly fulfilled\!  
1.Vishwaksenar, the commander of Vishnu’s mighty army  
2.Sree Ramanujacharya
