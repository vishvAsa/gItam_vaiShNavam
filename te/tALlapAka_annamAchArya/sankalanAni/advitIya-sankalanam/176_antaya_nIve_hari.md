**Aṃtayu Nīvē Hari**  
अन्तय नीवे हरि पुण्डरीकाक्ष  
चॆन्त नाकु नीवे श्री रघुरामा ॥॥ अन्तय ॥  
कुलमुनु नीवे गोविन्दुडा, ना \-  
कलिमिय नीवे करुणानिधी,  
तलपुनु नीवे धरणीधरा, ना \-  
नॆलवुनु नीवे नीरजनाभा ॥॥ अन्तय ॥  
तनुवुनु नीवे दामोदरा, ना \-  
मनिकिय नीवे मधुसूदना,  
विनिकिय नीवे विट्टलुडा ना \-  
वॆनकमुन्दु नीवे विष्टुदेवुडा ॥॥ अन्तय ॥  
पुट्टुगु नीवे पुरुषोत्तमा कॊन  
नट्टनडुम नीवे नारायण  
यिट्टॆ श्री वेङ्कटेश्वरुडा नाकु  
नॆट्टन गति यिङ्क नीवे नीवे ॥॥ अन्तय ॥  
**aṃtayu nīvē hari puṇḍarīkākṣa**  
**centa nāku nīvē śrī raghurāmā .... aṃtayu ..**  
**kulamunu nīvē gōvinduḍā, nā \-**  
**kalimiyu nīvē karuṇānidhī,**  
**talapunu nīvē dharaṇīdharā, nā \-**  
**nelavunu nīvē nīrajanābhā .... aṃtayu ..**  
**tanuvunu nīvē dāmōdarā, nā \-**  
**manikiyu nīvē madhusūdanā,**  
**vinikiyu nīvē viṭṭaluḍā nā \-**  
**venakamundu nīvē viṣṭudēvuḍā .... aṃtayu ..**  
**puṭṭugu nīvē puruṣōttamā kona**  
**naṭṭanaḍuma nīvē nārāyaṇa**  
**yiṭṭe śrī vēṅkaṭēśvaruḍā nāku**  
**neṭṭana gati yiṅka nīvē nīvē .... aṃtayu ..**  
Everywhere You are\! O Hari\! The lotus eyed\!  
Close by me, You are, O Sree Raghu Rama\!  
My clan you are, O Govinda\!  
My wealth, You are, O treasure of compassion\!  
Thought You are, O Dharanidhara\!  
My dwelling place, You are, O lotus naveled one\!  
Body also You are, O Dhamodhara\!  
My living, You are, O Madhusudhana\!  
Sense of hearing You are, O Vittala\!  
In front and at my back, You are, O Vishnu deva\!  
Birth You are, O Purushottama\!  
At the end, and in between, only You, O Narayana\!  
In this manner, O Sree Venkateshwara, for me  
the final destination is You and only You\!\!
