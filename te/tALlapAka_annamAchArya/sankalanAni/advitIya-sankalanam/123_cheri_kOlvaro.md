**Cēri Kolvarō**  
चेरि कॊल्वरो यीतडु श्रीदेवुडु  
यीरीति श्री वेङ्कटाद्रि निरवैन देवुडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
अलमेलु मङ्ग नुरमं दिडुकॊन्न देवुडु  
चॆलगि शङ्खचक्राल चेति देवुडु  
कल वरदहस्तमु गटिहस्तपु देवुडु  
मलसी श्रीवत्स वनमालिकल देवुडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
घन मकरकुण्डल कर्णमुल देवुडु  
कलन पीताम्बर शृङ्गार देवुडु  
ननिचि ब्रह्मादुल नाभिगन्न देवुडु  
जनिञ्चॆ बादाल गङ्ग सङ्गतैन देवुडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥  
कोटि मन्मथाकार सङ्कुलमैन देवुडु  
जाटपुगिरीटपु मिञ्चुल देवुडु  
वाटपु सॊम्मुलतोडि वसुधापति देवुडु  
यीटुलेनि श्री वेङ्कटेशुडैन देवुडु ॥॥ चेरि ॥

**nanici brahmādula nābhiganna dēvuḍu**  
**janiñce bādāla gaṅga saṅgataina dēvuḍu ..cēri ..**  
**kōṭi manmathākāra saṅkulamaina dēvuḍu**  
**jūṭapugirīṭapu miñcula dēvuḍu**  
**vāṭapu sommulatōḍi vasudhāpati dēvuḍu**  
**yīṭulēni śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍaina dēvuḍu .... cēri ..**  
Collectively offer worship\! He is the Lord of Sree\!  
He is the Lord who resides on Sree Venkatadri\!  
He is the Lord who has placed Alamelumanga on His chest  
He is the Lord whose hands joyfully wield the Shanka-Chakra  
He is the Lord with the Varada-Hasta1 and the Kati-Hasta2  
He is the Lord who is adorned by the Srivathsa and Vanamala\!  
He is the Lord whose ears are adorned by fish shaped ear rings  
He is the Lord whose is decorated in yellow sacred robes  
He is the Lord whose navel is the origin of Brahma and others  
He is the Lord from whose feet Ganga originated  
He is the Lord whose form is the sum total of crores of Manmathas  
He is the Lord who is resplendent with a shining crown  
He is the consort of Vasudha, decorated with beautiful jewellery  
He is the matchless Lord Sree Venkatesha \!\!  
1.The boon bestowing hand  
2.The hand placed upont the waist
