**Atisulabhambide Śrīpati**  
अतिसुलभं बिदॆ श्रीपति शरणमु अन्दुकु नारदादुलु साक्षि  
प्रतिले दिदिये नित्यानन्दमु बहुवेदम्बुलॆ यिवे साक्षि ॥॥ अति ॥  
वेसरकुमी जीवुडा वॆदकि वॆदकि दैवमुनु  
आसनपाटुगा हरि यन्नाडिदॆ अन्दुकु ब्रह्लादुडु साक्षि  
मोसपोकुमी जन्ममा मुञ्चिनयनुमानमुलनु  
सेसिन भक्तिकि जेटु लेदु यी नेत कॆल्ल ध्रुवुडे साक्षि ॥॥ अति ॥  
तमकिञ्चकुमी देहमा तगुसुखदुःखम्बुल नलसि  
अमितमु नरहरिकरुण नम्मिते नन्दुकु नर्जनुडे साक्षि  
भ्रमयकुमी विवेकमा बहुकालम्बुलु यीदीदि  
तमितो दास्यमु तनु रक्षिञ्चुनु दानिकि बलीन्द्रुडे साक्षि ॥॥ अति ॥  
मरिगिवुण्डुमी वोजिह्व मरि श्रीवेङ्कटपतिनुतुलु  
अरयग निदिये यीडेरिञ्चुनु आन्दुकु व्यासादुलॆ साक्षि  
तिरगकुमी विज्ञानमा द्रिष्टपुमायलकेनु लोगि  
सरिले दितनिपादसेवकुनु सनकादुलब्रदुके साक्षि ॥॥ अति ॥  
**atisulabhaṃ bide śrīpati śaraṇamu aṃduku nāradādulu sākṣi**  
**pratilē didiyē nityānandamu bahuvēdambule yivē sākṣi .. ati ..**  
**vēsarakumī jīvuḍā vedaki vedaki daivamunu**  
**āsanapāṭugā hari yunnāḍide aṃduku brahlāduḍu sākṣi**  
**mōsapōkumī janmamā muñcinayanumānamulanu**  
**sēsina bhaktiki jēṭu lēdu yī nēta kella dhruvuḍē sākṣi.. ati ..**  
**tamakiñcakumī dēhamā tagusukhaduḥkhambula nalasi**  
**amitamu naraharikaruṇa nammitē nanduku narjanuḍē sākṣi**  
**bhramayakumī vivēkamā bahukālambulu yīdīdi**  
**tamitō dāsyamu tanu rakṣiñcunu dāniki balīndruḍē sākṣi .. ..ati ..**  
**marigivuṇḍumī vōjihva mari śrīvēṅkaṭapatinutulu**  
**arayaga nidiyē yīḍēriñcunu āṃduku vyāsādule sākṣi**  
**tiragakumī vijñānamā driṣṭapumāyalakēnu lōgi**  
**sarilē ditanipādasēvakunu sanakādulabradukē sākṣi .. .. ati ..**  
This is the most easy- Surrender unto SreePathi\! For this Narada and others are the proof\!  
This alone is matchless, the eternally blissful\! For this, the Vedas are the proof\!  
O Jiva, do not fret constantly searching for the lord,  
Hari is there to be desired\! For this, Prahalada is the proof\!  
O life, do not be fooled by the overpowering doubts,  
Devotion never goes wasteful\! For this, Dhruva alone is the proof\!  
Do not be in a haste, O body, getting fatigued by pleasures and sorrows,  
Limitless is Nara-Hari’s compassion, if you believe\! For this, Arjuna is the proof\!  
O intellect, do not be deluded, swimming constantly through the many births,  
Eagerly if one offers servitude, He protects\! For this, king Bali is the proof\!  
O tongue, do get addicted to the praises of SreeVenkatapathi,  
Totally, this alone will fulfill\! For this, Vyasa and others are the proof\!  
O knowledge, do not get misled by the ‘Maaya’,  
Serving ‘His’ feet is alone matchless\! For this, the life of Sanaka and others is the proof\!
