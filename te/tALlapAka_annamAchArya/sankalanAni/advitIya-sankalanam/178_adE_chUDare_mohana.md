**Ade Cūḍarē Mōhana**  
अदॆ चूडरे मोहन रूपं  
पदि कोट्लु गल भावज रूपम् ॥॥ अदॆ ॥  
वॆलयग पदारुवेल मगुवलनु  
अलमिन घनमोहन रूपं  
वलचिन नन्दव्रजमु गॊल्लॆतल  
कुलुकु चूपुलकु गुरियगु रूपं॥॥ अदॆ ॥  
इन्दिर वनित नॆप्पुडु तन वुर \-  
मन्दु निलिपिन मोहन रूपं  
कन्दुव भूसति कौगिट सॊम्पुल  
विन्दुलु मरिगिन वेडुक रूपम् ॥॥ अदॆ ॥  
त्रिपुर सतुल बोधिञ्चि रमिञ्चिन  
अपुरूपपु मोहन रूपं  
कपुरुल श्री वेङ्कटपतियै इल  
उपमिञ्चगरानि वुन्नत रूपम् ॥॥ अदॆ ॥  
**ade cūḍarē mōhana rūpaṃ**  
**padi kōṭlu gala bhāvaja rūpaṃ .... ade ..**  
**velayaga padāruvēla maguvalanu**  
**alamina ghanamōhana rūpaṃ**  
**valacina nandavrajamu golletala**  
**kuluku cūpulaku guriyagu rūpaṃ.... ade ..**  
**iṃdira vanita neppuḍu tana vura \-**  
**mandu nilipina mōhana rūpaṃ**  
**kanduva bhūsati kaugiṭa sompula**  
**vindulu marigina vēḍuka rūpaṃ .... ade ..**  
**tripura satula bōdhiñci ramiñcina**  
**apurūpapu mōhana rūpaṃ**  
**kapurula śrī vēṅkaṭapatiyai ila**  
**upamiñcagarāni vunnata rūpaṃ .... ade ..**  
Behold this bewitchingly handsome form\!  
A beautiful form like ten crores of Manmathas\!  
This is the bewitching form which  
captivated sixteen thousand women\!  
This is the form that is looked at  
lovingly by the Nanda Vraja women folk\!  
This is the handsome form which has  
Lakshmi seated on the breast\!  
This is the glorious form which is addicted  
to the feast of Bhu-sati’s embrace\!  
This is the extraordinarily handsome form that  
taught the women of the three worlds\!  
This is the matchless supreme form  
that has assumed the form of Sree Venkatapathi\!
