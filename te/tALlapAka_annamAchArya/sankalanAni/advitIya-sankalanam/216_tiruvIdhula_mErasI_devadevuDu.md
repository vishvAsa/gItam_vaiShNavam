**Tiruvīdhula Merasī Dēvadēvuḍu**  
तिरुवीधुल मॆरसी देवदेवुडु  
गरिमल मिञ्चिन सिङ्गारमुल तोडनु ॥॥तिरु॥  
तिरुदण्डॆल पैनेगी देवुडिदे तॊलिनाडु  
सिरुल रॆण्डवनाडु शेषुनि मीद  
मुरिपेन मूडॊनाडु मुत्यालपन्दिरिक्रिन्द  
पॊरि नालुगोनाडु पुव्वु कोविल लोनु॥॥तिरु॥  
गक्कन ऐदवनाडु गरुडुनि मीद  
ऎक्कॆनु आरवनाडु येनुगु मीद  
चॊक्कमै एडवनाडु सूर्यप्रभलोननु  
यिक्कुव तेरुनु गुर्र मॆनिमिदोनाडु॥॥तिरु॥  
कनकपुटन्दलमु कदिसि तॊम्मिदोनाडु  
पॆनचि पदोनाडु पॆण्ड्लिपीट  
ऎनसि श्री वेङ्कटेशुडिन्ति अलमेल्मङ्गतो  
वनितल नडुमनु वाहनाल मीदनु ॥॥तिरु॥  
**tiruvīdhula merasī dēvadēvuḍu**  
**garimala miñcina siṅgāramula tōḍanu ....tiru..**  
**tirudaṇḍela painēgī dēvuḍidē tolināḍu**  
**sirula reṇḍavanāḍu śēṣuni mīda**  
**muripēna mūḍonāḍu mutyālapandirikrinda**  
**pori nālugōnāḍu puvvu kōvila lōnu....tiru..**  
**gakkana aidavanāḍu garuḍuni mīda**  
**ekkenu āravanāḍu yēnugu mīda**  
**cokkamai ēḍavanāḍu sūryaprabhalōnanu**  
**yikkuva tērunu gurra menimidōnāḍu....tiru..**  
**kanakapuṭandalamu kadisi tommidōnāḍu**  
**penaci padōnāḍu peṇḍlipīṭa**  
**enasi śrī vēṅkaṭēśuḍinti alamēlmaṅgatō**  
**vanitala naḍumanu vāhanāla mīdanu ....tiru..**  
In the sacred streets when taken on procession, He dazzles, this Lord of Lords,  
excelling in His glory along with various embellishments\!  
On the first day, the Lord moves seated on the golden palanquin  
on the second day, with all grandeur, He mounts Sesha  
on the third day, He is taken in a bower of pearls  
on the fourth day, He is seated in the flower-mantap during the procession.  
On the fifth day, He mounts Garuda  
and on the sixth day, He mounts the Elephant  
On the seventh day, He moves on the Surya Prabha Vahana  
and mounts the horse for the procession of the eighth day\!  
On the ninth day, He mounts the golden chariot  
and on the tenth day, He ascends the wedding seat\!  
Sree Venkatesha along with His consort Alamelumanga,  
dazzles amidsts the womenfolk and on all these Vahanas\!
