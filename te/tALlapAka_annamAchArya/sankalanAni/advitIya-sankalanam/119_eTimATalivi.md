**Ēṭimāṭalivi**  
एटिमाट लिवि विन निम्पय्यीना मदि \-  
नेटवॆट्टि दासुडौ टिदिसरिया ॥॥ एटिमाट ॥  
जीवुडे देवुडनि चॆप्पुदुरु गॊन्दरु  
दैवमुचेतलॆल्ला दम कुन्नवा  
आवल गॊन्दरु कर्ममदि ब्रह्म मन्दुरु  
रावणादु लवि सेसि रतिकॆक्किरा ॥॥ एटिमाट ॥  
मिगुल गॊन्दरु दैवमे लेदनॆन्दुरु  
तग नीप्रपञ्चमॆल्ला दनचेतला  
गगन मतडु निराकार मन्दुरु गॊन्द \-  
रॆगुव बुरुषसूक्त मॆऱगरा तामु ॥॥ एटिमाट ॥  
यॆनिमिदिगुणमुले यितनि वन्दुरु गॊन्द-  
रनयमु मिगिलिन ववि दमवा  
यॆनयग श्रीवेङ्कटेश्वरुदासुलै  
मनुट नित्यमुगाक मरि येमिनेला ॥॥ एटिमाट ॥  
**ēṭimāṭa livi vina nimpayyīnā madi \-**

**rāvaṇādu lavi sēsi ratikekkirā .... ēṭimāṭa ..**  
**migula gondaru daivamē lēdanenduru**  
**taga nīprapañcamellā danacētalā**  
**gagana mataḍu nirākāra manduru gonda \-**  
**reguva buruṣasūkta meṟagarā tāmu .... ēṭimāṭa**  
**yenimidiguṇamulē yitani vanduru gonda-**  
**ranayamu migilina vavi damavā**  
**yenayaga śrīvēṅkaṭēśvarudāsulai**  
**manuṭa nityamugāka mari yēminēlā .... ēṭimāṭa**  
What kind of words are these? Though pleasant to hear  
Compromising with such statements and becoming a Daasa, is it right??  
Some1 say that the Jiva itself is the Lord,  
All that the Lord does can they perform?  
Some2 others say that ‘Karma’ is Brahman  
Thinking thus, did Ravana and others indulge in sexual pleasures?  
Some3 others say that God does not exist\!  
Then all this world, is it their creation?  
Some say that He is the formless Sky, up above  
Don’t they know about the ‘PurushaSukta’?4  
Some others say that He possesses only eight qualities\!  
Then all the other attributes5, is it theirs?  
To live as SreeVenkateshwaras Daasas  
is alone eternal\! What else can be?

2.The Sankhyans  
3.The non believers  
4.Some say that ‘Akasha’ denotes Brahman. But the Purusha Sukta declares  
that the sky and everything else originates from Him alone. He is limitless,  
but exists as the indweller in everything  
5.The Lord is an ocean of limitless number of attributes and than too  
supremely auspicious ones.
