## हरि यितडु
<details><summary>विस्तारः (द्रष्टुं नोद्यम्)</summary>

This particular kirtana is promoted by smarthas to push the narrative that annamacharya was an Advaitin. The kirtana starts with calling Shiva and Vishnu to be one. 

Not present on copper plates. This is not the style of Annamacharyas compositions. There’s at least 50 plus kirtanas which state explicitly that shiva is not to be equated with Bhagavan .
</details>
