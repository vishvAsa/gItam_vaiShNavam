**Dinamudvādaśinēḍu Tīrthadivasamu**  
दिनमुद्वादशिनेडु तीर्थदिवसमु नीकु  
जनकुण्ड अन्नमाचार्युण्ड विच्चेयवे ॥॥दिनमु॥  
अनन्त गरुड मुख्यलैन सूरि जनुलतो  
घन नारदादि भागवतुलतो  
दनुज मर्दनुण्डैन दैवशिखामणितोड  
वॆनुकॊनि यारगिञ्च विच्चेयवे ॥॥दिनमु॥  
वैकुण्ठान नुण्डि आळुवारल लोपलनुण्डि  
लोकपु नित्यमुक्तुल लोन नुण्डि  
श्रीकान्त तोड नुन्न श्रीवेङ्कटेशुगूडि  
ई कड नारगिञ्च निण्टिकि विच्चेयवे ॥॥दिनमु॥  
सङ्कीर्तनमु तोड सनकादुलॆल्ल पाड  
पॊङ्कपु श्रीवॆङ्कटाद्रि भूमिनुण्डि  
लङ्कॆ श्री वेङ्कटगिरि लक्ष्मी विभुडु नीवु  
अङ्कॆल मायिण्टि विन्दुलारगिञ्चवे ॥॥दिनमु॥  
**dinamudvādaśinēḍu tīrthadivasamu nīku**  
**janakuṇḍa annamācāryuṇḍa viccēyavē ....dinamu..**  
**ananta garuḍa mukhyalaina sūri janulatō**  
**ghana nāradādi bhāgavatulatō**  
**danuja mardanuṇḍaina daivaśikhāmaṇitōḍa**  
**venukoni yāragiñca viccēyavē ....dinamu..**  
**vaikuṇṭhāna nuṇḍi āḷuvārala lōpalanuṇḍi**  
**lōkapu nityamuktula lōna nuṇḍi**  
**śrīkānta tōḍa nunna śrīvēṅkaṭēśugūḍi**  
**ī kaḍa nāragiñca niṇṭiki viccēyavē ....dinamu..**  
**saṅkīrtanamu tōḍa sanakādulella pāḍa**  
**poṅkapu śrīveṅkaṭādri bhūminuṇḍi**  
**laṅke śrī vēṅkaṭagiri lakṣmī vibhuḍu nīvu**  
**aṃkela māyiṇṭi vindulāragiñcavē ....dinamu..**  
The day is Dwadasi, the day of your demise,  
I beseech you to come, O Annamacharya, my dear father.  
Bringing along Anantha, Garuda, and the Nitya suris,  
the great Narada and other such devotees,  
and also with the destroyer of demons, the Lord of Lords,  
come to partake the offerings !  
From Vaikunta and from the hearts of the Alwars,  
from the inside of the Nityas and Muktas who transcended the mortal world,  
bringing along SreeVenkatesha with SreeMahalakshmi,  
come over to our house, to partake the offerings !  
With Sanaka and others singing sankirtanas,  
from the divine land of Sree Venkatadri,  
together, with the Lord of Lakshmi, the Lord of Venkatagiri,  
I beseech you to come as one group, to our home, to partake the offerings!
