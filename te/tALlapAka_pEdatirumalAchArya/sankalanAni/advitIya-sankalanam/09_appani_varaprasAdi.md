**Appani Varaprasādi**  
अप्पनि वरप्रसादि अन्नमय्य  
अप्पसमु माके कलडन्नमय्य ॥॥अप्पनि॥  
अन्तटिकि एलिकैन आदिनारायणु तन  
अन्तरङ्गान निलिपिन अन्नमय्य  
सन्तसान चॆलुवुन्दे सनकसनन्दनादु -  
लन्तटिवाडु ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य ॥॥अप्पनि॥  
बिरुदु टॆक्कॆमुलुगा पॆक्कु सङ्कीर्तनमुलु  
हरिमीद विन्नविञ्चॆ नन्नमय्य  
विरिविगलिगिनट्टि वेदमुल अर्थमॆल्ल  
अरसि तॆलिपिनवाडु अन्नमय्य ॥॥अप्पनि॥  
अन्दमैन रामानुजाचार्यमतमुनु  
अन्दुकॊनि निलिचिनाडु अन्नमय्य  
विन्दुवलॆ माकुनु श्री वेङ्कटनाथुनिनिच्चॆ  
अन्दरिलो ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य ॥॥अप्पनि॥  
**appani varaprasādi annamayya**  
**appasamu mākē kalaḍannamayya ....appani..**  
**aṃtaṭiki ēlikaina ādinārāyaṇu tana**  
**aṃtaraṅgāna nilipina annamayya**  
**santasāna celuvundē sanakasanandanādu -**  
**lantaṭivāḍu tāḷḷapāka annamayya ....appani..**  
**birudu ṭekkemulugā pekku saṅkīrtanamulu**  
**harimīda vinnaviñce nannamayya**  
**virivigaliginaṭṭi vēdamula arthamella**  
**arasi telipinavāḍu annamayya ....appani..**  
**aṃdamaina rāmānujācāryamatamunu**  
**aṃdukoni nilicināḍu annamayya**  
**vinduvale mākunu śrī vēṅkaṭanāthuninicce**  
**aṃdarilō tāḷḷapāka annamayya ....appani..**  
Almighty’s gifted boon is Annamayya,  
Forever he is ours, Annamayya!  
The presiding deity of the entire Universe, the primeval Narayana,  
he had placed within his heart, Annamayya!  
Reveling in supreme beatitude, as the likes of Sanaka, Sanandana,  
also is Tallapaka Annamayya!  
Ensigning His many qualities, he composed various Sankirtanas,  
on Lord Sree Hari, Annamayya!  
Having mastered the profound knowledge of the Vedas  
he taught the essence , Annamayya!  
The glorious philosophy of Ramanujacharya,  
he got initiated into and stood for it, Annamayya!  
Like a feast, he openly offered to us Sree Venkatanatha,  
that Tallapaka Annamayya!
