**Idiyenāku Matamu**  
इदियॆ नाकु मतमु इदि व्रतमु  
वुदुटुल कर्ममु वॊल्लनिङ्कनु ॥॥इदि॥  
निपुणत हरि ने निनु शरणनुटे  
तपमुलु जपमुलु धर्ममुलु  
नॆपमुन सकलमु नीवे चेकॊनु  
वुपमुल पुण्यमुलॊल्लने यिङ्कनु ॥॥इदि॥  
हरि नी दासुड ननुकॊनुटे ना  
परमुनु इहमुनु भाग्यमुनु  
धर नी मायल तप्पुतॆरुवुलनु  
वॊरगी सुकृतमु लॊल्ल ने यिङ्कनु ॥॥इदि॥  
नारायण नी नाममु दलचुट  
सारपु चदुवुलु शास्त्रमुलु  
ईरीति श्री वेङ्कटेश निन्नु गॊलिचिति  
वूरक इतरमु लॊल्ल ने यिङ्कनु ॥॥इदि॥  
**idiye nāku matamu idi vratamu**  
**vuduṭula karmamu vollaniṅkanu ....idi..**  
**nipuṇata hari nē ninu śaraṇanuṭē**  
**tapamulu japamulu dharmamulu**  
**nepamuna sakalamu nīvē cēkonu**  
**vupamula puṇyamulollanē yiṅkanu ....idi..**  
**hari nī dāsuḍa nanukonuṭē nā**  
**paramunu ihamunu bhāgyamunu**  
**dhara nī māyala tapputeruvulanu**  
**voragī sukṛtamu lolla nē yiṅkanu ....idi..**  
**nārāyaṇa nī nāmamu dalacuṭa**  
**sārapu caduvulu śāstramulu**  
**īrīti śrī vēṅkaṭēśa ninnu goliciti**  
**vūraka itaramu lolla nē yiṅkanu ....idi..**  
This itself is the philosophy for me, this is the vow  
Hastily performed actions, I will not resort to.  
O Hari, to surrender unto You is in itself a skill,  
penance, contemplation and righteousness,  
everything done under some pretext that it is for You,  
the merits arising out of such means, I will not accept  
O Hari, the mere thought that I am Your Dasa  
is my wealth for ‘here’ and ‘hereafter’  
On earth, though Your maya shows the wrong ways  
the fortunes obtained out of it, I will not accept  
O Narayana! The thought of Your name alone  
is the essence of all learning and scriptural knowledge  
In this manner, SreeVenkatesha, I worshipped You  
Anything other than this, I will not accept!
