Eṭṭihitōpadēśaku Ḍeṭuvaṇṭi
ऎट्टिहितोपदेशकु डॆटुवण्टि दयाळुवु
अट्टॆ ताळ्ळपाकन्नमाचार्युडु ॥॥ऎट्टि॥
पच्चितामसुल मम्मु बरमसात्विकुलगा
यिच्चटनॆ सेसिनाडु यॆन्तचित्तमु
इच्चगिञ्चि माकुलान नॆन्नडु लेनि वैष्णव-
मच्चमुगा गृपसेसॆ नन्नमाचार्युडु ॥॥ऎट्टि॥
मुदिरिन पापकर्ममुलु सेसिनट्टि मम्मु
यॆदुट बुण्युल जेसॆ नॆन्त सोद्यमु
कदिसि ये जन्मान गाननिसङ्कीर्तन-
मदन नुपदेशिञ्चॆ नन्नमाचार्युडु ॥॥ऎट्टि॥
गडुसुदनपु मम्मु गडुविवेकुल जेसि
यिडुमलॆल्ला बापॆ नेमरुडु (गुरुडु?)
नडुमने यन्नडु गाननि श्रीवेङ्कटनाथु-
नडियालमुग निच्चॆ नन्नमाचार्युडु ॥॥ऎट्टि॥
eṭṭihitōpadēśaku ḍeṭuvaṇṭi dayāḷuvu
aṭṭe tāḷḷapākannamācāryuḍu ….eṭṭi..
paccitāmasula mammu baramasātvikulagā
yiccaṭane sēsināḍu yentacittamu
iccagiñci mākulāna nennaḍu lēni vaiṣṇava-
maccamugā gṛpasēse nannamācāryuḍu ….eṭṭi..
mudirina pāpakarmamulu sēsinaṭṭi mammu
yeduṭa buṇyula jēse nenta sōdyamu
kadisi yē janmāna gānanisaṅkīrtana-
madana nupadēśiñce nannamācāryuḍu ….eṭṭi..
gaḍusudanapu mammu gaḍuvivēkula jēsi
yiḍumalellā bāpe nēmaruḍu (guruḍu?)
naḍumanē yannaḍu gānani śrīvēṅkaṭanāthu-
naḍiyālamuga nicce nannamācāryuḍu ….eṭṭi..
A great well wishing adviser! A highly compassionate one!
Such is Tallapaka Annamcharya!
Highly tamasik ones like us, into supremely sattvik ones
right here he transformed! What a wonder!
Annamcharya, by his free will, graced us and became the
mark of Vaishnavism, which our clan never had before!
We who had committed many a heinous sins,
he transformed into meritorious ones! What a wonder!
That Sankirtana which we never could see in our previous births
he initiated to us, our Annamacharya!
We who were full of vanity, into very wise ones he transformed
and thus averted our hardships, Our Guru!
That Sree Venkatesha whom we could never perceive earlier
like a divine token he bestowed on us, our Annamacharya!
