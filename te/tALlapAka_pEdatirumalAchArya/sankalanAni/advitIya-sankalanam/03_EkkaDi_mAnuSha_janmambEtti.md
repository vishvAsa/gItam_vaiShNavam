**Ekkaḍi Mānuṣa Janmambetti**  
ऎक्कडि मानुष जन्मम्बॆत्तिन फलमेमुन्नदि  
निक्कमु निन्ने नम्मिति नी चित्तम्बिकनु ॥॥ ऎक्कडि ॥  
मरुवनु आहरम्बुनु मरुवनु संसार सुखमु  
मरुवनु इन्द्रियभोगमु माधव नीमाय  
मरचॆद सुज्ञानम्बुनु मरचॆद तत्त्व रहस्यमु  
मरचॆद गुरुवुनु दैवमु माधव नीमाय ॥॥ ऎक्कडि ॥  
विडुवनु पापमु पुण्यमु विडुवनु नादुर्गुणमुलु  
विडुवनु मिक्किलि यासलु विष्णुड नीमाय  
विडिचॆद षट्कर्मम्बुल विडिचॆद वैराग्यम्बुनु  
विडिचॆद नाचारम्बुनु विष्णुड नीमाय ॥॥ ऎक्कडि ॥  
तगिलॆद बहुलम्पटमुल तगिलॆद बहुबन्धम्बुल  
तगुलनु मोक्षपु मार्गपु तलपुन यॆन्तैना  
अगपडि श्री वेङ्कटेश्वर अन्तर्यामिवै  
नगिनगि ननु नीवेलिति नाका यी माय ॥॥ ऎक्कडि ॥  
**ekkaḍi mānuṣa janmambettina phalamēmunnadi**  
**nikkamu ninnē nammiti nī cittambikanu ....ekkaḍi ..**  
**maruvanu āharambunu maruvanu saṃsāra sukhamu**  
**maruvanu iṃdriyabhōgamu mādhava nīmāya**  
**maraceda sujñānambunu maraceda tattva rahasyamu**  
**maraceda guruvunu daivamu mādhava nīmāya ....ekkaḍi ..**  
**viḍuvanu pāpamu puṇyamu viḍuvanu nādurguṇamulu**  
**viḍuvanu mikkili yāsalu viṣṇuḍa nīmāya**  
**viḍiceda ṣaṭkarmambula viḍiceda vairāgyambunu**  
**viḍiceda nācārambunu viṣṇuḍa nīmāya ....ekkaḍi ..**  
**tagileda bahulampaṭamula tagileda bahubandhambula**  
**tagulanu mōkṣapu mārgapu talapuna yentainā**  
**agapaḍi śrī vēṅkaṭēśvara aṃtaryāmivai**  
**naginagi nanu nīvēliti nākā yī māya ....ekkaḍi ..**  
What is the use of being born as a human?  
unless I believe in You always, in my mind!  
I don’t forget about food, nor about the comforts of Samsara  
I don’t forget the pleasures of the senses, O Madhava! It is your maya!  
I forget the right knowledge, and the secrets of the Supreme truths  
I forget my Guru, I forget the Lord, O Madhava! It is your maya!  
I don’t let go of sins and merits, neither my bad ways  
I don’t let go of the many desires, O Vishnu! It is your maya!  
I let go of the six main karmas, and even the dispassion  
I let go of my customs, O Vishnu! It is your maya!  
I come into contact with pleasures and attachments  
Never with the path to Moksha, not even in my thoughts!  
After You have manifested, O SreeVenkateshwara, as my inner self  
and gloriously ruled over me, why still for me this maya??
