**Ītaḍē Muktidōva Yītaḍē**  
ईतडे मुक्तिदोव यीतडे मा याचार्यु-  
डीतडु गलुगबट्टि इन्दरु बदिकिरि ॥॥ईतडे॥  
अदिवो ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमाचार्युलु  
यिदॆ वीडॆ श्रीवेङ्कटेशु नॆदुट  
वॆदवॆट्टि लोकमुलो वेदमुलन्निय मञ्चि-  
पदमुलु सेसि पाडि पावनमुसेसॆनु ॥॥ईतडे॥  
अलरुचु दाळ्ळपाक अन्नमाचार्युलु  
निलिचि श्रीवेङ्कटनिधिये तानै  
कलिदोषमुलु वाप घनपुराणमुलॆल्ल  
पलुकुल निञ्चि निञ्चि पाडिनाडु हरिनि ॥॥ईतडे॥  
अङ्गविञ्चॆ दाळ्ळपाक अन्नमाचार्युलु  
बङ्गारु श्रीवॆङ्कटेशु पादमुलन्दु  
रङ्गुमीऱ श्रीवेङ्कटरमणुनि यलमेलु-  
मङ्गनु यिद्दरि बाडि मम्मु गरुणिञ्चॆनु ॥॥ईतडे॥  
**ītaḍē muktidōva yītaḍē mā yācāryu-**  
**ḍītaḍu galugabaṭṭi iṃdaru badikiri ....ītaḍē..**  
**adivō tāḷḷapāka annamācāryulu**  
**yide vīḍe śrīvēṅkaṭēśu neduṭa**  
**vedaveṭṭi lōkamulō vēdamulanniyu mañci-**  
**padamulu sēsi pāḍi pāvanamusēsenu ....ītaḍē..**  
**alarucu dāḷḷapāka annamācāryulu**  
**nilici śrīvēṅkaṭanidhiyē tānai**  
**kalidōṣamulu vāpa ghanapurāṇamulella**  
**palukula niñci niñci pāḍināḍu harini ....ītaḍē..**  
**aṃgaviñce dāḷḷapāka annamācāryulu**  
**baṅgāru śrīveṅkaṭēśu pādamulandu**  
**raṅgumīṟa śrīvēṅkaṭaramaṇuni yalamēlu-**  
**maṅganu yiddari bāḍi mammu garuṇiñcenu ....ītaḍē..**  
He alone is the way to salvation! He alone is our Acharya!  
Because of him, everyone survived!  
Behold! Tallapaka Annamacharya he is!  
He is right in front of Sree Venkatesha!  
Spreading the Vedas in all the worlds, he composed them into songs  
and having sung them, he bestowed purity!  
Joyously, Tallapaka Annamacharya stood as  
the very embodiment of the treasure of Sree Venkatesha!  
To absolve the sins of Kali-Yuga, all the great Puranas,  
he filled into the words of his songs, and sung about Hari!  
He, Tallapaka Annamacharya, merged into  
the golden feet of Sree Venkatesha!  
In a beautiful manner, he sang on both Lord Sree Venkatesha  
and Alamelumanga, and graced all of us!
