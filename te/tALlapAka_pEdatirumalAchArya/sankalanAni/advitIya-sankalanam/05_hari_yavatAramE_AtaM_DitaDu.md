**Hari Yavatārame Ātaṃ Ḍitaḍu**  
हरि यवतारमॆ आतं डितडु  
परमु सङ्कीर्तन फलमुलो निलिपॆ ॥॥ हरि ॥  
उन्नाडु वैकुण्ठमुन उन्नाडु आचार्युनॊद्द  
उन्नतोन्नत महिम अन्नमय्य  
उन्नवि सङ्कीर्तनलु ऒट्टुक लोकमुलन्दु  
पन्निन नारदादुलु पैपैपाडगानु ॥॥ हरि ॥  
चरियिञ्चु नॊकवेळ सनकादिमुनुललो  
हरिबाडु ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य  
तिरमै याळुवारल तेजमु तानै यण्डु  
गरुडानन्त मुख्य घनुल सङ्गडिनि ॥॥ हरि ॥  
श्री वेङ्कटाद्रिमीद श्रीपतिकॊलुवुनन्दु  
आवहिञ्चे ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य  
देवतलु मुनुलुनु देवुण्डनि जयवॆट्ट  
कोविदुडै तिरुगाडी कोनेटि दण्डनु ॥॥ हरि ॥  
**hari yavatārame ātaṃ ḍitaḍu**  
**paramu saṅkīrtana phalamulō nilipe .... hari ..**  
**unnāḍu vaikuṇṭhamuna unnāḍu ācāryunodda**  
**unnatōnnata mahima annamayya**  
**unnavi saṅkīrtanalu oṭṭuka lōkamulandu**  
**pannina nāradādulu paipaipāḍagānu .... hari ..**  
**cariyiñcu nokavēḷa sanakādimunulalō**  
**haribāḍu tāḷḷapāka annamayya**  
**tiramai yāḷuvārala tējamu tānai yuṇḍu**  
**garuḍānanta mukhya ghanula saṅgaḍini .... hari ..**  
**śrī vēṅkaṭādrimīda śrīpatikoluvunandu**  
**āvahiñcē tāḷḷapāka annamayya**  
**dēvatalu munulunu dēvuṇḍani jayaveṭṭa**  
**kōviduḍai tirugāḍī kōnēṭi daṇḍanu .... hari ..**  
Hari’s avathara he is, ‘He’ only he is  
He made the supreme to be the fruits of his Sankirtanas!  
He is there in Vaikunta, he is there along with Acharya,  
the highest of highest glories is Annamayya!  
The are there, the Sankirtanas, in all worlds,  
which Narada and others sing everywhere!  
At one time he moves about with Sanaka and other sages,  
singing about Hari, Tallapaka Annamayya!  
Eternally, he himself remains as the brilliance of the Alwars,  
in the company of greats like Garuda and Anantha!  
On Sree Venkatadri, in the court of Sree-Pathi  
he joyously pervaded, Tallapaka Annamayya!  
As demigods and sages constantly praise the Lord,  
as a highly learned one he did so, and moved about near the Koneru!!
