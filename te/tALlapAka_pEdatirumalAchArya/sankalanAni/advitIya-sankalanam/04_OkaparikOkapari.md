**Okaparikokapari**  
ऒकपरि कॊकपरि कॊय्यारमै  
मॊकमुन कळलॆल्ल मॊलचिनट्लुण्डॆ ॥॥ ऒक ॥  
जगदेकपतिमेन चल्लिन कर्पूरधूळि  
जिगिकॊनि नलुवङ्क चिन्दगानु  
मॊगि चन्द्रमुखि नुरमुन निलिपॆगान  
पॊगरु वॆन्नॆल दिगबोसिनट्लुण्डॆ ॥॥ ऒक ॥  
पॊरिमॆरगु चॆक्कुल पूसिन तट्टुपुनुगु  
करगि इरुदॆसल कारगानु  
करिगमन विभुडु गनुक मोहमदमु  
तॊरिगि सामजसिरि तॊलिकिनट्लुण्डॆ ॥॥ ऒक ॥  
मॆरय श्रीवेङ्कटेशुमेन सिङ्गारमुगानु  
तरचैन सॊम्मुलु धरियिञ्चगा  
मॆरगु बोडि अलमेलु मङ्गय तानु  
मॆरुपु मेघमु गूडि मॆरसिनट्लुण्डॆ ॥॥ ऒक ॥  
**okapari kokapari koyyāramai**  
**mokamuna kaḷalella molacinaṭluṇḍe .... oka ..**  
**jagadēkapatimēna callina karpūradhūḷi**  
**jigikoni naluvaṅka cindagānu**  
**mogi candramukhi nuramuna nilipegāna**  
**pogaru vennela digabōsinaṭluṇḍe .... oka ..**  
**porimeragu cekkula pūsina taṭṭupunugu**  
**karagi irudesala kāragānu**  
**karigamana vibhuḍu ganuka mōhamadamu**  
**torigi sāmajasiri tolikinaṭluṇḍe .... oka ..**  
**meraya śrīvēṅkaṭēśumēna siṅgāramugānu**  
**taracaina sommulu dhariyiñcagā**  
**meragu bōḍi alamēlu maṅgayu tānu**  
**merupu mēghamu gūḍi merasinaṭluṇḍe .... oka ..**  
Each and every time, it is more beautiful than the previous time!  
It is as though all forms of art are sprouting from this countenance of the Lord!  
Taking along the brilliance of the One Lord of the Universe,  
the fine camphor powder spreads around!  
Since He has the moon faced consort on His breast,  
it seems as though there is thick moonlight radiating all around!  
The perfumed oil applied to His cheeks,  
is running down along both sides!  
Since He is the Lord of the one with the gait of an elephant (Lakshmi)  
it seems as though an elephant is secreting his flow of passion!  
The shine of Lord Venkateshwaras brilliant body,  
bedecked with sparkling ornaments  
and adorned by an equally radiant beauty of Alamelumanga  
seems as though the cloud and the lightning are shining together!
