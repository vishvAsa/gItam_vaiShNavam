**Telisinavāriki Teruvidi**  
तॆलिसिनवारिकि तॆरुविदि मरि लेदु  
नलिनाक्षु बॊगडॆडि नाममुलो नुन्नदि!॥ तॆलिसि ॥  
आकसान लेदु मोक्ष मटु पाताळमुन ले  
दीकड भूलोकमन्दु यॆन्दुलेदु  
पै कॊनि आसलॆल्ल पारदोलि वॆदकिते  
श्रीकान्तु बॊगडेटि चित्तमुलो नुन्नदि!॥ तॆलिसि ॥  
सुरलवद्द लेदु सोदिञ्च वमृतमु  
सरिदिक्कुलन्दु लेदु जलधिलो लेदु  
शरणागतुल पादजलमुलु चेर्चिकॊने  
हरिदासुल बूजिञ्चे अरचेतनुन्नदि!॥ तॆलिसि ॥  
राजसान सुखमेदि दासि कर्ममन्दु नेदि  
वोजतोड नियतुडै वुण्डेवानिदि  
साजानि श्रीवेङ्कटेशु सरिमुद्रलु धरिञ्चे  
तेजमुतो विज्ञानदेहमुलो वुन्नदि!॥ तॆलिसि ॥  
**telisinavāriki teruvidi mari lēdu**  
**nalinākṣu bogaḍeḍi nāmamulō nunnadi!.. telisi ..**  
**ākasāna lēdu mōkṣa maṭu pātāḷamuna lē**  
**dīkaḍa bhūlōkamandu yendulēdu**  
**pai koni āsalella pāradōli vedakitē**  
**śrīkāntu bogaḍēṭi cittamulō nunnadi!.. telisi ..**  
**suralavadda lēdu sōdiñca vamṛtamu**  
**saridikkulandu lēdu jaladhilō lēdu**  
**śaraṇāgatula pādajalamulu cērcikonē**  
**haridāsula būjiñcē aracētanunnadi!.. telisi ..**  
**rājasāna sukhamēdi dāsi karmamandu nēdi**  
**vōjatōḍa niyatuḍai vuṇḍēvānidi**  
**sājāni śrīvēṅkaṭēśu sarimudralu dhariñcē**  
**tējamutō vijñānadēhamulō vunnadi!.. telisi ..**  
For those who know, this is the way, there is none other!  
It is in praising the name of the Lotus eyed one!  
It is not in the sky, Moksha is not even in the nether world,  
not even here, anywhere on earth!  
Driving away all the overpowering desires, if sought  
it exists in the heart that praises the Lord of Sree!  
It is not with the Gods, even if you search, this Amrutha,  
not even in any direction, not even in the ocean!  
In the palms that accept holy water from the feet of Sharanagatas  
and in the palms that worship Hari-dasas, it exists!  
Where is the pleasure in kingship, where in doing menial works for others  
In those wo are diligent it exists!  
Those who whole heartedly wear SreeVenkateshwaras true Mudras  
in such enlightened bodies, brilliantly it exists!
