**Saṅkelēka Talacina Jāṇalaku**  
सङ्कॆलेक तलचिन जाणलकु  
सङ्कीर्तनमॆ पो सर्वशान्ति!॥ सङ्कॆ ॥  
भवहरमगु श्रीपति नाममॆ  
जवकट्टि तलचिते जन्मशान्ति  
तविलि हरिपूज ताजेयटे  
कुवलयमुन निदिगो गृहशान्ति!॥ सङ्कॆ ॥  
अन्दि धृववरदुड यनिनदॆ  
कन्दुवैन सर्वग्रहशान्ति  
यिन्दु रविनयनुनि नॆरुगुटॆ  
चन्दमैनदिदिवो नक्षत्रशान्ति!॥ सङ्कॆ ॥  
भाविञ्चि यनन्तुनि बलुकुटॆ  
काविञ्चिन दिदिवो कालशान्ति  
श्री वॆङ्कटनाधुनि चेरुटे  
देव मानवुलकुनु देहशान्ति!॥ सङ्कॆ ॥  
**saṅkelēka talacina jāṇalaku**  
**saṅkīrtaname pō sarvaśānti!.. saṅke ..**  
**bhavaharamagu śrīpati nāmame**  
**javakaṭṭi talacitē janmaśānti**  
**tavili haripūja tājēyuṭē**  
**kuvalayamuna nidigō gṛhaśānti!.. saṅke ..**  
**aṃdi dhṛvavaraduḍa yaninade**  
**kanduvaina sarvagrahaśānti**  
**yindu ravinayanuni neruguṭe**  
**candamainadidivō nakṣatraśānti!.. saṅke ..**  
**bhāviñci yanantuni balukuṭe**  
**kāviñcina didivō kālaśānti**  
**śrī veṅkaṭanādhuni cēruṭē**  
**dēva mānavulakunu dēhaśānti!.. saṅke ..**  
For wise people who believe without doubts,  
Sankirtana itself is the ‘Sarva-shanti’  
The destroyer of the miseries of Samsara, the name of Sree-Pathi  
eagerly contemplating on it bestows ‘Janma-shanti’  
Unfailingly if one performs the worship of Hari  
in this world, that itself bestows ‘Griha-shanti’  
If one realizes and chants the name of the Lord who blessed Dhruva,  
that alone is the appropriate ‘Graha-shanti’  
Knowing the one who has the sun and moon as eyes  
that itself bestows the beautiful ‘Nakshatra-shanti’  
With thoughts fixed on Anantha, saying His name  
that itself bestows ‘Kaala-shanti’  
Reaching unto SreeVenkatanatha is itself,  
for Gods and humans, the ‘Deha-shanti’!
