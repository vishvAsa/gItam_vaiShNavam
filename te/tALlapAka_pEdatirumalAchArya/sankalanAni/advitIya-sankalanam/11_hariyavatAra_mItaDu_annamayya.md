**Hariyavatāra Mītaḍu Annamayya**  
हरियवतार मीतडु अन्नमय्य  
अरय मा गुरुडीतडु अन्नमय्य ॥॥ हरि ॥  
वैकुण्ठनाथुनि वद्द वडिपाडुचुन्नवाडु  
आकरमै ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य  
आकसपु विष्णुपादमन्दु नित्यमै उन्नवाडु  
आकडीकड ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य ॥॥ हरि ॥  
क्षीराब्धिशायि निट्टे सेविम्पुचु नुन्नवाडु  
आरितेरि ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य  
धीरुडै सूर्यमण्डल तेजमुवद्द नुन्नवाडु  
आ रीतुल ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य ॥॥ हरि ॥  
ईवल संसारलील इन्दिरेशुतो नुन्नवाडु  
आवटिञ्चि ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य  
भाविम्प श्री वेण्टेशु पादमुलन्दु उन्नवाडु  
हावभावमै ताळ्ळपाक अन्नमय्य ॥॥ हरि ॥  
**hariyavatāra mītaḍu annamayya**  
**araya mā guruḍītaḍu annamayya .... hari ..**  
**vaikuṇṭhanāthuni vadda vaḍipāḍucunnavāḍu**  
**ākaramai tāḷḷapāka annamayya**  
**ākasapu viṣṇupādamandu nityamai unnavāḍu**  
**ākaḍīkaḍa tāḷḷapāka annamayya .... hari ..**  
**kṣīrābdhiśāyi niṭṭē sēvimpucu nunnavāḍu**  
**āritēri tāḷḷapāka annamayya**  
**dhīruḍai sūryamaṇḍala tējamuvadda nunnavāḍu**  
**ā rītula tāḷḷapāka annamayya .... hari ..**  
**īvala saṃsāralīla iṃdirēśutō nunnavāḍu**  
**āvaṭiñci tāḷḷapāka annamayya**  
**bhāvimpa śrī vēṇṭēśu pādamulandu unnavāḍu**  
**hāvabhāvamai tāḷḷapāka annamayya .... hari ..**  
Hari’s avatara he is, Annamayya!  
When understood rightly, our preceptor, Annamayya!  
In the presence of the Lord of Vaikunta, he sings with celerity  
the abundantly endowed Tallapaka Annamayya!  
In the Heavens, eternally residing at the feet of Vishnu,  
everywhere he is present, Tallapaka Annamayya!  
In serving the Lord who reclines on the milky ocean,  
he excels, Tallapaka Annamayya!  
A valiant one close to the brilliance of the orb of the sun  
In this manner he exists, Tallapaka Annamayya!  
Though in the grip of samsara, being close to the Lord of Indira  
he led his life, Tallapaka Annamayya!  
If perceived, he is at the feet of Sree Venkatesha  
Exulting with total adoration, Tallapaka Annamayya!!
