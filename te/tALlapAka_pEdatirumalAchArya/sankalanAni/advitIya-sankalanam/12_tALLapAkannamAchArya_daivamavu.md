**Tāḷḷapākannamācārya Daivamavu**  
ताळ्ळपाकन्नमाचार्य दैवमवु नीवु माकु  
वेळमॆ श्रीहरि गनेवॆर वानतिच्चितिवि ॥॥ ताळ्ळ ॥  
गुरुडवु नीवेसुम्मी कुमति नैननाकु  
सरवि ब्रह्मोपदेशमु सेसिति  
परमबन्धुड वैना परिकिम्प नीवे सुम्मी  
वरुस ने जॆडकुण्ड वहिञ्चुकॊण्टिवि ॥॥ ताळ्ळ ॥  
तल्लिवैन नीवेसुम्मी तगिन विषयाललो  
पल्लदान बडकुण्डा ब्रदिकिञ्चिति  
अल्लुकॊनि तोडुनीडवैना नीवे सुम्मी  
चिल्लरमायललोन जॆडकुण्डा जेसिति ॥॥ ताळ्ळ ॥  
दातवु नीवे सुम्मी तगु श्रीवॆङ्कटनाथु  
नातलपुलो निलिपि नम्म जेसिति  
येतल जाचिना नाकु नेडुगडय नीवॆ  
आतल नातल नन्नु नादुकॊनि काचिति ॥॥ ताळ्ळ ॥  
**tāḷḷapākannamācārya daivamavu nīvu māku**  
**vēḷame śrīhari ganēvera vānaticcitivi .... tāḷḷa ..**  
**guruḍavu nīvēsummī kumati nainanāku**  
**saravi brahmōpadēśamu sēsiti**  
**paramabandhuḍa vainā parikimpa nīvē summī**  
**varusa nē jeḍakuṇḍa vahiñcukoṇṭivi .... tāḷḷa ..**  
**tallivaina nīvēsummī tagina viṣayālalō**  
**palladāna baḍakuṇḍā bradikiñciti**  
**allukoni tōḍunīḍavainā nīvē summī**  
**cillaramāyalalōna jeḍakuṇḍā jēsiti .... tāḷḷa ..**  
**dātavu nīvē summī tagu śrīveṅkaṭanāthu**  
**nātalapulō nilipi namma jēsiti**  
**yētala jūcinā nāku nēḍugaḍayu nīve**  
**ātala nātala nannu nādukoni kāciti .... tāḷḷa ..**  
O Tallapaka Annamacharya! You are the God for us!  
You showed us the means to perceive Sree Hari!  
You alone are the Guru for me, an ignorant one I am,  
you performed my Brahmopadesham,  
you alone are my supreme relation if understood rightly,  
you took care to ensure that I do not go astray!  
In certain matters, you are like a mother  
you saved me from falling for undesirable things  
you were my constant companion,  
preventing me from getting lured by cheap trickery!  
You are the bestower! You placed Sree Venkatanatha  
in my thoughts and made me believe in Him!  
In which ever manner perceived, you are my protector  
In every way you rescued me and guarded me!
