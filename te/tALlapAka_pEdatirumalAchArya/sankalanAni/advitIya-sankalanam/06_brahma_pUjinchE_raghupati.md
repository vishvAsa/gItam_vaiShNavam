**Brahma Pūjiñce Raghupati**  
ब्रह्म पूजिञ्चॆ रघुपति विभीषणुन किच्चॆ  
ब्रह्मण्युडी डीरङ्गपति गॊलुवरो ॥॥ ब्रह्म ॥  
कावेरीमध्य रङ्गक्षेत्र मल्लदिगो  
श्रीविमान मदिगो शेषपर्यङ्क मिदे  
देवुं डल्लदे वाडे देवि श्रीलक्ष्मियदे  
सेविञ्चरो नाभिं जिगुरिञ्चॆ नजुण्डु ॥॥ ब्रह्म ॥  
एडुगोटलु नविगो यॆसगपूदोपुलवे  
कूडि दामोदरपुरगोपुर मदे  
तोग वेयिगम्बाल दॊड्डमण्टप मदिगो  
चूडरो पसिडिमिञ्चुलकम्ब मदिगो ॥॥ ब्रह्म ॥  
आळुवारलु वारे यङ्गरङ्गविभव मदे  
वालु श्रीवैष्णवपुजाड लविगो  
आलील श्रीवेङ्कटेशुण्डै वरमच्ची नी  
तालिमुल श्रीरङ्गदैवमुङ्गॊलुवरो ॥॥ ब्रह्म ॥  
**brahma pūjiñce raghupati vibhīṣaṇuna kicce**  
**brahmaṇyuḍī ḍīraṅgapati goluvarō .... brahma ..**  
**kāvērīmadhya raṅgakṣētra malladigō**  
**śrīvimāna madigō śēṣaparyaṅka midē**  
**dēvuṃ ḍalladē vāḍē dēvi śrīlakṣmiyadē**  
**sēviñcarō nābhiṃ jiguriñce najuṇḍu .... brahma ..**  
**ēḍugōṭalu navigō yesagapūdōpulavē**  
**kūḍi dāmōdarapuragōpura madē**  
**tōga vēyigambāla doḍḍamaṇṭapa madigō**  
**cūḍarō pasiḍimiñculakamba madigō .... brahma ..**  
**āḷuvāralu vārē yaṅgaraṅgavibhava madē**  
**vālu śrīvaiṣṇavapujāḍa lavigō**  
**ālīla śrīvēṅkaṭēśuṇḍai varamaccī nī**  
**tālimula śrīraṅgadaivamuṅgoluvarō .... brahma ..**  
Lord Brahma worshipped Him, Lord Rama gave Him to Vibheeshana!  
The Supreme Brahman is the Lord of Srirangam! Offer worship unto Him!  
Behold! The holy city of Srirangam, in the middle of Kaveri  
Behold the Sree Vimanam, and the couch of Sesha!  
The Lord Himself is there, and Goddess Lakshmi is there  
Worship this Lord from whose navel Brahma sprouted!  
Behold the seven forts, the beautiful flower gardens,  
and the gopuram of Lord Damodara!  
Behold the mantapam with a thousand pillars  
Behold the golden pillar in all splendor!  
Here are the Alwars! Behold the excellent Utsavas!  
Behold here all the glorious signs of Sree Vaishnavism!  
In this manner, as Sree Venkatesha He blesses us  
Worship whole heartedly this Lord of Srirangam!
