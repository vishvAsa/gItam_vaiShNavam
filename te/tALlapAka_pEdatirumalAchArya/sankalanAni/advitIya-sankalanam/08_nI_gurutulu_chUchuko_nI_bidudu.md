**Nī Gurutulu Cūcukō Nī Bidudu**  
नी गुरुतुलु चूचुको नी बिदुदु लॆञ्चुको  
यीगतिनि कलबण्टु निक नन्नु गाववे ॥॥नी गुरु॥  
कानीले नेनॆन्त कठिनचित्तुडनैन  
ना नॊसल निवे पट्टॆ नाममुलु  
मेनु तोलु नॆम्मुलतो मॆरसिनदैनानु  
पूनिति दम्मितुलसि पूसलपेरुलु॥॥नी गुरु॥  
भ्रमसि ने जेसिनदि पापकर्ममुलैनानु  
जमळिभुजाल निवे शङ्खचक्रालु  
अमरदऱचुगा नेनाडेवि कल्ललैना  
तमि बाडीनालिक नीतगु सङ्कीर्तनलु॥॥नी गुरु॥  
तलचिते नावोज तामसगुणमैनानु  
पिलिचेदि भुविलो नापेरु तिम्मडु  
अलर श्री वेङ्कटेश अन्निटने दुष्टुनैना  
तलकॊनॆ नायन्दु दासरितनमुलु॥॥नी गुरु॥  
**nī gurutulu cūcukō nī bidudu leñcukō**  
**yīgatini kalabaṇṭu nika nannu gāvavē ....nī guru..**  
**kānīlē nēnenta kaṭhinacittuḍanaina**  
**nā nosala nivē paṭṭe nāmamulu**  
**mēnu tōlu nemmulatō merasinadainānu**  
**pūniti dammitulasi pūsalapērulu....nī guru..**  
**bhramasi nē jēsinadi pāpakarmamulainānu**  
**jamaḷibhujāla nivē śaṅkhacakrālu**  
**amaradaṟacugā nēnāḍēvi kallalainā**  
**tami bāḍīnālika nītagu saṅkīrtanalu....nī guru..**  
**talacitē nāvōja tāmasaguṇamainānu**  
**pilicēdi bhuvilō nāpēru timmaḍu**  
**alara śrī vēṅkaṭēśa anniṭanē duṣṭunainā**  
**talakone nāyandu dāsaritanamulu....nī guru..**  
Behold these signs of Yours on me! Consider these marks of Yours!  
As I am your servant with all these, do protect me,  
Even if I am a very heard hearted one  
on my forehead there is the naamam!  
Though my body is made up of skin and bones  
I wear the subjugating strings of Tulasi beads!  
Even if I commit sinful deeds under illusion  
upon my shoulders are the marks of the Shanka-Chakra!  
Though I frequently may utter lies,  
with eagerness, my tongue sings Your Sankirtanas!  
To say rightly, my nature is that of Tamas  
Yet, upon this earth I am known as ‘Timmadu’1  
O Venkatesha! Even if I am evil in many ways  
there exist in me many qualities of a true Vaishnava servant!  
1.Pedda Tirumalacharyas name is the appellation of God
